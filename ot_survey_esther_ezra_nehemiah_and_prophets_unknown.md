# OT Survey: Esther, Ezra, Nehemiah, and Prophets
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview of Old Testament survey materials covering the historical books of Esther, Ezra, and Nehemiah, followed by an analytical study of the post-exilic prophets Haggai, Zechariah, and Malachi. It explores the themes of return from exile, temple restoration, religious reform, and the Messianic hope. Key historical figures include Zerubbabel, Ezra, and Nehemiah. The study connects the historical efforts to rebuild the temple and social order with the prophetic messages of encouragement and correction, culminating in the anticipation of a future spiritual king and priest.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Post-Exilic Renewal
The theological and practical process of restoring Jewish identity, temple worship, and community life after the Babylonian exile under Persian rule.

### Theme: Messianic Expectation
The prophetic anticipation of a future leader who embodies the unity of priest and king, often referenced in the context of Zerubbabel and the ultimate messianic vision.

### Theme: Religious and Social Reform
The efforts led by Ezra and Nehemiah, and later exhorted by Malachi, to address mixed marriages, social injustice, and spiritual apathy to align the people with God's law.
