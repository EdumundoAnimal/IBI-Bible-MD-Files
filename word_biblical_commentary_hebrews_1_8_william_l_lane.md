## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
The commentary highlights how the author of Hebrews constructs a complex argument to establish the pre-eminence of the Son over angelic beings through specific scriptural citations.

### Theme: Structure and Composition
The text analyzes the 'internal structure' of the passage, utilizing alternating segments of exposition and exhortation, often arranged in chiasmic or parallel patterns.

### Theme: Old Testament Usage
A deep study of how the writer of Hebrews utilizes a chain of Old Testament quotes to prove the Son's status as mediator and ruler, often incorporating interpretive traditions common to the era.
