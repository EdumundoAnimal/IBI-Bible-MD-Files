# Christology: Biblical Theology Course Syllabus
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This course in Christology is designed to help students understand the theological content of important biblical passages concerning the person of Jesus. Participants will reflect on how various portrayals of Jesus in the New Testament fit together within contexts. Aims include becoming equipped to interpret the Bible with awareness of its diversity and unity, and interacting critically with church history interpretations. The course covers course descriptions, assessment criteria including seminar presentations and essay submissions, and provides a bibliography of essential Christology texts.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Exegesis
The systematic examination of New Testament texts, specifically Hebrews 1-2, to understand the person and role of Jesus through both historical and modern theological lenses.

### Theme: Christological Unity and Diversity
The tension between the various biblical portrayals of Jesus and the pursuit of a unified, holistic Christian understanding of his identity.

### Theme: Practical Application
The pedagogical requirement that academic theological study must translate into personal faith and relevance for life within the church and the world.
