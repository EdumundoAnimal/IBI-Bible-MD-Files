# Christian View of God and the World
**Author:** [[James Orr]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text presents the Kerr Lectures for 1890-91, authored by Dr. James Orr. The document explores the doctrine of the Trinity and the Incarnation. It addresses theological concepts such as 'economic' vs 'ontological' Trinity, the nature of God, and the distinction of persons within the Godhead. The analysis includes a historical review of Greek and Latin terminology ('substance' and 'person') and critiques various theories including Sabellianism and Unitarian views. It further examines analogies for the Trinity based on human mind, memory, and will.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Doctrine
The text examines the distinction of three persons within the single essence of the Godhead, clarifying the historical Greek and Latin interpretations.

### Theme: Economic vs. Ontological Trinity
The author distinguishes between the 'economic' view, which focuses on the outward roles of Father, Son, and Spirit in redemption, and the ontological reality of their inner relations.

### Theme: Incarnation
The argument that the Incarnation of the Son is compatible with a rational understanding of God, rejecting the notion that Jesus is merely a man.
