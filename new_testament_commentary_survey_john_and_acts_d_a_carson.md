## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Criteria for Commentary Evaluation
Carson evaluates commentaries based on their handling of Greek text, historical-critical methodology, theological depth, and utility for contemporary preachers versus academic researchers.

### Theme: Evolution of Biblical Studies
The text traces the shift from traditional historical-critical analysis to more modern literary, socio-rhetorical, and narrative approaches in New Testament studies.
