## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of the Samaritan Pentateuch and a detailed exegetical commentary on John 7:37-41. The latter half of the text focuses on the Johannine passage regarding 'living water,' analyzing the historical context of the water-drawing rite during the festival, the theological significance of the Spirit as the source of life, and the subsequent division among the crowd regarding Jesus' messianic identity.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Samaritan Identity
Historical perspective on the Samaritan community, their relationship with the Jewish temple at Mt. Gerizim, and their distinctive textual traditions centered on the Torah.

### Theme: Johannine Exegesis
Analysis of John 7:37-39, specifically the 'living water' metaphor, connecting it to Jewish temple rituals (water-drawing) and the later outpouring of the Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Messianic Expectation
Exploration of how the title 'the prophet' and 'the Messiah' were perceived in the first century, particularly regarding the expectation of a 'prophet like Moses' in the context of the Fourth Gospel.
