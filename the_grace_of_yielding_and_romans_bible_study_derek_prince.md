# The Grace of Yielding and Romans Bible Study
**Author:** [[Derek Prince]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a spiritual framework for the Christian principle of yielding, emphasizing surrendering to God's will over self-assertion. It outlines biblical examples of yielding, such as Abraham's faith in sacrificing Isaac and Jesus as the ultimate model. Additionally, it offers a guide for thanking and praising God through scriptural references. The latter part of the document serves as a study guide for Romans chapters 1-4, focusing on themes of justification by faith, the reality of human sinfulness, and the nature of Abraham's faith.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Principle of Yielding
Yielding is presented as a spiritual discipline of maturity, where believers surrender their own rights and self-assertion to align with God's will, mirroring the nature of Christ.

### Theme: Justification by Faith
Drawing from the book of Romans, the text argues that justification is an objective status credited by God through faith, independent of human works or adherence to the Mosaic law.

### Theme: The Reality of Human Sinfulness
Sin is described as a fundamental corruption that manifests in human legal standing, minds, motives, wills, and interpersonal relationships, necessitating reliance on God's grace.
