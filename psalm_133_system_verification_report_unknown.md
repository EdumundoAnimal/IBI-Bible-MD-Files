## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Solidarity
The psalm emphasizes that true unity is not a human-manufactured social contract but a vertical, descending gift from God, modeled on the High Priestly anointing and the life-giving dew of Hermon.

### Theme: The Hermon-Zion Paradox
The text contrasts the physical majesty and moisture of Mount Hermon in the north with the arid, modest hill of Zion, illustrating that life-giving vitality is supernaturally channeled to the sanctuary by divine decree rather than natural geography.
