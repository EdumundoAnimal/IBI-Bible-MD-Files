# The 2012 Prayer & Fasting Schedule & Guidelines
**Author:** [[Apostle Alton R. Williams]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document serves as a comprehensive guide for a 21-day prayer and fasting program focused on household salvation. It includes specific prayers, theological justifications for fasting, practical guidelines for conduct during the fast, and a detailed breakdown of permitted and prohibited foods for the Daniel Fast. The overarching theme is the application of biblical principles to achieve spiritual breakthrough and personal discipline.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Fasting Doctrine
Fasting is portrayed as a voluntary act of self-denial aimed at crucifying the flesh, gaining spiritual clarity, and increasing sensitivity to God's voice.

### Theme: The Daniel Fast
A specific dietary regimen based on Daniel 10:2-3, characterized by the consumption of plant-based whole foods and the avoidance of meat, dairy, refined sugars, and stimulants.

### Theme: Intercessory Prayer
The alignment of personal fasting with the specific goal of household salvation, utilizing biblical precedents like the salvation of the Philippian jailer's household (Acts 16:31).
