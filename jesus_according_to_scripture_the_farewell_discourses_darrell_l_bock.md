# Jesus according to Scripture: The Farewell Discourses
**Author:** [[Darrell L. Bock]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of scholarly commentary and structural outlines regarding the Farewell Discourses of Jesus, specifically focusing on John 15. The text examines key theological concepts such as the 'True Vine' metaphor, the role of the Advocate (Holy Spirit), the nature of the disciple's mission, and the relationship between love, obedience, and endurance in a hostile world.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
A central metaphor in John 15 illustrating the organic, dependent relationship between Jesus and his followers. Abiding in the vine is presented as the prerequisite for spiritual fruitfulness.

### Theme: Missional Identity
The document emphasizes that disciples are chosen and appointed to bear fruit, which involves both internal spiritual development and the external mission to the world.

### Theme: The Role of the Advocate
The Holy Spirit is identified as the witness for Jesus who empowers and guides believers, serving as a critical support in the context of global hostility.

### Theme: Relational Obedience
Obedience is not legalistic but grounded in the love between the Father and the Son, extended to the disciples as a marker of their friendship with Jesus.
