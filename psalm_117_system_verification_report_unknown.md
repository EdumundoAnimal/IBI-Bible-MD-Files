## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Universalism
The argument that the specific covenantal mercy shown to Israel is the catalyst and portal for universal salvation and the inclusion of all nations (gôyīm) into the worship of YHWH.

### Theme: Redemptive Doxology
The concept that praise is not a blind emotion but a rational, objective response to the historical, conquering power of God's grace (hesed) and truth (emet).

### Theme: Eschatological Ingathering
The biblical trajectory where the summons of the nations to praise God in Psalm 117 finds its ultimate consummation in the unified choir of every tribe, tongue, and nation before the Lamb in Revelation.
