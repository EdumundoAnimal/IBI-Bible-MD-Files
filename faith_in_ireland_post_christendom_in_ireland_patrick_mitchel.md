# Faith in Ireland: Post-Christendom in Ireland
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document, titled 'FAITH in IRELAND: Post-Christendom in Ireland' by Patrick Mitchel, explores the shifting role of Christianity in Irish society. It defines 'post-Christendom' as a culture where the Christian faith has lost its dominant influence and institutional coherence. The document utilizes an Irish Times poll regarding social views on sexuality, celibacy, and marriage to illustrate the secularizing trends. It identifies several areas of decline for the institutional Church, including political influence, healthcare, social welfare, media, and family life. Finally, it outlines necessary paradigm shifts for the church in this new era, moving from a position of control and privilege at the center to one of witness and mission on the margins.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Post-Christendom
A cultural state where the Christian faith has lost its role as the primary framework for society, leading to a decline in the influence of traditional Christian institutions.

### Theme: Ecclesiological Shift
The argument that the church must transition from being a central, privileged, and maintenance-oriented institution to a marginalized, movement-based, and witness-focused community.
