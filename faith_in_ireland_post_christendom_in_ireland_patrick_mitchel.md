## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Post-Christendom
Defined as the cultural state where the Christian faith loses its societal coherence and the institutions that once expressed Christian convictions decline in influence.

### Theme: Ecclesiological Shifts
The document outlines necessary shifts for the church in a post-Christendom context, moving from a position of central power and institutional maintenance to one of witness, movement, and minority status on the margins.
