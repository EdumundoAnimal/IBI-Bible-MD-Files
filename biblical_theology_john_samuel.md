# Biblical Theology
**Author:** [[John Samuel]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### 1. What are the main, basic kinds of theology?

Q = the Key Question R = the Net Result Systematic Q = what should Christians believe NOW about any aspect of the faith? R = Christian doctrine e.g. Who is God? What is man? How are we saved? What is the end? cf. Grudem, Berkhof, etc. Historical Q = what have Christians believed about their faith at different times of Christian history since the time of the NT? R = record of the development of Christian doctrine e.g. Who is Jesus? The debate in the early church re 'Christology'. e.g. How are we saved? The rediscovery of justification by faith in 16th C. cf. The Lion Concise Book of Christian Thought, Tony Lane, Lion 1984 Pastoral Q = how do Christians help one another to grow in maturity in the faith? R = care & growth in the local church Biblical/Exegetical Q = by what process has God within Scripture progressively revealed Himself to mankind? R = correctly relating the whole, & any part, of the Bible to our Christian life now "Biblical Theology teaches us how to read the Bible. Systematic Theology tells us what we know after we have read the Bible." (John Woodhouse, Principal of Moore Theological College, Sydney)

### 2. Biblical Theology

'Helicopter' theology - looks at the Big Picture & provides a map of the whole story of the Bible (Globe, Googlemap...) - Jesus must have done this in Luke 24:27 Progressive theology - respects the development of the Bible over time, especially the progression from OT -> NT + the unity of its message - OT -> NT is the crucial step up - see Hebrews 8 - E.g. applies to the doctrine of the Holy Spirit - compare Ps 51:11 & John 14:16,17 Christ-centred theology - relates the whole of the Bible to its central focus - Jesus Christ & his Cross - e.g. Luke 24:27,44,45 & 1 Cor 2:1,2 Creation ----------------BC---------------- † ----------------AD---------------- Consummation ↳ when he returns. Key questions to ask: - Where are we in the Bible? E.g. 1 Samuel 17 - How does this relate to Christ & his coming/work? - How can we legitimately preach Christ from here?

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theological Categorization
The document distinguishes between Systematic, Historical, Pastoral, and Biblical/Exegetical theology, defining each by its key question and net result.

### Theme: Biblical Theology Framework
Defines Biblical Theology through three lenses: 'Helicopter' theology (big picture), Progressive theology (development from OT to NT), and Christ-centred theology (focus on the Cross).

### Theme: Christological Exegesis
The document applies a Christ-centred framework to Colossians 1:15-20, identifying Christ as the 'Visible Creator', 'Head', and the agent of 'Incarnational Redemption'.
