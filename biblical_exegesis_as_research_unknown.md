## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Methodological Exegesis
The necessity of using a disciplined, multi-step research process to ensure objective biblical interpretation rather than subjective or biased reading.

### Theme: Contextual Integrity
The requirement to respect the canonical, historical, geographical, and socio-economic contexts of the original audience.

### Theme: Theological Application
The goal of exegesis is not merely academic but serves as a bridge to understanding theological meaning for contemporary life and church practice.
