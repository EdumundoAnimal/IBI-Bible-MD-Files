# Biblical Exegesis as Research
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines a structured, step-by-step process for conducting biblical exegesis as a research method. It emphasizes the importance of following a logical flow: determining the canonical context, establishing the text, establishing the translation, establishing the meaning (syntax and word study), establishing the historical/geographical context, establishing the original theological meaning, and finally, establishing the application for today. The text provides recommended reference materials such as concordances, grammars, and theological dictionaries for both Old and New Testaments. It concludes with a proposed layout for an exegetical paper.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Exegetical Methodology
A systematic seven-step approach to interpreting biblical texts, ensuring that analysis remains grounded in historical context and linguistic accuracy before moving to application.

### Theme: Research Rigor
The necessity of using primary sources, original language tools (grammars, concordances, lexicons), and consulting a variety of scholarly commentaries to avoid interpretive bias.

### Theme: Canonical Integrity
The importance of understanding the text within its broader scriptural and historical framework to maintain the unity and authority of the Bible.
