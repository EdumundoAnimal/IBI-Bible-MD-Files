# The Roman Empire
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### The Roman Empire

From 27 BC, Rome was ruled by emperors (with the title 'Caesar'), and the Emperor Augustus brought peace, prosperity and stability to the Empire. By the time of his death in AD 14, the frontiers of the empire had been secured. Thus by the first century AD the Romans had taken control of the Mediterranean area. They soon began to unite their empire with roads, armies and a vast public building programme, and with the Greek language and 'Hellenistic' culture.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Pax Romana and Imperial Stability
The text highlights the transition to imperial rule under Augustus, noting that this shift brought 'peace, prosperity and stability' to the Roman Empire, which provided the geopolitical context for the first century AD.

### Theme: Hellenization and Infrastructure
The document identifies the mechanisms of Roman unification, specifically citing the development of roads, the presence of armies, and the spread of the Greek language and 'Hellenistic' culture as foundational elements of the empire's reach.
