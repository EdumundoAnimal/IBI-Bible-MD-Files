# IBI Academic Guidance Seminars 2010
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A. Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
IBI Academic Guidance Seminars for 2nd and 3rd years: 2010. Time Table for September 8th Wednesday: AGP from 10 to 1pm. Orientation from 2 to 5pm. Each level will be catered for. Introduction to seminar: 10 to 11:15. 15 min break. Seminar continues: 11:30 to 1pm. 1pm: Conclusion of AGP. Seminar Topics: Introduction for 2nd and 3rd years (Mimi), Grading criteria, cognitive skills for levels 5 and 6, Critical thinking, Handout Article, Quotes, direct, indirect, paraphrase, alluding, Definitions of words used in essays and assignments, Examples of good evaluative research, IBI online resources/introduction to Zotero (Stephen), Question and discussion time, Dissertation discussion for level 6. Lunch from 1 to 2pm. Orientation begins at 2pm for 2nd and 3rd years. Provisional Time Table for Academic Guidance Seminars: First Semester 2010. Schedule details: Thursday 23rd September (12-1pm dissertation 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years), Thursday 7th October (12-1pm 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years), Thursday 21st October (12-1pm 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years), Thursday 4th November (12-1pm 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years), Thursday 18th November (12-1pm 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years), Thursday 2nd December (12-1pm 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years), Thursday 16th December (12-1pm 3rd years, 1-2pm 2nd years).
