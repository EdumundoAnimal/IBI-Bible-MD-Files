# IBI Academic Guidance Seminars 2010
**Author:** [[Dr Miriam A Kelly]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### IBI Academic Guidance Seminars for 2nd and 3rd years: 2010

Time Table for September 8th Wednesday: AGP from 10 to 1pm. Orientation from 2 to 5pm. Each level will be catered for. Introduction to seminar: 10 to 11:15. 15 min break. Seminar continues: 11:30 to 1pm. 1pm: Conclusion of AGP. Seminar: Introduction for 2nd and 3rd years: Mimi. Grading criteria, cognitive skills for levels 5 and 6. Critical thinking? what is it? Handout Article. Quotes; direct, indirect, paraphrase, alluding to etc. Definitions of various words used in essays and assignments. Examples of good evaluative research. IBI online resources/introduction to Zotero: Stephen. Question and discussion time. Dissertation discussion for level 6. Lunch from 1 to 2pm. Orientation begins at 2pm for 2nd and 3rd years.
