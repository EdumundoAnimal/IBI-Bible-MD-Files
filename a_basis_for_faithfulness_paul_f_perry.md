**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Lesson 4: A Basis for Faithfulness - Hebrews 4:15 - 5:10

In Hebrews 3: 1-4:14 the pastor sets forth for his friends a basis for faithfulness in the fact that the Son of God is faithful. Passage Breakdown: 3:1 - 6a Consider Jesus... who was faithful to the one who appointed him. 3:6b - 4:13. 4:14. We are called to consider the faithfulness of Jesus as an incentive to faithfulness in our own lives. The basis of our confidence that we are not abandoned in a hostile world and that we can be faithful in that world is the faithfulness of our priest. Jesus compared to Moses - Why is the question Necessary? 1. The person of Moses the mediator Israel's covenant and worship was of central importance to Hebrews Author. 2. The author has emphasized that Jesus is the transcendent Son of God the mediator of God's final word (1:1 - 2:18). -this authority claimed for the Son invites comparison with the unique authority of Moses. Outline of this passage: 3:1-2 Comparison between Jesus and Moses. 3:3 Jesus superior to Moses. 3:4-6a Explanation for this superiority. 36b Significance of this superiority.

### Page 3 Content

The preacher interpreted Ps. 95 7-11 in terms of the account of Israel at Kadesh-Barnea - Numbers 13-14. Near the end of the story - The People responded to the Lord with (Numbers 14:10; Hebrews 3:8, 15). What is hardness of heart? Two conclusions: 1. Become partakers of God. We must be faithful, firm intention to hold to the end what we had in the beginning. 2. Israel was unable to enter Gods rest, because of unbelief. Hebrews 4:1-14. Rest... study verses 3, 11, and 14. What is this rest? When is this rest? How do we attain this rest? Repeated plea to calling us to faithfulness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Faithfulness as an Incentive
The document argues that the faithfulness of Jesus serves as the primary incentive and basis for believers to remain faithful in a hostile world.

### Theme: Superiority of Christ over Moses
The author establishes that while Moses was a servant in God's house, Jesus is the Son over God's house, marking a transition from the old covenant mediator to the transcendent Son of God.

### Theme: The Danger of Unbelief
Drawing from the account of Israel at Kadesh-Barnea, the text warns that hardness of heart and unbelief prevent believers from entering into God's rest.
