# A Basis for Faithfulness
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Lesson 4: A Basis for Faithfulness, Hebrews 4:15-5:10. This lesson explores the faithfulness of Jesus compared to Moses. It highlights that Jesus is the Son of God and the mediator of a new covenant. Key sections include a comparison of Jesus and Moses in terms of their faithfulness to God and their roles as servant versus son. The document also discusses Psalm 95:7-11, the hardening of hearts in Israel, and the theological concept of God's 'rest' and the call to faithfulness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The document posits Jesus as the transcendent Son of God and mediator, positioning his authority as superior to that of Moses, the mediator of the old covenant.

### Theme: Faithfulness and Unbelief
The central argument identifies the faithfulness of Jesus as the incentive for human faithfulness, contrasting it with Israel's historical unbelief that prevented them from entering God's rest.

### Theme: Theological Rest
A thematic focus on the concept of 'God's rest' as both a historical reference to the wilderness period and a present call to maintain a firm intention of belief.
