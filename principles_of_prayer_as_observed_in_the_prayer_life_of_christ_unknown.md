# Principles of prayer as observed in the prayer life of Christ
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
In surveying the prayer life of Christ I will highlight several key principles of prayer that can be applied to all believers. In Jesus' prayer for His disciples in John 17 we see that His main request for His disciples is that, 'they may be one as we are one ... Father, just as you are in me and I am in you'. This fundamental focus on God is also seen in the Greek translation of the word for prayer found in Matthew, which includes the meaning, 'to pray to God, that is, supplicate, worship.' The rest of the prayer is then primarily concerned with obedience to God. Jesus therefore shows us the way to restored relationship and obedience when He says 'give us our daily bread'. He provided this through His blood shed on the cross, as He teaches elsewhere; this requires from us a humble and penitent attitude, honestly confessing our sins before God. Only then, through faith, can we be forgiven and transformed and cry out to God effectively for deliverance from future rebellion. This great prayer of faith and intercession for a return to obedience to God through Christ's finished work also encourages us to intercession. We have seen how God's desire for us to be in intimate relationship with Him is demonstrated through Jesus' teaching on prayer and the priority He gave to this intercession, which gave Him the ability to be remain in the Father's will in the most testing of times. Our response to this should be to, 'draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith' and to seize hold, 'of the eternal life which you were called'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Theology of Prayer
Prayer is framed as a fundamental act of worship, reverence, and submission to God's will, modeled on Jesus' own practice.

### Theme: Obedience and Intercession
Prayer is intrinsically linked to the believer's pursuit of obedience to God, acknowledging sin, and participating in ongoing intercession.

### Theme: Intimacy with the Father
The document emphasizes the symbiotic relationship between Jesus and the Father, suggesting that true prayer derives from this established intimacy.
