# Church History Survey: The Life and Ministry of St Patrick
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the life, mission, and theological foundations of St. Patrick, focusing on his personal 'Confessio'. It details his conviction, love for the Irish, and reliance on Scripture. A significant portion of the text argues for the independence of the ancient Celtic Church from the Roman Catholic Church, citing historical perspectives on missionary work, the absence of Roman sanctions, and the distinct nature of Celtic traditions regarding Easter, tonsure, and church organization. It also provides background on the origins of the Ancient Celts and their culture.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Missionary Theology
Patrick's missionary approach was characterized by personal calling, fluency in the local language, deep immersion in Irish culture, and an intense eschatological urgency rooted in his interpretation of biblical prophecy.

### Theme: Ecclesiological Independence
The document posits that the early Celtic Church in Ireland, established by figures like Patrick, operated independently of the Roman Catholic Church hierarchy, as evidenced by differences in doctrine, church governance, and the absence of recorded Roman sanction for Celtic missions.
