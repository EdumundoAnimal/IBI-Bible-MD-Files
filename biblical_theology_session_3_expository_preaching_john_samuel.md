# Biblical Theology: Session 3 - Expository Preaching
**Author:** [[John Samuel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Biblical Theology: 1. What are the main, basic kinds of theology? Systematic: what should Christians believe NOW about any aspect of the faith? (Christian doctrine). Historical: what have Christians believed about their faith at different times of Christian history since the time of the NT? Pastoral: how do Christians help one another to grow in maturity in the faith? Biblical/Exegetical: by what process has God within Scripture progressively revealed Himself to mankind? 2. Biblical Theology: 'Helicopter' theology (Big Picture); Progressive theology (OT to NT unity); Christ-centred theology (relates the whole of the Bible to Christ and his Cross). Creation to Consummation timeline. Analysis of Colossians 1:15-20: Focus on Christ as the visible creator, head of the body, and his work of incarnational redemption.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divisions of Theology
The text categorizes theology into four primary branches: Systematic (doctrine), Historical (chronological development of belief), Pastoral (community growth), and Biblical/Exegetical (progressive revelation within Scripture).

### Theme: Biblical Theology Approaches
Three lenses are provided to understand the Bible: 'Helicopter' view (holistic mapping), 'Progressive' view (continuity between Old and New Testaments), and 'Christ-centred' view (focus on Jesus as the primary focal point).

### Theme: Christology in Colossians
An exegetical examination of Colossians 1:15-20 emphasizing Jesus Christ's dual role as the visible Creator of all things and the redemptive head of the church.
