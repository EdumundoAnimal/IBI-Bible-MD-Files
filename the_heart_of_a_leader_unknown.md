## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Character-Driven Leadership
The argument that effective leadership is built primarily on a foundation of integrity and spiritual health, rather than just technical skills or image.

### Theme: The Impact of Emotional Insecurity
Leaders often resort to sabotage, comparison, or manipulation as a reaction to internal insecurities rather than leading from a place of purpose.

### Theme: Biblical Foundationalism
Utilizing scripture, particularly the lives of figures like Paul, Abraham, and Jacob, to define the qualities of an ideal leader and to diagnose common leadership failures.
