# God's Special Agents: Angels
**Author:** [[Millard J. Erickson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a theological examination of angels, covering their definition, origin, nature, status, activities, and the existence of evil angels (demons). It explores biblical references, contrasting the roles of good angels as agents of God with the nature of demons, and concludes with the theological role of this doctrine for believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Nature and Origin of Angels
Angels are spiritual, created beings, subordinate to God and distinct from humans, lacking physical bodies despite occasional appearances.

### Theme: The Role of Evil Angels
Demons are considered fallen beings who oppose God's work, though their power is limited and subject to divine permission.

### Theme: Theological Significance
The study of angels serves to reassure believers of God's protection and to warn against the reality of spiritual struggle and temptation.
