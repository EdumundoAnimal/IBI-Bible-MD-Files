## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Supremacy of Christ
The central argument of Hebrews that Christ is superior to angels, serving as the ultimate revealer of God's word and the mediator of a new era.

### Theme: Theological Methodology
An approach to biblical study that combines Old Testament motifs with the New Testament revelation to define the person and role of the Son.

### Theme: Warning and Encouragement
A pastoral component of the text addressing backsliding, the importance of listening, and the danger of rejecting the divine message.

### Theme: Created vs Eternal
The distinction made between angels as created beings and Christ, who is the agent of creation and remains while the world perishes.
