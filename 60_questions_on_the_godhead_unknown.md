# 60 Questions on the Godhead
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains sixty questions and answers regarding the nature of the Godhead as found in the Bible. It explores the relationship between the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, addressing common questions often posed about Oneness theology versus Trinitarian perspectives. The text provides biblical citations to support its claims that God is a single entity revealed in different roles or manifestations.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness Theology
The central argument that the Godhead consists of a single entity, not three distinct persons. God is understood to have revealed Himself in different roles (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) to humanity.

### Theme: Biblical Literalism
The use of specific verse citations to argue against the existence of a trinity and to defend the deity of Jesus Christ as the fullness of God.

### Theme: Exegetical Interpretation
The document attempts to reconcile verses that suggest plurality in the Godhead by reinterpreting them within the framework of a Oneness, non-Trinitarian perspective.
