# From Paradise to the Promised Land: An Introduction to the Pentateuch
**Author:** [[T.D. Alexander]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an analysis of the Book of Exodus, exploring the themes of knowing God through personal experience and the relationship between God and the Israelites. It details the narrative of Moses' call, the signs and wonders in Egypt, the Sinai Covenant, and the construction of the tabernacle. It also draws New Testament connections, specifically comparing the Exodus narrative to themes found in the Gospels and the letters of the New Testament.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Knowing God
The central theme of the Book of Exodus is the process of God revealing his nature, sovereignty, majesty, holiness, and compassion to the Israelites through personal experience.

### Theme: New Testament Connections
The author explores how the Exodus narrative informs the New Testament's understanding of Jesus, linking the plagues and miracles to Jesus' divine nature and the covenantal relationship.

### Theme: The Sinai Covenant
The establishment of a formal agreement between God and the Israelites at Mount Sinai, emphasizing obedience, holiness, and the construction of the tabernacle as a dwelling for God.
