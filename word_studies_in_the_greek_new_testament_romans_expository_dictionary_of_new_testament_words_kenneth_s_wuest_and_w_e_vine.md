## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Christian's Relation to the Law
Analysis of the transition from being under the law to being under grace, emphasizing that the law exposes sin but cannot deliver the believer from the 'flesh'.

### Theme: The Flesh vs. The Holy Spirit
The struggle within the believer between the indwelling evil nature (flesh) and the life-giving power of the Holy Spirit, which provides the capacity for obedience.

### Theme: Expository Linguistics
The use of original Greek etymology to clarify the precise meanings of biblical terms for English readers to avoid misinterpretation.
