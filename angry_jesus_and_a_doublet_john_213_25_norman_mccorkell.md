# Angry Jesus and a Doublet? | John 2:13-25
**Author:** [[Norman McCorkell]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the narrative of Jesus cleansing the temple, comparing the account in the Gospel of John to the Synoptic Gospels. It addresses the 'doublet' theory—whether Jesus cleansed the temple once at the end of his ministry or twice at different times—and examines various scholarly perspectives on the event's timing and significance. The text highlights reasons for Jesus's actions: exploitation, defilement, and obstruction of worship, ultimately framing him as the 'restorer of worship' who signifies the replacement of the temple.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Temple Cleansing
An analysis of Jesus's authoritative actions in the temple, questioning what motivated the event.

### Theme: Doublet Theory
A scholarly debate regarding whether the cleansing of the temple occurred once (Synoptic account) or twice (John's account), with arguments for and against the Johannine chronology.

### Theme: Messianic Significance
The theological interpretation that the cleansing represents Jesus as the restorer of true worship and the replacement of the physical temple.
