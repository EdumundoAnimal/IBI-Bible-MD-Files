# Angry Jesus and a Doublet? John 2:13-25
**Author:** [[Norman McCorkell]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Class Presentation: John 2:12-25. October 7 2010. Theme: Angry Jesus and a Doublet. John 2:13-25. Discussion points: Reasons for Jesus' authoritative action regarding the temple, identified as Exploitation, Defilement, and Obstruction of the house for all nations. The document provides factual observations on the Johannine account, noting specific details such as the whip (Phragellion) and animals, and contrasts the timing of this cleansing event with other Gospels. It explores the 'Doublet' theory—whether Jesus cleansed the temple twice—by presenting arguments from scholars like Köstenberger, Carson, Ridderbos, and Barrett, both favoring and opposing the Johannine chronology. Concluding significance relates to Jesus as the 'Restorer of Worship' and the replacement of the temple in the life of God's people.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cleansing of the Temple
Analysis of Jesus' actions in the temple, identifying motives such as exploitation, defilement, and the obstruction of a house intended for all nations.

### Theme: The 'Doublet' Theory
The theological debate on whether the temple cleansing occurred once (as presented in synoptic gospels) or twice (as implied by the distinct Johannine chronology), with supporting and opposing scholarly perspectives.

### Theme: Messianic Significance
Interpretation of the cleansing event as a symbolic act, establishing Jesus as the restorer of true worship and the replacement of the temple structure itself.
