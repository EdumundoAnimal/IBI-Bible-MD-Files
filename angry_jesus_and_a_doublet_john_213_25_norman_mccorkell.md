# Angry Jesus and a Doublet? John 2:13-25
**Author:** [[Norman McCorkell]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Class Presentation: John 2:12-25. October 7, 2010. Angry Jesus and a Doublet? John 2:13-25. Discussion of what moved Jesus to such authoritative action, referencing Matthew 21:12-14, Mark 11:15-17, Luke 19:45-46. Key themes identified include exploitation, defilement, and obstruction of the temple as a house for all nations. The document contrasts the Johannine chronology with other gospels, citing scholars like Köstenberger, Carson, Ridderbos, and Barrett regarding whether the temple cleansing was a single event or a 'doublet' (occurring twice). The significance of the cleansing is interpreted as Jesus acting as the restorer of true worship and the replacement of the temple.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Temple Cleansing Motives
Exploration of the factors driving Jesus's actions in the temple, specifically addressing issues of commercial exploitation, spiritual defilement, and the obstruction of the temple's purpose as a house for all nations.

### Theme: Johannine Chronology vs. Synoptics
Academic debate regarding the timing of the temple cleansing. It examines whether the event was a single occurrence or a doublet, comparing the positions of various biblical scholars.

### Theme: Messianic Significance
The theological interpretation of the cleansing event as a symbolic act designating Jesus as the restorer of true worship and the replacement of the physical temple.
