# The Treasury of David: Psalm 90
**Author:** [[Charles Haddon Spurgeon]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an expository analysis of Psalm 90 by Charles Haddon Spurgeon, detailing the theme of human mortality, divine eternity, and the prayer for God's favor. The commentary breaks down the Psalm verse by verse, exploring the fragility of man compared to the everlasting nature of God, the consequences of sin, and the call for divine restoration and establishment of human labor in the context of eternity.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Eternity vs. Human Mortality
Spurgeon emphasizes the sharp contrast between God's eternal nature and the transient, fleeting existence of man, often utilizing the imagery of dust, fading grass, and passing time.

### Theme: The Impact of Sin
The author argues that human suffering and the brevity of life are consequences of divine indignation against sin, stressing that secret sins are visible to God.

### Theme: The Necessity of Wisdom
The commentary highlights the importance of 'numbering our days'—practicing spiritual mindfulness to live with eternal purpose rather than procrastination.

### Theme: Divine Favor and Restoration
Despite the judgment described, the text focuses on the hopeful turn toward seeking God's compassion, mercy, and the lasting establishment of human labor through divine grace.
