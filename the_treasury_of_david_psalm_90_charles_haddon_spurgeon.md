# The Treasury of David: Psalm 90
**Author:** [[Charles Haddon Spurgeon]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a detailed exposition by Charles Haddon Spurgeon on Psalm 90, traditionally attributed to Moses. It explores the themes of human frailty, the eternal nature of God, the consequences of sin, and the brevity of life compared to divine permanence. The text breaks down the Psalm verse by verse, providing theological reflections on the transience of man—likened to grass that withers—and the need for divine grace and wisdom to 'number our days.' It emphasizes that while man's works are fleeting, God's glory and work are eternal. The exposition concludes with an appeal for divine favour to be established in the work of God's servants for future generations.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Human Mortality and Transience
The text argues that human life is short, likened to grass, a sleep, or a watch in the night, emphasizing the stark contrast between human frailty and the eternal nature of God.

### Theme: Divine Justice and Sin
The author discusses how human suffering and the shortness of life are consequences of sin under the wrath of God, highlighting the necessity of God's mercy and atonement.

### Theme: Wisdom in Numbering Days
Drawing from Psalm 90:12, the text asserts that true wisdom involves realizing the brevity of life and orienting one's time toward God rather than procrastination.

### Theme: Legacy and Divine Permanence
The exposition emphasizes that while individual lives are temporary, the work of God persists, and believers should pray for God's favour to establish their labors for future generations.
