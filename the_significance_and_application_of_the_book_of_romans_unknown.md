## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The central theological argument that humans are made righteous in God's eyes solely through faith in Jesus Christ, not by works of the Law.

### Theme: The Role of the Law
The Law serves to reveal sin and human inadequacy rather than providing a path to salvation, ultimately pointing toward the need for God's grace.

### Theme: Practical Christian Living
Believers are called to offer themselves as living sacrifices, exercise love, respect authority, and avoid being conformed to worldly patterns.

### Theme: Universal Need for Salvation
Both Jews and Gentiles are equally in need of God's salvation due to universal sinfulness, with the offer of grace extended to all through faith.
