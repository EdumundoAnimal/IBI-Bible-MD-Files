## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The central argument that humanity is made right with God not through works of the Law, but through faith in Jesus Christ, receiving credited righteousness.

### Theme: Universal Need for Salvation
Both Jews and Gentiles have sinned and fallen short, requiring God's gracious gift of salvation available to all through faith.

### Theme: The Role of the Law
The Law serves to reveal sin and human helplessness rather than provide a mechanism for righteousness; it is temporary in its function of condemning, pointing ultimately to the need for Christ.

### Theme: Practical Sanctification
Faith in Christ should lead to a transformed life, characterized by presenting one's body as a living sacrifice, loving others, and living in the Spirit rather than according to the sinful nature.
