## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The central theological argument that humans are made right with God not through works of the law, but by placing faith in Jesus Christ, as exemplified by Abraham.

### Theme: The Role of the Law
The Law serves to reveal sin and human inability to achieve righteousness, but it is not the mechanism for salvation; believers are freed from the Law's condemnation through Christ.

### Theme: Living Sacrifice
Practical application of the gospel, requiring believers to offer their bodies to God, avoid conforming to the world, and live in love and service to others.

### Theme: Grace and Imputed Righteousness
The concept that God credits righteousness to the believer as a gift, similar to how Abraham was treated as righteous despite his imperfections.
