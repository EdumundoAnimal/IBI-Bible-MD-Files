## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Wisdom and Knowledge
Wisdom is defined as the ability to rightly apply facts, while knowledge is the ability to correctly acquire them; both are anchored in the fear of the Lord.

### Theme: Literary Forms in Wisdom Literature
The text highlights the usage of parallelisms, specifically synonymous, antithetical, and intensifying structures as primary tools for Hebrew poetic expression.

### Theme: Comparative Wisdom Literature
The study suggests cultural intersections between Hebrew, Egyptian (Amenemope), and Babylonian wisdom traditions, emphasizing shared human concerns regarding suffering and justice.

### Theme: The Cardinal Rule of Proverbs
Proverbs are categorized as observations and generalizations rather than absolute promises, requiring wisdom for proper situational application.
