# Biblical Theology - Expository Preaching Session 3
**Author:** [[John Samuel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines the fundamentals of Biblical Theology, distinguishing it from Systematic, Historical, and Pastoral theology. It defines Biblical Theology as 'Helicopter' theology (the big picture), Progressive theology (development from OT to NT), and Christ-centered theology. It provides a brief framework for analyzing biblical texts, followed by an example study of Colossians 1:15-20, which focuses on Christ as the visible creator, head, and source of incarnational redemption.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Theology Framework
A tripartite methodology for reading scripture that includes surveying the big picture (Helicopter), understanding historical progression (Progressive), and maintaining a focus on Jesus Christ as the central theme.

### Theme: Theological Taxonomy
Distinguishing between different branches of theology, where systematic focuses on current doctrine, historical on the development of belief, and biblical/exegetical on the progressive revelation of God within the biblical narrative.

### Theme: Christology in Colossians
An exegetical study of Colossians 1:15-20 emphasizing the dual roles of Christ as the visible Creator and the mediator of incarnational redemption.
