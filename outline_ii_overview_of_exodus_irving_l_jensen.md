# Outline II: Overview of Exodus
**Author:** [[Irving L. Jensen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as a lecture outline for the book of Exodus. It defines the book's purpose as the delivery of Israel from Egyptian slavery, judgment of Egypt, and the establishment of Israel as God's special possession. It provides a structured outline of the book, covering the slavery in Egypt, the call of Moses, the plagues, the journey from Egypt to Sinai, and the establishment of the Mosaic Law and the Tabernacle.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Message of Exodus
The text identifies three core themes: the judgment of the oppressor nation, Egypt; the deliverance of Israel from slavery; and the setting apart of Israel as God's special possession.

### Theme: Covenant and Ethical Behavior
The Decalogue serves as the foundation for the covenant relationship, with the first four commandments focusing on obligations toward Yahweh, and the last six focusing on responsibilities toward fellow humans.

### Theme: The Tabernacle
Represented as a movable dwelling where Yahweh met His people, characterized by specific architectural elements like the Ark, a heavy veil, and specific construction materials.
