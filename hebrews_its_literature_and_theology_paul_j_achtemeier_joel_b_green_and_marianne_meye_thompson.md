# Hebrews: Its Literature and Theology
**Author:** [[Paul J. Achtemeier, Joel B. Green, and Marianne Meye Thompson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The provided text is an analytical study of the Epistle to the Hebrews from the New Testament. It explores the origins, audience, purpose, and theological themes of the document. The text examines how Hebrews uses the Old Testament, interprets the figure of Christ as a superior high priest, and addresses the challenge of faith and perseverance amidst persecution. Key topics include the pre-existence of Christ, the comparison between Jesus and angels, the motif of 'Sabbath rest,' and the 'pilgrim people of God' theme.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
Hebrews presents a high Christology, describing Jesus as the Son, the agent of creation, the reflection of God's glory, and a superior high priest who offers a better sacrifice than the Old Testament Levitical system.

### Theme: Audience and Context
The readers are likely Jewish Christians facing external pressure or persecution, tempted to abandon their faith and revert to former practices or avoid the threat of martyrdom.

### Theme: Exhortation to Perseverance
A core aim of the text is to persuade the audience to remain faithful despite difficulties, using the examples of Israel in the wilderness and the 'cloud of witnesses' to warn against falling away.
