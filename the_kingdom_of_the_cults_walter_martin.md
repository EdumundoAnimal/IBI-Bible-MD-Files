# The Kingdom of the Cults
**Author:** [[Walter Martin]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is an excerpt from the book The Kingdom of the Cults, specifically focusing on Chapter 17, titled The Jesus of the Cults. The text analyzes how various religious cults and groups distort the biblical identity of Jesus Christ to conform to their own theological agendas. Walter Martin examines how groups like Christian Science, Jehovah's Witnesses, and Mormonism reframe Jesus as a created being, a pantheistic manifestation, or a deity among many, thereby diverging from orthodox Christian doctrine. The work emphasizes the historical and biblical obligation of Christians to discern and refute these distortions, citing Pauline and other apostolic warnings against false teachers and 'another Jesus'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Orthodoxy vs. Cultic Distortion
The author argues that cults systematically reinterpret or misappropriate biblical figures, particularly Jesus, to fit their own unique theological frameworks, deviating from historical Christian orthodoxy.

### Theme: The Necessity of Apologetics
Martin maintains that Christians have a moral and spiritual responsibility to defend the faith against what he characterizes as perversions of the gospel, using specific warnings from the New Testament as a foundation for this duty.

### Theme: The Pauline Warning against 'Another Jesus'
The text heavily references the Apostle Paul's concern about false apostles preaching 'another Jesus', using this as the primary framework for identifying modern religious movements that claim Christian terminology while stripping it of traditional Christological significance.
