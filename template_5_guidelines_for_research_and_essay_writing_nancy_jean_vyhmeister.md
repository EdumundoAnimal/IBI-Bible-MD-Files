## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theological Research Methodology
Research is viewed as a systematic investigative process that transcends mere information gathering to include objective synthesis and critical analysis, essential for theological studies.

### Theme: Source Evaluation Criteria
Emphasis is placed on distinguishing primary sources (Vatican Documents, Bible) from secondary commentary and critically evaluating authors, publishers, and the scholarly nature of journals to ensure academic rigor.

### Theme: Cognitive Levels of Academic Writing
The document organizes the writing process around Bloom-style taxonomy (Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Evaluation, and Synthesis) to guide students in reaching higher levels of academic engagement.
