# John's Wisdom: A Commentary on the Fourth Gospel
**Author:** [[Ben Witherington, III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological commentary on the Fourth Gospel, specifically focusing on the Farewell Discourses of Jesus (John 13:31-17:26). It examines themes such as the 'True Vine' metaphor, the role of the Advocate (Holy Spirit), the nature of the disciples' relationship with Jesus, and the anticipated opposition from the world. The text provides scholarly analysis of key Greek phrases, historical context for the Johannine community, and theological explanations regarding discipleship, obedience, and the nature of love within the Christian community.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
An analysis of John 15:1-8, where Jesus is the true vine and the disciples are branches. It emphasizes that discipleship requires 'abiding' in Jesus to produce spiritual fruit, and that external connection alone (like Judas) does not guarantee life.

### Theme: The Role of the Advocate
The Holy Spirit (the Advocate) is presented as the one who reinforces Jesus' teachings, convicts the world, and guides disciples, acting as a crucial component of the post-departure ministry of Jesus.

### Theme: Mutual Love and Obedience
The commentary argues that obedience to Jesus' commandments is the outward evidence of union with Christ. This love is not a mere feeling but an active, self-sacrificial commitment to one another as Christ loved them.

### Theme: Hostility from the World
The discourse predicts that because the disciples do not belong to the world, they will face hatred and persecution, paralleling the rejection Jesus himself experienced.
