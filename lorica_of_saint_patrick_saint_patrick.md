# Lorica of Saint Patrick
**Author:** [[Saint Patrick]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
I arise today. Through a mighty strength, the invocation of the Trinity, Through a belief in the Threeness, Through confession of the Oneness Of the Creator of creation. I arise today. Through the strength of Christ's birth and His baptism, Through the strength of His crucifixion and His burial, Through the strength of His resurrection and His ascension, Through the strength of His descent for the judgment of doom. I arise today. Through the strength of the love of cherubim, In obedience of angels, In service of archangels, In the hope of resurrection to meet with reward, In the prayers of patriarchs, In preachings of the apostles, In faiths of confessors, In innocence of virgins, In deeds of righteous men. I arise today. Through the strength of heaven; Light of the sun, Splendor of fire, Speed of lightning, Swiftness of the wind, Depth of the sea, Stability of the earth, Firmness of the rock. I arise today. Through God's strength to pilot me; God's might to uphold me, God's wisdom to guide me, God's eye to look before me, God's ear to hear me, God's word to speak for me, God's hand to guard me, God's way to lie before me, God's shield to protect me, God's hosts to save me From snares of the devil, From temptations of vices, From every one who desires me ill, Afar and anear, Alone or in a multitude. I summon today all these powers between me and evil, Against every cruel merciless power that opposes my body and soul, Against incantations of false prophets,

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Protection (The Lorica)
The text serves as a 'breastplate' prayer, invoking the armor of God and the attributes of the Trinity to protect the believer from spiritual and physical harm.

### Theme: Trinitarian Theology
The prayer centers on the belief in the Threeness and the Oneness of God as the fundamental source of strength and existence.

### Theme: Incarnational Christology
The prayer grounds the believer's strength in the historical and salvific events of Christ's life, from His birth through His ascension and future judgment.
