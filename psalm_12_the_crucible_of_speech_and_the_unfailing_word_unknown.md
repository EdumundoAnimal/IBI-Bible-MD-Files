## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Linguistic Weaponization
The argument that human speech is often used as an instrument of power and manipulation, specifically through flattery and duplicity, which leads to societal exploitation of the vulnerable.

### Theme: Divine Epistemic Integrity
The concept that God's Word serves as the only absolute, pure, and refined truth, contrasting sharply with the 'hyperinflated' and untrustworthy nature of fallen human rhetoric.

### Theme: Preservation Amidst Tension
The theological stance that while the righteous live in a world where moral decay and wickedness persist, they are preserved by the integrity of God's promises rather than immediate societal improvement.
