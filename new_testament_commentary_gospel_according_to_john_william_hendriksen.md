# New Testament Commentary: Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[William Hendriksen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains a commentary on the Gospel according to John, specifically focusing on Chapter 15. The text discusses the allegory of the vine and the branches, interpreting the metaphorical relationship between Christ and his followers. It also addresses the historical reliability of the Gospel of John in comparison to the Synoptic Gospels, exploring themes of authorship, provenance, and the theological context of the Farewell Discourse.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Allegory of the Vine and the Branches
Christ is the true vine and believers are the branches. The text emphasizes the necessity of maintaining a connection to the vine to bear fruit, warning that those who do not remain are severed and cast aside, reflecting themes of spiritual vitality and responsibility.

### Theme: Historical Reliability of John
An analysis of the authenticity of the Gospel of John, addressing the debate regarding its independence from or dependence on the Synoptic Gospels. It covers issues of provenance, the target audience, and the theological motivations behind the unique content in John.

### Theme: The Farewell Discourse
Examination of chapters 13-17 of John, focusing on Jesus' final instructions to his disciples before his crucifixion, including promises of the Paraclete (Holy Spirit) and the command to love one another.
