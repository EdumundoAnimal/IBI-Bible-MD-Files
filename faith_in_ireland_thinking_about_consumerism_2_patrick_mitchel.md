## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Consumerism in Faith
The critique of how consumerist ideologies impact religious practice, leading to 'me-centered' churches and the commodification of spiritual or personal experiences.

### Theme: Personal Consequences of Materialism
The text argues that consumerism creates a culture of instant desire, superficial life experiences ('life as confetti'), and emotional anxiety.

### Theme: Theological Rebuttal
The document contrasts the 'me-centered' narrative of consumerism with a Christian calling that advocates for a more noble, selfless purpose.
