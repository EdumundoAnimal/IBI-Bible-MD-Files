**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Expository Preaching principles and Bible study structure. Guidelines for preaching include letting the text do the work, asking questions about the text, and using alliteration. Key concepts discussed: colloquialisms, metaphors, and cultural challenges in interpreting scripture. Structural advice for Bible study curricula: identify growth areas, presuppositions, time/culture/language barriers, and a suggested four-part framework: 1. Salvation, 2. Forgiveness, 3. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Expository Preaching
Methodology emphasizing letting the biblical text speak for itself, utilizing alliteration, and avoiding over-reliance on commentaries.

### Theme: Cultural Exegesis
The necessity of addressing cultural barriers, such as colloquialisms and ingrained social beliefs, when interpreting scripture for a modern audience.

### Theme: Curriculum Design
Structuring discipleship or Bible study programs based on assessing current growth needs and building foundational spiritual concepts over a set period.
