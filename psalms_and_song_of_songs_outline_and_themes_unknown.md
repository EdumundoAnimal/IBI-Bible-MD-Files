## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Structure of Psalms
The Book of Psalms is organized into five books, emulating the structure of the Torah of Moses, serving as a comprehensive response of Israel to God.

### Theme: Types of Psalms
Psalms are categorized into functional types including individual and communal laments, thanksgiving psalms, hymns, wisdom psalms, royal psalms, and imprecatory psalms.

### Theme: Hebrew Poetic Devices
Hebrew poetry utilizes specific structures such as synonymous, antithetic, and synthetic parallelism, as well as chiasm and acrostics to communicate feeling and deepen memory.

### Theme: Interpretation of Song of Songs
The Song of Songs is analyzed both as a celebration of human love and marriage, and as an allegorical analogy for the relationship between God and his people.
