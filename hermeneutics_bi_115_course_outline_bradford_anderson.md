# Hermeneutics (BI 115) Course Outline
**Author:** [[Bradford Anderson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The course Hermeneutics (BI 115) at Carraig Eden Theological College covers the history, methods, and application of biblical exegesis. It includes an assessment comprising a 1,250-word essay on a selected passage and a final examination. The syllabus provides a comprehensive bibliography of recommended reading materials for students.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Exegesis
The systematic study and critical interpretation of biblical texts to understand their historical, literary, and theological context.

### Theme: Hermeneutical Method
The application of grammatical and historical interpretive methods to understand different genres of biblical literature, including figures of speech and literary devices.

### Theme: Academic Rigor
Strict requirements for written assessment, including formatting, length, and the inclusion of formal citations and bibliographies.
