# The Hedgehog Concept
**Author:** [[Jim Collins]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The Hedgehog Concept outlines the core strategy of companies that transition from good to great. By simplifying complex realities into a single, crystalline idea at the intersection of three circles—what you can be the best in the world at, what drives your economic engine, and what you are deeply passionate about—organizations achieve sustained success. The text emphasizes that discipline, introspection, and the courage to confront brutal facts are essential for discovering this unique concept, rather than relying on bravado or imitation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Three Circles
A strategic framework requiring organizations to identify the overlap between their deepest passions, their unique economic drivers, and their ability to be the absolute best in the world.

### Theme: The Stockdale Paradox
The practice of maintaining unwavering faith in the ultimate success while simultaneously confronting the most brutal, current facts of one's reality.

### Theme: Denominators of Profit
The necessity of finding a single, powerful economic metric that, when optimized, generates the greatest growth and sustainable profit for the organization.
