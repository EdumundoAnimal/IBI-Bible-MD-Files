# The Hedgehog Concept
**Author:** [[Jim Collins]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document introduces the 'Hedgehog Concept' as a core framework for transforming good companies into great ones. Based on an ancient parable, the concept posits that companies should simplify their complex worlds into a single, unifying idea. This idea is derived from the intersection of three circles: what a company can be the best in the world at, what drives its economic engine, and what it is deeply passionate about. The text illustrates this with case studies of companies like Walgreens, Wells Fargo, and Nucor, emphasizing the importance of brutal honesty and deep understanding over simple growth-seeking strategies.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Hedgehog Concept
A framework for achieving greatness by simplifying complexity into a singular focus at the intersection of passion, economic engine, and unique capability.

### Theme: Economic Denominator
The concept that companies should identify the single most important ratio—like 'profit per customer visit'—that impacts their performance most effectively.

### Theme: Brutal Honesty
The essential discipline of confronting the current reality and acknowledging what a company is not naturally equipped to be the best at, regardless of aspirations.
