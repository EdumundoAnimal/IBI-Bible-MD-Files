## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Deliverance and Salvation
Exodus illustrates God acting as a warrior to liberate his people not just from physical slavery, but as a foreshadowing of God overcoming sin and Satan in the New Testament.

### Theme: Covenant Community
God establishes a personal relationship with the Israelites, distinguishing them as his own people through a covenant that defines their identity and behavior.

### Theme: Knowledge of God
God reveals himself to his people through his actions and his representative, Moses, inviting them into an intimate, adventurous knowledge of his nature.

### Theme: Land
The Promised Land serves as a geographical reality in the Old Testament, which is transformed into a theological concept of living under God's authority in the New Testament.

### Theme: Typology of Moses and Jesus
A comparative analysis demonstrating how the events and figures of the Exodus serve as types or patterns fulfilled and surpassed by Jesus Christ.
