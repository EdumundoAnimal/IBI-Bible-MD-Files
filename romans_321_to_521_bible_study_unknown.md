## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a series of theological notes and reflections on the book of Romans, chapters 3:21 through 5:21. It utilizes chiasm structures to explain the theological arguments regarding justification by faith versus works. Key themes include the definition of saving faith as a 'trust transfer', the rejection of self-righteousness, the nature of the 'fear of God', and the importance of relying solely on the finished work of Jesus Christ rather than one's own religious deeds or perceived goodness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Saving Faith
Defined not as mere belief in God, but as a 'trust transfer' where one removes hope from self-performance and places it entirely on Christ.

### Theme: Justification by Grace
The theological argument that human works, even religious ones, cannot earn justification, which is given freely by God through the redemption in Christ.

### Theme: Fear of God
An inner attitude of awe, respect, and trembling joy rather than servile terror, based on the understanding of God's forgiveness and greatness.

### Theme: Anti-Self-Righteousness
The critique that relying on 'good works' for salvation leads to pride or spiritual anxiety, creating an obstacle to receiving grace.
