## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The central argument that human beings are made righteous before God solely through faith in Jesus Christ, entirely apart from personal performance or good works.

### Theme: Rejection of Self-Righteousness
A critique of religious performance that seeks to gain God's favor, labeling such efforts as 'good works' that lead to pride or anxiety rather than genuine salvation.

### Theme: The Fear of God
Redefined not as servile fear of punishment, but as an attitude of awe, respect, and trembling joy before the greatness and forgiveness of God.

### Theme: Faith as 'Trust Transfer'
The concept that saving faith involves consciously removing one's trust from personal merit and placing it exclusively in God's promise and Christ's work.
