## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of Christ
The author argues that Christ is superior to the angels, who are merely ministering servants. This is established through the 'Catena' of Old Testament quotes in Hebrews 1:5-14, which identifies the Son as the divine mediator and creator, exalted above the angelic hierarchy.

### Theme: Qal Wahomer (Lesser to Greater)
A primary theological argument used throughout the text to demonstrate that if the Old Testament law (mediated by angels) required punishment for disobedience, then the New Covenant (mediated by the Son) demands a much more severe penalty for those who reject it, as the revelation through the Son is of greater value.

### Theme: Apostasy and Drifting
The author warns the Jewish Christian recipients against 'drifting away' from the faith. This is framed as a danger of neglecting the gospel rather than outright rejection, using nautical metaphors to describe the peril of losing one's moorings in Christ.

### Theme: Divine Revelation
The document asserts that while God spoke in the past through prophets and angels, He has now spoken definitively through His Son. This finality of revelation is a central theme in establishing the authority of the New Covenant.
