# AT212 New Testament: Hebrews
**Author:** [[Eamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The author of Hebrews uses the Old Testament to develop his case for the superiority of Christ with the Angels. The document explores the theological themes of the Book of Hebrews, specifically focusing on the supremacy of Christ over angels, the warning passages against apostasy, and the call to hold fast to the faith. It incorporates scholarly perspectives on the intended response of the recipients who were facing persecution and potential drifting away from their new faith.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Supremacy of Christ over Angels
The author demonstrates that Christ, as the Son of God, holds a higher status than the angels, who are created beings serving God's purposes.

### Theme: Apostasy and Warning Passages
A series of warnings addressed to recipients who are in danger of 'drifting' from their faith, emphasizing that the consequences of rejecting the greater revelation of Christ are more severe than those under the old covenant.

### Theme: Lesser-to-Greater Argument
The text employs a 'qal wahomer' argument, contending that if the Old Testament law was binding and its violations punished, then the new revelation of Christ demands even greater attention and allegiance.
