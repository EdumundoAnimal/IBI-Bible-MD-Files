# Pastor Dropout Rate
**Author:** [[Rowland Croucher and Eric Tiansay]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article discusses the high rate of clergy leaving ministry. It cites sources like the Alban Institute, noting that 17 percent of clergy suffer from burnout or stress. Various reasons for resignation are explored, including lack of unity, loss of control over church power, unrealistic expectations to perform and entertain, and financial pressure. Organizations like the Pastors Institute and Church of Refuge are highlighted for their efforts in counseling and supporting displaced pastors.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Clergy Burnout and Dropout

### Theme: Ecclesial Pressure and Expectations

### Theme: Support Systems for Former Pastors
The emergence of specific ministries and programs designed to provide counseling, prayer, and housing to pastors who have left their pulpits.
