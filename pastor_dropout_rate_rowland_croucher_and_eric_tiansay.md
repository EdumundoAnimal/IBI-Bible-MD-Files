# Pastor Dropout Rate
**Author:** [[Rowland Croucher and Eric Tiansay]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document discusses the high rate of clergy members leaving their ministries. It highlights testimonials and research from organizations like the Alban Institute, Sunscape Ministries, and the Pastors Institute. Common reasons cited for pastor dropout include stress, burnout, disillusionment, pressure to entertain churchgoers, congregational factionalism, and lack of unity. The text explores the systemic and personal pressures faced by pastors, emphasizing the need for support systems and further research into the phenomenon.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Clergy Burnout
The psychological and physical exhaustion experienced by ministers, often driven by high performance expectations and lack of congregational support.

### Theme: Institutional Pressure
The shift in church culture toward marketing and entertainment, which adds undue pressure on clergy to 'perform' rather than solely preach the gospel.

### Theme: Support Structures
The emergence of organizations like the Pastors Institute and WoundedShepherds.com dedicated to aiding displaced pastors in their transition.
