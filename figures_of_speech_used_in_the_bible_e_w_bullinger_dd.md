# Figures of Speech Used in the Bible
**Author:** [[E. W. Bullinger, D.D.]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document serves as an introduction to the study of figures of speech within biblical texts. It defines the importance of understanding these linguistic tools to interpret scripture correctly. The author explains the etymology of 'figure' (Latin: fingere) and the Greek concept of 'Schema'. A significant portion of the provided text focuses on the grammatical device of 'Parenthesis', detailing its function as an explanatory insertion that is complete in itself but relies on the broader context. Specific biblical examples such as Hebrews 2:9 and 2 Peter 1:19 are utilized to demonstrate how parenthetical phrases clarify or deepen the meaning of the surrounding verses.
