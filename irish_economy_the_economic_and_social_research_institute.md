## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Economic Evolution
Ireland transformed from an agrarian-based economy to one centered on hi-tech and internationally traded services, fueled by EU membership and foreign investment.

### Theme: Post-2008 Recession
A significant economic downturn characterized by a contraction in housing construction, a rise in unemployment, and a move from surplus to a significant General Government Deficit.
