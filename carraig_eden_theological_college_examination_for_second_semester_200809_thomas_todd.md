# Carraig Eden Theological College Examination for Second Semester 2008/09
**Author:** [[Thomas Todd]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Examination Paper

Carraig Eden Theological College. Examination for Second Semester 2008/09. Programme: Bachelor of Theology, Validated by University of Wales. Level: 4. Romans. Module Instructor: Thomas Todd. Date and time of exam: 11 May 2009. Time allotted: 1 1/2 hours. Instructions: Attempt two of the following five questions. Use a pen (not a pencil) and write on one side of the paper only. Use examination booklets provided. An unmarked Bible provided by the college may be used. All questions carry equal marks. 1. Describe Paul's view of baptism as expressed in Romans 6:1-13. 2. Explain Paul's argument for the greatness of the sacrifice of Jesus in Romans 5:12-20. 3. Outline Paul's teaching in Romans as to why God gave the Law at Mt. Sinai. 4. Summarise what we know about the structure and origins of the church in Rome. 5. Elucidate Paul's approach to dealing with divisive issues such as Jewish food laws in Romans 14.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Nature of Sin and the Law
The document argues that the Law is spiritual, but the individual is carnal and a slave to sin. It emphasizes that the Law is not sinful, but rather acts as a separate entity that reveals the lack of good in the flesh.

### Theme: Justification and Grace
The text posits that justification is attained by faith, not by works of the Law. It highlights the contrast between Adam's disobedience and Christ's obedience, noting that grace abounds more than the trespass.

### Theme: Election and Sovereignty
The document explores the tension between human responsibility and divine sovereignty, discussing various views on election, including the Arminian and Barthian perspectives, and the idea that God is sovereign in mercy.
