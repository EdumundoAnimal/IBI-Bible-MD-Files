## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Royal-Cosmic Kingship
The psalm depicts Yahweh as the supreme King whose majesty is reflected in the cosmos, contrasting ancient Near Eastern royal propaganda that restricted divine image-bearing to monarchs.

### Theme: Human Dignity and Frailty
The tension between the 'enosh' (frail, dust-bound humanity) and the status of being crowned with glory and honor as vice-regents of the Creator.

### Theme: Christological Redemption
The movement from the original creation mandate to its fulfillment in Christ, specifically through the New Testament interpretation in Hebrews 2.

### Theme: Stewardship vs. Exploitation
The re-evaluation of the dominion mandate (Rada) as administrative care and responsibility rather than exploitative power.
