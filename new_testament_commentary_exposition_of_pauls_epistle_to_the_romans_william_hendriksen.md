# New Testament Commentary: Exposition of Paul's Epistle to the Romans
**Author:** [[William Hendriksen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This commentary provides an exposition of the Epistle to the Romans, specifically focusing on Romans 7:7-13. The text explores the Pauline experience with the Law, the role of sin, and the internal struggle described in the text. Hendriksen analyzes the 'I' in the passage, discusses the nature of the tenth commandment, and addresses theological interpretations regarding whether Paul is speaking as a believer or an unbeliever. The text includes specific theological considerations, such as the relationship between the Mosaic Law and the 'newness of the Spirit,' and clarifies the psychological and spiritual state of the subject when the commandment triggers the recognition of sin.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Law and Sin
Hendriksen argues that the Law is good and holy, yet its interaction with the sinful human nature triggers an awareness of sin and leads to spiritual death.

### Theme: Pauline Autobiography
The commentary analyzes the use of the first-person singular 'I' in Romans 7, concluding that it reflects Paul's own experience and spiritual struggle.

### Theme: Newness of Spirit vs Oldness of Letter
A contrast between the outward observance of the Mosaic code and the internal transformation provided by the Holy Spirit.
