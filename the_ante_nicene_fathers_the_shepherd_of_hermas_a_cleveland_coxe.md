# The Ante-Nicene Fathers: The Shepherd of Hermas
**Author:** [[A. Cleveland Coxe]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document contains introductory notes, visions, and similitudes from The Pastor of Hermas, part of the Ante-Nicene Fathers collection. Key content includes a discussion on the historical context and dating of the text (Muratorian Canon), a Vision regarding purity of thought, and Similitudes concerning Christian conduct, detachment from worldly wealth, and the innocence of those represented as children.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Purity of Thought
The text emphasizes that a righteous person must guard against wicked desires, as even mental intent can defile the spirit.

### Theme: Detachment from Materialism
The Shepherd teaches that Christians should treat their worldly possessions and lands as temporary, prioritizing stewardship and serving the needy over accumulation.

### Theme: Innocence and Faith
Children are used as symbols of spiritual innocence and singleness of heart, representing those who are most honorable before God.
