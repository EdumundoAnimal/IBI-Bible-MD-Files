# New Testament Times
**Author:** [[Merrill C. Tenney]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the societal, religious, and economic pressures faced by the early Christian church. It highlights the influence of paganism, emperor worship, and the political climate of the Roman Empire on the development and spread of Christianity. Key topics include the challenge of monotheism in a polytheistic culture, the impact of various 'Mystery Religions', the role of the state, and the economic pressures experienced by early followers. The text examines how the church maintained its identity amidst these external, and often hostile, cultural environments.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Emperor Worship
The political requirement for Christians to worship the state ruler as a god, which created direct conflict with Christian monotheistic belief in Jesus as Lord.

### Theme: Paganism and Mystery Religions
An analysis of the prevailing cultural atmosphere dominated by pagan fertility rites, astrology, and mystery cults like the cult of Serapis or Isis, which early Christianity had to navigate or contest.

### Theme: Economic Pressure and Persecution
The economic impact of state policies, including the potential for boycott or discrimination against Christians due to their refusal to conform to official state religious practices, as exemplified by the imagery of Revelation 13.

### Theme: Socio-Political Tension
The precarious position of Christians as a minority group holding a message that challenged the existing Roman social order and state-imposed religious requirements.
