# Lectures in Systematic Theology
**Author:** [[Henry Clarence Thiessen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a scholarly theological text titled 'Lectures in Systematic Theology'. It provides a structured analysis of Christian doctrine, specifically focusing on the Nature of God, the Trinity, and divine attributes. The text examines biblical evidence for the triune nature of God, the personality of the Holy Spirit, and the divinity of Christ. It addresses historical heresies such as Sabellianism and Tritheism while providing scriptural support for orthodox beliefs. The structure includes chapters detailing God's essence, attributes, unity, and the specific roles and eternal relationships between the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Essence and Attributes of God
The text defines God as a spirit, self-existent, eternal, and possessing specific attributes such as immutability, omniscience, and omnipresence, which are differentiated into moral and non-moral categories.

### Theme: The Doctrine of the Trinity
A detailed argument for the triune nature of God, emphasizing the co-equality and co-eternality of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, while critiquing erroneous views like Tritheism and Sabellianism.

### Theme: Biblical Evidence for Christ's Divinity
The text synthesizes various New Testament passages to demonstrate Christ's divinity, including his pre-existence, omniscience, and his divine names/titles such as 'The Word' and 'Immanuel'.

### Theme: Personality and Deity of the Holy Spirit
Arguments identifying the Holy Spirit as a person, supported by his ability to act, feel, and be insulted, as well as his divinity in relation to the Father and the Son.
