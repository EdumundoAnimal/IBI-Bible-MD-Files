## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutical Methodology
The systematic approach to understanding biblical texts through historical, literary, and theological lenses, specifically advocating for reading epistles in their entirety and viewing them as occasional documents.

### Theme: Communal Nature of Scripture
The interpretation of 1 Corinthians 11:17-34 highlighting that the primary concern is communal relationships and unity in the body of Christ rather than strictly individual personal piety.

### Theme: Contextual Interpretation
The necessity of grounding interpretation in the original historical and cultural setting, such as the social dynamics of Corinth or the specific cultural customs like the Feast of Tabernacles.
