## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Fellowship
The concept that human identity and security are not found in autonomous individualism, but in relational, communal, and vertically anchored life within the covenant community.

### Theme: Eschatological Zion
The development of the city of Jerusalem as a sacred space and a model for global, eschatological restoration through the throne of the Davidic King.

### Theme: Davidic Christology
The identification of Jesus Christ as the true Davidic King and the fulfiller of the typology of the House of the Lord, centering the Church's life in His righteousness.
