# Ante-Nicene Fathers and Commentary on Romans
**Author:** [[Various (A. Cleveland Coxe, William Hendriksen)]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a collection of excerpts from the Ante-Nicene Fathers and a New Testament Commentary by William Hendriksen on Paul's Epistle to the Romans. The first section includes the Pastor of Hermas, focusing on Visions and Similitudes, highlighting the call to repentance and the spiritual state of believers. The second section provides a scholarly commentary on Romans chapter 7, detailing the conflict between the law and the nature of sin, specifically examining the tenth commandment and the theological implications of Paul's autobiographical language.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Role of the Law
Exploration of the Mosaic law in relation to human sin and the necessity of the Holy Spirit for justification.

### Theme: Repentance and Innocence
Focus on the spiritual requirement of childlike innocence and the struggle against sinful desires.

### Theme: Pauline Theology in Romans
Analysis of the autobiographical nature of Paul's writing in Romans 7 regarding the struggle with internal desire.
