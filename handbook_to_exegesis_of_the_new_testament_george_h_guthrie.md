# Handbook to Exegesis of the New Testament
**Author:** [[George H. Guthrie]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text discusses critical methodologies for interpreting New Testament books, specifically focusing on the Catholic Epistles. It emphasizes the importance of structural analysis, historical-critical methods, and the impact of the interpreter's presuppositions. The document also explores rhetorical criticism, analyzing how different interpreters use rhetorical labels and structures to explain the theological arguments in books like Hebrews, James, 1 Peter, and 2 Peter. Key debates mentioned include the interpretation of angels, the role of structural dynamics in theological argumentation, and the nuances of linguistic analysis in biblical studies.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Structural Analysis in Exegesis
The author argues that a commentator's understanding of a book's structure significantly influences the theological claims made, specifically citing debates surrounding the role of angels in Hebrews.

### Theme: Rhetorical Criticism
The application of Greco-Roman rhetorical categories to New Testament letters, allowing scholars to categorize texts according to ancient speech types like deliberative rhetoric.

### Theme: Presuppositions in Interpretation
An analysis of how an exegete's subjective lenses, or 'glasses', can either elucidate or distort the biblical text during the interpretive process.
