## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Doxological Teleology
The argument that worship is the cosmic destiny and definitive purpose of human existence, moving beyond individual lament to universal celebration.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
Reading the Psalm as a precursor to the work of Christ, where heaven and earth are reconciled and the breath of God (nəšāmāh) is restored through the Spirit.

### Theme: Organological Symbolism
The use of an eightfold musical instrument list to symbolize total, universal praise that exhausts all human acoustic categories.

### Theme: Sympathetic Resonance
A theological metaphor where the human instrument, when re-tuned by grace and regeneration, naturally vibrates in response to the glory of God.
