# Pastor Dropout Rate
**Author:** [[Eric Tiansay]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document discusses the concerning trend of clergy leaving the ministry, a phenomenon often referred to as 'pastor burnout' or 'dropout.' It highlights the work of former pastor Michael Ross, who is conducting a nationwide study to understand why so many ministers quit. Various statistics are cited, including estimates that at least 17 percent of clergy suffer from stress or burnout, and that nearly 100 Southern Baptist Convention ministers leave their ministry each month. According to Sunscape Ministries, approximately 1,600 ministers per month across all denominations are either terminated or forced to resign. The text identifies several key reasons for this attrition, including pressure to be 'entertaining,' lack of unity within congregations, power struggles, family pressure, and feelings of unfulfillment. Support systems such as WoundedShepherds.com and the Church of Refuge are mentioned as resources for displaced pastors.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Clergy Attrition
The high turnover rate of ministers due to stress, burnout, termination, and disillusionment within the church structure.

### Theme: Modern Expectations of Pastors
The shift in expectations where contemporary churchgoers demand that pastors perform and act as entertainers, contributing to the pressure leading to dropout.

### Theme: Institutional Support Systems
The emergence of specialized organizations and programs designed to support, counsel, and house ministers who have left their positions.
