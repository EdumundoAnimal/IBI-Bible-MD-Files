## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Anthropological Transience (Hebel)
The biblical understanding of human life as fleeting and fragile, contrasting sharply with the permanence and sovereign majesty of God.

### Theme: Divine Warrior and Cosmic Theophany
The portrayal of Yahweh as a divine rescuer who intervenes in human history through cosmic phenomena to shatter deceit and establish covenant order.

### Theme: Eschatological Shalom
The vision of societal flourishing—marked by generational integrity, economic abundance, and civil security—as a byproduct of covenant allegiance to Yahweh.
