# Outline of the Book of Exodus
**Author:** [[Irving L. Jensen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an academic overview of the book of Exodus, outlining its key events, occasions, and purpose. The lecture content is divided into three major sections: Israel in Egypt (Slavery, the call of Moses, and the Plagues), The journey from Egypt to Sinai (Exodus, wilderness travels, and the song of Moses), and Covenant and Law at Sinai (The Decalogue, the Covenant code, and detailed specifications for the Tabernacle). The text emphasizes the deliverance of Israel from Egyptian bondage and their establishment as a special people under a covenant relationship with Yahweh.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Deliverance
The central narrative arc of Exodus involves the miraculous liberation of the Israelites from slavery in Egypt through the 'mighty arm' of Yahweh.

### Theme: Covenant Relationship
The establishment of a moral and spiritual contract between God and Israel, defined by the Decalogue and the subsequent covenant code, requiring obedience and ethical conduct.

### Theme: Sacred Space
The construction of the Tabernacle as a movable dwelling for Yahweh to meet with His people, signifying divine presence among the Israelites.
