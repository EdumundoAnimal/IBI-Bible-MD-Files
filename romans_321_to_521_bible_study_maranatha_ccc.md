# Romans 3:21 to 5:21 Bible Study
**Author:** [[Maranatha CCC]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This study guide provides a chiasm analysis of Romans 3:21 to 5:21, focusing on justification by faith, the example of Abraham, and God's promise realized through faith. It explores themes of righteousness apart from works, the nature of true faith versus religious performance, and the necessity of coming to Christ with 'empty hands' rather than relying on one's own virtues or good deeds. The document incorporates teachings from George Whitefield, John Gerstner, and Martyn Lloyd-Jones to emphasize the difference between human self-righteousness and the righteousness offered freely by grace in Jesus Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
Righteousness is not earned through works or observance of the law, but is credited to individuals by God through faith in Jesus Christ.

### Theme: The Danger of Self-Righteousness
Religious people often rely on their 'good deeds' to gain favor with God, which effectively becomes a form of pride and a hindrance to receiving grace.

### Theme: True Saving Faith
Saving faith is defined as a 'trust transfer' where one removes hope from self-performance and places it entirely on the finished work of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: The Fear of God
The fear of God is characterized as an inner attitude of awe and respect, acknowledging God's holiness and man's reliance on his forgiveness.
