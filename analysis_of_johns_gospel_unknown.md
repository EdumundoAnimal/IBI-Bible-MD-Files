# Analysis of John's Gospel
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The fourth Gospel is anonymous and explores the life and ministry of Jesus. The text contains themes such as the role of the Holy Spirit (Pneumatology), a specific vocabulary of opposites, and a structured layout involving the Book of Signs and the Book of Passion. It details historical controversies regarding authorship, mentions apostolic tradition, and emphasizes the purpose of the signs as demonstrating Jesus as the Messiah. The gospel is structured into specific discourses and events, concluding with the resurrection appearances and the confession of Thomas.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Authorship
Historical debates regarding the authorship of the Fourth Gospel, noting the anonymous nature of the text, early church traditions linking it to John the son of Zebedee, and later critical challenges.

### Theme: Pneumatology
The theological presentation of the Holy Spirit as the 'Paraclete' who provides aid and baptizes believers, connecting the Spirit's role to the exaltation of the Son.

### Theme: Theological Structure
The Gospel utilizes a structure of binary opposites (life/death, light/dark, sight/blindness) to communicate the revelation of Jesus as the Messiah.
