# Hebrews Essay Assignment Guide
**Author:** [[Paul Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as an essay guide for students studying the book of Hebrews, dated September 2009. It provides specific essay prompts for both diploma and degree-level students. For diploma students, questions focus on the superiority of Christ in comparison to angels (Hebrews 1:4-14) or comparing the Old Covenant and priestly work with the work of Christ (Hebrews 7:23-28 & 9:11-14). The document also outlines the academic expectations for degree versus diploma level work, emphasizing that degree-level responses require more critical engagement, broader scholarly sources, and nuance in argumentation. All assignments are capped at 3000 words.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
Exploration of the superiority of Christ as demonstrated in the book of Hebrews, specifically regarding his role compared to angels and his function as a high priest.

### Theme: Academic Expectations
Distinctions between diploma and degree-level scholarship, noting that degree work demands greater depth, critical source engagement, and diagnostic analytical skills.

### Theme: Application of Scripture
The pedagogical intent to bridge historical theological truths found in the letter of Hebrews with contemporary encouragement for believers.
