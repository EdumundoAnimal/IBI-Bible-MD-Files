## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Substitutionary Dereliction
The theological paradox where Christ absorbs the covenantal curse and judicial separation from the Father to achieve universal redemption.

### Theme: Macro-Chiastic Structure
The design of Psalm 22 centered on verse 21b, pivoting from individual lament and physical agony to communal praise and eschatological victory.

### Theme: Typological-Prophetic Messianism
The interpretation of David's royal lament as a literal description of the Passion of Jesus Christ, corroborated by linguistic and historical evidence.
