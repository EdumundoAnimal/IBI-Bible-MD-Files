# Developing disciples - IBI
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Key Quality: - Repentant

Description: A person you recognises that he is a sinner and salvation can only be found in the finished work of Christ on Calvary. (Acts4:12) Measured in terms of head, heart, and hands (2-3 measurements in each area) Head Knowledge They will know that: Christ's death is the only means of salvation. That their sins have been dealt with and will continue to be dealt with through Christ's blood. Faith itself is a gift from God. Hands Skills They will know how to: To seek forgiveness and to extend forgiveness to others. To point others to Jesus as the Saviour. Worship the Lord ,giving thanks for all He has done for them. Heart Attitudes or belief They feel: At peace with God. The fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, begin to grow in their lives. Secure in the knowledge that God loves and accepts them unconditionally.

### Key Quality: -Teachable

Description: A Disciple is someone who is a student willing to learn from Jesus, through the study of His life and Word. Measured in terms of head, heart, and hands (2-3 measurements in each area) Head Knowledge They will know that: All Scripture is given for training in Godliness.(2Tim 3:16) They need to co-operate with the Holy Spirit to allow God's Word to bring about character reformation. Hands Skills They will know how to: Handle the Scriptures correctly. Spend time in the study of the Word. Share what God has revealed to them for the building up of the Church. Heart Attitudes or belief They feel: Humbled to be in relationship with the living God Renewed through the changing power of God's word applied to their heart. The need for God's law's to be implemented in society.

### Key Quality: - Prayerful

Description: A person who, like Jesus, has learned to live a life of communication with the Father.(Mark1:35) Measured in terms of head, heart, and hands (2-3 measurements in each area) Head Knowledge They will know that: That God is constantly at work in all situations. For relationship to grow good communication is vital. Hands Skills They will know how to: Spend deliberate times of prayer. How to bring their needs to God in expectancy. Heart Attitudes or belief They feel: Desire to live in dependency on God Desire to see God at work in lives around them.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Holistic Discipleship
The document structures spiritual growth into three distinct dimensions: Head (Knowledge), Hands (Skills), and Heart (Attitudes/Beliefs), suggesting that true discipleship requires intellectual, practical, and emotional transformation.

### Theme: Christ-Centered Foundation
The document emphasizes that the starting point of discipleship is the recognition of sin and reliance on the finished work of Christ, as evidenced by the 'Repentant' quality.

### Theme: Scriptural Authority
The 'Teachable' quality highlights the necessity of Scripture for training in godliness and the role of the Holy Spirit in applying the Word to character reformation.
