### Theme: Discipleship Framework
The document categorizes disciple development into three specific domains: 'Head Knowledge' (intellectual/theological understanding), 'Hands Skills' (practical behavioral application), and 'Heart Attitudes' (emotional and spiritual posture).

### Theme: Key Qualities of a Disciple
The text identifies three primary attributes for a maturing disciple: being Repentant (acknowledging sin and Christ's work), Teachable (engaging with the Word of God), and Prayerful (maintaining active communication with the Father).
