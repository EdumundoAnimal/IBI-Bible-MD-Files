## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Two Ways
The unyielding anthropological binary presented in Psalm 1, which categorizes humanity into the trajectory of the righteous (planted by the water) and the wicked (chaff to be blown away).

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The interpretation that Jesus Christ is the ultimate 'Blessed Man' who perfectly fulfilled the Torah, serving as the bridge for believers to be transplanted into covenantal life.

### Theme: Heteronomy vs. Autonomy
The tension between the ancient worldview of conforming to an external, objective law (Torah) and the modern secular worldview of prioritizing internal self-law.
