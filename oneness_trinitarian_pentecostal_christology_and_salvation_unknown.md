# Oneness-Trinitarian Pentecostal Christology and Salvation
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the theological dialogue between Oneness and Trinitarian Pentecostals regarding Christology, the nature of the Godhead, and the doctrine of salvation. Joint affirmations emphasize the deity and humanity of Jesus Christ and salvation through grace. Oneness Pentecostals stress the absolute oneness of God and reject the 'three persons' terminology, viewing 'Father' and 'Son' as roles rather than distinct beings. Trinitarian Pentecostals emphasize that while they believe in one God, the term 'persons' is used to describe the internal relations of the Godhead, though they acknowledge the limitations of such language. The text concludes with a discussion on holiness and the practical outworking of salvation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness vs. Trinity
The fundamental theological disagreement regarding whether God exists as one undifferentiated person (Oneness) or as three distinct but inseparable persons (Trinitarianism).

### Theme: Incarnational Christology
A focus on how God was incarnated in Jesus Christ, with Oneness theology emphasizing Jesus as the full expression of the Godhead, whereas Trinitarianism seeks to balance unity with internal relational distinctions.

### Theme: Salvation and Holiness
Shared belief in salvation via the grace of God through Jesus Christ, extended into the practical application of a holy life, personal modesty, and righteous living.
