## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Reciprocity
The theological framework where human devotion—specifically the desire to establish a dwelling for God—is met by God's unconditional oath to establish the Davidic dynasty.

### Theme: Mənuḥāh (Divine Rest)
More than psychological tranquility, this term signifies royal-theocratic enthronement following victory, where God finds His permanent sanctuary in Zion.

### Theme: Messianic Typology
The interpretation of Psalm 132's emblems—the horn, the lamp, and the crown—as prophetic references to the person and reign of Jesus Christ.
