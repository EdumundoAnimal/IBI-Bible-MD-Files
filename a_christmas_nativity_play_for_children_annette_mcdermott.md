# A Christmas Nativity Play for Children
**Author:** [[Annette McDermott]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
A Christmas Nativity Play for Children is a script adapted from the New International Version of the Bible (Luke 1 and 2), designed for children's performances. The play includes a list of characters (Narrator, Mary, Gabriel, Innkeeper, Joseph, Messenger Angel, Shepherds, Wise Men, Angel Choir), costume requirements using simple household items, stage setting instructions for a stable, and a list of classic Christmas hymns to accompany the narrative. The script follows the biblical account of the annunciation, the journey to Bethlehem, the birth of Jesus in the stable, the announcement to the shepherds, and the visit of the Wise Men.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Narrative
The play provides an accessible dramatization of the Gospel of Luke chapters 1 and 2, focusing on the historical and religious account of Jesus' birth.

### Theme: Service and Devotion
The characters demonstrate themes of obedience and faith, particularly through Mary's response 'I happily serve the Lord' and the worship offered by the shepherds and Wise Men.

### Theme: Theological Significance
The script emphasizes the role of Jesus as a 'Savior' and 'Son of God' who brings hope and salvation to believers, contextualizing the Nativity within Christian soteriology.
