# The 7 AI Skills Applied to Homiletics
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The 7 AI Skills Applied to Homiletics. 1. System Design: Defining the sermon's boundaries, selecting the specific scripture passage, and establishing strict time limits for the message. 2. Tool Design: Setting explicit as restricting it to synthesize insights only from selected theologians (e.g., Tim Keller) rather than the entire internet. 3. Retrieval Engineering: Utilizing a curated library of commentaries, theological texts, and historical contexts to inform the exegesis. 4. Reliability Engineering: Fact-checking and cross-referencing your interpretations against trusted sources. 5. Theological Synthesis: Blending individual research or sermon styles with structural elements of renowned preachers like Tim Keller using LLMs (like Claude or Obsidian) to find a balanced writing style. 6. Evaluation and Observation: Refining the sermon's tone, pacing, and clarity based on listener personas. This includes analyzing YouTube Audience Retention graphs to pinpoint exactly where viewers skip or drop out to improve future delivery. 7. Product Thinking: Focusing on the ultimate end goal—delivering a clear, message and scaling its reach. Additional tools mentioned: ElevenLabs (11.ai) Voice Automation for converting final manuscripts into natural-sounding audio in your own voice, ensuring personal delivery and multi-format accessibility. The long-term goal is creating a seamless ecosystem where Obsidian, Claude, and ElevenLabs collaborate to learn your unique research patterns and speech cadences, ultimately helping you automate deep-dive audio sermons rooted in your own extensive studied material.
