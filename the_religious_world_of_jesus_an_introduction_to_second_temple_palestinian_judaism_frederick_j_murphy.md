# The Religious World of Jesus: An Introduction to Second Temple Palestinian Judaism
**Author:** [[Frederick J. Murphy]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a historical overview of the Jewish Revolt against Roman rule. It details the socio-political climate in Judea under Roman procurators, noting the tension caused by taxation and Roman policies. The text identifies the eruption of the revolt as a result of cumulative injustices, including economic oppression and the suppression of religious practices. It notes that prior to the violent revolt, there were attempts at nonviolent resistance and direct appeals to Roman authorities. The conclusion emphasizes that the collapse of Herodian rule led to direct Roman governance, which exacerbated existing social and religious tensions, eventually leading to the cessation of Temple sacrifices and open warfare.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Socio-political Unrest
The transition from Herodian rule to direct Roman governance created severe economic and social pressure, leading to significant unrest in Judea.

### Theme: Resistance Movements
Jewish resistance ranged from nonviolent petitions and appeals to Roman authorities to active, violent armed uprisings fueled by eschatological beliefs.

### Theme: Eschatological Influence
Religious expectations, including the belief in divine intervention and the coming of a messiah to liberate the people from Roman oppression, played a critical role in shaping the mindset of the populace.
