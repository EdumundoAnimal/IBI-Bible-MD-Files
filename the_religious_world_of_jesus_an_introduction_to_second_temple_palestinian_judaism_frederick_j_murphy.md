# The Religious World of Jesus: An Introduction to Second Temple Palestinian Judaism
**Author:** [[Frederick J. Murphy]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text discusses the socio-political climate in Judea leading up to the Jewish Revolt. It highlights how Roman procurators, specifically Florus and Cumanus, exacerbated tensions through taxation and mistreatment of the Jewish populace. The narrative outlines a progression from nonviolent resistance to armed conflict, fueled by apocalyptic expectations, banditry, and the heavy tax burden imposed by Rome. The conclusion emphasizes that these injustices, coupled with the erosion of traditional power structures, culminated in a violent eruption against Roman authority.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Socio-Political Unrest
The document details how taxation and mismanagement by Roman procurators in Judea created a volatile environment that made armed revolt inevitable.

### Theme: Eschatological Influence
The presence of prophetic voices and expectations of divine intervention played a significant role in motivating resistance against the Roman occupation.

### Theme: Shift in Protest Strategy
The transition from nonviolent civil disobedience to violent, organized armed rebellion against the Roman state structure.
