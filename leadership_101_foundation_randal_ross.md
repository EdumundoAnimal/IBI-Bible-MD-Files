# Leadership 101 Foundation
**Author:** [[Randal Ross]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains notes on leadership principles within a ministry and church context. It outlines the foundational definitions of a leader (guide, shepherd, military leader), characteristics of a godly leader (positive attitude, servant heart, integrity), and the distinction between leadership (inspiring, creating momentum) and management (keeping things going). It covers vision casting, staff-pastor relationships, the importance of clarity, and strategies for church growth, emphasizing personal development, mentorship, and reliance on God. The document also touches on turn-around strategies for failing ministries and maintaining team health through mutual service and clear communication.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Servant Leadership
Leadership is defined as a privilege to serve rather than a position of power, requiring a 'servant heart' and a commitment to nurturing others.

### Theme: Vision Casting
The necessity of clearly communicating an attractive, realistic vision that the congregation can 'buy into' to generate momentum and guide church growth.

### Theme: Leadership vs. Management
Distinguishes between leadership, which creates resources and momentum, and management, which sustains and organizes the resources produced by leadership.

### Theme: Staff and Pastoral Dynamics
Explores the critical relationship between pastors and staff, focusing on accountability, clear boundaries, and the 'armour bearer' concept for maintaining unity.
