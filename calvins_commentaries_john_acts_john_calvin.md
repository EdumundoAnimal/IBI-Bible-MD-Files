# Calvin's Commentaries: John-Acts
**Author:** [[John Calvin]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains a commentary on the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on chapters and verses concerning the divinity of Christ, the relationship between God and believers, the concept of being grafted into Christ (The Vine), and the apostolic commission. The text details Calvin's theological perspectives on the nature of grace, the necessity of perseverance, and the divine election of believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Election
Calvin argues that election is not based on human merit or skill but is an act of sovereign grace by which God chooses individuals to be His children.

### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
An analysis of the metaphor where Christ is the vine and believers are branches; union with Christ is essential for spiritual life, fruit-bearing, and persistence in faith.

### Theme: Perseverance of the Saints
The doctrine that believers are kept in the faith through the power of God, not through their own strength or industry.

### Theme: Apostolic Commission
The discussion on the appointment of apostles, noting that their authority and success in their ministry are gifts from God rather than products of their own labor.
