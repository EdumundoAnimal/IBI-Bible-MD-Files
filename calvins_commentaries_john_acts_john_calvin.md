# Calvin's Commentaries: John-Acts
**Author:** [[John Calvin]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains commentary on the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on chapters addressing the nature of the Word, the concept of the true vine, and the divine election of the disciples. It explores theological themes regarding Christ's divinity, the necessity of faith and perseverance, and the practical implications of being called by God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Eternal Divinity of Christ
The commentary asserts that Christ, as the Word, existed with God from the beginning, sharing in the same nature as the Father and being the source of life.

### Theme: Divine Election
Calvin argues that the disciples did not choose Christ, but were chosen by Him, emphasizing that this election is based on free grace rather than human merit or effort.

### Theme: Perseverance in Faith
True faith requires consistent growth and remaining in the 'vine' of Christ; it is not a temporary experience but a lifelong commitment supported by divine grace.

### Theme: The Role of Prayer
Prayer is framed as an essential channel for believers to receive help and strength, reflecting their dependence on God rather than their own abilities.
