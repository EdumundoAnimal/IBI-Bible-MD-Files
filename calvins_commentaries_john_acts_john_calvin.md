# Calvin's Commentaries: John-Acts
**Author:** [[John Calvin]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text comprises a theological commentary on the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on chapters 15 and associated theological discourse regarding Christ as the True Vine. It explores concepts of Divine grace, the nature of apostolic calling, the necessity of prayer, and the doctrine of election. The author examines the relationship between the vine and its branches as a metaphor for the union between Christ and the believer, emphasizing that fruitfulness in the Christian life is entirely dependent on the indwelling of the Spirit and the work of God rather than human effort or autonomous will. It further addresses the distinction between servants and friends of God, the assurance of answered prayer, and the divine appointment of believers to bear fruit that persists.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Union with Christ
The metaphor of the vine and branches illustrates that believers possess no inherent life or ability to bear fruit; their spiritual efficacy is derived solely from their vital connection to Christ.

### Theme: Sovereign Election
The assertion that Christ chose the disciples, rather than the reverse, signifies that spiritual fruitfulness and apostolic commission are initiated by Divine grace, not human agency or skill.

### Theme: Nature of Prayer
Prayer is framed as an activity sustained by God, where the promise of answered prayer is contingent upon the believer's alignment with Christ's will and words.
