# Does the Holy Spirit proceed from the Father, from the Son, or from both?
**Author:** [[Norbert Link]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document, published by the Church of the Eternal God, addresses the theological question of the procession of the Holy Spirit. It argues that the Holy Spirit is the power and mind of God, not a separate person. The author uses various New Testament passages—including John 14:16-17, Titus 3:5-6, Galatians 4:6, and others—to argue that both the Father and the Son, Jesus Christ, send the Holy Spirit. The text concludes that because God the Father and God the Son dwell in believers through the Holy Spirit, they both share in this action. The latter portion of the provided text (pages 184-197) is an excerpt from a book criticizing Oneness Pentecostalism, contrasting its view of God as a solitary person with the Trinitarian view of God as essentially social, relational, and loving by nature.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Procession of the Holy Spirit
The argument that the Holy Spirit acts as the power of God and is sent by both the Father and Jesus Christ, rather than proceeding from only one.

### Theme: Divine Indwelling
The belief that the Father and the Son dwell within believers through the Holy Spirit, facilitating a familial relationship with God.

### Theme: Trinitarian vs. Oneness Theology
A critique of the Oneness Pentecostal view, arguing that it reduces the love of God to a mere human display, whereas Trinitarian theology emphasizes an eternal, interpersonal love within the Godhead.

### Theme: Sociality of God
The theological assertion that God is essentially relational and loving, meaning love is not a created attribute but intrinsic to God's nature.
