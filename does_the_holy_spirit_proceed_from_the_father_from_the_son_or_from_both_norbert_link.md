# Does the Holy Spirit proceed from the Father, from the Son, or from both?
**Author:** [[Norbert Link]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a religious Q&A examining the procession of the Holy Spirit. It asserts that the Holy Spirit is the power and mind of God, not a separate person. It argues that the Holy Spirit proceeds from both the Father and the Son, providing scriptural evidence that the Father gives the Spirit and that Jesus also sends the Spirit. The document later transitions into a theological critique of Oneness Pentecostalism, arguing that the Trinity is essential to understanding God's nature as inherently loving and relational, rather than a solitary unit.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Procession of the Holy Spirit
The theological stance that the Holy Spirit proceeds from both the Father and the Son, supported by specific biblical references like John 14:26 and 16:7.

### Theme: Critique of Oneness Pentecostalism
An argument that Oneness theology renders God's love 'pseudo-interpersonal' and solitary, failing to reflect the inherent relational nature of the Trinity.

### Theme: The Practicality of Trinitarian Theology
The assertion that a Trinitarian view of God enables a more profound understanding of unconditional love and social relationality as modeled by the Godhead.
