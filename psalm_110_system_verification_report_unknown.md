## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Session
The theological understanding of Christ seated at the right hand of YHWH, representing absolute executive authority and co-regency.

### Theme: Melchizedekian Priesthood
An eternal, non-Levitical priesthood that unites the offices of King and Priest, grounded in an unbreakable divine oath.

### Theme: Eschatological Conquest
The active process of pacification and divine judgment where the Warrior-Priest subdues opposition and shatters corrupt systemic structures.

### Theme: Willing Consecration
The response of the elect who offer themselves freely as a consecrated, priestly host in the day of the King's power, moving beyond autonomous self-determination.
