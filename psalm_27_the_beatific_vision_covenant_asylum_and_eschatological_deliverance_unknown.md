## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Beatific Vision
The transformative, radiant experience of God's presence, articulated through the term 'nōʿam', representing the highest end of human consciousness.

### Theme: Covenant Asylum
The theological reality of God as an eternal fortress (māʿôz) and sanctuary (sukkâ) providing absolute legal and spiritual protection for the vulnerable.

### Theme: Eschatological Patience (qāwâ)
An active, muscular tension of faith where the believer waits on God in anticipation of divine intervention, contrasting with passive resignation.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The reading of Psalm 27 as a Messianic text, where Christ is the Slandered King, the True Temple, and the ultimate recipient of the Father's restoration.
