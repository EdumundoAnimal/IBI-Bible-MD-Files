# iChurch: All We Like Sheep
**Author:** [[Skye Jethani]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text explores the intersection of consumerist culture and the modern church, arguing that a consumer-driven approach to faith undermines Christian discipleship. The author recounts a story of congregants leaving a church based on personal preference and convenience. He analyzes the historical development of consumerism, noting how advertising creates artificial needs and how the economy relies on constant desire. The text critizes the church for adopting marketing strategies that treat congregants like customers, leading to a fragmented 'iChurch' model where spiritual growth is replaced by personal customization, ultimately leading to the commodification of God and human relationships.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Consumerist Church
The adoption of market-driven strategies in church leadership, where the pastor functions as a service provider and congregants as customers with individual preferences.

### Theme: Commodification of Faith
The trend of treating religious experience and even God as products to be consumed, which strips these elements of their context, tradition, and transformative power.

### Theme: Individualism vs. Community
The conflict between modern 'choice-based' Christianity and the traditional model of community-based growth and submission to spiritual discipline.
