## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Hermeneutics
The systematic study of the principles and methods used for the interpretation of biblical texts, focusing on historical, literary, and theological analysis.

### Theme: Contextual Analysis
The methodology of evaluating a biblical passage through various layers of context, including historical background, canonical placement, and literary genre.

### Theme: Interpretation Methodology
Strategies for engaging with specific biblical genres such as narratives, parables, epistles, and poetry to ensure accurate theological understanding.
