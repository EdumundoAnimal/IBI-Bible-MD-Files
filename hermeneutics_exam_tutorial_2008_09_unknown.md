## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Historical Context
The necessity of understanding the temporal and cultural setting of biblical passages, including specific applications to prophetic books like Zechariah and Jeremiah.

### Theme: Literary Genre Analysis
An analytical approach to interpreting distinct biblical forms, specifically narratives, Hebrew poetry, parables, and epistles, requiring different methodological tools.

### Theme: Hermeneutical Methodology
The theoretical framework of biblical interpretation, examining the transition of methods across history from the Intertestamental period through the Reformation.
