## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Hermeneutics
The systematic study of the principles and methodology behind interpreting biblical texts, including historical, literary, and theological analysis.

### Theme: Contextual Analysis
The importance of considering historical, literary, and canonical backgrounds to correctly interpret the meaning of specific scriptural passages.

### Theme: Literary Genre
The application of specific interpretive rules based on the genre of the text, such as narratives, Hebrew poetry, gospels, and epistles.
