# Peril or Promise: Lesson 5
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines a lesson on Hebrews 5:11-6:20, exploring the themes of hearing, obedience, spiritual immaturity, and the stability of God's promise. The text analyzes the author of Hebrews' attempt to address a congregation struggling with lethargy and a lack of spiritual progress, emphasizing the necessity of moving beyond basic teachings toward maturity. It also contrasts the peril of falling away with the promise of God's reliability.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Immaturity
The author of Hebrews critiques the congregation for becoming 'hard of hearing' and failing to progress in their faith, remaining stuck in basic teachings instead of moving toward maturity.

### Theme: Peril vs. Promise
The lesson presents a dichotomy where listeners face either the peril of turning away from God or the promise of stability found in holding firmly to God's reliability.

### Theme: Hearing and Obedience
The text establishes that 'hearing' in Hebrews implies a comprehensive, wholehearted response to the message of God, directly linking cognitive listening to active obedience.
