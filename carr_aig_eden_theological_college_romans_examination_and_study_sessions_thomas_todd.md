## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Theology of Justification
Justification by faith is presented as the foundational state for believers, establishing peace with God through Christ's sacrifice, independent of human merit or Law observance.

### Theme: The Believer's Relationship to Sin
Paul's argument, particularly in Romans 6-7, suggests that while believers are dead to sin's power, they still struggle with the flesh and must actively pursue sanctification.

### Theme: Divine Sovereignty and Election
The text explores God's unconditional election, asserting that God's sovereign choice for salvation or judgment remains within His prerogative, emphasizing His mercy and righteousness over human complaints.

### Theme: Christian Liberty and Unity
Paul encourages unity within the church despite differing views on non-essential practices, emphasizing that the 'strong' should accommodate the 'weak' to maintain community harmony.
