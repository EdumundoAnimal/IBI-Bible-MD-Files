## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theocracry to Monarchy
The transition of Israel from a loosely governed covenant community to a centralized monarchy under the rule of human kings, characterized by the tensions between divine mandate and human inadequacy.

### Theme: Covenantal Promise
The recurring pattern of 'taking, sharing, settling, and holding' onto the promise of land and inheritance given to Israel, often cycling between obedience and apostasy.

### Theme: Hope and Redemption
The movement from emptiness to fullness as seen in the story of Ruth, demonstrating faithfulness and the role of the kinsman redeemer in sustaining the Davidic line.
