# Psalm 25: A Theological and Exegetical Monograph
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a comprehensive verse-by-verse exegesis of Psalm 25, integrating linguistic, historical, canonical, and philosophical perspectives. It explores the poem's acrostic structure, its covenantal themes of steadfast love (ḥesed) and divine guidance, and its christological fulfillment. The study contrasts ancient Near Eastern pagan frameworks with the Yahwistic framework of Psalm 25, while providing an apologetic engagement with modern existentialism.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Fidelity
The argument that Yahweh's mercy is rooted in His eternal character and nature (ḥesed) rather than in human merit or performance.

### Theme: Acrostic Structure as Pedagogy
The deliberate, disciplined alphabetic structure of Psalm 25 serves as a curriculum of covenant discipleship (Torah) encompassing the full spectrum of human vulnerability.

### Theme: Theodicy and Humility
Humility (ănāwāh) is reframed not as psychological weakness, but as reality-based sanity that opens the human heart to divine wisdom and guidance.
