# The Teaching of Amenemope and its Connection with the Book of Proverbs
**Author:** [[John Ruffle]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The Teaching of Amenemope and its connection with the book of Proverbs explores the historical scholarly consensus regarding the parallel relationship between the Egyptian text and the biblical book of Proverbs. Initial scholarly recognition began in 1888 with Renouf and was significantly advanced by Budge in 1922 and Erman in 1924, particularly concerning the 'Words of the Wise' (Proverbs 22:17-24:22). The text provides comparative examples of wisdom literature across various ancient sources, including the Counsels of Wisdom and the Hymn to Ninurta, emphasizing moral conduct, truthful speech, and the dangers of slander and hypocrisy as shared values.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Comparative Wisdom Literature
The study of cross-cultural parallels between ancient Near Eastern didactic texts and biblical wisdom literature, specifically the Book of Proverbs.

### Theme: Ethical Speech and Integrity
A common theme across both Amenemope and Proverbs highlighting the avoidance of slander, false witness, and insincerity in favor of truthful, circumspect speech.
