# John 4:1-42: Commentary
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a detailed exegetical commentary on the passage of John 4:1-42, focusing on the interaction between Jesus and the Samaritan woman, the themes of living water, and the shifting landscape of Jewish-Samaritan theological disputes. It explores the transition from traditional cultic sites like Mount Gerizim and Jerusalem to the worship of God in spirit and truth through Jesus as the Messiah.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Living Water
Metaphorical usage of living water representing Jesus' gift of salvation and the Holy Spirit, contrasting with the literal well water sought by the Samaritan woman.

### Theme: Shift in Worship
The theological transition from place-bound, institutionalized worship (Jerusalem vs. Mount Gerizim) to universal worship centered on Jesus, who is the fulfillment of Old Testament promises.

### Theme: Jesus' Messianic Identity
The progression of the Samaritan woman's perception of Jesus from a thirsty traveler to a prophet, and finally to the potential Messiah.
