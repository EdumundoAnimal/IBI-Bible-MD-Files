## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Cardinal Rule of Proverbs
Proverbs are observations and generalizations, not universal guarantees or absolute promises. Wisdom involves understanding when and how a specific proverb applies to a situation.

### Theme: Wisdom and Knowledge
A major theme in the literature, defined as the ability to acquire facts (knowledge) and correctly apply them (wisdom). Both are rooted in the 'Fear of the Lord'.

### Theme: Theodicy and Suffering
The book of Job is presented as an inquiry into the justice of God, specifically how the righteous suffer, with comparisons to other ancient Near Eastern literature like 'The Babylonian Job'.

### Theme: Ancient Near Eastern (ANE) Wisdom Parallels
Biblical wisdom literature shares stylistic and thematic similarities with other ANE works, such as the Egyptian 'Instruction of Amenemope', reflecting a universal human attempt to cope with existence.
