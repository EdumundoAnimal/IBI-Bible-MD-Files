## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
The text identifies the vine as a Jewish symbol for Israel, now fulfilled in Christ. The act of pruning (καθαίρει) is described as a necessary agricultural process to ensure fruitfulness, which is then applied exegetically to the spiritual life of the disciples.

### Theme: The Role of the Paraclete
The text analyzes the function of the Holy Spirit (Paraclete) as one who convicts the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment. It highlights the legal connotation of ἐλέγχω (to convict/cross-examine) in the context of legal proceedings.

### Theme: Mutual Love and Persecution
The text notes that the command to love one another is given in the context of impending persecution. The disciples are warned that they will be expelled from the synagogue, and that their persecutors will believe they are serving God by killing them.
