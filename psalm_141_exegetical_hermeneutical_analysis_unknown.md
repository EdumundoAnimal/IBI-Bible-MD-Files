### Psalm 141

Theological Macro-Theme: Preservation Through Liturgical Sanctification, Severe Mercy, and Eschatological Vindication. Homiletical Format: Expository Verse-by-Verse / Christological-Theological Framework. Target Word Count Range: Comprehensive Exegetical & Hermeneutical Standard (approx. 5,000–6,500 words). PHASE 1: THE EXEGETICAL NETWORK (The First-Century Chair). 1. Linguistic & Syntax Report. PSALM 141: CONCENTRIC & STRUCTURAL PROGRESSION. [A] Cultic Invocation & Priestly Offering of the Person (vv. 1–2). Focus: Ascending incense (קְטֹרֶת), evening sacrifice (מִנְחַת־עָרֶב). [B] Apotropaic Petition: Guarding Mouth, Heart, & Table (vv. 3–4). Focus: Preservation from moral assimilation & wicked dainties (מַנְעַמֵּיהֶם). [C] The Crucible: Righteous Discipline vs. Calamity of the Wicked (vv. 5–7). Center: Severe mercy (שֶׁמֶן רֹאשׁ), scattered bones (לְפִי שְׁאוֹל). [B'] Apotropaic Petition: Preservation from Traps & Snares (vv. 8–9). Focus: Refuged gaze (אֵלֶיךָ יְהוִה עֵינָי), nets of evil (פַּח / מֹקְשׁוֹת). [A'] Eschatological Deliverance & The Passing Over (v. 10). Focus: The wicked fall into their own nets; the psalmist passes through (עַד־אֶעֱבוֹר).

### Textual Variants (BHS, 4QPs, LXX Apparatus)

Verse 5a: יֶהֶלְמֵנִי־צַדִּיק חֶסֶד וְיוֹכִיחֵנִי. Masoretic Text (MT): יֶהֶלְמֵנִי־צַדִּיק חֶסֶד וְיוֹכִיחֵנִי שֶׁמֶן רֹאשׁ אַל־יָנִי רֹאשִׁי (yehelmēnî-ṣaddîq ḥeseḏ wəyôḵîḥēnî šemen rōʾš ʾal-yānî rōʾšî). The verb יָנִי (yānî) is vocalized as a Hiphil jussive of נוא (nwʾ, "to forbid, refuse, break, annul"; HALOT 683) or an apocopated Hiphil of נוף (nwp, "to shake, sprinkle"). Septuagint (LXX, Ps 140:5): παιδεύσει με δίκαιος ἐν ἐλέει καὶ ἐλέγξει με, ἔλαιον δὲ ἁμαρτωλοῦ μὴ λιπανάτω τὴν κεφαλήν μου ("Let the righteous discipline me in mercy and reprove me; but let not the oil of a sinner anoint my head"). The LXX translators read šemen rōʾš as šemen rāšā‘ (שֶׁמֶן רָשָׁע, "oil of a wicked one"), positing an antithesis between righteous rebuke and wicked flatteries/anointings. Dead Sea Scrolls (4QPs^b / 4Q84 & 11QPs^a): Fragmentary witnesses confirm the consonants of the MT base text (אל יני ראשי), supporting the reading that the psalmist refuses to reject ('al-yānî) the head-oil (šemen rō'š) consisting of righteous correction.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultic Transference
The document argues that when the physical temple is inaccessible, the believer's prayer and bodily posture (lifting hands) function as a spiritualized equivalent to the evening incense and grain offering.

### Theme: Severe Mercy
The text interprets the 'reproof of the righteous' (v. 5) not as an act of harm, but as a necessary, painful correction that acts as 'anointing oil' to prevent the soul from consuming the 'dainties' of wickedness.
