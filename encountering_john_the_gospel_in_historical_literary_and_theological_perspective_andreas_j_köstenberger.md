# Encountering John: The Gospel in Historical, Literary, and Theological Perspective
**Author:** [[Andreas J. Köstenberger]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a theological and historical analysis of the Gospel of John, focusing on major themes such as Christology, the Holy Spirit, salvation, and the New Covenant community. It explores the relationship between John and the Synoptic Gospels, discussing how John supplements them through a unique perspective. Key topics include the 'True Vine' allegory (John 15), the concept of the Spirit as helper and advocate, and the nature of the New Covenant community. The text also addresses the eternal subordination of the Son and the disciples' mission in the context of a world hostile to the message.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
Explores the identity of Jesus as both God and Son, his relationship with the Father, and his role in salvation history as established in the Gospel of John.

### Theme: The Holy Spirit
Examines the role of the Spirit as the Paraclete, who teaches, reminds, and bears witness to Jesus, and aids in the conviction of the world.

### Theme: Replacement Theology
Discusses the perspective that the new covenant community, through faith in Jesus, replaces the Israel of the Old Testament as the focus of God's redemptive work.

### Theme: Eschatology
Addresses the realized eschatology of the Fourth Gospel, where eternal life is presented as a present reality through faith in Jesus.
