## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The provided text is an academic exploration of the Gospel of John, covering topics such as the historical background of the Gospel, the relationship between John and the Synoptic Gospels, and major theological themes including the divinity of Christ, the role of the Spirit, and the conceptual background of the Johannine community. The text explores how John distinguishes his theological focus, emphasizing the identity of Jesus as the Son of God and the new covenant community formed by the Spirit. Significant emphasis is placed on imagery such as the 'vine,' 'shepherd,' and 'temple' as replacements within the new covenant framework. The text also delves into the internal dynamics of the Johannine community, the concept of salvation, and the historical reality of the persecution faced by its early followers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Replacement Theology
The argument that John's Gospel presents Jesus and the Christian community as the true replacements for the Jewish temple, religious festivals, and the old covenant structures.

### Theme: Johannine Christology
A focus on the high Christology of John, emphasizing Jesus' preexistence, his role as the Son of God, and his ontological unity with the Father.

### Theme: Pneumatology
The specific role of the Holy Spirit in John as the 'Paraclete' who teaches, convicts, and guides the community after the departure of Jesus.
