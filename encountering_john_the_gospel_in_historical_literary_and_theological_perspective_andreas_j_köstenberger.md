## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an in-depth theological study of the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on its historical background, major themes, and the specific theological message contained within. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The central theological focus of the Gospel of John is the presentation of Jesus as the Son of God, both preexistent and incarnate, whose mission constitutes the final revelation of God.

### Theme: The Holy Spirit
The Spirit serves as the 'Paraclete' or advocate for the disciples, whose function is to teach, remind, bear witness to Jesus, convict the world, and guide believers into truth.

### Theme: New Covenant Community
The Johannine community is defined not as a sect, but as a new community created by the Spirit, replacing the temple and the old covenant structures, united by their mission and relationship with Jesus.

### Theme: Salvation
Salvation in John is fundamentally linked to faith in Jesus as the Son of God, bringing eternal life, and it carries both a present reality and a future eschatological expectation.
