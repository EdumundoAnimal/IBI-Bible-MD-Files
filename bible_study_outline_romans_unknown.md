# Bible Study Outline: Romans
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Outline of Romans

0 Intro - Recap. 1) Righteousness through faith (Rom 3:19-31) 2) The example of Abraham (Rom 4:1-12) 3) God's promise realised through faith (Rom 4:13-25) 4) Results of Justification (Rom 5:1-11) 5) Adam + Christ (Rom 5:12-21)

### Blessings that come from Justification

Blessings that come from Justification 1 Peace 2 Position of Grace 3 New reason to rejoice 4 New reason to rejoice even in hardship 5 The love of God in our hearts 6 Freedom from the coming wrath of God 7 Eternal life with God in Paradise.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The document outlines the theological progression of justification as presented in the book of Romans, starting from righteousness through faith and moving through the example of Abraham to the results of justification.

### Theme: Blessings of Justification
The text lists seven specific benefits resulting from justification, including peace, grace, joy in hardship, the indwelling love of God, deliverance from divine wrath, and eternal life.
