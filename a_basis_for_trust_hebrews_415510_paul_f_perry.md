# A Basis for Trust: Hebrews 4:15–5:10
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a bible study guide exploring Hebrews 4:15-5:10, focusing on the high priestly ministry of Jesus Christ. The study details the solidarity of Jesus with humanity, his ability to sympathize with human weakness, and his obedience to God's will. The text utilizes a chiastic structure to analyze the passage, emphasizing the transition from the old office of high priest to the new, superior office of Christ. It highlights Jesus's prayer life, his suffering, and his role as a mediator who grants believers access to the throne of grace.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: High Priestly Solidarity
Jesus is presented as a high priest who is not detached from human experience but uniquely qualified to sympathize with human weaknesses and temptations through his own earthly suffering.

### Theme: Chiastic Structure of Hebrews 5:1-10
The passage is analyzed using a chiasmus (A-B-C-C'-B'-A'), contrasting the limitations of the old Levitical priesthood with the superior, divine appointment of Christ.

### Theme: Obedience and Suffering
The document argues that Jesus's obedience was learned through suffering, which qualified him for his high priestly role, and that this obedience serves as an example for the faith of believers.
