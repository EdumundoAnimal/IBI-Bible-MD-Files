# Theology I: Course Notes and Assignments
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of a collection of lecture notes and assignments for a Theology I course. Topics covered include theological language, the distinctions between Biblical, Systematic, and Dogmatic theology, critical methods for biblical understanding (textual, source, form, redaction, historical, comparative-religion, structural, and reader-response criticism), synoptic gospel studies, and the theological concepts of divine immanence and transcendence.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Critical Methods
An analysis of various methodologies for interpreting the Bible, ranging from seeking the original text (textual criticism) to investigating the theological concerns of the final authors (redaction criticism).

### Theme: Divine Immanence and Transcendence
Exploration of the duality of God: being present and near within creation (immanence) while remaining separate and above creation (transcendence).

### Theme: Theological Language
Discussion on how cognitive propositions are verified, distinguishing between analytical (a priori) and synthetic (a posteriori) statements as viewed by logical positivism.
