# Introductory NT Greek Course Materials
**Author:** [[Sandie Tutty]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of study guides and examination papers for an Introductory NT Greek course. It includes paradigms for First and Second Declension nouns (masculine, feminine, and neuter), the Koine Greek alphabet, verb conjugation tables (Present Indicative Active), and rules for verb contraction. Also included are two exam papers containing translation exercises from and into Koine Greek.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Koine Greek Morphology
The study focuses on the grammatical structure of Koine Greek, specifically the declension of nouns and the conjugation of present active verbs.

### Theme: Biblical Language Pedagogy
The materials are structured for academic assessment, requiring students to demonstrate proficiency in vocabulary, script, and syntactical translation relevant to New Testament texts.
