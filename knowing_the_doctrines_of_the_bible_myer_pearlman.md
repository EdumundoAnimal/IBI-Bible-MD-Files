# Knowing the Doctrines of the Bible
**Author:** [[Myer Pearlman]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the Doctrine of the Trinity, defining it as a concept accessible only through divine revelation. It argues for the unity of God while acknowledging the distinct functions of the Father (creation), the Son (redemption), and the Holy Spirit (sanctification). It explains the Trinitarian nature within both Old and New Testaments, utilizing biblical references to support the personality and work of each Person of the Trinity. The text also employs illustrations, such as human relationships and nature analogies (water, sun, triangle), to help conceptualize these complex theological truths.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinity by Revelation
The argument that the Trinity is a mystery of God that cannot be discovered by human reason alone but must be revealed through Scripture.

### Theme: Functions of the Trinity
A functional division within the Godhead where the Father is credited with creation, the Son with redemption, and the Holy Spirit with sanctification.

### Theme: Doctrine Illustration
The use of nature analogies (water, sun, triangle) and human relational concepts to provide human minds with a framework for understanding divine plurality in unity.
