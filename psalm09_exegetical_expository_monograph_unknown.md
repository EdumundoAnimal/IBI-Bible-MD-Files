## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Theocracy and Cosmic Rectification
The central argument that Yahweh sits as the eternal Judge over an objective moral cosmos, vindicating the afflicted and holding imperial powers accountable.

### Theme: The Theology of the 'Anî (Afflicted)
Exploration of the Hebrew terms describing those who, having been stripped of earthly defense, cast themselves entirely upon Yahweh's covenant loyalty (ḥesed).

### Theme: Christological Typology
The interpretation of King David as an imperfect type of the Suffering Servant, whose deliverance from the 'gates of death' anticipates the resurrection of Christ.

### Theme: The Dialectic of Theodicy
Addressing the tension between divine silence and systemic evil by contrasting secular postmodern nihilism with the biblical promise of eventual retributive justice.
