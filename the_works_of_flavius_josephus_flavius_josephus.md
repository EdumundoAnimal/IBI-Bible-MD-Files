# The Works of Flavius Josephus
**Author:** [[Flavius Josephus]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains a compendium of works by Flavius Josephus, translated by William Whiston. It includes the contents and specific chapters from 'The Antiquities of the Jews' and 'The Wars of the Jews', detailing Jewish history, customs, and the conflict with Rome.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Historical Context of Judea
Josephus documents the historical progression of the Jewish people from creation through the Roman occupation, providing crucial background for New Testament studies.

### Theme: Sectarian Practices
Detailed descriptions of the distinct practices of the Pharisees, Sadducees, and Essenes, particularly their views on fate, the soul, and daily rituals.
