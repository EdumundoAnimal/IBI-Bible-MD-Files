# Ditto
**Author:** [[Teresa Lynn Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The sermon explores the theological transition from being a servant of God to a friend of God, using the colloquial term 'Ditto' to signify agreement or a shared sentiment. The author reflects on the nature of friendship, betrayal, and the deep desire for companionship beyond transactional relationships. Drawing on John 15:15, the text highlights Jesus' invitation to move into a deeper relationship, emphasizing that true friendship is based on shared interests, time, and fellowship rather than merely seeking material needs or miracles. The message concludes with a call to accept Jesus as both Lord and friend.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Friendship
The transition from being a servant of God to a friend, characterized by deeper intimacy, communication, and shared interests rather than just transactional worship.

### Theme: Relational Trust
Exploration of the human struggle with vulnerability, trust, and the fear of betrayal which hinders deeper relationships with both humans and the Divine.

### Theme: Transactional vs. Relational Faith
Critique of believers who only approach God as a 'supplier' of needs, contrasted with the desire for genuine presence and fellowship.
