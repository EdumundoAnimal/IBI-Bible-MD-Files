# Ditto
**Author:** [[Teresa Lynn Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This sermon discusses the transition from being a servant of God to being a friend of God, centered on John 15:15. The author uses the word 'Ditto' as a metaphor for echoing Jesus' call to friendship. The text explores the vulnerability required in friendship, the nature of Jesus' relationship with his followers, and the importance of seeking a personal connection with God beyond viewing Him solely as a provider or supplier of needs. The sermon concludes by inviting readers to accept Jesus as both Lord and friend.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Servant vs. Friend
Explores the biblical shift in the disciples' relationship with Jesus from servants who do not know the master's intent to friends who are privy to his teachings.

### Theme: Vulnerability and Betrayal
Discusses the human tendency to guard oneself due to the fear of betrayal, and how building true friendship requires overcoming these defensive barriers.

### Theme: Authentic Faith
Argues that faith should not be purely transactional or based on seeking material provision, but rather rooted in personal intimacy and fellowship with God.
