# Ditto
**Author:** [[Teresa Lynn Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The sermon 'Ditto' by Teresa Lynn Murray explores the transition in the relationship between Jesus and his disciples, moving from a master-servant dynamic to a friendship based on intimacy and fellowship. Drawing from John 15:15, the author challenges the congregation to move beyond viewing God primarily as a provider or a figure to be served, and instead embrace the offer of friendship with Him. The text touches on the human difficulty of forming trust due to past betrayals and contrasts superficial popularity with genuine, sacrificial friendship. It concludes with an invitation to reconcile with God and recognize Him as both Lord and friend.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Transition from Servitude to Friendship
The author focuses on the theological shift described in John 15:15, where Jesus elevates the disciples from servants to friends, emphasizing the intimacy and shared purpose in their relationship.

### Theme: The Nature of Friendship with God
The sermon argues that true friendship with God requires spending time in His presence and seeking holiness and righteousness, rather than just treating God as a supplier of needs.

### Theme: Human Barriers to Intimacy
The text explores the psychological and emotional guards humans build—often due to previous betrayals—which prevent them from opening up fully even to God, as referenced in Proverbs 18:19.
