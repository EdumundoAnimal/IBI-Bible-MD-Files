# Can Man Live Without God
**Author:** [[Ravi Zacharias]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text explores the necessity of objective truth and the divine in human existence. It argues that modern society suffers from a loss of truth, leading to moral and spiritual decline. Zacharias contrasts human efforts to construct meaning through political or technological means with the Christian claim that truth is not just propositional but rooted in the person of Jesus Christ. The text utilizes anecdotes, historical observations, and scriptural references to defend the necessity of God, addressing both the intellectual challenges posed by atheism and the practical implications of a world view disconnected from transcendent reality.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Objective Truth
The argument that truth exists independently of human opinion or cultural trends, and that ignoring this reality leads to societal decay.

### Theme: The Imago Dei
The biblical assertion that humans are created in the image of God, which serves as the foundation for human dignity and the reason for the gravity of moral transgressions.

### Theme: Jesus as the Embodiment of Truth
The perspective that truth is not merely a set of propositions but is fully realized in the person and life of Jesus Christ, making it personal rather than abstract.
