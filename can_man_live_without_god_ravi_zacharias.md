# Can Man Live Without God
**Author:** [[Ravi Zacharias]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text explores the necessity of God in human existence and the nature of truth. The author argues that modern life, influenced by cultural shifts and technology, attempts to move away from moral absolute and divine reality. He uses examples such as the fall of the Berlin Wall, interviews regarding aviation accidents, and the trial of Jesus Christ to illustrate that humans are searching for meaning, yet often suppressing truth. Zacharias asserts that truth is not merely propositional but personified in Jesus Christ, and that the search for meaning must ultimately lead back to God, who provides both the source and the standard for morality and existence.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Nature of Truth
Truth is framed as an 'endangered species' in a modern world characterized by subjective morality and information saturation. The author argues that absolute truth exists, is knowable, and ultimately resides in the person of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: The Personification of Truth
Rejecting the idea that truth is only a concept or a set of propositions, the author argues that biblical Christianity posits truth as a person, specifically Jesus Christ, who acts as the bridge between God and man.

### Theme: The Burden of Moral Responsibility
The author discusses how humans attempt to live 'without God' to avoid accountability, but concludes that this leads to a decay in societal morality and a loss of objective direction, as seen in the examples of political and personal failure.
