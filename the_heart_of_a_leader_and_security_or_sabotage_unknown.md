### Theme: Leadership Integrity
Leadership is grounded in character over skills, with integrity serving as a prerequisite for influence and trust.

### Theme: Emotional Insecurity
Emotional insecurity causes leaders to sabotage their effectiveness by becoming defensive, comparing themselves to others, or failing to trust in God's providence.

True leadership is modeled after Christ, characterized by humility, service, and a complete reliance on God's purpose rather than personal ambition.
