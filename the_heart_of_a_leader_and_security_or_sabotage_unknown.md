## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Servant Leadership
Leadership is defined by humility and the willingness to serve others, mirroring Christ's attitude and actions.

### Theme: Integrity as a Foundation
True leadership is built upon a foundation of character and integrity, which influences followers more effectively than charisma alone.

### Theme: Emotional Insecurity in Leadership
Insecurity leads to poor leadership behaviors such as defensiveness, sabotage, comparison with others, and victim mentality.

### Theme: Biblical Truth vs. Self-Deception
Effective leaders must replace internal lies and insecurities with God's truth to maintain healthy psychological and spiritual balance.
