**Author:** [[Edward L. Dalcour]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a comparative analysis of Trinitarian theology and Oneness Pentecostal arguments concerning the Godhead. The first segment discusses the historical development of Trinitarian thought, focusing on the works of figures like Augustine, Richard of St. Victor, and Aquinas, exploring the tension between divine unity and the distinction of persons. The second segment, titled '60 Questions on the Godhead,' presents a series of theological questions and Bible-based answers commonly used in Oneness apologetics to challenge the traditional doctrine of the Trinity, emphasizing the unity of God and identifying Jesus Christ as the full manifestation of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Historical Development
Analysis of the philosophical and theological evolution of the doctrine of the Trinity, highlighting the shift from emphasizing divine essence to the relational distinction of persons.

### Theme: Oneness Theology Apologetics
A set of scriptural interrogatives designed to refute Trinitarian definitions of God by focusing on the exclusivity of the Father's deity and identifying Jesus as the sole manifestation of the Godhead.
