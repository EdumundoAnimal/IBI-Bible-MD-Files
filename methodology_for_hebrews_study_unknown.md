## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of Christ
The central theological argument that Christ is greater than angels, serving as the definitive revealer of God's word and the mediator of a new era.

### Theme: Old Testament Integration
The author of Hebrews utilizes Old Testament motifs, including Psalms and other writings, to validate the divine status and historical mission of Jesus.

### Theme: Warning and Encouragement
A pastoral component of the text designed to prevent believers from drifting, backsliding, or ignoring the confirmation of the Gospel.
