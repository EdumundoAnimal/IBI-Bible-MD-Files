# Isaiah: a commentary
**Author:** [[Brevard S. Childs]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text is an academic theological commentary on Isaiah 63:7-64:12, focusing on form, structure, and historical context. It explores the 'communal complaint' literary form, the theological themes of God's 'servants' and 'tribes of the heritage,' the manifestation of God as Savior, Father, and the presence of the 'angel of the presence' or 'holy spirit.' The commentary discusses the historical setting post-exile, referencing the destruction of the temple in 587 BCE, and examines the interplay between Israel's rebellion, divine judgment, and God's saving acts through the lens of traditional salvation history.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Communal Complaint
A specific literary form in the Psalter and prophetic literature characterized by a recital of God's past mercies, a confession of sin, and an urgent plea for divine intervention.

### Theme: Divine Presence and Mediation
An analysis of the 'angel of the presence' and the 'holy spirit' in Isaiah 63, debating whether these refer to manifestations of God or intermediate agents in the theology of the late postexilic period.

### Theme: Salvation History
The theological framework connecting the Exodus tradition, the role of Moses, and the wilderness journey to the current cry for salvation during the devastation of the Babylonian exile.
