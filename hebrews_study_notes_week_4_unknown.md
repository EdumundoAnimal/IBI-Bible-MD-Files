### Theme: The Life of Christ
The notes structure the life of Christ into three distinct temporal phases: his incarnation, his exaltation, and his future victory, reflecting the 'already but not yet' theological framework.

### Theme: Purposes of the Incarnation
Exploration of why Jesus suffered death for humanity, focusing on the concepts of completing (teleiwoai) and his role as a pioneer.
