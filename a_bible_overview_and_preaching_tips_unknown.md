## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Homiletics (Preaching Strategy)
A structured approach to sermon preparation that prioritizes clarity, prayer, and Christ-centered communication, advising speakers to keep messages short and focused.

### Theme: Kingdom Theology
A conceptual framework that organizes biblical history into distinct phases of God's kingdom—Pattern, Promised, Partial, Prophesied, Present, Preached, and Perfected—centered on the rule of God over His people.

### Theme: Biblical Narrative Continuity
The document emphasizes a linear progression from the Fall in Eden to the final restoration in the New Testament, linking the Old Testament 'Christ Promised' to the New Testament 'Christ Proclaimed'.
