## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Koine Greek Grammar
Foundational study of noun declensions, verb conjugations (present active indicative), and rules of contraction for stems ending in epsilon.

### Theme: Theological Pedagogy
Use of linguistic training to prepare students for reading the New Testament in its original language, evidenced by exam questions focused on translation and identifying grammatical structures.

## 4. Evidence Matrix & Verbatim Assertions
> "Every Verb has a Stem (the first part that does not change) and an Ending (the second part that does change)."
*Context Source: Lesson 1-2: Present Indicative Active Verbs*
