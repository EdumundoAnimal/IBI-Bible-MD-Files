# Christian Theology: God's Special Agents: Angels
**Author:** [[Millard J. Erickson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text explores the biblical and theological doctrine of angels. It covers topics such as their origin, nature, appearance, organization, and activities, including the distinction between good angels and demons. The author examines biblical references to angelic beings, their roles as divine messengers, their limitations, and their relationship to God's plan for humanity. The text also addresses common misconceptions, the state of demonology in modern theology, and the ultimate victory of God over evil.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Nature of Angels
Angels are created, spiritual, and personal beings with intelligence and will, who are subordinate to God and fulfill his purposes.

### Theme: Angelic Activities
Angels act as messengers of God's word, protectors of believers, and participants in God's judgment and the future consummation.

### Theme: Demonology
The study of fallen angels or demons who, while active in the world, are under the ultimate authority of God and are destined for defeat.
