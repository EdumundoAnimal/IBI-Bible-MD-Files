# Introductory New Testament Greek Study Materials and Examinations
**Author:** [[Sandie Tutty]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as a collection of learning resources and examinations for an Introductory New Testament Greek course. It includes paradigms for First and Second Declension nouns (masculine, feminine, and neuter), instructions on forming Present Indicative Active verbs, a guide for 'epsilon-contract' verbs, the Greek alphabet, and sample mid-term and end-of-term examinations from Carraig Eden Theological College.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Nominal Inflection
The systematic breakdown of noun cases (Nominative, Vocative, Accusative, Genitive, Dative) across First and Second Declensions to facilitate biblical exegesis.

### Theme: Verbal Morphology
Exploration of the Present Indicative Active system, focusing on stem-ending combinations and specific rules for contract verbs.

### Theme: Theological Pedagogy
The use of formal examination structures at theological colleges to test students' mastery of biblical Greek vocabulary, translation, and grammatical identification.
