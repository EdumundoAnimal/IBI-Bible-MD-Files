# Romans Volume 2: The Reign of Grace
**Author:** [[James Montgomery Boice]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological exposition of the Epistle to the Romans, specifically focusing on Romans 7:7-12 and 7:13-20. It explores the function of the Law in revealing sin, provoking human rebellion, and leading individuals to a realization of their need for Christ. The text further applies these theological principles to the practical application of the Ten Commandments, examining each commandment as a means to expose human sinfulness and the subsequent requirement for divine grace.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Function of the Law
The law is presented not as a means of salvation, but as a mirror to reveal human sin, provoke innate rebellion, and ultimately lead the believer to despair of self-righteousness, thereby pointing toward the necessity of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Human Depravity
Boice argues that the human condition is fundamentally flawed, characterized by a persistent tendency to misuse law, distort truth, and engage in self-justification, requiring constant reliance on divine grace.

### Theme: Practical Sanctification
The text moves from high theology to practical living, using the Decalogue as a standard by which Christians are challenged to examine their thoughts, speech, and conduct in relation to God and neighbors.
