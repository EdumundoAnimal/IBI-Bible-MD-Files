# Preaching Christ in All of Scripture
**Author:** [[Edmund P. Clowney]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text explores the biblical concept of God's presence among His people, specifically analyzing the Old Testament tabernacle and the New Testament fulfillment in Jesus Christ. The author discusses the paradox of God's holiness versus the desire for intimacy, the historical rebellion of the Israelites at Sinai, and how the tabernacle served as a symbol of both God's dwelling and the separation caused by sin. Ultimately, the text argues that the New Covenant fulfills these ancient symbols through the incarnation and ongoing presence of the Holy Spirit in the church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Transcendence and Immanence
The tension between God's fearful holiness—often represented by distance and terror at Mount Sinai—and His desire to dwell in the midst of His people as an act of covenant love.

### Theme: Covenantal Symbolism
The interpretation of the tabernacle, the ark, the cherubim, and the priestly duties as types and shadows that point directly to the person and work of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Mediated Presence
The argument that because of human sinfulness, God's presence must be mediated, originally through the tabernacle and priesthood, and finally through the sacrifice and intercession of the Son.
