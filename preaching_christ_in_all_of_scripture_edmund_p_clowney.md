# Preaching Christ in All of Scripture
**Author:** [[Edmund P. Clowney]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text examines the biblical narrative of God dwelling in the midst of his people, tracing the theme from the tabernacle at Mount Sinai through the Incarnation of Jesus Christ. The author explores the paradox of a holy God who desires communion with his sinful people. Key sections include the crisis of the golden calf, the significance of the tabernacle layout, and how the New Covenant ultimately fulfills the Old Testament symbols through the presence of Christ, the true temple.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Presence
The theological tension between the holiness of God and his desire to dwell among his people, historically manifested in the tabernacle and fully realized in Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Covenantal Grace
The assertion that God's willingness to be present with Israel—and by extension, the church—is rooted in grace despite human sinfulness and rebellion.

### Theme: Typology and Fulfillment
The argument that Old Testament structures like the tabernacle and its rituals serve as types that point toward and are fulfilled by the person and work of Christ.
