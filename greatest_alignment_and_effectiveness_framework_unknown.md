# Greatest Alignment and Effectiveness Framework
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### The Alignment Diagram

Passions: What excites you - Where you find real (not perceived) motivation. Spiritual Gifts: Given by the Spirit and developed by God through experiences and training. Talents: Learned skills: what you do well but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion. GREATEST ALIGNMENT & EFFECTIVENESS: The intersection of Passions, Spiritual Gifts, and Talents. Limited sustainable energy & results: The intersection of Spiritual Gifts and Talents. Limited sustainable energy & results: The intersection of Passions and Talents. What re-charges you: The intersection of Passions and Spiritual Gifts.

### Actionable Guidance

Focus on how God has created and developed you / calls you to contribute most significantly. What re-charges you? Release those things you do well but not best. Release those things that sap your energy and pass them on to those who are energized by those tasks..

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Stewardship of Gifts
The document emphasizes that individuals should focus their efforts on the intersection of their passions, spiritual gifts, and talents to achieve the greatest alignment and effectiveness in their calling.

### Theme: Energy Management
The text distinguishes between tasks that provide sustainable energy and those that sap energy, advising individuals to delegate tasks that do not align with their God-given design.
