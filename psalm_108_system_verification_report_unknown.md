## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Redactional Fusion
The deliberate assembly of previously independent lament psalms (57 and 60) to create a unified hymn of eschatological triumph, removing traces of past defeat.

### Theme: Covenantal Fidelity
The pairing of ḥesed and 'ĕmet as a hendiadys denoting Yahweh's unshakeable faithfulness to the Davidic covenant, transcending historical crisis.

### Theme: Sovereign Territoriality
God acting as the divine surveyor who apportions land, reclaiming ancestral territory and subjugating adversaries through His immutable decree.

### Theme: Monergistic Victory
The rejection of human self-reliance and technocratic salvation in favor of complete dependence on divine intervention, viewed as the only path to true victory.
