# Pneumatology: The Gifts of the Spirit in Paul
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the nature, relevance, and use of the gifts of the Spirit within the Pauline epistles. It presents various lists of spiritual gifts found in 1 Corinthians 12, Romans 12, and Ephesians 4. The text contrasts the theological perspectives of scholars like James Dunn, who views charismata as supernatural workings for the common good, and Max Turner, who argues for a broader interpretation that includes 'ordinary' services as charismata, noting that the term does not necessarily mean 'spiritual gifts' in a restrictive, miraculous sense.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Definition of Charismata
A debate regarding whether charismata refers exclusively to supernatural/miraculous phenomena or encompasses a broader range of service and administrative functions within the church.

### Theme: Contextual Interpretation
The necessity of understanding Paul's rhetorical purpose in 1 Corinthians 12, specifically addressing the Corinthians' pride and their fixation on 'spiritual' status.

### Theme: Diversity of Gifts
Recognition of multiple, distinct lists of gifts provided by Paul in his letters, reflecting varying emphases on supernatural empowerment versus community service.
