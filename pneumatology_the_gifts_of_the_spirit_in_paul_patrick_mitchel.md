# Pneumatology: The Gifts of the Spirit in Paul
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the nature, relevance, and usage of the gifts of the Spirit in the Pauline epistles. It presents various lists of gifts found in 1 Corinthians 12, Romans 12, and Ephesians 4. The text examines the theological perspectives of scholars like James Dunn, who views charismata as visible, supernatural workings of God for the common good, and Max Turner, who argues for a broader interpretation that includes 'ordinary' acts of service as charismata, challenging the strict separation of 'supernatural' versus 'natural' gifts. It also references Fee's agreement with Turner regarding the interpretation of charismata in the context of Romans 12.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Charismata Definition
An investigation into whether charismata strictly denotes 'supernatural spiritual gifts' or a broader spectrum of gifts given by God, including ordinary acts of service.

### Theme: Interpretation of Pauline Epistles
The debate between defining spiritual gifts as immediate, powerful manifestations versus inclusive, varied contributions within the church community.
