# Christian Discipleship Manual and Romans Study
**Author:** [[Pastor Brian Gingles]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as a guide for Christian discipleship, focusing on the definition of a disciple, the cost of following Christ, and an exegetical study of Romans chapters 1 through 4. It emphasizes the importance of faith over works, the theological significance of justification, and the practical application of these truths in the life of a believer. The text includes specific reflections on the nature of salvation, the reality of sin, and the historical faith of Abraham as a paradigm for believers today.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Discipleship vs. Conversion
Distinguishes between initial conversion ('new birth') and the ongoing, disciplined process of Christian maturity, characterized by submission and transformation.

### Theme: Justification by Faith
Explores the core Pauline argument that righteousness is credited to the believer through faith, apart from works of the law, using Abraham as the primary example.

### Theme: The Cost of Discipleship
Emphasizes the necessity of death to self, self-denial, and accepting the 'cross' as the standard for genuine Christian living.

### Theme: The Reality and Effects of Sin
Categorizes the various impacts of human sinfulness on the mind, will, tongue, and social relationships to demonstrate the universal need for grace.
