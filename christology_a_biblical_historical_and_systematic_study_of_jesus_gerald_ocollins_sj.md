**Author:** [[Gerald O'Collins, SJ]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The provided text explores the theological theme of 'presence' within Christology. It examines the multifaceted ways in which Jesus' presence is mediated through Scripture, history, and systematic theology. Key topics include the incarnation, the resurrection, the role of the Holy Spirit, the Jewishness of Jesus, and the integration of masculine and feminine imagery in understanding Christ. The text further discusses the pastoral and spiritual implications of Christ's presence for contemporary believers, emphasizing an approach that respects historical specificity and encourages interpersonal connection with Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christ's Presence
An exploration of how Jesus remains present to humanity through incarnation, resurrection, the Holy Spirit, the Church, and sacramental life.

### Theme: Jewishness of Jesus
The necessity of grounding Christology in the Jewish context of Jesus, emphasizing the fulfillment of Old Testament promises and divine presence.

### Theme: Masculine and Feminine Imagery
An analysis of Jesus' ministry which transcends gender stereotypes, utilizing both traditional masculine authority and feminine imagery (e.g., mother hen, wisdom) to describe his relationship with humanity.

### Theme: Pastoral/Spiritual Theology
The application of theological concepts to the spiritual lives of believers, focusing on interpersonal intimacy and finding meaning in suffering.
