# Irish Economy
**Author:** [[The Economic and Social Research Institute (ESRI)]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview of the Irish economy, covering performance from the 1990s through 2010. It discusses GDP growth, consumer prices, unemployment rates, and public finances. The report highlights the economic boom driven by EU membership, foreign investment, and construction, followed by a downturn beginning in 2007 due to the global financial crisis and the collapse of the housing market.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Economic Transformation
Ireland evolved from an agrarian and traditional manufacturing economy to one heavily reliant on high-tech sectors and internationally traded services.

### Theme: Impact of the 2008 Financial Crisis
The reversal of growth trends, characterized by a housing market crash, rising unemployment, and a shift from budget surplus to significant deficit.
