# Towards a New Testament Theology of Money, Stewardship, and Giving
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text discusses the complexities of forming a biblical theology regarding money and possessions. It emphasizes that New Testament imperatives are universal yet context-specific. Key themes include the warning against material obsession, the importance of communal reciprocity, and the rejection of prosperity gospel interpretations. It highlights that true stewardship involves a heart aligned with God's will rather than just the accumulation of wealth, and that giving should be sacrificial, motivated by equality within the body of Christ, and reflective of a deeper spiritual reliance on God rather than earthly security.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Stewardship
The necessity of moving beyond surface-level readings of Scripture to a comprehensive, life-encompassing theology of money that centers on God's sovereignty and our role as stewards.

### Theme: Anti-Prosperity Gospel
A strong rejection of the idea that material wealth is a guaranteed indicator of God's blessing, warning that material obsession can lead to spiritual ruin and idolatry.

### Theme: Communal Reciprocity
The New Testament mandate for Christians to practice mutual support and equality, where those with abundance help those in need, fostering unity and dependency on God rather than individual hoarded resources.
