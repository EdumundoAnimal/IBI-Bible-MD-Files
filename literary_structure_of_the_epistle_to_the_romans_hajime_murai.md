## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Chiasmic Structure
The use of inverted parallelism (ABBA or ABCBA patterns) to emphasize core theological points in biblical texts.

### Theme: Justification by Faith
The recurring argument that humans are justified through faith in Christ rather than through works of the law.

### Theme: The Elect and Israel
Exploration of God's elective plan, the role of Israel, and the ultimate inclusion of Gentiles in salvation.
