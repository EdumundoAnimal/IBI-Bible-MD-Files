# New Testament Christian Origins
**Author:** [[Vernon Ralphs]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an introductory study on New Testament parables and the Book of Acts. It explores the definition and use of parables in Jesus' ministry, the challenges of modern interpretation, and the classification of parables. Additionally, it examines the Book of Acts as a narrative bridge between the Gospels and the Epistles, highlighting how the early church spread and established its structure.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Parable Theory
Explores the definition of 'parable' as a multifaceted teaching tool, derived from the Greek 'parabole' and Hebrew 'mashal', intended to test listeners and reveal spiritual truth to those with insight.

### Theme: Literary Criticism of Parables
Discusses the evolution of interpreting parables, from early allegorical views to modern challenges by scholars like Jülicher, who emphasized the historical context of Jesus' ministry.

### Theme: The Book of Acts as Bridge
Posits the Book of Acts as a transitional document that connects the life and ministry of Jesus (Gospels) to the theological teachings found in the Epistles, illustrating the church's expansion.
