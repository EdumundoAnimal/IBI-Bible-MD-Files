# Good to Great: Level 5 Leadership
**Author:** [[Jim Collins]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the concept of Level 5 Leadership, characterized by a paradoxical blend of extreme personal humility and professional will. It contrasts these leaders with comparison company leaders who often relied on outsized egos. The text examines individuals like Darwin Smith of Kimberly-Clark, Colman Mockler of Gillette, and others, arguing that sustained organizational success requires leaders who look into the mirror to apportion responsibility for poor results and out the window to attribute credit for success. It also emphasizes the importance of setting up successors for success and the rejection of the 'star power' model of leadership.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Level 5 Leadership
A leadership style defined by the paradoxical combination of intense personal humility and unyielding professional will.

### Theme: Window and Mirror Attribution
Level 5 leaders attribute success to factors outside themselves ('the window') and accept full responsibility for failures ('the mirror'), unlike comparison leaders who do the opposite.

### Theme: Successor Planning
The commitment to ensuring the long-term success of the company beyond the current leader's tenure, often by prioritizing the company's future over individual ego.
