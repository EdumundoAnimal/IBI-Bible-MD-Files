## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Discipleship and Abiding
Exploration of the metaphorical relationship between the vine and the branches, emphasizing the necessity of remaining in Jesus to bear spiritual fruit.

### Theme: The Comforter (Holy Spirit)
Jesus' promises to his disciples regarding the sending of the Holy Spirit to guide, teach, and provide peace after his departure.

### Theme: Christ's High Priestly Prayer
An analysis of Jesus' prayer for his own glorification and for the protection and sanctification of his followers as they navigate the world.
