## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
A metaphor for the relationship between Jesus and his followers, emphasizing the necessity of abiding in Him to bear spiritual fruit.

### Theme: High Priestly Prayer
Jesus's final intercessory prayer before his arrest, focusing on unity, glorification, and the protection of his followers.

### Theme: The Promise of the Holy Spirit
Jesus instructs his disciples about the coming 'Counselor' or 'Helper' who would guide them into truth after his departure.
