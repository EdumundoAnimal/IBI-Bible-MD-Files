## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Incarnation and Exaltation of Christ
The notes outline a three-fold progression in the life of Christ: the incarnation ('A Little Lower'), the exaltation ('Crowned him with glory + honour'), and the final victory ('Dominion'). It notes that this victory is 'inaugurated but yet is coming'.

### Theme: The Purpose of the Incarnation
The text identifies the purpose of the incarnation as Christ tasting death for humanity (suffering) and 'telewoai' (completing), while also highlighting the 'Pioneer aspect' of Christ as referenced in Hebrews 2:11 and 2:14.

### Theme: Priesthood of Jesus
The notes conclude with a prompt regarding the implications of the Priesthood of Jesus, specifically referencing Hebrews 2:17.
