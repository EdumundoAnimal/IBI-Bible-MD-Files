# Jesus and Money: A Theology of Money, Stewardship, and Giving
**Author:** [[Ben Witherington III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This chapter explores the New Testament perspective on money, stewardship, and giving, arguing for a theology that balances material needs with spiritual faithfulness. It critiques prosperity gospel interpretations, emphasizes reciprocal generosity within the body of Christ, and highlights the importance of work, contentment, and avoiding the snare of greed. The text analyzes biblical passages, including 2 Corinthians 8-9, to redefine Christian giving as a means of establishing equality and supporting the needs of fellow believers rather than a transactional mechanism for personal gain.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Critique of Prosperity Gospel
The text rejects the notion that the New Testament encourages storing up wealth or that prosperity is a guaranteed sign of God's favor, identifying such views as a snare and a corruption of spiritual contentment.

### Theme: Theological Basis for Reciprocity
Drawing on 2 Corinthians 8-9, the author argues that Christian giving is designed to create equality within the church community, ensuring that the abundance of some addresses the needs of others.

### Theme: The Necessity of Work and Stewardship
It asserts that biblical stewardship requires believers to work for their own needs rather than living in idleness, framing work as a duty and a way to support the ministry and the poor.
