# The Gospel According to John - Chapter XV
**Author:** [[Leon Morris]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a scholarly commentary on John 15:1-25. It explores the metaphor of the True Vine, the theological importance of abiding in Christ, the nature of discipleship as fruitfulness, the command to love, and the inevitability of persecution for those who follow Christ. The text includes detailed linguistic and theological exposition on the concepts of 'abiding', the vine-dresser, and the relationship between Christ and his followers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Metaphor of the Vine
Christ identifies himself as the True Vine, emphasizing that the vitality and fruitfulness of the believer are strictly dependent on an organic, continuous connection to Him.

### Theme: The Theology of Abiding
Abiding is defined as the essential prerequisite for Christian life, representing a mutual indwelling between Christ and the believer which is not static but active and obedient.

### Theme: Christian Discipleship and Love
Discipleship is characterized by obedience to Christ's commands, primarily the command to love one another, modeled after Christ's sacrificial love for his friends.

### Theme: Persecution as Inevitable
Because believers are identified with Christ, they face inevitable opposition and persecution from the world that hated Christ first.
