## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The documents provided outline the key themes and structures for the Book of Psalms and the Song of Songs. Psalms is organized into five books, reflecting the structure of the Torah. Key genres identified include complaints (individual and communal), thanksgivings, and hymns. Technical aspects of Hebrew poetry such as parallelism (synonymous, antithetic, and synthetic) and chiasm are noted. The Song of Songs is analyzed as a collection of love poems, discussing the relationship between two lovers, the role of the 'daughters of Jerusalem', and its canonical status as an analogy for the relationship between God and His people.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Psalms Structure and Genre
Psalms serves as the prayer book of Israel, organized into five books. Classification is complex, but key types include individual and communal complaints, thanksgivings, and hymns of praise.

### Theme: Hebrew Poetry Features
Hebrew poetry is defined by parallelism (the second line complementing, contrasting, or repeating the first) and structural devices like chiasm.

### Theme: Song of Songs Interpretation
The book is interpreted both as erotic human love poetry and as an allegory for the divine relationship between God and His people.
