## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Redemption
The psalm emphasizes that deliverance is not earned through merit but is a result of unilateral divine grace, characterizing the relationship between Yahweh and the believer.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The document posits that Psalm 116 finds its ultimate meaning in the passion of Jesus Christ, specifically regarding the 'cup of salvation' and his resurrection victory over the bonds of death.

### Theme: Critique of Commercial Religion
The report contrasts ancient and modern transactional religion (works-based merit) with the biblical reality of grace, where the only 'repayment' is the acceptance of divine gifts.
