# Hebrews and Revelation Study
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a structured theological study on the Epistle to the Hebrews and the Book of Revelation. For Hebrews, it explores themes of Christ's superiority over the Old Covenant, the nature of faith, and warnings against apostasy. For Revelation, it outlines various interpretive schools—Preterist, Historicist, Idealist, and Futurist—and examines the book's apocalyptic genre and purpose as a message of hope and cosmic triumph for the early church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
Christ is presented as superior to angels, the Old Covenant priesthood, and the sacrificial system, serving as the mediator of a new and eternal covenant.

### Theme: Apocalyptic Genre
Revelation is analyzed as literature meant to provide divine perspective during times of persecution, characterized by symbols, visions, and a focus on cosmic judgment.

### Theme: Eschatological Schools
The text categorizes interpretations of Revelation into four primary schools: Preterist, Historicist, Idealist, and Futurist, noting their distinct approaches to prophecy and history.
