## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sovereign Covenant Mercy (Ḥesed)
Yahweh's unmerited, covenantally pledged, and relentless lovingkindness that defines His relationship with humanity, superseding retributive justice.

### Theme: Human Transience and Divine Compassion
The biblical paradox where humanity is acknowledged as fragile dust, which instead of provoking divine disgust, serves as the catalyst for God's fatherly tenderness and pity.

### Theme: Substitutionary Redemption
The theological framework where God, acting as the Gō’ēl (kinsman-redeemer), removes guilt and restores the dispossessed, ultimately fulfilled in the sacrificial work of Christ.
