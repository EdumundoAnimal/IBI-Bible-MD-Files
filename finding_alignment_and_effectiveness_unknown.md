# Finding Alignment and Effectiveness
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a visual Venn diagram illustrating the intersection of Spiritual Gifts, Passions, and Talents to achieve 'Greatest Alignment & Effectiveness'. Spiritual Gifts are defined as being given by the Spirit and developed by God through experiences and training. Passions are described as what excites you and where you find real (not perceived) motivation. Talents are described as learned skills: things you do well, but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion. The intersections create specific dynamics: Limited sustainable energy and results exist where gifts meet talents, or where talents meet passions. The center point where all three intersect represents the Greatest Alignment & Effectiveness. The text provides two calls to action: Focus on how God has created and developed you to contribute most significantly and answer what re-charges you. It advises releasing things you do well but not best, and passing off tasks that sap your energy to those energized by them.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Stewardship of Gifts
The biblical concept that one should focus their efforts on God-given spiritual gifts to contribute most significantly to the community.

### Theme: Sustainable Ministry
The practice of identifying one's unique 're-charge' points to ensure longevity and prevent burnout in service or vocation.

### Theme: Delegation and Wisdom
The principle of releasing tasks that one does well but that do not align with their core calling, allowing others to flourish in those areas.
