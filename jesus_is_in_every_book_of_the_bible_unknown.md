The theological framework that asserts Jesus Christ is the central theme and identity represented in every individual book of the Bible, spanning both Old and New Testaments.

### Theme: Typology and Metaphor
The use of symbolic roles (e.g., 'Passover lamb', 'High Priest', 'Sun of Righteousness') to explain the multi-faceted nature of Christ's work throughout scriptural history.
