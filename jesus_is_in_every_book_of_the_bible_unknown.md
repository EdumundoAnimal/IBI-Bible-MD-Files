## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christocentric Biblical Interpretation
The theological belief that Jesus Christ is the unifying subject of all biblical books, representing different facets of His divinity and salvific work.

### Theme: Typology
The identification of persons, events, or institutions in the Old Testament as 'types' that prefigure or foreshadow the life and ministry of Jesus.
