# Hebrews: Sept. 2009
**Author:** [[Paul Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines the Diploma and Degree question options for a Bible study assignment on the Book of Hebrews, dated September 2009. The Diploma section provides two main prompts. Option 1 asks students to compare and contrast various views regarding the author's comparison of Christ to the Angels (Hebrews 1:4-14, 50% weight) and explain how his points encourage believers today (Conclusion 150, 30% weight). Option 2 asks students to compare the priests' work under the Old Covenant with Christ's work in Hebrews (Hebrews 7:23-28 & 9:11-14, 45% weight) and how these truths encourage believers today (30% weight). The Degree section offers two options as well. Option 1 concerns Hebrews 6:4-8 (70% weight for evaluating scholarly views, 30% for practical application). Option 2 concerns Hebrews 1:1-2:4, focusing on the author's identification with his audience and the interplay between exposition, encouragement, and warning (70% weight for connections, 30% for modern relevance). The document also notes the difference between Diploma and Degree level expectations, emphasizing that Degree level work requires more diverse scholarly engagement and nuance.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Exegesis and Application
The assignments require a dual focus: analyzing the historical and scholarly context of the text (exegesis) and applying these theological truths to modern belief and practice.

### Theme: Christology
A recurring theme involves exploring the superiority of Christ, both in relation to the angels and in comparison to the Old Covenant priestly system.

### Theme: Academic Rigor
The document distinguishes between Diploma and Degree levels, where the latter demands critical engagement with multiple scholarly perspectives rather than just surface-level summary.
