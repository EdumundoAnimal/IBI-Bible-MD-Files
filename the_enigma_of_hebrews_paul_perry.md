## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Chiasmic Structure
The author identifies a chiastic pattern in Hebrews 1:1-4, where framing statements (A/A') surround internal thematic developments (B/B' and C/C'), highlighting the divinity and role of the Son.

### Theme: Scholarly Uncertainty
A recurring theme is the academic debate surrounding the 'audience question' of Hebrews. Because historical data regarding the recipients and the precise purpose of the letter is sparse, many scholars argue that reconstructions remain speculative.

### Theme: Rhetorical Style
The text notes that Hebrews is distinguished by its sophisticated Greek composition, marked by specific literary devices like alliteration and periodic sentence structures that emphasize the high-level artistry of the author.
