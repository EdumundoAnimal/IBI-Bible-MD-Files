## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Chiastic Structure
The use of a symmetrical A-B-C-C-B-A pattern in the opening verses of Hebrews (1:1-4) to emphasize the supremacy of the Son.

### Theme: Literary Sophistication
Hebrews is noted for its high-level rhetoric, periodic sentences, and stylistic elegance, distinguishing it from other NT documents.

### Theme: Historical Reconstruction
Scholars struggle with the 'audience question' due to a lack of concrete external evidence, leading to debates on the validity of speculative reconstructions.
