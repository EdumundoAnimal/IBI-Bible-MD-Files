# Vine's Expository Dictionary of New Testament Words
**Author:** [[W. E. Vine]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an exhaustive, scholarly examination of Greek words found in the New Testament. It functions as a comprehensive dictionary, cataloging Greek terms alongside their precise English meanings, usage in the original context, and distinctions between variations in the Authorised and Revised Versions of the Bible. The text details the etymology and semantic range of specific Greek verbs, particularly focusing on variations of terms for 'come' or 'arrive' (e.g., erchomai, paraginomai), and terms related to comfort (paraklesis). It emphasizes that understanding the specific nuances of these original language terms is essential for accurate biblical exegesis.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Expository Linguistics
The methodology of defining New Testament Greek words by their specific etymological roots and semantic differences compared to standard English translations.

### Theme: Contextual Semantic Nuance
The argument that the English language frequently generalizes various Greek terms; therefore, identifying the specific Greek word used in a passage is necessary to determine the exact meaning intended by the author.

### Theme: Historical Philology
The evolution of the Koine Greek language as a result of the spread of Greek culture and its role as the lingua franca of the Mediterranean world at the time of the New Testament.
