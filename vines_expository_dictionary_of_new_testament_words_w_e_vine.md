# Vine's Expository Dictionary of New Testament Words
**Author:** [[W. E. Vine]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text provides an exhaustive lexicographical analysis of Greek words in the New Testament translated as 'come'. It covers compound verbs including erchomai, eiserchomai, exerchomai, eperchomai, and others. The entries detail grammatical nuances, including prefixes and their impact on the meaning (e.g., 'para' meaning 'by or away', 'ana' meaning 'up'). Each entry includes textual references to New Testament books and verses, noting differences between Authorized (A.V.) and Revised Versions (R.V.). The text also briefly touches on related concepts like 'COLT' and 'COMFORT'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Lexicographical Analysis
The document serves as a specialized reference work detailing the precise meanings of original Greek terms to clarify translation variations between English Bible versions.

### Theme: Biblical Linguistics
An exploration of how common Greek prefixes (e.g., ana-, para-, kata-, epi-) modify the root meaning of verbs of motion, specifically those related to 'coming'.

### Theme: Expository Method
Utilizing historical and context-based research to explain the nuances of New Testament language, acknowledging the transition of Greek into Koine for biblical writing.
