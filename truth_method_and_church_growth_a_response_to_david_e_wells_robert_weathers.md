# Truth, Method, and Church Growth: A Response to David E Wells
**Author:** [[Robert Weathers]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article analyzes the intersection of church growth methodologies with the changing cultural landscape of the postmodern West. It examines David E. Wells' critique of the 'seeker-targeted' church movement, which Wells argues has prioritized marketing and cultural accommodation over theological substance. Weathers evaluates how churches can maintain biblical integrity while reaching contemporary audiences through new forms of engagement, including small groups, conversational teaching, and an emphasis on redemptive community.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Seeker-Targeted Paradigm
An approach to church growth that tailors form and content to the perceptions and preferences of the unchurched, criticized by David Wells as a form of cultural accommodation that risks eroding core theology.

### Theme: Postmodernism and Evangelicalism
The struggle for the church to remain relevant in a post-Enlightenment society that favors relativism, subjectivity, and experiential knowledge over traditional doctrinal foundations.

### Theme: Theological Integrity vs. Marketing
The conflict between the need for churches to be understood by their community and the danger of reducing the Gospel to a marketable commodity to satisfy the therapeutic needs of the self.
