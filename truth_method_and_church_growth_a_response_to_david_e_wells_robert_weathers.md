# Truth, Method, and Church Growth: A Response to David E. Wells
**Author:** [[Robert Weathers]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This article analyzes the 'seeker-targeted' movement in American evangelicalism in light of critiques from David E. Wells. It explores the shift from modernism to postmodernism in Western culture and how churches are adapting their communication methods to reach a postmodern audience. The author discusses the dangers of market-driven ministry, the importance of retaining theological depth, and the necessity of moving from self-centeredness to Christ-centeredness. Ultimately, it argues that while methodologies may need to adapt to reach contemporary culture, they must not compromise the integrity of the gospel or the fundamental truths of Scripture.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Seeker-Targeted Church Growth
An approach to ministry that adapts communication methods to reach the unchurched by addressing their needs and postmodern worldview.

### Theme: Theological Integrity vs. Cultural Adaptation
The tension between remaining relevant to a postmodern culture and maintaining the central truths of the gospel, as critiqued by David Wells.

### Theme: Postmodernism and the Church
The impact of a relativistic, post-Enlightenment cultural climate on evangelical theology and church practice, necessitating a shift from institutionalism to participation.
