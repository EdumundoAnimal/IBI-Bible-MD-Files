# Baker's Dictionary of Theology
**Author:** [[Everett F. Harrison]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of the front matter of 'Baker's Dictionary of Theology' followed by an explicit statement of the 'Beliefs of AGI' and academic theological entries for 'Unitarianism' and 'Unity'. The 'Beliefs of AGI' outlines twelve fundamental tenets including the inspiration of the Bible, the Trinity, the Virgin Birth, substitutionary atonement, baptism, and the gifts of the Holy Spirit. The latter sections provide scholarly definitions and historical overviews of the concepts of Unitarianism and Unity from a Christian theological perspective.
