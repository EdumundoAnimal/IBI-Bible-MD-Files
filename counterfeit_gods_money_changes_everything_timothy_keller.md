# Counterfeit Gods: Money Changes Everything
**Author:** [[Timothy Keller]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the intersection of spiritual life and financial behavior, focusing on the biblical concept of greed and idolatry. It references the Yellowstone Club bankruptcy as a case study of greed and utilizes the story of Zacchaeus as a model for spiritual transformation. The text argues that money is often treated as a 'deep idol' that drives behavioral patterns such as the desire for power, approval, and security. True freedom is presented as a reorientation toward the gospel, moving away from materialistic dependence and toward grace.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Idolatry
The tendency for humans to replace God with 'deep idols' such as money, social approval, or power, which then dictate their identity and security.

### Theme: Biblical Perspective on Money
The assertion that greed is not just a lack of money but an anxiety and a failure to trust in God, exemplified by Jesus' teachings in Luke.

### Theme: The Gospel as Transformation
The argument that behavioral changes regarding wealth are only lasting when they stem from a heart transformation through the gospel, rather than legalistic compliance.
