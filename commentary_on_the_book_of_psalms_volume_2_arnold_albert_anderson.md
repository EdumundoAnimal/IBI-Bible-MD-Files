# Commentary on the Book of Psalms: Volume 2
**Author:** [[Arnold Albert Anderson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume provides a scholarly commentary on the Book of Psalms, specifically Book IV, covering Psalms 90 through 106. The text offers detailed exegesis of Psalm 90, exploring its themes of divine sovereignty, human mortality, and the brevity of life. The commentary includes analysis of theological terminology, linguistic nuances in the Hebrew text, and historical context for the authorship and purpose of these specific psalms. It examines the structure of individual poems, noting the use of lament, petition, and wisdom literature conventions within the broader framework of Israelite worship and prayer.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Human Mortality and Transience
The text explores the brevity of human life, frequently contrasted against the eternal nature of God. The psalmist uses metaphors such as 'sleep' and 'grass' to emphasize that man's existence is fleeting and subject to the divine wrath for sin.

### Theme: Divine Sovereignty and Transcendence
The commentary highlights God's pre-existence and his role as the architect of creation, existing before the world was brought forth, contrasting this with man's dependence and eventual return to dust.

### Theme: Wisdom and Penitence
Psalm 90 is analyzed as both a communal lament and a wisdom poem. It serves as a pedagogical tool intended to help the reader acquire 'a heart of wisdom' through the recognition of divine judgment and the need for divine favor.
