## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Unconditional Covenant Fidelity
The argument that God's commitment to His people, rooted in the Abrahamic Grant, is irrevocable and sovereign, independent of human failure.

### Theme: Sovereign Teleology
The view that history is not chaotic or random but is governed by God's purposeful plan, where even adversity and affliction serve as instruments of divine refinement.

### Theme: Torah as Liberty
The concept that true freedom is not the absence of restraint but the opportunity for joyful, holy alignment with divine instructions (Torah) within the covenant relationship.
