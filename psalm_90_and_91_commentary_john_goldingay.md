# Psalm 90 and 91 Commentary
**Author:** [[John Goldingay]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains detailed scholarly commentary on Psalm 90 and the beginning of Psalm 91. It provides a formal translation of Psalm 90, followed by an interpretation section that delves into the themes of human mortality, divine eternity, divine wrath, and the human response to suffering. The author discusses the historical and theological context of the psalms, specifically examining the plea for Yhwh to turn and show favor to his people. It includes extensive scholarly notes, theological implications, and linguistic analysis of Hebrew terminology.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Eternity vs. Human Mortality
The commentary highlights the stark contrast between the eternal, unchanging nature of Yhwh and the brief, transient nature of human life, often compared to grass that withers.

### Theme: Theodicy and Divine Wrath
The text explores the theological struggle of the community facing suffering and affliction, framing it within the context of living under divine judgment or wrath.

### Theme: Communal Plea and Restoration
The author argues that the psalms function as a prayer for Yhwh to cease his anger, restore the community, and provide a lasting foundation for their labor and existence.
