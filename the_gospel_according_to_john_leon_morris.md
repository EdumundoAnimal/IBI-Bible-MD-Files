# The Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[Leon Morris]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text is a scholarly commentary on John 15:1-16, titled 'The True Vine.' It explores the theological significance of Jesus' analogy of the vine and the branches, focusing on themes of divine pruning, the necessity of abiding in Christ to bear fruit, and the nature of Christian love and friendship with God. The commentary delves into the distinction between the Father as the vinedresser and Jesus as the vine, the imperative of obedience, and the concept of 'joy' as a finished, imparted work of God in the believer. It also contextualizes the passage within the broader scope of Johannine thought on discipleship, prayer, and the inevitability of persecution for those who follow Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Abiding in Christ
The necessity of a vital, continuing connection with Jesus for the believer to live a fruitful life and perform works in accordance with his will.

### Theme: Divine Pruning
The process by which the Father 'cleanses' the believer to ensure productive growth, emphasizing that even active believers require spiritual refinement.

### Theme: Christian Friendship
The transformation of the relationship between Jesus and his disciples from master-servant to friends, characterized by self-sacrificial love and full disclosure of the Father's will.

### Theme: Joy as a Finished Work
The idea that Christian joy is not an emotional fleeting state but a solid, imparted quality from Christ that is intended to be complete in the believer.
