## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of Christ
The author of Hebrews systematically uses Old Testament references to establish that Christ is superior to angels, who are described as created beings.

### Theme: Divine Revelation
The text emphasizes that the Son is the primary vehicle through which God speaks, serving as the greatest and final revealer of God's word.

### Theme: Messianic Coronation
The document references Psalm 45:6-7 regarding the 'Oil of Gladness' as a sign of Christ's coronation as the effective High Priest.
