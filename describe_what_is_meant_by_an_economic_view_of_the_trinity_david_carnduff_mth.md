# Describe what is meant by an economic view of the Trinity
**Author:** [[David Carnduff MTh]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the theological doctrine of the Trinity, noting that while the specific term does not appear in the Bible, the concept is implied throughout Scripture. It categorizes biblical evidence for the Trinity through the Old Testament, the Gospels, Acts, and the Pauline and non-Pauline epistles. It further examines how each member of the Trinity—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—participates in specific divine works such as sanctification, resurrection, and adoption. The text also includes various analogies to illustrate the concept of oneness in plurality, such as the tripartite nature of time, volume, and matter.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Implicit Trinitarianism
The argument that while the word 'Trinity' is absent from the Bible, the Scriptures contain fundamental teachings regarding God's nature that necessitate the Trinitarian doctrine.

### Theme: Oneness in Plurality
The use of natural and scriptural illustrations—such as light passing through a prism, the human spirit/soul/body, and the marital union—to conceptualize how unity can coexist with distinct persons or expressions.

### Theme: Economic Trinity in Action
The systematic breakdown of how the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit each play a distinct, functional, and collaborative role in divine activities like sanctification, resurrection, and the adoption of believers.
