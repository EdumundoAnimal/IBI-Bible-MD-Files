The text classifies the study of the Bible into three lenses: 'Helicopter' (Big Picture), 'Progressive' (development over time), and 'Christ-centered' (the focus on Jesus).

### Theme: Expository Preaching
Emphasizes letting the text do the work, staying faithful to the original context, and using illustrations effectively to bridge the gap between biblical truth and modern culture.

### Theme: Application and Maturity
Preaching is categorized not just as information transfer but as a tool for personal growth, accountability, and maturity within the local church.
