## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theological Context
Identifying the spiritual issues a biblical text addresses and bridging the gap to apply those truths to current situations.

### Theme: Exegesis Subjectivity
Acknowledging that the application and theological interpretation phase represents the most subjective stage of the exegetical process.

### Theme: Canonical Context
Connecting the themes of a specific passage to the broader biblical corpus to see how it relates to other scriptures.

### Theme: Application (Significance)
The process of translating the identified theological truth into relevant actions or insights for the modern reader.
