# Hebrews 1:1-14 Commentary
**Author:** [[William L. Lane]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a scholarly commentary on the opening chapter of the Epistle to the Hebrews, specifically focusing on verses 1 through 14. The text examines the literary structure of the passage, the use of Old Testament quotations, and the theological argument regarding the superiority of the Son over the angels. It highlights the use of diverse rhetorical tools, including chiasm, alliteration, and the systematic arrangement of biblical citations to establish the divine nature and eternal sovereignty of the Son.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The text argues for the supremacy of the Son over the angels by highlighting his role as the mediator of creation, his eternal nature, and his final exaltation to the right hand of God.

### Theme: Literary Structure of Hebrews
The author analyzes the sophisticated literary architecture of Hebrews 1:1-14, noting how the writer employs alternating types of literary forms like exposition and exhortation to create a cohesive theological argument.

### Theme: Use of Old Testament Quotations
A central theme is the systematic use of Old Testament texts to build a catena of evidence that confirms the Son's status as divine and superior to the angelic order.
