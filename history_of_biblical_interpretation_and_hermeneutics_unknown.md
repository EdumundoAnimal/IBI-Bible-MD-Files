## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutical Evolution
The progression of biblical interpretation from early Jewish practice through historical-critical methods to modern theological and plurality-based approaches.

### Theme: Contextual Interpretation
The critical necessity of integrating historical, geographical, and literary contexts to accurately interpret biblical texts, avoiding 21st-century biases.

### Theme: Theological Interpretation
The synthesis of academic tools with faith-based, devotional, and theological goals to read the Bible as the word of God for current edification.
