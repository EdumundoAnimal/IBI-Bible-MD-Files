# Finding Alignment and Effectiveness: Spiritual Gifts, Talents, and Passions
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a Venn diagram framework for personal growth and ministry effectiveness. It highlights three intersecting areas: Spiritual Gifts (God-given, developed by experience/training), Talents (skills one does well but can be draining if overused), and Passions (what excites you). The intersection of these three defines 'Greatest Alignment & Effectiveness'. It includes practical advice: focus on what recharges you, release things that do not fit your gifts/passions, and delegate tasks that sap your energy.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Alignment and Effectiveness
The state achieved when spiritual gifts, learned talents, and personal passions converge to produce sustained energy and results.

### Theme: Stewardship of Energy
The practice of identifying tasks that recharge the individual versus those that sap energy, recommending the release or delegation of the latter.
