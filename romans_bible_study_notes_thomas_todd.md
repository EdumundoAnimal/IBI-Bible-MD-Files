### Romans 8:28-30 and Election

Ch 8:28-30. Called according to his purpose. Foreknew. Predestined - to be like Jesus. 1) Election is a sovereign act. 2) Not based on merit. 3) Foreknows who will believe they are in Christ. 4) God gives all men the ability to believe. Men are only chosen because he will enable them to believe. Double predestination. Irresistible election. Jesus is God's chosen one.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Law and Sin
The text explores the relationship between the Law and sin, noting that the Law can arouse sinful desire (Rom 7:5) and that sin is a powerful master personified (hamartia).

### Theme: Election and Sovereignty
A comparison between Arminian and Calvinist views on election, focusing on whether it is a sovereign act based on foreknowledge or a specific choice enabled by God.

### Theme: Israel's Role in Salvation
The notes discuss the hardening of Israel as a temporary state to allow Gentiles to receive mercy, with the ultimate hope that Israel will be saved.
