# Romans Bible Study Notes
**Author:** [[Thomas Todd]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
These notes provide an overview of key themes in the Epistle to the Romans. Topics covered include the role of the Law, the nature of sin (hamartia), the distinction between 'weak' and 'strong' Christians, predestination, and the relationship between Israel and the Gentiles. The study moves through various chapters (5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 14) exploring concepts like justification, the 'spirit of life,' the remnants of Israel, and Christian liberty.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sin and the Law
Exploration of the legalistic view of sin, the personification of sin as a master, and the purpose of the Law in exposing human sinful nature.

### Theme: Theological Perspectives on Salvation
A comparative analysis between Arminian and Calvinist views on election, predestination, and the human capacity to accept or reject divine grace.

### Theme: Israel and the Remnant
Discussion regarding God's relationship with Israel, the hardening of hearts, the inclusion of Gentiles, and the belief that God has not rejected His people.

### Theme: Christian Liberty
An analysis of Romans 14 regarding non-essentials in the faith, specifically the distinction between 'weak' believers bound by restrictions and 'strong' believers who are free.
