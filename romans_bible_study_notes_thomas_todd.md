# Romans Bible Study Notes
**Author:** [[Thomas Todd]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Comprehensive notes covering the Book of Romans, exploring theological concepts including the role of the Law, sin, predestination, election, and Christian ethics. The notes address key chapters such as Romans 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, and 14, highlighting debates between Arminian and Calvinist interpretations of election, the interplay between faith and works, and the nature of the Christian life.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Role of the Law
Analysis of the Law's function, particularly how it relates to sin, its inability to save, and the transition from living under the Law to living under the Spirit.

### Theme: Sovereignty and Election
Exploration of the theological divide between Arminian and Calvinist views on divine election, predestination, and human response.

### Theme: Christian Ethics and Liberty
Examination of practical Christian life, particularly regarding 'non-essentials' like food restrictions and holy days, and the distinction between 'weak' and 'strong' believers.
