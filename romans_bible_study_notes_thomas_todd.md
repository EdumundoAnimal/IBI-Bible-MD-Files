# Romans Bible Study Notes
**Author:** [[Thomas Todd]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains Bible study notes on the Book of Romans, dated January 2009. The notes cover theological concepts including sin, the Law, the role of Christ versus Adam, election (Arminian vs. Calvinist views), the status of Israel, and Christian liberty concerning non-essentials. The study progresses through chapters 5, 7, 8, 11, 13, and 14 of Romans.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sin and the Law
Exploration of sin as a powerful master (hamartia) and the law's role in arousing sinful desires in the flesh.

### Theme: Election and Sovereignty
A comparative study between Arminian and Calvinist interpretations of election, merit, and the nature of salvation.

### Theme: Israel's Role
Discussion on whether there is hope for Israel, focusing on the concept of the 'remnant' and the inclusion of Gentiles.

### Theme: Christian Liberty
Analysis of 'strong' versus 'weak' believers regarding non-essential practices like food choices and holy days in Romans 14.
