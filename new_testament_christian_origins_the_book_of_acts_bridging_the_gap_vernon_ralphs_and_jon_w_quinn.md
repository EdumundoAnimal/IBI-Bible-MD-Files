**Author:** [[Vernon Ralphs and Jon W. Quinn]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of 'The Parabolic Teaching of Jesus' and a study titled 'The Book of Acts: Bridging the Gap'. It covers the definitions, forms, and historical interpretation of parables in the ministry of Jesus. Subsequently, it transitions to an analysis of the Book of Acts as the historical bridge between the Gospels and the Epistles, detailing the spread of the church from Jerusalem to the end of the earth as commanded in the Great Commission.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Parabolic Teaching
An analysis of the linguistic and literary forms used by Jesus to teach, including parables, riddles, allegories, and proverbs, emphasizing that these methods were used to both reveal and conceal truth based on the listener's receptivity.

### Theme: The Bridging Role of Acts
The argument that the Book of Acts serves as the critical historical and theological transition between the Gospels (the life and ministry of Jesus) and the Epistles (the expansion and establishment of the church among Gentiles).

### Theme: Literary Interpretation
The evolution of the interpretation of Gospel parables, moving from historical allegorization (e.g., Augustine) to the modern critical approach which seeks original contexts and intentions.
