# New International Biblical Commentary: Hebrews
**Author:** [[Donald A. Hagner]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of introductory excerpts from a commentary on the Epistle to the Hebrews. It explores authorship theories (Paul, Apollos, Barnabas), the purpose of the letter (addressing the danger of apostasy and Jewish-Christian suffering), and the literary form (sermon-treatise). It also discusses the theological emphasis on Christ's superiority and the covenant, as well as the textual classification of Hebrews manuscripts.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Apostasy and Faithfulness
The primary purpose of the letter is identified as preventing the readers from falling away from the faith and warning against turning from the living God due to pressure or internal weakness.

### Theme: Theological Superiority of Christ
The central argument of the epistle is the presentation of Christ as the finality of God's revelation, superior to all previous dispensations.

### Theme: Authorship Debates
The document reviews historical speculation regarding the identity of the author, noting that it remains a subject of uncertainty among scholars.
