## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutics
Defined as the art and science of interpretation, focusing on the principles needed to bridge the gap between ancient text and modern understanding.

### Theme: The Four Distances
The major obstacles to biblical interpretation: time, culture, geography, and language, which create a barrier between the reader and the text's original intent.

### Theme: Contextual Study
The method of interpretation that balances the historical background, literary genre, and theological significance of a passage to derive accurate meaning.
