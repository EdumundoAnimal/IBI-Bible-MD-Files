# Becoming More Like Christ
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document outlines various qualities of Christ-likeness. These include maintaining a consistent intimacy with Christ, understanding one's sense of destiny as a cooperative process with God, committing to the Great Commission, maintaining active involvement with the body of Christ, listening for God's voice, fostering a proven love for Scripture, practicing moderation in all things except Christ, and maintaining a righteous fear of God while navigating suffering and attack.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christ-likeness
The transformative journey of becoming more whole, characterized by relational maturity, spiritual discipline, and divine purpose.

### Theme: Relational Intimacy
The importance of a dynamic, ongoing relationship with God that moves beyond stereotypes and into personal, daily interaction.

### Theme: Spiritual Resilience
Developing the capacity to handle suffering, misunderstanding, and attacks by remaining humble and focused on Christ's example.
