# The Epistle to the Hebrews: Introduction and Study Notes
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Hebrews 1:1-2:4: A New Revelation

God has spoken in this final age to us by his Son. God spoke in the past to the fathers through the prophets. God has spoken in this final age to us by his Son. God spoke in the past to the fathers through the prophets. God's Former Speaking - God's Present Speaking. The Old Revelation - The New Revelation - our response - A New Revelation.

### The Greatness of Jesus

A. Jesus is God's prophetic voice. B. Jesus is God's Son. 1. Jesus is God's appointed heir. Right of the firstborn son. Also - Ps 2:8; Phil 2:10; Col 1:16. 2. Jesus is God's creative agent. Also - 1 Cor 8:6; Col 1:16; John 1:3. The Son thus belongs with God as the fountainhead of creation and is not to be ranked with the creation as a created being [Hagner, 42]. 3. Jesus is God's personified Glory. 'the radiance of God's glory'. Radiance [gk. apaugasma] = reflection back - radiate out from (used only here in NT). Shining forth as in the rays of the sun - the brightness of God's glory is seen in the Son. Also - Lk 9:32; John 1:14; 2:11; 17:5; Rom 8:17; 1Cor 2:8; Phil 3:21; 2 Thes 2:14. 4. Jesus is God's perfect revelation. 'the exact imprint of God's very being' NRSV. See also - 2 Cor 4:4; Col 1:15; John 1:2; 1:18; 14:9. 5. Jesus is God's cosmic sustainer. 'sustaining' (gk. present participle) = present and ongoing activity in upholding all that exists [Hagner, 43]. See also - Col 1:17; Ish 40:12-26. 6. Jesus is God's unique sacrifice. Will be central later - chapters 9 & 10. 7. Jesus has been exalted to God's right hand. This last and climatic phrase refers again to the uniqueness of the Son, who, sitting at the right hand of God, shares His sovereign authority [Hagner, 44]. Allusion to Psalm 110:1 (see v.13 sit... enemies... footstool). Sitting down - work complete... Kingly throne > Lord of the Past, Future & Present. See also - Rom 3:24-26. His name is greater than theirs: he is acclaimed as "my Son" (v. 5).

### Hebrews 2:1-4

For this reason we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away from it. For if the word spoken through angels proved unalterable, and every transgression and disobedience received a just penalty, how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation? After it was at the first spoken through the Lord, it was confirmed to us by those who heard, God also testifying with them, both by signs and wonders and by various miracles and by gifts of the Holy Spirit according to His own will.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Superiority of Christ
The document argues that Christ is superior to the prophets, angels, and Moses, serving as the final and complete revelation of God.

### Theme: The Necessity of Faith and Endurance
The text emphasizes that believers must pay close attention to the word of God to avoid drifting away, citing the warnings against unbelief and the need for perseverance.
