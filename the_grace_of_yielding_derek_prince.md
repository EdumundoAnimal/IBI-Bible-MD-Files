## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Principle of Yielding
Yielding is presented as a mark of spiritual maturity and a requirement for inheriting God's promises, contrasting with self-assertion or the spirit of the age.

### Theme: Lordship and Sin
The notes argue that man is not ruler over his own destiny; refusing to yield to God's Lordship inevitably results in becoming a slave to sin.

### Theme: The Example of Christ
Jesus is the supreme model of yielding, evidenced by His incarnation, sacrifice, and resurrection, which establishes Him as Lord.

### Theme: Universal Apostleship
Analysis of Romans 1 shows that the Gospel is not limited to Jewish background but is a universal call to obedience for all nations under the authority of Christ.
