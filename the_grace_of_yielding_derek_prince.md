# The Grace Of Yielding
**Author:** [[Derek Prince]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of study notes on the Christian principle of yielding. It covers theological concepts regarding the spirit of Christ, the necessity of sacrificing one's will, and biblical examples of yielding, such as Abraham's life and the sacrifice of Isaac. Additionally, it contains handwritten sermon notes on the Roman Epistle, exploring themes of humanity, Lordship, the Gospel of Christ, and the nature of sin.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Principle of Yielding
Yielding is presented as a mark of spiritual maturity, essential for resolving certain life conflicts and fully inheriting what God intends, as demonstrated by Abraham's obedience.

### Theme: Lordship and Sin
The notes argue that man is inherently under a form of Lordship. Sin is defined as the lack of subjection to divine Lordship, and this power of sin is broken only through submission to Christ.

### Theme: The Nature of Sacrifice
True spiritual life requires denying oneself and taking up the cross daily. This is modeled by Jesus' self-sacrifice and the requirement to 'lay our Isaac on the altar'—surrendering what is most precious to God.

### Theme: The Universal Gospel
Analysis of the Romans passage highlights that the Gospel is not limited to Jews but extends to all nations, establishing Christ's authority over all through his resurrection.
