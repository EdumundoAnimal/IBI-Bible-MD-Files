## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Principle of Yielding
Yielding is presented as a mark of spiritual maturity and a requirement to resolve conflicts, emphasizing the abandonment of self-assertion in favor of Christ's nature.

### Theme: Lordship and Sin
The text argues that humanity is not self-ruling but under the power of sin until subjection to Christ's Lordship is restored, as mere human effort is insufficient to break the power of sin.

### Theme: Abrahamic Faith and Sacrifice
Abraham serves as a model of yielding through obedience, culminating in the willingness to sacrifice Isaac, illustrating the principle that yielding to God leads to multiplied blessing.
