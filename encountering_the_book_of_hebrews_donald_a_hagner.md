# Encountering the Book of Hebrews
**Author:** [[Donald A. Hagner]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological introduction to the Book of Hebrews, covering authorship, historical context, and the structure of the Epistle. It addresses scholarly debates regarding the identity of the author, noting that while tradition has often pointed toward Paul, the literary style and internal evidence suggest other possibilities such as Apollos, Barnabas, or Priscilla. It provides an analysis of the audience, the historical setting (likely in the 60s AD), and the primary purpose of the text, which is to warn against apostasy and highlight the supremacy of Christ over the Old Testament system.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Authorship of Hebrews
Explores the scholarly debate regarding who wrote the Book of Hebrews, noting that early Church tradition differed from modern critical analysis, which highlights differences between Hebrews and Pauline letters.

### Theme: Historical and Theological Setting
Analyzes the likely social and temporal context of the readers, situated in the mid-to-late 60s AD, facing potential persecution and the need for theological reinforcement of Christ's superiority.

### Theme: Purpose of the Epistle
The central function of the book is defined as an exhortation to remain faithful to Christ, warning against returning to or lapsing from the New Covenant into old patterns of belief.
