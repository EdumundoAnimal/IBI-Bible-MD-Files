# Missions and Money
**Author:** [[Jonathan J. Bonk]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an extensive theological, ethical, and biblical examination of the relationship between Western missionary activity and financial affluence. It explores Old and New Testament teachings on wealth, poverty, ownership, and the responsibility of the wealthy. It critiques contemporary missionary practices that rely heavily on Western wealth, suggesting that such affluence often creates spiritual barriers and conflicts with the core tenets of Christian mission.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Critique of Affluence
The text argues that the Bible consistently warns against the dangers of wealth, emphasizing that spiritual security is found in God rather than material possessions.

### Theme: Missiological Integrity
The central argument is that the reliance of missions on Western financial structures creates a form of dependency and spiritual compromise, undermining the gospel's message.

### Theme: Economic Justice
The text highlights biblical mandates regarding the protection of the poor, the rights of laborers, and the imperative for restitution and debt forgiveness.
