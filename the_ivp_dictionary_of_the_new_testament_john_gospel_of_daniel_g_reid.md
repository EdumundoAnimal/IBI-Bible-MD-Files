# The IVP Dictionary of the New Testament: John, Gospel of
**Author:** [[Daniel G. Reid]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article analyzes the Gospel of John, covering its historical context, purpose, structure, and theological themes. It explores the community setting, potential influence of Diaspora Judaism and Hellenistic philosophy, and the Gospel's focus on unity and the mission of the church in the world.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Purpose of the Gospel
The author argues that John 20:31 serves as the central purpose statement, aiming to confirm Jesus as the Messiah and Son of God to foster belief and eternal life among believers, while also serving as a pastoral exhortation for a community facing conflict and ostracism.

### Theme: Ecclesiology and Unity
The text highlights the Johannine emphasis on the unity of believers, modeled on the relationship between the Father and the Son, intended to witness to the truth in a world that is often hostile.

### Theme: Historical and Philosophical Context
The document addresses the complex background of the Gospel, including the conflict with the synagogue, the potential influence of Hellenism (e.g., Logos doctrine), and its composition relative to Jewish and Gentile Christians.
