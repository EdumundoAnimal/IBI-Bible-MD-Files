## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Expository Preaching
The necessity of letting the text speak for itself, relying on cross-references rather than secondary commentaries, and utilizing illustrations for personal application.

### Theme: Audience Engagement
Utilizing colloquial language, metaphors, and cultural examples to explain difficult biblical concepts like 'being under sin' to modern listeners.

### Theme: Curriculum Design
A methodical approach to Bible study involving the assessment of learner growth areas and structuring material into sequential 6-week modules.
