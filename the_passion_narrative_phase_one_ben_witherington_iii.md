# The Passion Narrative: Phase One
**Author:** [[Ben Witherington, III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text provides an exegetical and theological analysis of the Farewell Discourses in John 13-17, focusing particularly on the 'Vine and Branches' metaphor (John 15:1-17). It explores the relationship between disciples and Jesus, defined by 'abiding,' love, and obedience. The document emphasizes that the Christian life is not solitary but rooted in union with Christ, which manifests in fruit-bearing through the power of the Holy Spirit. Furthermore, it addresses the tension between the Christian community and the hostile world, positing that this hatred is a continuation of the world's opposition to Jesus himself, for which the Advocate (the Holy Spirit) provides testimony and strength.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Theology of Abiding
A core concept where the believer's life is entirely dependent on being 'in the vine' (Christ), necessitating continuous connection to produce spiritual fruit.

### Theme: The Role of the Advocate
The Holy Spirit serves as the witness and empowering presence for the community of faith, ensuring they can stand against the pressures of a hostile world.

### Theme: Fruit-bearing and Discipline
The process by which the Father 'prunes' believers, removing what is unfruitful and refining their character to ensure greater maturity.
