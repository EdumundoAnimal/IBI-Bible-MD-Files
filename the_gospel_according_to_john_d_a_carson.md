# The Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[D. A. Carson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text is a theological commentary on John 15:1-16. It explores the vine and branches metaphor, analyzing the relationship between Jesus and his disciples, the concept of abiding in Christ, the necessity of bearing fruit, and the implications of being chosen by God. It addresses theological themes of perseverance, the nature of Christian obedience, the distinction between servants and friends of Jesus, and the opposition believers face from the world.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine and the Branches Metaphor
Analysis of John 15:1-8, focusing on the interconnectedness of Jesus as the vine and believers as the branches. It emphasizes that fruitfulness is a result of remaining in Christ and that the metaphor serves as a warning against apostasy while highlighting the necessity of divine intervention through the Father, the gardener.

### Theme: Mutual Indwelling and Obedience
The commentary argues that remaining in Jesus is not a passive act but an active commitment demonstrated through keeping his commandments. It explains the mutual indwelling—believers in Christ and Christ's words in believers—as the foundation for true spiritual fruitfulness.

### Theme: The Nature of Christian Friendship
Examines the transition from servant to friend in the discipleship model. It distinguishes between the hierarchy of master and servant versus the intimacy of friendship, emphasizing that Jesus defines this friendship through his sacrificial death and his command to love one another.
