# The Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[D. A. Carson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is an academic theological commentary on the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on chapters 15 and 16. It explores the 'vine and the branches' metaphor, the theme of 'remaining' in Jesus, the nature of Christian obedience, the meaning of 'friendship' with Jesus, and the opposition believers face from the world. The text provides detailed exegetical analysis, interaction with other scholarship, and theological reflection on the Farewell Discourse.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
An analysis of the metaphor in John 15, emphasizing the necessity of remaining in Jesus to bear fruit, with fruit representing obedience and character rather than just numerical growth.

### Theme: Mutual Indwelling
The theological concept that Jesus remains in the believer and the believer remains in Jesus, creating a relationship of mutual love and obedience.

### Theme: Friendship with God
Exploration of the shift from the status of 'servant' (douloi) to 'friend' through Christ, redefining the nature of submission to God as an act of voluntary, loving obedience rather than forced servitude.

### Theme: Opposition from the World
The argument that because Jesus chose his followers out of the world, the world will naturally harbor hatred toward them, mirroring the hatred it directed toward Jesus.
