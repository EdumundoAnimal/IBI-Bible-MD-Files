# The Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[D. A. Carson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This book is a theological commentary on the Gospel of John, specifically analyzing John 15:1-16:33. It examines the 'Farewell Discourse', the extended metaphor of the vine and the branches, the concept of mutual indwelling between Jesus and believers, the requirement of obedience, and the relationship between love, mission, and the world's hatred toward the community of faith.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Mutual Indwelling
The theological state where Jesus remains in the believer and the believer remains in Jesus, characterized by obedience to Jesus' commandments and a shared life.

### Theme: The Vine and the Branches
An extended metaphor representing the dependent relationship between Jesus and his followers, where fruitfulness is contingent upon remaining connected to the vine.

### Theme: The World's Hatred
The inevitable opposition faced by the community of faith, stemming from their allegiance to Jesus and their separation from the values of the world.

### Theme: Pruning and Cleansing
The process by which the Father purifies the believer to ensure greater fruitfulness, often involving trials or challenges that test commitment.
