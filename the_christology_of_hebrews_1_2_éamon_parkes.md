## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Apostasy and Persecution
The document argues that the primary purpose of Hebrews is to address the risk of apostasy caused by state-sanctioned persecution of the early Christian community, which was not recognized as a legitimate religion by Rome.

### Theme: Lapsed Christianity
The author identifies three specific forms of lapsed Christianity: legalistic Judaism (law above grace), Gentile Paganism (state-run idol worship), and Proto-Gnosticism (angel worship).

### Theme: Community Decline
Citing Achtemeier and Green, the text notes a decline in the community's spiritual vitality, specifically the loss of their initial conviction, joyful service, and endurance in the face of property loss.
