# Documents of the Christian Church and The New International Dictionary of the Christian Church
**Author:** [[Henry Bettenson (Editor), J.D. Douglas (Editor), Walter A. Elwell (Editor)]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as a collection of historical theological texts, specifically focusing on the evolution of Christian Creeds (Apostles' Creed, Nicene Creed, and the Athanasian Creed). It includes editorial notes on the historical development of these creeds, their authorship, and their significance in the orthodox faith. The text further explores the theological concept of the Trinity, explaining the divinity of the Son and the Holy Spirit within the context of monotheistic doctrine, while also providing critical entries from historical theological dictionaries.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Doctrine
The theological framework defining God as one essence existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, as articulated in the Athanasian and Nicene creeds.

### Theme: Historical Authorship of Creeds
Critical analysis of whether certain creeds, like the Athanasian Creed, were truly authored by the namesake saints or developed later by church councils to counter specific heresies like Arianism.

### Theme: Theological Orthodoxy
The struggle to define and maintain consistent belief systems across centuries, particularly regarding the Incarnation and the nature of the Godhead against various historical challenges.
