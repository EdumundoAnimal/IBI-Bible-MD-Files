# The Epistle to the Romans
**Author:** [[John Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume is an exposition of the Epistle to the Romans, focusing on chapters 7 through 8. It analyzes the transition from self-righteousness to conviction, the internal conflict of the believer (Romans 7:14-25), and life in the Spirit (Romans 8:1-39). The text explores the nature of sin, the role of the law, and the struggle of the believer as described by the Apostle Paul, emphasizing concepts like 'the law of sin' and 'the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Internal Conflict
The struggle of the regenerate believer, who despite knowing the law of God, faces the persistent influence of sin within their members, leading to the internal cry, 'Wretched man that I am!'

### Theme: The Law of Sin
The principle or power of sin that resides in the human flesh, leading to a state of captivity and conflict even after one has been justified by faith in Christ.

### Theme: Life in the Spirit
The liberation from the power of sin and the law of death made possible through the 'law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus,' characterized by an ongoing walk in the Spirit.
