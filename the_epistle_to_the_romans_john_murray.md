# The Epistle to the Romans
**Author:** [[John Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an exposition of the Epistle to the Romans, specifically focusing on chapters 7-8. It covers the 'Transitional Experience' where the apostle discusses the internal conflict between the law and the flesh. The text explores the nature of sin, the role of the law, the believer's struggle, and the ultimate victory found in Christ Jesus, transitioning into the 'Life in the Spirit' described in Romans 8.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Internal Conflict of the Believer
Analysis of Romans 7:14-25, examining the dichotomy between the believer's 'inner man' which delights in the law of God and the 'flesh' which remains under the power of sin.

### Theme: The Law and Sin
Exploration of the law's holiness in contrast to the sin that exploits the commandment to produce death, while clarifying that the law itself is spiritual and good.

### Theme: The Role of the Spirit
Explication of Romans 8:1-39, focusing on the freedom from condemnation and the liberation from the law of sin and death through the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus.
