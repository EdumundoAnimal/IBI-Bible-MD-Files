# The Epistle to the Romans
**Author:** [[John Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an expository commentary on the Epistle to the Romans, specifically focusing on chapters 7 through 8, exploring the transitional experience of the believer, the internal contradictions between the law of God and the law of sin, and the life in the Spirit. The text dissects the internal struggle of the apostle Paul, the nature of indwelling sin, and the promise of liberation through Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Law and Sin
Murray argues that the Law is inherently holy, yet it exposes human sinfulness by commanding obedience that the fallen nature cannot fulfill, thereby revealing the need for grace.

### Theme: The Internal Conflict
Analysis of the 'wretched man' in Romans 7, concluding that this reflects the ongoing struggle of a regenerate person experiencing the tension between the Spirit and the flesh.

### Theme: Life in the Spirit
The transition in Romans 8 represents the victory over condemnation and the empowerment of the believer through the Holy Spirit to walk in newness of life.
