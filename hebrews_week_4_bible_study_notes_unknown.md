## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Three Phases of Christ
The author outlines the life of Christ through three distinct theological stages: Incarnation, Exaltation, and the final inaugurated yet future victory.

### Theme: Purpose of Incarnation
Analysis of Hebrews 2:9-18 exploring why Jesus took on human form, specifically highlighting his suffering, his 'tasting death' for humanity, and his role as a pioneer.

### Theme: The Priesthood of Jesus
An inquiry into the theological and practical implications of the priesthood of Jesus as referenced in Hebrews 2:17.
