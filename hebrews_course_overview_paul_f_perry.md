# Hebrews Course Overview
**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a course overview for a study of the book of Hebrews, organized by the Irish Bible Institute in 2009. The curriculum is divided into 10 lessons, each mapping specific chapters of Hebrews to themes and corresponding page references from W. L. Lane's commentary.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The High Priestly Character of the Son
Exploration of the divine nature and intercessory role of Jesus as established in the book of Hebrews.

### Theme: Revelation through the Son
The theological framework that Jesus is the culmination and finality of God's message to humanity.

### Theme: Christian Resilience
Focus on the call to persevere in faith while living as Christians in a hostile environment.
