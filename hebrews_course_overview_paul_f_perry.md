## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The High Priestly Office
The course outlines the theological progression of Christ's role as the High Priest and the ultimate sacrifice, spanning from Hebrews 7 through 9.

### Theme: Persevering Faith
The curriculum emphasizes the necessity of loyalty to God through persevering faith, particularly in the context of a hostile environment for Christians.
