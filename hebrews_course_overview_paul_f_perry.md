# Hebrews Course Overview
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview of a Bible study course on the Book of Hebrews. It outlines a 10-lesson curriculum mapping specific passages of Scripture to theological themes such as the High Priestly office of the Son, the nature of faith, and Christian life in a hostile world. The content is structured to guide students through the authorship, purpose, and audience of Hebrews, utilizing historical analysis.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The study focuses heavily on the High Priestly office and character of the Son, identifying Christ's role as both Priest and Sacrifice.

### Theme: Persevering Faith
Explores the necessity of endurance and loyalty to God as fundamental characteristics for Christians living in a hostile environment.

### Theme: Theological Pedagogy
Structured curriculum designed to move from foundational revelation to the practical application of obedience and pilgrimage.
