## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Revelation of God through His Son
The central theological focus of the early chapters of Hebrews regarding divine disclosure.

### Theme: The High Priestly Character of the Son
An examination of Christ's role as the mediator and ultimate sacrifice.

### Theme: Orientation for Life as Christians in a Hostile World
The practical application of faith, endurance, and obedience in challenging environments.
