# New Testament Commentary Survey
**Author:** [[D. A. Carson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text provides an extensive bibliographical survey and critical assessment of various New Testament commentaries, with a particular focus on the Gospel of John and the Book of Acts. D. A. Carson evaluates scholarly, academic, and practical commentaries, highlighting their theological perspectives, historical-critical approaches, and suitability for different audiences, ranging from academic scholars to laypeople and preachers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Commentary Evaluation
The author provides evaluative criteria for biblical commentaries, considering factors like theological depth, historical-critical methodology, accessibility for lay readers, and utility for sermon preparation.

### Theme: Historical-Critical Methodology
The survey frequently references the 'historical-critical' approach, highlighting how different authors handle source criticism, redaction criticism, and the historical background of the New Testament texts.

### Theme: Target Audience Utility
A recurring theme is the classification of commentaries based on their intended purpose, distinguishing between scholarly resources and works suitable for the 'unsophisticated Sunday school teacher' or the average preacher.
