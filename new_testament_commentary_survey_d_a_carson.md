# New Testament Commentary Survey
**Author:** [[D. A. Carson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This excerpt from the New Testament Commentary Survey provides a bibliographical evaluation of various scholarly and popular commentaries on the books of John and Acts. D. A. Carson assesses the utility of these works for preachers, students, and academics, critiquing their handling of text-critical, theological, and historical issues. The analysis spans several pages, categorizing works into categories such as scholarly, popular, and those focused on specific themes.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Commentary Evaluation
The author maintains strict criteria for assessing the value of commentaries, favoring those that balance theological depth, historical awareness, and practical application for preachers.

### Theme: Textual and Critical Scholarship
A recurring theme is the evaluation of how different commentaries engage with source-critical, redaction-critical, and narrative-critical approaches to the Gospel of John and Acts.

### Theme: Accessibility vs. Scholarly Rigor
The author distinguishes between works suited for academic research and those intended for the 'unsophisticated Sunday school teacher' or layperson, often criticizing works that fail to adequately serve either audience.
