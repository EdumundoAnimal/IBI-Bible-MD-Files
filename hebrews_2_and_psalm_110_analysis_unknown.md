## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology of Suffering and Incarnation
The text explores the necessity of Jesus taking on humanity and suffering to experience death on behalf of humanity, thereby perfecting the author of salvation and enabling him to empathize with the tempted.

### Theme: Messianic Kingship and Priesthood
The inclusion of Psalm 110 juxtaposes divine kingship with the eternal priesthood in the order of Melchizedek, framing Jesus as both a sovereign ruler over enemies and a merciful high priest.
