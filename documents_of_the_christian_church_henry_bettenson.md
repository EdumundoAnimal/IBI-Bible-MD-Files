# Documents of the Christian Church
**Author:** [[Henry Bettenson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This book provides a collection of significant historical documents illustrating the development of the Christian Church. It includes foundational creeds such as the Apostles' Creed, the Nicene Creed, and various early church declarations. The text explains the theological nuances of these creeds, including historical debates regarding the divinity of Christ, the role of the Roman See, and early schisms. The content covers the primary theological frameworks that shaped orthodox Christian belief in the early centuries of the Church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theological Orthodoxy
The establishment of formal creeds to standardize Christian belief and combat heresies, particularly those concerning the nature of Christ.

### Theme: Ecclesiastical Authority
The assertion of the Roman See's authority over the Church, as evidenced by the Edict of Valentinian III.

### Theme: Christology
The philosophical and theological development of defining the person of Jesus Christ as fully divine and of one substance with the Father.
