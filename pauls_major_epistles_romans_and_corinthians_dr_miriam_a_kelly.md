# Paul's Major Epistles: Romans and Corinthians
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A. Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview of Paul's major epistles, including Romans, 1 & 2 Corinthians, Galatians, and 1 & 2 Thessalonians. It covers backgrounds, authorship, purposes, outlines, and theological themes for each book, emphasizing Paul's apostolic ministry, the defense of his gospel, and the challenges faced by the early church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The foundational theological argument in Romans and Galatians, emphasizing that salvation is received through faith in Christ rather than through works of the law.

### Theme: Apostolic Authority
Paul's ongoing defense of his apostleship and the integrity of his gospel message against critics, particularly in 2 Corinthians and Galatians.

### Theme: Eschatology and Church Order
Paul's instructions to the Thessalonian church regarding the Parousia (Lord's return), the correction of misunderstandings, and the necessity of maintaining order and avoiding idleness.

### Theme: Pastoral Care
The practical application of Christian doctrine to resolve specific church problems such as immorality, litigation, and divisions, as seen in the Corinthian epistles.
