## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
The central Pauline argument that salvation is received through faith in Jesus Christ, not by adherence to the Mosaic law or human works.

### Theme: Pastoral Care
Paul's ongoing engagement with the practical, moral, and doctrinal issues faced by early churches, demonstrating his role as a spiritual father and mentor.

### Theme: Eschatology
The teaching regarding the Parousia (the return of Christ) and how believers should live and maintain faith in the interim, particularly addressing confusion and false teachings.
