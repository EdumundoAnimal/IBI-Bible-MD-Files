# Christology Course Syllabus
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
IBI Christology 2008-9. This course explores the person of Jesus through the New Testament, biblical theology, and church history. Objectives include understanding the theological content of passages, reflecting on the 'big picture' of Jesus, and connecting academic study to personal faith and practical ministry. The course emphasizes both ancient interpretations and modern applications. Assessment includes seminar presentations, an exegesis essay on Hebrews 1-2, and written notes, focusing on theological analysis, contextual understanding, and critical reflection on contemporary relevance.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Methodology
The course employs an interdisciplinary approach, bridging ancient biblical interpretation with modern historical and theological inquiry.

### Theme: Integration of Faith and Academics
A core objective is to ensure that biblical and theological study informs personal faith, church life, and practical ministry rather than remaining abstract.

### Theme: Diversity of Interpretation
Students are expected to interact critically with various interpretations of the biblical material throughout church history.
