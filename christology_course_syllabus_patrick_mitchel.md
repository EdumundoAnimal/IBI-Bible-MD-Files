# Christology Course Syllabus
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This Christology course syllabus outlines the aims, course description, format, and assignments for students. The primary purpose is to explore biblical texts to know Jesus better and worship him more deeply, understanding his person and his implications for life today. The course requires students to handle and interpret the Bible critically and engage with various theological perspectives including ancient and modern interpretations. Assessments include seminar presentations, exegesis essays (specifically on Hebrews 1-2), and written reflections. A bibliography of key Christological texts is provided, covering the quest for the historical Jesus and general theology.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The systematic study of the person and work of Jesus Christ, integrating biblical exegesis with historical and contemporary theological perspectives.

### Theme: Biblical Exegesis
The critical explanation and interpretation of biblical texts to determine their original meaning and contemporary relevance.

### Theme: Practical Theology
The application of theological knowledge to personal faith, ministry, and church life in the modern world.
