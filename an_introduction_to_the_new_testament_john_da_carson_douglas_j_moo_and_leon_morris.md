# An Introduction to the New Testament: John
**Author:** [[D.A. Carson, Douglas J. Moo, and Leon Morris]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The provided text is a scholarly introduction to the Gospel of John, covering authorship, provenance, date, purpose, text, and its contribution to the New Testament. It discusses academic debates regarding the Johannine 'community', the relationship between John and the synoptic gospels, and the historical context of its composition. The document critically reviews various scholarly theories regarding sources, Gnostic influences, and the theological distinctiveness of the fourth gospel.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine Authorship
Explores the complex scholarly arguments surrounding the identity of the author, balancing external tradition with internal evidence and the potential role of a 'Johannine school' or 'community'.

### Theme: Relationship to Synoptics
Analyzes why the Gospel of John differs significantly from the Synoptic gospels in content, chronology, and theological emphasis, discussing theories of dependence or independence.

### Theme: Provenance and Date
Examines the historical and geographic origins of the Gospel, weighing evidence for an Ephesian provenance and dates ranging from A.D. 70 to the end of the first century.

### Theme: Theological Distinctiveness
Highlights the unique christology of the Fourth Gospel, its presentation of Jesus as the Son of God, and its distinctive eschatological and pneumatological themes.
