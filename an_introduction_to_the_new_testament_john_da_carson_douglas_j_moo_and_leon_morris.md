# An Introduction to the New Testament: John
**Author:** [[D.A. Carson, Douglas J. Moo, and Leon Morris]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This section provides an introduction and analysis of the Gospel of John, covering authorship, historical context, provenance, and key theological contributions. The authors explore debates surrounding the 'Johannine Community,' stylistic unity, dating of the text, destination, and the relationship between John and the Synoptic Gospels. It examines critical scholarly perspectives on the authorship of the Fourth Gospel, the theological distinctives such as the role of the Holy Spirit, eschatology, and the unique Christology of John, emphasizing the evangelist's purpose to reveal the Father through the Son.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine Authorship and Sources
The authors analyze the debate over the authorship of the Fourth Gospel, examining arguments for apostolic origin versus the theory of a 'Johannine Community' or school.

### Theme: Provenance and Dating
A discussion of the geographical and historical setting of the gospel, including the case for an Ephesian provenance and dating the text toward the end of the first century.

### Theme: John and the Synoptics
Exploration of the conceptual and literary relationship between the Fourth Gospel and the Synoptic accounts, addressing the differences in chronology, geography, and thematic presentation.

### Theme: Theological Distinctives
Analysis of John's contribution to New Testament theology, specifically regarding Christology, the mission of the Holy Spirit, eschatology, and the nature of salvation.
