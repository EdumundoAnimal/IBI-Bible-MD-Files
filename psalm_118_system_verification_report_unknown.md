## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This report provides a multi-movement exegetical exposition and Christological synthesis of Psalm 118. It examines linguistic and syntactic features, structural mechanics (including Inclusio frameworks), and the historical-cultural context of the psalm's origin as a royal thanksgiving liturgy and its subsequent post-exilic institutionalization. The document explores the architectural metaphor of the 'rejected stone,' its New Testament re-appropriation by Jesus and the early Church, and provides a philosophical dialogue regarding the critique of human autonomy and political technocracy, concluding with a homiletical application for modern existential reflection.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Vindication
The theological movement from personal distress and encirclement to corporate celebration of Yahweh's fidelity (Chesed), framing the King's deliverance as a model for the nation.

### Theme: Architectural Paradox
The transformation of the rejected, irregular stone into the chief cornerstone, serving as a metaphor for the Christological reversal of human value structures at the Cross.

### Theme: Soteriological Critique of Autonomy
A polemic against the foundational dogma of modernity and political messianism, asserting that human systems ('princes' and 'builders') are mortal and impotent against existential 'metzar' (distress).
