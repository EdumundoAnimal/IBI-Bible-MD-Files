# Hebrews: Sept. 2009 Essay Questions
**Author:** [[Paul Perry]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Diploma question options

Questions; Marking Weight; Primary Chapter(s). Compare and contrast various views as to why the author of Hebrews compares Christ with the Angels. 20%; Hebrews 1:4-14. Explain how the author develops his case for the superiority of Christ to the Angels in the above text. 50%; Hebrews 1:4-14. In what way would his points have encouraged the original hearers, and how could they be used to encourage the faith of believers today? 30%; Hebrews 1:4-14. Or. Compare and contrast the priests work under the Old Covenant and the priestly work of Christ demonstrated in Hebrews. 45%; Hebrews 7:23-28 & 9:11-14. How is the superiority of Christ's priesthood demonstrated by the author? 25%; Hebrews 7:23-28 & 9:11-14. Consider what ways these truths would have encouraged the believers who received the letter of Hebrews and could be used to encourage believers today. 30%; Hebrews 7:23-28 & 9:11-14. Dip.: 3000 words.

### Primary similarities and differences between the degree and dip. questions

Similar: Both groups are asked to attempt 'one' question of 3000 words in length. All questions combine elements of application within them. All questions require elements of analysis and engagement with both texts and scholarly writings. Differences: While dip. question does require comparing and discerning of various views; the degree level question expects a deeper critical engagement with views combined with clear argumentation and strong justification for one's chosen proposals. Following from the above point the degree level questions set, concern texts with a more diverse range of scholarly viewpoints, which are generally surrounded by more nuanced debate or approaches. The degree student is expected to critically engage with a broader range of [degree quality] sources than the dip. student. His / her paper should demonstrate developed diagnostic and creative skills in the exegesis and application of the text. It should be noted that a high level honours answer by a dip. student will undoubtedly demonstrate similar qualities to those required by all degree level answers, as outlined above. However, as also noted above, the degree questions posed allow more scope for, and indeed demand such interaction as a basic requirement of all such degree level answers. This is not an exhaustive list, but merely a basis for a beginning of a class discussion around this issue. Paul Perry - For class Hebrews IBI Sept 2009

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Superiority
The document focuses on the theological argument of the book of Hebrews regarding the superiority of Christ, specifically in relation to the angels and the Old Covenant priesthood.

### Theme: Practical Application of Theology
The essay questions emphasize the necessity of connecting theological exegesis to the encouragement of both the original recipients of the letter and contemporary believers.

### Theme: Academic Rigor and Exegesis
The text outlines the expectations for academic engagement, requiring students to compare scholarly views, utilize diverse sources, and demonstrate diagnostic and creative skills in biblical interpretation.
