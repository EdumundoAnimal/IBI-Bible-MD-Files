## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Divine Warrior
Psalm 144 presents Yahweh as the ultimate Divine Warrior who intervenes in human history. The text demythologizes Ancient Near Eastern royal ideology by stripping the king of inherent divinity and transferring all cosmic agency to Yahweh, who 'bows the heavens' to judge deceit and shatter destructive chaos.

### Theme: Hebel (Human Transience)
The psalm utilizes the term 'hebel' (vapor/breath) to establish an absolute anthropological contrast between human ontological frailty and divine majesty. Human existence is described as a 'passing shadow' (ṣēl ʿ ôbēr), emphasizing that human significance is not found in autonomous power but in being remembered and redeemed by the Creator.

### Theme: Covenantal Shalom
The flourishing of the community (vv. 12-15) is presented as the downstream consequence of divine covenant allegiance. True societal blessedness is not self-generated through human engineering but is the fruit of a people whose God is Yahweh, standing in contrast to the 'right hand of falsehood' (v. 8).
