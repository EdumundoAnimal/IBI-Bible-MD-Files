## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Redemptive Covenantalism
The interpretation of Tsedeq as not just ethical behavior but as 'God who vindicates my covenantal standing'.

### Theme: Spiritual Antifragility
Applying the systems engineering concept of antifragility to the believer's response to distress, where faith grows stronger through shock rather than simply withstanding it.

### Theme: The Righteous Sufferer
The Christological motif linking David's betrayal and distress to the suffering, death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: The Theology of Sleep
Viewing sleep as an act of trust and total abdication of autonomy to God, contrary to the modern anxiety of needing to construct one's own meaning and security.
