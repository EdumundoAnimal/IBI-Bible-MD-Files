# Deprogramming Ourselves from a Lifestyle of Conspicuous Consumption and Self-Gratification
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article discusses the necessity for Christians to detach from consumer culture. It outlines nine practical steps for deprogramming oneself from materialistic tendencies and aligning financial practices with New Testament teachings. The author argues that modern prosperity theology often contradicts the self-sacrificial nature of the gospel. Key areas covered include: redefining needs versus luxuries, prioritizing sacrificial giving, evaluating budgets, avoiding debt, practicing forgiveness of debts, and critically engaging with capitalist frameworks through a biblical lens. The text serves as a manual for counter-cultural living, emphasizing spiritual stewardship over material accumulation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Counter-cultural Discipleship
The argument that following Jesus necessitates rejecting societal norms of conspicuous consumption and embracing a lifestyle focused on sacrifice and service to the poor.

### Theme: Biblical Stewardship
The view that wealth and possessions are not personal entitlements but resources to be managed with divine discernment for the sake of the kingdom.

### Theme: De-programming from Consumerism
A deliberate process of evaluating one's financial habits and mindset to dismantle the influence of modern marketing and the 'prosperity gospel' which equates wealth with spiritual blessing.
