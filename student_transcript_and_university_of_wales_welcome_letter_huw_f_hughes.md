### University of Wales Welcome Letter

Dear Eamon, It is my pleasure to welcome you onto the University of Wales validated scheme BTh in Applied Theology at the Carraig Eden Theological College, County Wicklow, Ireland. If you are a postgraduate student, your registration is subject to you meeting the University's matriculation requirements on entry to the scheme. A letter confirming your matriculation status is provided to you via the Institution at which you are studying. You have now joined a global community of over 80,000 students registered with the University of Wales. The University, which was founded by Royal Charter in 1893, is the second largest degree-awarding body in the United Kingdom and confers around 15,000 initial degrees and more than 4,000 higher degrees each year. For more information on its work within Wales, and globally, go to http://www.wales.ac.uk/validation. Your unique University Student Number (USN) is 071 1866863950. You will need to use this number to access the University's Online Library - a dedicated virtual library service available to all students on University of Wales validated courses. To find out more about this, extremely valuable resource, go to http://www.library.wales.ac.uk/. Carraig Eden Theological College, County Wicklow, Ireland will provide you with a student handbook which will include information on, and access to, University of Wales Regulations, Policies and Procedures - these are also available on the University's Validation web pages. Upon the successful completion of your course, you may wish to take the opportunity to meet some of your fellow graduates at our annual graduation celebration in Cardiff. More information on this event can be found at http://www.wales.ac.uk/graduation. Should you have any queries regarding the University of Wales and its validated courses please contact validation@wales.ac.uk. In the meantime, I wish you every success in your studies and look forward to your continuing association with the University of Wales. Yours sincerely, Huw F. Hughes, Director of the Validation Unit.
