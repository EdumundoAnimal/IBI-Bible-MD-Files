# The Gospel of John: A Commentary (Volume II)
**Author:** [[Craig S. Keener]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The commentary covers the Gospel of John, specifically chapters 11-16. It explores the theological significance of the 'vine' imagery, the concept of Christian friendship with Jesus, the mandate of the Love Commandment, and the reality of the world's hatred toward believers. It contextualizes these teachings within both the biblical tradition and the broader Greco-Roman culture of the time.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Vine Imagery
Explores the metaphor of Jesus as the vine and disciples as branches, emphasizing the necessity of remaining in him for fruitfulness and the role of pruning in spiritual maturity.

### Theme: Friendship with Jesus
Analyzes the shift from the master-servant relationship to one of friendship between Jesus and his disciples, rooted in sacrificial love and mutual trust.

### Theme: The Love Commandment
Examines the commandment for disciples to love one another as Jesus loved them, framing it as the defining characteristic of the community and the source of their strength.

### Theme: The World's Hatred
Discusses the sectarian nature of the Johannine worldview, where the world is depicted as hostile toward those who follow Jesus and do not belong to its patterns.
