## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Critical vs. Devotional Scholarship
The text differentiates between resources intended for academic research versus those designed for homiletical or devotional purposes, noting that 'devotional' or 'sermon' labels often indicate a lack of suitability for critical academic analysis.

### Theme: Source Hierarchy
A ranking system (1-5 stars) is employed to help researchers evaluate the reliability and academic utility of various reference books, dictionaries, and commentaries.

### Theme: Research Methodology
The guide emphasizes that research should be exhaustive rather than relying on a single publisher, and warns that newer sources are not inherently better, though recent scholarship is often preferred.
