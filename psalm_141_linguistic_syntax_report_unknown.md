## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultic Transference
The theological movement from physical temple rituals, such as incense burning and grain offerings, to the interiorized, vocalized prayer of the righteous as an acceptable spiritual sacrifice.

### Theme: Apotropaic Sanctification
The guarding of one's speech and heart as a form of spiritual warfare, preventing moral assimilation or 'wicked dainties' from infiltrating the personal sanctuary.

### Theme: Severe Mercy
The view that righteous rebuke and correction, while painful, serve as a healing anointing oil that prevents self-deception and destruction.

### Theme: Eschatological Vindication
The expectation that God will overthrow corrupt judges and agents of chaos, leading the faithful safely through the snares of death.
