# The Pastoral Epistles and Biblical Criticism
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A. Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an introductory overview of the Pastoral Epistles (1 and 2 Timothy and Titus) and various traditional methods of biblical criticism. It covers authorship questions, historical backgrounds for these epistles, and outlines for 1 Timothy. Additionally, it details methodological approaches to biblical study, including Textual Criticism, Form Criticism, Tradition-Historical Criticism, Redaction Criticism, and Reader-Response Criticism, among others.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Pastoral Epistles Authorship
Discussion of the tension between the traditional view of Pauline authorship and critical scholarship that questions it.

### Theme: Biblical Criticism Methods
A survey of scholarly approaches to biblical text analysis including textual, form, redaction, tradition-historical, and reader-response criticisms.

### Theme: Historical Contextualization
The importance of understanding the specific historical settings—such as imprisonment or missionary travels—to correctly interpret the original intent of the letters.
