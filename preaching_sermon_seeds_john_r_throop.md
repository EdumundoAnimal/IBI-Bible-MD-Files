# Preaching: Sermon Seeds
**Author:** [[John R. Throop]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains sermon seeds and lectionary resources for October 26, 2008, and October 31, 2008. It provides liturgical readings for RCL, Roman Catholic, Episcopal, and Lutheran traditions. It includes theological reflections on Psalm 90:1-6, 13-17 regarding human frailty and eternal life, citing Elisabeth Kübler-Ross and J.I. Packer. It also offers children's sermon activities for Reformation Day and liturgical prayers. A second section focuses on Psalm 46, analyzing it as a 'Jerusalem celebration' psalm, discussing its historical context, structure, and the meaning of the word 'selah'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Human Frailty and Transience
Reflecting on Psalm 90, the text emphasizes the transitory nature of human life compared to the eternal nature of God.

### Theme: Liturgical Celebration
Examination of Psalm 46 as a 'Jerusalem celebration' psalm associated with pilgrimage and temple worship.

### Theme: Theological Continuity
The argument that despite historical shifts, the fundamental nature of the human condition and God's relationship to it remains constant.
