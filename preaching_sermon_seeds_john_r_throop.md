# Preaching: Sermon Seeds
**Author:** [[John R. Throop]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains lectionary-based sermon materials for two dates: October 26, 2008 (Proper 25) and October 31, 2008 (Reformation Day). For October 26, the focus is on Psalm 90:1-6, 13-17, exploring themes of human frailty, the transitory nature of existence, and divine sovereignty. It provides liturgical readings from RCL, Roman Catholic, Episcopal, and Lutheran traditions. It also offers theological reflections on mortality, incorporating perspectives from Elisabeth Kübler-Ross and Henry Wadsworth Longfellow. For October 31, the document focuses on Psalm 46, highlighting 'The Values of Refuge and Strength.' It includes a children's sermon illustration regarding the eternal nature of God, sample prayers (Call to Worship, Confession, Dedication), and specific hymn suggestions for the respective scriptures.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Human Mortality and Divine Eternity
Reflects on the contrast between human frailty and the eternal nature of God, emphasizing the acceptance of death as part of the human condition.

### Theme: Liturgical Continuity
The use of standardized lectionary readings across different denominational traditions to ground sermon preparation.

### Theme: Theology of Refuge
Explores Psalm 46 as a 'Jerusalem celebration' psalm, focusing on divine protection and the presence of God amidst political and historical instability.
