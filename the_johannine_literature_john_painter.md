# The Johannine Literature
**Author:** [[John Painter]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of the Johannine literature, specifically examining the Gospel of John, the Epistles of John, and Revelation. It discusses authorship, the nature of the Johannine school, dating, provenance, and the theological dualism characteristic of these texts. The text analyzes the structure of the Fourth Gospel, its purpose, and its unique linguistic patterns, particularly emphasizing the 'signs' that reveal the presence of light in a world of darkness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Authorship and School
The text explores the debate surrounding the authorship of the Johannine corpus, noting that while tradition links these works to John the son of Zebedee, modern scholarship often attributes them to a 'Johannine school' or a later community context.

### Theme: Johannine Dualism
A core theological concept in the Johannine writings, characterized by the antithesis of light and darkness, truth and falsehood, and the current 'world' ruled by evil versus the coming reign of God.

### Theme: Purpose of the Fourth Gospel
The Gospel was composed over a period (c. 85-90 CE) with the dual purpose of maintaining the identity of the 'Johannine community in exile' and persuading outsiders to believe in Jesus as the Messiah.
