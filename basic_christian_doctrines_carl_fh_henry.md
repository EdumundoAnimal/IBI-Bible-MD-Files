## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Sovereignty
The text argues that God's sovereignty encompasses the direction of all world events in accordance with His divine plan, while maintaining consistency with human responsibility.

### Theme: Trinitarian Theology
An examination of the Holy Trinity as a divine, three-personed nature, emphasizing the structural togetherness of the persons and the unique, sacrificial role of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Christ-Centered Prayer
The text addresses the practical theological question of whom to address in prayer, specifically discussing the tendency in some Protestant traditions to prioritize addressing Christ over the Father.
