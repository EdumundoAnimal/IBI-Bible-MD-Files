# Basic Christian Doctrines
**Author:** [[Carl F.H. Henry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text serves as a collection of theological essays on foundational Christian doctrines. It covers topics including the knowledge of God, the inspiration of the Bible, the attributes of God, the Trinity, creation, angels, providence, and human nature. The document specifically details J. Kenneth Grider's analysis of the Holy Trinity, emphasizing the concept of the triune God as a structural togetherness and an eternal society. It also discusses the roles of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit in Christian prayer and theology, noting the necessity of a balanced approach to the Trinity in evangelical practice.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Sovereignty
The theological assertion that God governs all events according to His plan without negating human freedom or responsibility.

### Theme: The Holy Trinity
An exploration of the tri-personal nature of God, characterizing it as an 'eternal society' and a 'structural togetherness' rather than a bare monad.

### Theme: Evangelical Liturgy
Observations regarding how current evangelical practices, such as prayer and the observation of Trinity Sunday, might better reflect the fullness of the Trinitarian nature of God.
