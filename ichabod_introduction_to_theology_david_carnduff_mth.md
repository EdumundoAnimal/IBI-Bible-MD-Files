# Ichabod: Introduction to Theology
**Author:** [[David Carnduff M.Th.]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a theological exploration of God's presence and revelation. It begins by chronicling the manifestation and departure of the Glory of the Lord from Israel's history—from the Tabernacle in the wilderness to Shiloh, Solomon's Temple, and Herod's Temple, eventually addressing the concept of the Church. It transitions into 'Introduction to Theology,' defining terms such as Biblical, Systematic, Dogmatic, and Historical theology. The text discusses the contrast between Modern and Post-Modern worldviews, highlighting the loss of absolute standards. Finally, it outlines the concepts of General and Special Revelation, detailing how God reveals Himself through nature, history, and humanity.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Glory of the Lord
The presence of God manifest in historical structures (Tabernacle, Temple) which departs due to Israel's rebellion and lack of faithfulness.

### Theme: Modern vs. Post-Modern Worldviews
An analytical comparison between the objective, authority-driven Modern era and the subjective, individualistic, and cynical Post-Modern age.

### Theme: General vs. Special Revelation
The distinction between God's universal revelation through nature, history, and conscience, and His specific revelation through Christ and Scripture.

### Theme: The Necessity of Theology
The argument that Christian belief (Theology) is essential to Christian practice (behavior) and that sound doctrine acts as a foundation against cultural chaos.
