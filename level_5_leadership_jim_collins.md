# Level 5 Leadership
**Author:** [[Jim Collins]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the concept of Level 5 Leadership, a paradox of intense personal humility and professional will. It highlights the research findings from 'Good to Great,' examining how companies transitioned from good to great through specific leadership characteristics. Central to these findings are the 'Window and the Mirror' metaphor—where effective leaders look in the mirror to apportion responsibility for poor results and out the window to attribute credit to other factors for success—and the emphasis on selecting the right people before setting a strategy (the 'bus' analogy). The text contrasts Level 5 leaders with celebrity, egocentric CEOs of comparison companies and details biographical accounts of leaders like Darwin Smith of Kimberly-Clark and Colman Mockler of Gillette.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Level 5 Leadership
A leadership style characterized by a paradoxical combination of personal humility and intense professional will. Level 5 leaders are driven by the need to build an enduring institution rather than personal ego.

### Theme: The Window and the Mirror
A metaphor for accountability. Level 5 leaders look in the mirror to blame themselves for poor results and look out the window to attribute success to other factors and people.

### Theme: First Who, Then What
The principle that great transformations begin by getting the right people on the bus (and the wrong people off) before determining the strategic direction of the company.
