## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Primeval History (Genesis 1-11)
Explores the origins of the world and humanity, emphasizing the sovereign Creator and the subsequent moral failure of humanity as evidenced by stories like The Fall, the Flood, and the Tower of Babel.

### Theme: Patriarchal Narratives (Genesis 12-50)
Focuses on the lives of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph, illustrating the development of God's covenantal relationship with a specific chosen line, characterized by election, promise, and faithfulness.

### Theme: Theological Foundations
Examines key doctrines such as creation ex nihilo, the Imago Dei (image of God), the nature of sin, the necessity of God's grace, and the covenantal structure of redemption.
