# Research and Essay Writing Template
**Author:** [[Nancy Jean Vyhmeister]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Research is a process of discovery it is investigation. Research can be defined as a method of study that, through careful investigation of all evidence bearing on a definable issue, topic or problem, arrives at a solution, and that to research is, to collect, organise, evaluate and present data. This process involves analysis and synthesis, for research is more than a compilation of information. Tips to get you started: Systematic search, adequate information, objective knowledge, specific topic. Essay format, Introduction, main body and a conclusion, with proper referencing. Correct use of language, including spelling and grammar. Clarity of expression, Logical organisation, Appropriate use of headings or sections, Correct formatting. To Begin your Essay you need to: Define issue, problem or topic, Identify approach, Prepare preliminary Bibliography, Design a methodology, Draft working outline. Evaluate: In an evaluation you are expected to present a careful appraisal of the issue, stressing both advantages and limitations. Discuss: The term discuss, directs you to examine, analyse carefully, and present considerations for and against regarding the issue or topic. Analysis: It is examining the evidence piece by piece. Synthesis: It begins with two or more ideas or systems. After studying how each one works, you put the parts of the old together to form a new one. Make it easy for the person reading essay. Do not make silly errors, Answer the question, Stick to the point, Do not use devotional language, No jokes, colloquialisms, abbreviations (except for biblical books), euphemisms etc. Primary sources: Vatican 11 Documents, Bible. Secondary sources: Books that comment or interpret primary sources. Reference Material: Concordance, Dictionaries, Encyclopaedias, etc. Reading and Evaluating Sources. In any Essay the reader is looking for: Factual accuracy, Critical awareness and interaction, Logical development, Evidence of wide reading, And Good English.
