## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Theology
The approach of understanding the Bible as a cohesive narrative of God's redemptive plan, categorized into specific branches such as Historical, Systematic, and Pastoral.

### Theme: Expository Preaching
A method of preaching that prioritizes the text of the Bible, ensuring that the sermon's message, structure, and application arise directly from the passage being studied.

### Theme: Sermon Evaluation
A structured assessment process used to review a sermon's aim, interpretation of the text, relevance of applications, effectiveness of illustrations, and delivery.
