# Deprogramming Ourselves from Conspicuous Consumption and Self-Gratification
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text discusses biblical perspectives on wealth, consumption, and discipleship. It argues that modern Christians often mirror secular consumerist values rather than the countercultural teachings of Jesus. The chapter proposes specific actions such as divesting from excessive possessions, practicing intentional giving, simplifying one's lifestyle, evaluating discretionary spending, and forgiving debts. The author emphasizes that true discipleship involves decoupling oneself from the 'prosperity gospel' and aligning personal financial habits with the kingdom of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Countercultural Discipleship
The necessity for Christians to move away from prevailing consumerist norms and toward a lifestyle reflective of kingdom values, characterized by sacrifice and divestment.

### Theme: Biblical Stewardship and Debt
A theological exploration of money that challenges both unchecked capitalism and prosperity theology, advocating for a debt-free lifestyle and the radical forgiveness of debts as a spiritual practice.

### Theme: Deprogramming Consumption
Practical steps to break free from the psychological and social pressures of consumerism, including evaluating one's possessions, habits, and assumptions about material wealth.
