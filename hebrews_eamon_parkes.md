**Author:** [[Eamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The assignment explores the theological arguments of the Epistle to the Hebrews, specifically focusing on the superiority of Christ over the angels, the sovereignty of the Son, and the warning passages regarding apostasy. The text analyzes how the author of Hebrews utilizes scripture to reinforce faith among readers facing external pressure and internal spiritual weakness. Key discussions include the interpretation of Psalm 102, the use of rhetorical devices such as qal wahomer, and the cultural context of the first century regarding angelic worship and divine revelation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
The theological assertion that Jesus Christ occupies a position superior to angels, rooted in his status as the Son of God, the sovereign King, and the unchanging Creator.

### Theme: Theological Warning Passages
The pedagogical intent of the Epistle to the Hebrews to prevent apostasy by using 'lesser-to-greater' (qal wahomer) arguments to emphasize the severity of ignoring divine revelation.

### Theme: Ancient Contextualization
Interpretation of biblical texts through the lens of Greco-Roman worldviews, Jewish-Christian audience reception, and historical developments like the destruction of the Temple.
