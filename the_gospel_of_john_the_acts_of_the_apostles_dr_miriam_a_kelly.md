# The Gospel of John / The Acts of the Apostles
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview of the Gospel of John and the Acts of the Apostles. It discusses the authorship, date, and purpose of John, exploring theological themes like the divinity of Christ, 'I Am' statements, and the role of the Paraclete. It also examines the unity of Luke-Acts, emphasizing the theological transition from the ministry of Jesus to the expansion of the church in the power of the Holy Spirit as detailed in the Acts of the Apostles.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Divinity of Christ
Explores the Johannine emphasis on the Word as God, the 'I Am' declarations, and the incarnation as a revelation of divine glory.

### Theme: The Role of the Paraclete
Analyzes the Holy Spirit's mission as a counselor who teaches, reminds, and guides the church into truth following Christ's departure.

### Theme: Literary Unity of Luke-Acts
Discusses scholarly arguments for viewing the Gospel of Luke and Acts as a single, cohesive two-volume work written by the same author.
