## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Enclosure (Sābîḇ)
The geographical reality of Jerusalem's surrounding mountains serves as a sacramental, visible metaphor for YHWH's surrounding, protective presence over His people.

### Theme: Theodicy of the Rod
The 'scepter of wickedness' is recognized as a temporary, bounded imperial reality, limited by divine decree specifically to prevent the righteous from losing their moral integrity.

### Theme: Functional Integrity vs. Crooked Shortcuts
The text draws a binary distinction between the upright (yəšārîm) and those who adopt evasive, devious paths (ʿaqalqallôtām) under the pressure of systemic injustice.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The immovable foundation of Zion is transposed from a geographical location to the Person and Cross of Jesus Christ, who absorbed the 'rod of iniquity' and became the unshakeable Rock of the Church.
