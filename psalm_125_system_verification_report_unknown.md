## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Preservation
The text argues that the security of the believer is not an internal psychological state but an objective enclosure by the sovereign presence of God (YHWH sāḇîḇ lə-ʿammô), extending from the historical present into open-ended eternity.

### Theme: Theodicy of Territorial Inheritance
The psalm addresses the crisis of foreign imperial occupation (the 'scepter of wickedness') resting upon the 'lot of the righteous' (gôral haṣ-ṣaddîqîm). It asserts that God limits the duration of tyranny to prevent the moral disintegration of the righteous.

### Theme: Eschatological Vindication
The text concludes with a sharp distinction between those who maintain integrity (yəšārîm) and those who turn to 'crooked paths' (ʿaqalqallôt). The latter are consigned to the same eschatological expulsion as the 'workers of iniquity'.
