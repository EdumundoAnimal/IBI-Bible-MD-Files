It explores the nature of prayer, the concept of faith, salvation by grace, and the 'scandal of particularity' in revelation. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Personalism
An approach to theology that emphasizes personal encounter with a living God over abstract philosophical or rationalist constructs.

### Theme: The Nature of Prayer
Prayer as a struggle and a personal engagement, distinct from mere meditation or ritualistic practices, centered on dependence on God.

### Theme: Salvation and Grace
Refutation of works-based righteousness, emphasizing Sola Gratia (grace alone) and the role of faith as a response to divine initiative.

### Theme: The Scandal of Particularity
The theological tension that God reveals Himself through specific historical events and a specific person (Jesus) rather than universal truths discoverable by human reason.
