### Theme: Academic Rigor in Biblical Studies
The text outlines a hierarchy of publishers and commentaries to guide students in selecting high-quality sources, prioritizing scholarly peer-reviewed works over devotional or sermon-based material.

### Theme: Categorization of Research Sources
A clear distinction is made between primary (original/non-interpreted), secondary (analyzed/evaluated), and tertiary (factual/reference) sources to help students structure their research methodology.

Students are urged not to rely solely on secondary sources or summaries, but to engage directly with primary source material to form their own academic arguments.
