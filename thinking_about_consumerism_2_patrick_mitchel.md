## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Personal Effects of Consumerism
The document identifies consumerism as a force that shapes individual identity through 'life as confetti,' the normalization of desire, and a focus on experiences where self-pleasure becomes the primary purpose of life.

### Theme: Consumer Christianity
The text critiques the infiltration of consumerist values into the church, referred to as 'ME CHURCH' or 'Consumer religion,' and contrasts this with a 'grander calling' offered by Jesus.

### Theme: Commodification of Morality
The document highlights the porn industry as a case study for how sexual pleasure has been transformed into a 'purchasable commodity' within contemporary Irish society.
