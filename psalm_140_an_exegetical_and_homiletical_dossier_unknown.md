## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Retributive Providence
The concept that the moral universe functions through lex talionis, where the destructive energy of evil boomerangs back upon its author, as seen in the retributive justice of the burning coals and the pit.

### Theme: Serpentine Violence
The biblical motif tracing from Genesis 3 to Revelation 20, characterizing human speech disconnected from God as weaponized, venomous, and deceptive, manifesting in character assassination and political sedition.

### Theme: Covenantal Vindication
The movement from the horizontal terror of human hunters to the vertical sanctuary of Yahweh, where the afflicted (ʿānî) and needy (ʾeb̄yônîm) find their defense in the sovereign God.

### Theme: Christological Overthrow
The re-reading of Psalm 140 in light of the Passion, where Christ is the ultimate sufferer who absorbs the serpentine venom of the cross and, through resurrection, silences the accuser.
