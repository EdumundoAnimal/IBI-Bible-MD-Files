# 1. What is a Disciple?
**Author:** [[Pastor Brian Gingles]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text explores the distinction between a convert and a disciple in Christianity. Conversion is described as the beginning of a new life, while discipleship is presented as an ongoing process of growth, submission to God, and living according to the teachings of the Bible. The document uses various Biblical passages, such as Colossians 2:13-14 and Matthew 16:24-26, to emphasize themes of transformation, the importance of inner change, and the cost of discipleship. It highlights the importance of total surrender to God, including the concept of 'dying to self' or being 'crucified daily'. The text concludes with a reflective piece on John Newton, author of 'Amazing Grace,' illustrating that conversion and subsequent discipleship can be a journey marked by personal growth and eventual total surrender to God's will.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Conversion vs. Discipleship
Conversion is the initial acceptance of Christ as Saviour, while discipleship is the lifelong process of maturing, obeying, and submitting to Him as Lord.

### Theme: Sacrificial Living
The requirement for a true disciple to deny themselves, take up their cross, and present their body as a living sacrifice to God, dying to selfish desires.

### Theme: Divine Dependence
The reliance on the Holy Spirit for transformation, revelation, and the strength to endure temptations and trials.
