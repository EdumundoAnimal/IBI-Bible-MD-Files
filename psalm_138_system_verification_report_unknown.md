## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Faithfulness (Ḥesed wə-ʾEmet)
The foundational theological anchor of the psalm, contrasting unilateral divine commitment (ḥesed) with absolute ontological reliability (ʾemet).

### Theme: Divine Transcendence in Lowliness
A theological paradox where God's greatness is manifest not by distance, but by His condescension and regard for the abased (šāp̄āl).

### Theme: Eschatological Sovereign Completion
The belief that God is actively working to bring His redemptive purposes to fruition (gāmar) despite human brokenness and temporal distress.

### Theme: The Audacity of Faith
The act of singing praise 'before the gods' (neged ʾĕlōhîm), serving as a polemic against competing cultural and spiritual authorities.
