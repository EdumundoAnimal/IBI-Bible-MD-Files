## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Faithfulness (Ḥesed wə-ʾEmet)
The document defines ḥesed as unilateral, covenant-bound loyalty and unmerited steadfast love, distinct from Hellenistic sentimentality. When paired with ʾemet (trustworthiness/reliability), it forms a hendiadys denoting covenantal reliability that cannot be subverted by historical contingencies.

### Theme: Divine Transcendence in Lowliness
The psalm presents a paradox where God's greatness is not demonstrated by aloofness, but by His condescension to the 'šāp̄āl' (the abased/lowly). This is contrasted with the 'gābōah' (the proud), who are kept at a distance.
