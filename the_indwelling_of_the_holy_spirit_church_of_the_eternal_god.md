# The Indwelling of the Holy Spirit
**Author:** [[Church of the Eternal God]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the theological concept of the Holy Spirit dwelling within Christians. It argues that while God the Father, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit are distinct in certain manifestations, they share a unified oneness of character and purpose. The text references various biblical passages, including John 14, 1 Corinthians 12, and 1 John 3, to support the belief that God and Christ dwell in believers through the Holy Spirit. It emphasizes that those who lack the Spirit of God are not true Christians, and that true belief involves living in accordance with God's commandments.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness of God and Christ
The author argues that God the Father, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit are united in character, purpose, and essence, rather than being mere separate entities.

### Theme: Indwelling of the Spirit
The central premise that the Spirit of God is the mechanism by which the Father and Son dwell within the believer.

### Theme: The Necessity of the Holy Spirit
A theological claim that possession of the Holy Spirit is a mandatory condition for being a true Christian.
