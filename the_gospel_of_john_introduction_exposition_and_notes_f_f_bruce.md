# The Gospel of John: Introduction, Exposition and Notes
**Author:** [[F. F. Bruce]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an expository commentary on chapters 9 and 10 of the Gospel of John. It examines the healing of the man born blind, the subsequent interrogation by religious authorities, and the ensuing theological debates regarding Jesus' identity and authority. Following this, the text explores the Parable of the Good Shepherd, addressing themes of the shepherd and the flock, the contrast between the true shepherd and hirelings, and Jesus' unique relationship with the Father. The commentary also covers the Feast of Dedication and the escalation of conflict between Jesus and the religious leaders, concluding with Jesus' departure beyond the Jordan.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Identity
The text explores the Johannine presentation of Jesus' divine origin, his role as the Good Shepherd, and his unity with the Father, which often triggers charges of blasphemy from religious leaders.

### Theme: Conflict and Judgment
Analysis of the recurring tension between Jesus and the religious establishment, wherein Jesus' works and words act as a 'judgment,' dividing those who accept his testimony from those who remain spiritually blind.

### Theme: Spiritual Blindness vs. Revelation
A thematic exploration based on the healing of the blind man, contrasting the Pharisees' refusal to 'see' the obvious miracle with the genuine faith of those who receive Jesus' teaching.
