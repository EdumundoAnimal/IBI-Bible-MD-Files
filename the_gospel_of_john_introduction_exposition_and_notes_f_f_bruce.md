# The Gospel of John: Introduction, Exposition and Notes
**Author:** [[F. F. Bruce]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an exegetical exposition of the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on chapters 9 and 10. The text covers the interrogation of the blind man healed by Jesus, the subsequent conflict with the religious authorities, and the presentation of Jesus as the Good Shepherd. The author discusses the theological implications of Jesus' claims to divine origin, the reaction of the Jewish authorities, and the broader mission of Jesus to both Jews and Gentiles.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Claims
The text explores Jesus' repeated declarations of his divine unity with the Father and his role as the Good Shepherd, which provoke intense opposition from Jewish religious leaders.

### Theme: Conflict with Religious Authority
An analysis of the escalating tension between Jesus and the Pharisaical authorities, characterized by the legalistic rejection of Jesus' miraculous works and his interpretation of Torah.

### Theme: The Motif of Sight and Blindness
A symbolic investigation of the physical healing of the blind man in Chapter 9, serving as a metaphor for spiritual enlightenment and the self-imposed blindness of those who reject Jesus.
