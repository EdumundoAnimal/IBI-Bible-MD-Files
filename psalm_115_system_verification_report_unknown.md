## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Law of Ontological Assimilation
The biblical principle that human beings are hardwired to reflect the nature of whatever they worship, leading to either life in God or spiritual deadness when worshiping idols.

### Theme: Polemic against Idolatry
An analysis of how Psalm 115 dismantles Ancient Near Eastern idol worship by exposing the manufactured nature of images that lack life, speech, or agency.

### Theme: Sovereignty and Transcendence
The contrast between the localized, man-made idols of surrounding nations and the sovereign, transcendent God of Israel who resides in the heavens.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
Viewing Psalm 115 through the lens of Jesus' participation in the Passover Hallel, His passion, and His resurrection as the resolution to the 'silence' of Sheol.
