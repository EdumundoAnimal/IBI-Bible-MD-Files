## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Economic Transformation
Ireland transitioned from an agrarian and manufacturing base to a hi-tech and services-oriented economy by 2007.

### Theme: Impact of 2008 Recession
The combination of a housing market crash, construction decline, and global credit crunch led to significant GDP contraction, deflation, and high unemployment.

### Theme: Fiscal Policy Challenges
Reliance on capital tax revenues led to a severe fiscal deficit when the property sector collapsed, increasing national debt levels.
