# Oneness-Trinitarian Pentecostal
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the theological dialogue between Oneness and Trinitarian Pentecostal perspectives regarding Christology, the Godhead, and Salvation. It presents joint affirmations of belief in Jesus Christ as the incarnation of God, while acknowledging ongoing debates regarding the terminology of 'persons' versus the oneness of God's essence. It also addresses the definition of holiness and the role of the Bible as the authoritative guide for Christian life.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Incarnation
Both traditions affirm Jesus as the living Logos, fully God and fully human, emphasizing that in Him the fullness of the Godhead dwells bodily.

### Theme: Godhead and Personhood
Oneness Pentecostals argue for the absolute unity of God without distinct 'persons' or consciousness centers. Trinitarian Pentecostals, while affirming one God, maintain that the term 'persons' is a useful but fallible tradition for describing relational life within the Godhead.

### Theme: Salvation via Grace
There is a shared affirmation that salvation is achieved through the unmerited grace of God in Jesus Christ and the empowering life of the Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Holiness and Conduct
Holiness is presented not as a manufactured set of rules, but as an empowerment by the Holy Spirit leading to godly attitudes, personal modesty, and liberating social relationships.
