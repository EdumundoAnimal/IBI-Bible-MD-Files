## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of approaches to New Testament exegesis, specifically focusing on structural, rhetorical, and linguistic analysis. It includes scholarly discussions on the methodologies applied to texts like Hebrews, Peter, and James. Additionally, it contains an academic assignment outline for students studying the Epistle to the Hebrews, outlining comparison and contrast tasks regarding the priesthood of Christ, the Old Covenant, and the comparison with angels, aiming to bridge academic study with application for contemporary believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Exegesis Methodologies
The text explores the application of structural, rhetorical, and linguistic methodologies to New Testament texts to maintain objectivity and uncover the original intent.

### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
A core theological theme is the superiority of Christ in Hebrews, specifically comparing His role to angels and the priesthood of the Old Covenant.

### Theme: Rhetorical Criticism
Utilizing Greco-Roman rhetorical conventions to understand how authors of New Testament letters structured their arguments to persuade and exhort their audiences.
