## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Vindication
The psalm is analyzed as a forensic appeal where David seeks righteousness not as self-merit, but as loyalty to the Yahwistic covenant order in the face of false accusation.

### Theme: Eschatological Beatific Vision
The text argues that verse 15 points beyond present physical circumstances to an ultimate hope in resurrection and direct, transformative communion with God.

### Theme: The Immanent Frame vs. Transcendent Teleology
Contrasts the 'earthbound' pursuit of worldly satiety and generational legacy with the 'covenant' orientation towards eternal life in God's presence.
