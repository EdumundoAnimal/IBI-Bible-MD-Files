# Why Remember
**Author:** [[Colin Bain]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The sermon reflects on the meaning of remembrance and sacrifice through the story of Maximilian Kolbe, a Catholic priest who volunteered to die in a Nazi starvation cell to save the life of Francis Gajowniczek, a Polish sergeant. The author links this sacrificial love to the Christian concept of laying down one's life for friends, as referenced in John 15:9-15:17. Furthermore, the text contrasts worldly conflicts with the spiritual "war" of doing good, referencing Galatians 6:7-9, which emphasizes reaping what one sows. It concludes by framing Jesus' death as the ultimate act of sacrificial love, calling the congregation to remember this sacrifice in their daily lives.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sacrificial Love
The sermon centers on the theological definition of love as the willingness to lay down one's life for others, exemplified by Maximilian Kolbe and ultimately by Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Spiritual Warfare
The author recontextualizes the concept of war, shifting from physical conflict to the persistent, godly struggle of doing good in the face of evil, as supported by scriptural mandates.

### Theme: The Law of Sowing and Reaping
Using Galatians 6:7-9, the author argues that actions in this life have spiritual consequences, encouraging perseverance in virtuous deeds despite the hardship of the world.
