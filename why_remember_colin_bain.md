# Why Remember
**Author:** [[Colin Bain]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article 'Why Remember' uses the story of Maximilian Kolbe, a Polish Catholic priest who sacrificed his life for a fellow prisoner, Francis Gajowniczek, in Auschwitz as a focal point for a theological reflection on sacrificial love and the Christian call to service. Drawing on John 15:9-17 and Galatians 6:7-9, the author encourages the reader to move beyond passive observation of history and engage in active, altruistic service. The text contrasts worldly conflicts with the 'war' of the believer, which is characterized by acts of kindness, dedication to God's creation, and persistent goodness. By highlighting the lives of Kolbe, William Booth, and the example of Jesus, the author argues that true faith requires tangible, selfless action that honors the greatest sacrifice.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sacrificial Love
The principle of laying down one's life for one's friends, exemplified by Maximilian Kolbe and ultimately by Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Active Faith
The argument that Christianity is not a passive belief but an active commitment to 'doing good' and fighting against evil, as urged in Galatians 6:9.

### Theme: Commemoration and Legacy
The importance of remembering historic acts of heroism to inspire current and future generations to live with empathy and purpose.
