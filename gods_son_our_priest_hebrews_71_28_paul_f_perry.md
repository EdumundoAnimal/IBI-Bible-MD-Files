# God's Son: Our Priest - Hebrews 7:1-28
**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an exegetical study of Hebrews 7:1-28, examining the theological argument concerning Jesus' priesthood as being 'like Melchizedek' and superior to the Levitical priesthood. The text emphasizes that Jesus holds a permanent priesthood grounded in divine oath rather than physical descent. Key themes include the typological significance of Melchizedek, the limitations of the Mosaic Law's priestly system, and the 'indestructible life' of Christ as the basis for his role as our high priest. The study breaks down the structure of Hebrews 7, contrasting the transitory, physical qualifications of Levitical priests with the eternal, superior nature of Jesus' priestly work, ultimately focusing on his role in establishing a 'better covenant'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Melchizedek Typology
The text uses Melchizedek as a historical and scriptural type to argue for a priesthood that exists outside the constraints of the Levitical lineage.

### Theme: Superiority of the New Priesthood
Christ's priesthood is argued to be superior because it is founded on an eternal, unchangeable oath of God, unlike the Levitical system regulated by physical descent.

### Theme: Indestructible Life
The author highlights that Jesus' high priesthood is empowered by his resurrection and an 'indestructible life', rendering him a permanent intercessor.
