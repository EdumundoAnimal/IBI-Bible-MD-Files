## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Koine Greek Pedagogy
The pedagogical approach to teaching New Testament Greek focuses on translation exercises, identifying grammatical structures (declensions, tenses), and applying these to New Testament passages.

### Theme: Pauline Ecclesiology
Robert Banks explores the communal nature of early house churches, emphasizing their social and cultural setting and Paul's distinct approach to forming communities that interact with their surrounding environments.

### Theme: Cultural Context of New Testament
The text highlights the necessity of understanding the Graeco-Roman world, including stoicism, epicureanism, and contemporary religious mystery cults, to grasp the social pressures on early Christians.
