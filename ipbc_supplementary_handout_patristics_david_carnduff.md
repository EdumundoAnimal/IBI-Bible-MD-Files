# I.P.B.C. Supplementary Handout - Patristics
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a chronological overview of key Ante-Nicene and Post-Nicene Church Fathers, detailing their geographical locations, dates, and historical/theological contributions. It outlines the development of the Nicene Creed, the First Council of Nicaea, and the later Second Council of Nicaea. The text documents the intellectual shifts in early Christianity, including the influence of Neoplatonism, the rise of the Allegorical method, and key theological debates surrounding the Trinity, Arianism, and the nature of the Church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Development of Doctrine
Traces the evolution of early Christian theology from Apostolic roots to the formulation of the Nicene Creed and the formalization of Trinitarian dogma.

### Theme: Theological Conflicts
Highlights internal church disputes, specifically the Arian controversy regarding the nature of Christ, and the ongoing tension between Literal and Allegorical schools of biblical interpretation.

### Theme: Ecclesiology
Examines the structural development of the church, including the emergence of the episcopacy and the role of the Church as a necessary mediator of salvation.

### Theme: Influence of Philosophy
Notes the synthesis of Greek thought, particularly Platonic and Neoplatonic concepts, with Christian theology by figures like Origen and Augustine.
