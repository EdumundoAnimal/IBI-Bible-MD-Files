## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Expository Preaching
The methodology of letting the biblical text dictate the message, utilizing cross-references and structural analysis rather than personal opinion.

### Theme: Contextualization
Adapting the delivery of theological truths to the specific cultural, linguistic, and temporal needs of the audience, specifically addressing Irish cultural perceptions of innate goodness.

### Theme: Counselling Framework
A developmental approach to spiritual guidance that assesses current strengths and growth needs through the lenses of salvation, forgiveness, and the Spirit-filled life.
