# Dictionary of the Later New Testament & Its Developments
**Author:** [[Ralph P. Martin and Peter H. Davids]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is an excerpt from the Dictionary of the Later New Testament & Its Developments. It covers theological concepts related to 'Spirits', referencing early Christian literature including the Shepherd of Hermas, 1 Clement, the Didache, and the writings of Justin Martyr and Irenaeus. It also includes an entry on Stephen, the first Christian martyr in the Acts of the Apostles, analyzing his trial, defense, and the theological significance of his death.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Pneumatology in Early Christian Literature
Explores how early non-canonical writers like Hermas and the author of 1 Clement interpreted the concept of 'spirits' (pneumata), often personifying them as forces of virtue or vice in contrast to Pauline theology.

### Theme: The Martyrdom of Stephen
Analyzes the narrative of Stephen in the book of Acts, emphasizing his role as the first Christian martyr and the theological implications of his speech against the Jewish establishment of the time.

### Theme: Patristic Theology and Demonology
Examines how Church Fathers like Justin Martyr and Irenaeus utilized concepts of spirits, deceitful spirits, and demonic influences to define orthodoxy and defend against Gnostic or heretical interpretations.
