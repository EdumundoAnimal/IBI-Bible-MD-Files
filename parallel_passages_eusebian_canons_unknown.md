## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Eusebian Canons
A system of cross-referencing parallel passages in the Gospels developed by Eusebius of Caesarea to help readers identify accounts shared by the Synoptic Gospels and John.

### Theme: Gospel Harmony
The practice of aligning identical or complementary narratives across the four canonical Gospels to create a single chronological or thematic overview of Jesus's life.
