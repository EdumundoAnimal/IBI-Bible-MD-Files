# The Indelible Image: The Theological and Ethical Thought World of the New Testament
**Author:** [[Ben Witherington III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document covers The Indelible Image, Volume One, The Individual Witness. It explores key Johannine themes, particularly the theology of the Fourth Gospel, the significance of 'I Am' statements, the role of the Spirit, the necessity of abiding in Christ, and the passion and resurrection of Jesus. The analysis focuses on how Johannine literature presents Jesus as the revealer of the truth, utilizing metaphors like the vine and water. It also delves into the interaction between Wisdom literature and New Testament theology, emphasizing the incarnation and the ethical implications for believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine 'I Am' Sayings
Exploration of the 'I Am' statements as self-revelation of Jesus, linking his identity to divine wisdom, the source of life, and his role as the revealer of truth.

### Theme: The Role of the Spirit
Examination of the Spirit as an advocate and agent of Jesus on earth, tasked with indwelling the believer, testifying to the truth, and equipping the community.

### Theme: Abiding in Christ
Analysis of the theology of 'abiding' (vine imagery), representing a continuous, dependent relationship that produces ethical behavior and spiritual fruit.
