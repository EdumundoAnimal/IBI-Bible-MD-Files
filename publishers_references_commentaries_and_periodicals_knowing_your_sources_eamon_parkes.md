# Publishers, References, Commentaries, and Periodicals: Knowing Your Sources
**Author:** [[Eamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This guide outlines the hierarchy of research sources for theological and biblical studies, detailing publishers, types of commentaries, periodicals, and the nature of primary, secondary, and tertiary resources. It provides a visual categorization of publishers based on academic rigor and offers examples of source types for specific research topics such as Calvin's theology and the Documentary Hypothesis.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Source Evaluation
The importance of distinguishing between scholarly resources and devotional or populist materials when conducting academic research.

### Theme: Source Hierarchy
Categorization of information into primary (original documents), secondary (evaluative scholarship), and tertiary (reference/fact-checking) levels.

### Theme: Publisher Reliability
A heuristic approach to determining the scholarly weight of a resource based on its publisher and the academic rigor of the commentary series.
