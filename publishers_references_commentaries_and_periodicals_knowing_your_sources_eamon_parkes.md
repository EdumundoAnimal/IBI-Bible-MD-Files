# Publishers, References, Commentaries, and Periodicals: Knowing Your Sources
**Author:** [[Eamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This guide outlines the hierarchy of academic sources for theological research, categorizing publishers by scholarly reputation, defining the distinction between primary, secondary, and tertiary sources, and offering guidance on navigating commentaries and periodicals for academic study. It emphasizes a structured research approach, moving from reference materials to comprehensive secondary and primary source analysis.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Academic Source Hierarchy
The document posits a spectrum of reliability and scholarly depth for publishers and commentaries, advising students to prioritize university presses and highly critical academic series for formal research.

### Theme: Research Methodology
A cyclical approach to research is recommended: starting with reference materials for foundational background, moving to secondary sources for critical analysis, and engaging with primary sources for original data.

### Theme: Distinguishing Source Types
The author provides a crucial framework for differentiating between primary (raw data/original texts), secondary (scholarly evaluation/interpretation), and tertiary (factual/encyclopedic) sources.
