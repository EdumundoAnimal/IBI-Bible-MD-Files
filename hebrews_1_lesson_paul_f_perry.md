# Hebrews 1 Lesson
**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Lesson 1: Hebrews 1:1-4. The Old Revelation vs. The New Revelation. God's Former Speaking to the fathers through the prophets in the past incompletely vs. God's Present Speaking to us in this final age by his Son completely with finality. The Greatness of Jesus: 1. Jesus is God's appointed heir. 2. Jesus is God's creative agent. 3. Jesus is God's personified glory. 4. Jesus is God's perfect revelation. 5. Jesus is God's cosmic sustainer. 6. Jesus is God's unique sacrifice. 7. Jesus has been exalted to God's right hand. Jesus and Angels comparison. Hebrews 2:1-4 warns against drifting from the message. Christ and Personified Wisdom: Jewish writers assigned four tasks to wisdom: (1) creation of the world, (2) providential sustaining, (3) revelation of truth, (4) reconciliation of persons to God. The status of Jesus is greater than angels in dignity, status, and function.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
Exploration of the unique nature of Jesus as the final and complete revelation of God, surpassing the prophets of the Old Testament.

### Theme: Wisdom Theology
Connection of Christ to the Jewish concept of personified Wisdom, attributing creative and sustainer roles to him.

### Theme: Supremacy of Christ over Angels
Argument that Jesus possesses greater dignity, status, and function than the angelic beings.
