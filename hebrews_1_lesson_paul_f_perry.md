## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Supremacy of Christ
The document argues that Jesus is superior to angels, serving as God's appointed heir, creative agent, and perfect revelation. This is supported by his exaltation to God's right hand and his role as the sustainer of the cosmos.

### Theme: Progressive Revelation
The text contrasts God's former speaking through prophets in the past with His final, complete speaking through His Son in the present age.

### Theme: Wisdom Christology
The author links the person of Jesus to the Jewish concept of Wisdom, attributing to Him the tasks of creation, providential sustaining, revelation of truth, and reconciliation.
