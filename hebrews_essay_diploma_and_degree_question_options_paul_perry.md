# Hebrews Essay: Diploma and Degree Question Options
**Author:** [[Paul Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines essay question options for a Hebrews course, divided between Diploma and Degree levels. For the Diploma level, students are asked to choose one question of 3000 words. The options involve comparing Christ with the angels (Hebrews 1:4-14) or contrasting the Old Covenant and the priestly work of Christ (Hebrews 7:23-28 & 9:11-14). The document also notes the primary similarities and differences between Diploma and Degree questions, emphasizing that Degree level questions require deeper critical engagement, broader scholarly research, and stronger argumentation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
The examination of the theological argument regarding the superiority of Christ over the angels and the transition from the Old Covenant priesthood to the priesthood of Christ.

### Theme: Application of Biblical Truths
The pedagogical requirement to bridge the gap between historical theological context of the Epistle to the Hebrews and contemporary encouragement for modern believers.

### Theme: Academic Rigor
The distinction between Diploma and Degree level theological inquiry, where degree level work demands broader research, critical debate, and advanced exegesis.
