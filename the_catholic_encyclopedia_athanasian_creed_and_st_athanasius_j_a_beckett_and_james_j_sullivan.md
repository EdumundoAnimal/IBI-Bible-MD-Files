# The Catholic Encyclopedia: Athanasian Creed and St. Athanasius
**Author:** [[J. A. Beckett and James J. Sullivan]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains entries from Volume II of The Catholic Encyclopedia. It covers the definition and atavism, the Athabasca Vicariate Apostolic, and detailed theological and historical examinations of the Athanasian Creed and the life of Saint Athanasius. The text explains the origins, authorship, and ecclesiastical significance of the Athanasian Creed, as well as the historical biography of St. Athanasius, his defense of the doctrine of the Incarnation, and his influence in early Church history.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Doctrine
The text details the Athanasian Creed's exposition of the Trinity and the Incarnation, emphasizing the co-equality and co-eternity of the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.

### Theme: Ecclesiastical Authority and Authorship
A critical examination of the historical uncertainty surrounding the authorship of the Athanasian Creed, noting that while it bears the name of Athanasius, its origins are likely found in fifth-century provincial synods.

### Theme: Orthodoxy vs. Heresy
The discussion of the 'damnatory clauses' within the Creed, which state that belief in the described doctrines is necessary for salvation, framing these as fundamental to Catholic teaching.

### Theme: Hagiography of St. Athanasius
An account of the life of Athanasius, emphasizing his role as a champion of the Incarnation and his leadership during a period of transition in the fourth-century Church.
