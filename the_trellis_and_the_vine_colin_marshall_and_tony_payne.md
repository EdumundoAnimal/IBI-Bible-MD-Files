# The Trellis and the Vine
**Author:** [[Colin Marshall and Tony Payne]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The book presents a foundational shift in how Christian ministry is conceived. It distinguishes between 'trellis' work (the supporting structures, programs, and logistics of church life) and 'vine' work (the actual growth of the gospel through the word of God and the discipleship of people). The authors argue that many churches have become overly consumed by maintaining the trellis, often at the expense of nurturing the vine, which is the primary, person-to-person work of gospel growth. The text advocates for a transition away from event-driven, leader-centered models towards building a culture where all Christians are empowered and trained to be 'vine workers,' emphasizing disciple-making as a fundamental mandate derived from the Great Commission.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trellis vs. Vine
The structural distinction between necessary church organization (trellis) and the essential, relational work of teaching, training, and discipleship (vine).

### Theme: The Great Commission
Re-interpreting the Great Commission not just as a global missions mandate, but as a call for every Christian to be actively involved in making disciples in their local context.

### Theme: Ministry Mind-Shift
The necessity of moving from a model of clergy-led administrative church management to a collaborative, people-focused model where church members are equipped for active ministry.
