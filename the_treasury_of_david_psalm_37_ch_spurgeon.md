# The Treasury of David: Psalm 37
**Author:** [[C.H. Spurgeon]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume is part of C.H. Spurgeon's 'The Treasury of David', an exegesis and devotional commentary on the Psalms. Specifically, this section covers Psalm 37, which explores themes of divine providence, the prosperity of the wicked versus the righteous, and the call to patience and trust in God's ultimate justice. Spurgeon provides exposition, homiletic notes, and excerpts from other writers to illustrate the biblical text.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Providence
The argument that God ultimately upholds the righteous and that the prosperity of the wicked is fleeting and doomed to destruction.

### Theme: Call to Patience
Exhortations to 'fret not thyself' when witnessing the temporary success of the wicked, urging believers to wait for God's timing.

### Theme: The Fate of the Wicked
Analysis of the psalmist's description of the wicked as being cut off like grass, highlighting the transience of worldly success versus eternal spiritual standing.
