# The Treasury of David: Psalm 37
**Author:** [[C.H. Spurgeon]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a detailed expository and devotional commentary on Psalm 37 from Volume I of C.H. Spurgeon's 'The Treasury of David'. It features a section-by-section analysis of the Psalm, covering verses 1 through 40. The text includes detailed exposition, explanations of key concepts, cross-references, and insights from various theological scholars and writers. It examines themes of patience, trust in God, the contrast between the righteous and the wicked, and the finality of God's judgment.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Patience and Trust in God
Spurgeon emphasizes that the core instruction of Psalm 37 is for the righteous to avoid envy of the wicked's temporary prosperity, urging them to trust in the Lord and wait patiently for His justice and timing.

### Theme: Divine Justice
The commentary argues that while the wicked may appear to flourish in the short term, their destruction is certain, and the righteous will ultimately inherit the earth through God's intervention.

### Theme: The Fate of the Wicked vs. The Righteous
A recurring theme contrasting the ephemeral nature of the wicked (likened to grass or fading smoke) with the enduring legacy and spiritual security of those who walk in God's ways.
