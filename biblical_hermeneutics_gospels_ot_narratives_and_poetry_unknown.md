## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of presentation slides covering biblical hermeneutics, focusing on special interpretation principles for specific genres: Old Testament Narratives, The Gospels, and Old Testament Poetry. Key themes include historical, literary, and theological context, the necessity of reading Gospels both horizontally and vertically, and the function of poetry in expressing human emotion and worship to God, particularly through the Psalms and Lamentations.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: General vs. Special Hermeneutics
Distinguishes between general principles applicable to all biblical literature and special principles tailored to specific genres like narratives, poetry, and gospels.

### Theme: Interpreting OT Narratives
Argues that narratives often illustrate doctrines rather than teaching them directly, characters are frequently flawed rather than exemplary, and the reader is responsible for interpreting events even when explicit judgment is absent.

### Theme: Interpreting The Gospels
Advocates for a multi-dimensional approach: historical context (first-century Judaism), vertical reading (within a specific Gospel's structure), and horizontal reading (comparing synoptic versions to discern unique theological emphases).

### Theme: Function of OT Poetry
Highlights poetry as a vehicle for honest, authentic expression of the full range of human emotions, including lament, frustration, and complaint, which serves to deepen relationship and worship with God.
