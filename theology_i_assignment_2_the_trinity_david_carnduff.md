# Theology I - Assignment 2: The Trinity
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the theological concept of the Trinity, noting that while the specific term is not found in the Bible, the doctrine is derived from scriptural teachings regarding the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. The study provides numerous biblical illustrations for the Trinity, examining concepts of unity in plurality within natural science, human relationships, and the church. It proceeds to catalog Old Testament allusions to the Trinity and traces its manifestation through the New Testament—specifically in the Gospels, the Paulines, and the Non-Pauline literature. The final sections demonstrate how each member of the Trinity—Father, Son, and Spirit—is accredited with active roles in the theological works of Sanctification, Resurrection, and Adoption.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Basis of the Trinity
The argument that while the explicit word 'Trinity' is absent from scripture, the divine nature of the three persons is fundamentally implied throughout the biblical text.

### Theme: Unity in Plurality
The theological attempt to use analogies—such as white light through a prism, the structure of the church, and human relationships—to explain how one essence exists in three distinct persons.

### Theme: Divine Participation in Human Salvation
The systematic examination of how the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit cooperate uniquely and collectively in the processes of sanctification, resurrection, and adoption of believers.
