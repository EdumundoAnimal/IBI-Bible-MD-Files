The document explores the foundational principles of Pentecostal theology, emphasizing that it is built primarily on religious experience rather than intellectual endeavor. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theology of Encounter
The conviction that Pentecostal theology is not a codified doctrine but a reaction to radical religious experience, where God is encountered before being studied.

### Theme: Pneumatological Hermeneutics
The movement from a strictly rational Bible study toward a Spirit-led epistemology that recognizes the role of the Holy Spirit in understanding the Word.

### Theme: Oneness vs. Trinitarianism
The debate regarding the nature of the Godhead, with Oneness Pentecostals rejecting the 'Trinity' as a non-scriptural invention, preferring to see Father, Son, and Holy Spirit as manifestations of one God.

### Theme: Pauline Charismata
An analytical approach to spiritual gifts (charismata) in the New Testament, emphasizing edification of the Body of Christ and the criterion of intelligibility.
