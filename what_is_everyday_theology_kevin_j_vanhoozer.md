# What Is Everyday Theology?
**Author:** [[Kevin J. Vanhoozer]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article explores the intersection of theology and culture, defining 'everyday theology' as the pursuit of understanding how biblical truth intersects with the complexities of daily life. Vanhoozer argues that Christians must become 'competent interpreters' of the cultural texts that permeate their environment. Drawing on concepts like cultural literacy, the 'signs of the times,' and the role of cultural narratives, he emphasizes that the church's mission involves critically engaging with culture rather than withdrawing from it. He highlights the need to read culture through the lenses of creation, fall, and redemption, treating culture as a 'work of meaning' that can be shaped and redeemed by a faithful witness to Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Everyday Theology
The application of biblical truth to the entirety of human existence, moving beyond formal theology to address the practicalities of daily living.

### Theme: Cultural Literacy
The capacity to interpret and critique cultural texts, artifacts, and societal patterns through a theological framework.

### Theme: Competent Interpretation
The necessity for Christians to act as interpreters of culture, utilizing biblical discernment to navigate the 'signs of the times' and engage with the world redemptively.
