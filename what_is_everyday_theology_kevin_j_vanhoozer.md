# What Is Everyday Theology?
**Author:** [[Kevin J. Vanhoozer]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document, titled 'What Is Everyday Theology?,' serves as an introduction to a theory of cultural interpretation from a Christian perspective. The author argues that theology is the 'ministry of the Word to the world,' specifically applying biblical teachings to everyday life. He asserts that Christians must develop 'cultural literacy' to interpret the signs of the times, much like theologians study scripture. He critiques a 'reductionist' view of culture that solely focuses on economics or power dynamics, advocating instead for a perspective that incorporates the doctrine of creation, fall, and redemption. The text emphasizes that Christians must be 'competent interpreters' who read culture as they would a text, discerning its underlying worldviews while maintaining faithfulness to the Gospel. It concludes with guidelines for 'everyday theologians' to approach culture as a community of interpreters, navigating the complexities of modern society while staying rooted in Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Everyday Theology
Defined as the ministry of the Word to all areas of life, moving beyond the academy to interpret culture through a biblical framework.

### Theme: Cultural Literacy
The ability to read and interpret cultural texts—films, books, media—with the same attention given to reading the Bible.

### Theme: Theological Hermeneutics
The application of theological lenses to cultural phenomena, viewing culture as a complex system similar to language.

### Theme: The Three Worlds of a Text
A method of interpreting cultural works by examining the 'world behind' (context), the 'world of' (the work itself), and the 'world in front' (reader impact).
