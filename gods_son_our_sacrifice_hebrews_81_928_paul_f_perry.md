# God's Son: Our Sacrifice (Hebrews 8:1 - 9:28)
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a bible study lesson examining the book of Hebrews, specifically chapters 8 and 9. It focuses on the superiority of Jesus' priesthood and the transition from the Old Covenant to the New Covenant. The text explores the priestly ministry of Christ, the limitations of the earthly tabernacle, and the sufficiency of Christ's blood as an eternal sacrifice. Key themes include the contrast between the earthly and heavenly sanctuaries, the inadequacy of the Levitical sacrifices, and the establishment of a new, better covenant through Jesus Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: New Covenant Superiority
The lesson argues that the ministry attained by Jesus is superior to that of the Old Covenant priests, as it is built on better promises and a more effective sacrifice.

### Theme: Limitations of the Old Covenant
The document highlights that the Levitical system and the earthly tabernacle had inherent limitations, such as the mortality of priests and the need for repeated sacrifices, which could not permanently cleanse the conscience.

### Theme: Christ as the Final Sacrifice
The core argument is that Christ’s death and blood provided a once-for-all, eternal offering, replacing the repeated offerings of the Old Covenant and providing true access to God.
