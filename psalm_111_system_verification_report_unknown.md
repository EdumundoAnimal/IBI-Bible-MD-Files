## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Fidelity
The foundational argument that God's historical acts are not isolated events but tethered to an unbreakable covenantal framework.

### Theme: Epistemology of Wisdom
The assertion that true knowledge and wisdom are not the result of detached rationalism, but begin with holy reverence (fear of the Lord) and ontological humility.

### Theme: Redemptive Historiography
The view that history is an open theater of divine intervention, contrasting with the modern secular narrative of material naturalism and randomness.

### Theme: Liturgical Memory
The practice of 'remembering' (zēḵer) not as cognitive recall, but as an active, sacramental re-actualization of God's redemptive deeds.
