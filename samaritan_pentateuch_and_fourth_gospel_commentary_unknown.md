## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Samaritan Theology
Exploration of the Samaritan Pentateuch, emphasizing their focus on Mount Gerizim and specific textual deviations from Jewish scripture, such as the adaptation of the Ten Commandments.

### Theme: Johannine Christology
Analysis of John 7:37-39, interpreting Jesus' declaration of living water in relation to Jewish festive rituals and the promise of the Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Messianic Expectations
Examination of the crowd's reaction to Jesus, specifically the distinction between the 'Prophet like Moses' and the 'Messiah' in both Qumran and broader first-century Jewish thought.
