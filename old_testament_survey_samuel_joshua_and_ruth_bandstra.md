# Old Testament Survey: Samuel, Joshua, and Ruth
**Author:** [[Bandstra]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides thematic and chronological outlines of the Old Testament books of Samuel, Joshua, and Ruth. It examines the transition from a theocracy to a monarchy in Israel, the military campaigns and settlement under Joshua, and the narrative of Ruth as a story moving from emptiness to fullness. It also highlights the cycle of judges, the establishment of the Davidic kingdom, and the theological significance of divine covenants and human faithfulness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: From Theocracy to Monarchy
Explores the transition in Israel's leadership from judges and divine rule to the establishment of a king under Saul and David, highlighting the consequences of disobedience and the necessity of divine endorsement.

### Theme: The Cycle of the Promise
Details the process of 'Taking,' 'Sharing,' 'Settling,' and 'Holding onto' the Promised Land, emphasizing human-divine partnership and the requirement of faith for inheritance.

### Theme: Emptiness to Fullness
Analyses the book of Ruth as a narrative of redemption, where loss and famine are replaced by hope, harvest, and the continuation of the Davidic royal line.
