# Baker Commentary on the Old Testament: Wisdom and Psalms
**Author:** [[John Goldingay]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a scholarly commentary on Psalms 90 and 91, authored by John Goldingay. It provides a detailed exegetical analysis, covering translation, interpretation, and theological implications. The text explores themes of divine eternity versus human mortality, the nature of prayer and lament, and the relationship between Yhwh and his people across generations. Specific attention is given to the linguistic structure of the Hebrew original, historical contexts, and theological significance of the psalmist's requests for restoration and divine commitment.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Eternity vs. Human Mortality
The commentary highlights the tension between the enduring nature of God and the fleeting, transient life of humanity, often using the metaphor of grass that flourishes in the morning and withers by evening.

### Theme: Theology of Lament
Exploration of how the psalmist uses lament as a form of protest and plea for restoration, grounding the community's suffering in the historical relationship with Yhwh.

### Theme: Prayer as Covenantal Appeal
Analysis of the psalmist's approach to God as not just a sovereign deity, but a covenantal partner whose commitment to the community is invoked through formal prayer.
