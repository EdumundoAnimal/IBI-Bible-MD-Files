# Introduction to the New Testament: The Gospel of John
**Author:** [[Werner Georg Kümmel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The provided text is an academic theological examination of the Gospel of John, covering its historical and religious context, authorship, aim, and dating. The text engages with scholarly debates regarding the Gospel's relationship to Gnosticism, Mandaean tradition, and the Synoptic Gospels. It explores the 'beloved disciple' tradition, the role of Ephesus, and the authorship attributed to John, the son of Zebedee, ultimately questioning the traditional claims through critical analysis.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: History-of-Religions Setting
Analysis of the Gospel of John's connection to Hellenistic, Gnostic, and Mandaean religious backgrounds, and its dualistic terminology.

### Theme: Authorship and the Beloved Disciple
Examination of the traditional attribution to John the son of Zebedee and the identity of the 'beloved disciple,' focusing on the lack of internal evidence.

### Theme: Polemical Context
The Gospel's relationship to the Synoptic Gospels and the historical polemic against Jewish authorities, reflected in its theological aim.
