## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theology of Encounter
Pentecostal theology prioritizes personal, radical experience with God over abstract intellectualism, positing that experience precedes theological understanding.

### Theme: Spirit-Led Hermeneutics
The interpretation of Scripture is viewed through a lens of Spirit-led understanding, emphasizing the necessity of the Holy Spirit for valid interpretation while warning against twisting the Word.

### Theme: Oneness vs. Trinitarianism
Oneness Pentecostalism maintains that God is indivisibly one, rejecting traditional Trinitarian doctrine as a post-biblical invention, while proponents of Trinitarianism argue for the co-eternal existence of three persons in one Godhead.

### Theme: Charismata and the Priesthood of All Believers
The exercise of spiritual gifts is supported by the doctrine of the 'priesthood of all believers,' encouraging active participation of all members in the Body of Christ rather than clerical domination.
