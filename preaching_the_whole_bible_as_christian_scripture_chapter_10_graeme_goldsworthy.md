# Preaching the Whole Bible as Christian Scripture: Chapter 10
**Author:** [[Graeme Goldsworthy]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Chapter 10 focuses on preaching from Old Testament historical narrative texts. It emphasizes that these texts must be understood within the broader context of biblical theology, specifically relating to the overarching theme of the kingdom of God and the unfolding of salvation history. The chapter warns against purely moralistic or exemplary interpretations and advocates for connecting narrative elements to the ultimate fulfillment in Christ. It provides practical examples using the stories of Noah, Ruth, the ark to Jerusalem, and Nehemiah, illustrating how historical accounts function as part of the unified story of God's redemptive plan.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Theology of History
Historical narratives are not isolated stories but part of a structured salvation history (the kingdom of God) that culminates in Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Rejection of Exemplarism
The author argues against interpreting Old Testament figures merely as moral role models, which risks missing the redemptive-historical purpose of the texts.

### Theme: Christ-Centered Hermeneutics
True biblical preaching requires connecting the historical events of the Old Testament to their ultimate fulfillment in the gospel of Jesus Christ.
