Ruler: Xerxes II of Persia, Reigned: 424 BC. Ruler: Sogdianus of Persia, Reigned: 424-423 BC. Ruler: Darius II of Persia Nothus, Reigned: 423-405 BC. Ruler: Artaxerxes II of Persia Mnemon, Reigned: 404-359 BC. Ruler: Artaxerxes III of Persia Ochus, Reigned: 358-338 BC. Ruler: Arses of Persia (Artaxerxes IV), Reigned: 338-336 BC. Ruler: Darius III of Persia Codomannus, Reigned: 336-330 BC, Comments: Was defeated by Alexander the Great at the battle of Issus and Gaugamela in 333BC.

### OT Survey: Historical Kings and Restoration

Historical kings: Belshazzar - destroyed - hand writing on the wall. Cyrus (539-529 BC). Darius (522-486 BC). Xerxes (485-465 BC) - 300 - spartans. Artaxerxes (465-424 BC). Darius defeated by Alexander the Great. Persians replaced by Greeks. Restoration and Protection: 1st return in 537 BCE - Led by Sheshbazzar. 2nd return in 522 BCE - led by Zerubbabel and Jeshua. Story of Esther during reign of Xerxes. 3rd return in 456 BCE - led by Ezra. 4th return in 444 BCE - led by Nehemiah.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Restoration and Reform
The document outlines the historical progression of the Jewish return from exile, emphasizing the roles of leaders like Zerubbabel, Ezra, and Nehemiah in rebuilding the temple and reforming the people's adherence to the Law.

### Theme: Divine Sovereignty and Judgment
The text highlights God's judgment on nations (Babylon, Edom, Tyre) and Israel, noting that God uses historical events and foreign powers to discipline or restore His people.
