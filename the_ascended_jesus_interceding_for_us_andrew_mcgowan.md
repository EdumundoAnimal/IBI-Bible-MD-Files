# The Ascended Jesus Interceding for Us
**Author:** [[Andrew McGowan]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article explores the intercession of Jesus Christ as the ascended Lord, emphasizing his role as priest, king, and prophet. It examines how this doctrine applies to the believer's life, specifically in terms of atonement, forgiveness, and advocacy. Drawing from biblical texts such as Hebrews 7:23-28 and 1 John 1:1-2:6, the author argues that Jesus' intercession is not just a past event but a present reality that guarantees salvation and hope for those who sin. Furthermore, the text addresses the historical context of Hebrews, noting its hortatory nature, characterized by rhetorical questions and an intentional alternating structure of exposition and exhortation. The document concludes by reflecting on the responsibilities of gratitude and the relational nature of divine grace, using Greco-Roman patronage models to explain the obligation of believers to honor Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christ's Intercession
The theological argument that Jesus acts as a mediator for believers, providing atonement and advocating before God, thereby assuring believers of their salvation.

### Theme: Genre of Hebrews
An analysis of Hebrews as a 'word of exhortation' (homily) that utilizes alternating structures of theological exposition and moral exhortation.

### Theme: Patronage and Gratitude
An examination of the social obligations of gratitude, where divine grace functions similar to ancient patronage systems, requiring honor and loyalty from the beneficiary.
