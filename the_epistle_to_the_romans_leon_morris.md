# The Epistle to the Romans
**Author:** [[Leon Morris]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an analytical commentary on the Epistle to the Romans, specifically focusing on Romans 7, the role of the law, and the nature of human conviction before and after conversion. The text explores Paul's rhetorical strategies, the distinction between the regenerate and unregenerate perspectives, and the theological implications of the law in the believer's life.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Role of the Law
An investigation into whether the Mosaic law is the cause of sin or merely the instrument that exposes it, emphasizing its role in the divine scheme to highlight human helplessness.

### Theme: Regenerate vs. Unregenerate Perspectives
An analysis of whether Paul is speaking from his pre-conversion experience as a Pharisee or from his current state as a Christian believer, examining the internal conflict of the ego.

### Theme: The Nature of Sin
The concept of sin as an active, enslaving power rather than just a transgression, which operates within the physical body and human desire.
