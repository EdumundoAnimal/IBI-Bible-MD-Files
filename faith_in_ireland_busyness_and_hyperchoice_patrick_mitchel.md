## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultural Analysis
The document seeks to evaluate the characteristics of modern culture, specifically focusing on the contrast between historical and contemporary life in an Irish town (Lucan) to identify the challenges posed to Christian faith and mission.

### Theme: Contentment
The document introduces 'Contentment' as a thematic focal point for the discussion of faith and culture, suggesting it as a counter-cultural value in the face of modern 'busyness and hyperchoice'.
