## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultural Transformation
The material aims to track how societal life in an Irish town (Lucan) has shifted between 1950 and 2010, focusing on community dynamics, environment, and social values.

### Theme: Christian Faith and Modernity
The pedagogical objective is to evaluate the influence of contemporary culture, specifically 'busyness' and 'hyperchoice,' on the practice of faith and Christian mission.
