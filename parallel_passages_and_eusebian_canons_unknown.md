## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Gospel Harmony
The study of cross-referencing parallel events and teachings across the four canonical Gospels to provide a unified chronology and narrative.

### Theme: Eusebian Canons
A system of numbering and organizing Gospel sections devised by Eusebius of Caesarea to help readers locate parallel passages in the New Testament.
