## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Harmonization
The systematic alignment of Gospel narratives to identify cross-references and shared thematic content across the synoptic gospels and the Gospel of John.

### Theme: Eusebian Canons
A traditional system for referencing Gospel parallels created by Eusebius of Caesarea, allowing scholars to navigate sections common to all four evangelists.
