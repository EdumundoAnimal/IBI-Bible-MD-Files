Literary tools help readers develop 'literary competence': the ability to discern cues within the text that indicate what kind of literature it is, and thus what to expect & not to expect from it. Genre. One major literary tool is the identification of GENRE, or the general literary type. Genres include narrative, law material, poetry, prophecy, wisdom, gospels, epistles, etc. Genre. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Literary Context
The document emphasizes that taking a passage out of its literary context violates the author's 'flow of thought'. It defines context as a series of related ideas and argues that context is the most reliable guide for determining the meaning of words.

### Theme: Circles of Context
A hierarchical model for exegesis where the interpreter moves from the immediate passage to the paragraph, the chapter, the entire book, the author's other works, and finally the entire canon of Scripture.

### Theme: Genre Competence
The document posits that because the Bible is literature, readers must develop 'literary competence' to identify genres (narrative, poetry, prophecy, etc.) and their sub-categories to manage expectations and interpret the text correctly.

### Theme: Theological Unity
The text asserts that despite the diversity of biblical writers and their distinct slants, there is a theological unity in the Bible because it is inspired by the Holy Spirit and tells the story of the same God.
