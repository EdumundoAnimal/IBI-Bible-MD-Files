## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Literary Context
The practice of reading a passage within its immediate and broader surrounding text to understand the author's flow of thought and intended meaning, preventing the isolation of verses.

### Theme: Biblical Genre
The classification of biblical literature into types such as narrative, poetry, prophecy, and epistles, which dictates the interpretative rules and expectations for the reader.

### Theme: Theological Unity of Scripture
The belief that despite diversity of authors and perspectives, the entire Bible maintains theological consistency under the inspiration of the Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Circles of Context
A hierarchical approach to interpretation starting from the immediate paragraph, moving through the book division, the entire book, books by the same author, and finally the entire biblical canon.
