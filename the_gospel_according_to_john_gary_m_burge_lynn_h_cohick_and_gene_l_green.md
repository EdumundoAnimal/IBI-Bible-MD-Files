# The Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[Gary M. Burge, Lynn H. Cohick, and Gene L. Green]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a detailed examination of the Gospel of John, covering its literary form, theological themes, setting, and questions of authorship and date. It explores the 'I am' sayings, the structure of the Book of Signs and the Book of Glory, and the theological significance of Jesus as the logos. The text also delves into historical context, examining early church traditions regarding John's ministry in Ephesus, his connection to the synoptic gospels, and the debate surrounding the identity of the 'beloved disciple'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: I Am Sayings
A collection of self-revelatory statements made by Jesus in John's gospel, emphasizing his divine nature and relationship with God.

### Theme: Book of Signs vs. Book of Glory
The structural division of the gospel: the first half focuses on public ministry and signs, while the second half focuses on the passion, death, and glorification of Jesus.

### Theme: Authorship and Date
An investigation into the identity of the 'beloved disciple' and the scholarly debate regarding the gospel's composition, generally estimated between AD 80 and 100.

### Theme: Theological Affirmations
John's focus on the divinity of Christ, the Incarnation, and the function of the Holy Spirit as the Paraclete.
