# Romans: An Exposition of Chapters 7:1-8:4
**Author:** [[D. M. Lloyd-Jones]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is an exposition of Romans 7:1-8:4 by D. M. Lloyd-Jones, focusing on the function and limits of the Law. It explores the relationship between the Law, sin, and the Christian experience, emphasizing the spiritual nature of the Law and the necessity of realizing one's own helplessness apart from Christ. The author addresses misconceptions about sanctification, the nature of indwelling sin, and the historical and spiritual context of the Apostle Paul's argument.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Function of the Law
The Law acts as a mirror to expose sin, revealing human spiritual bankruptcy and the inability of the flesh to achieve holiness, thereby pointing to the necessity of grace.

### Theme: The Deceitfulness of Sin
Sin is described as active and deceptive, using the Law as an occasion to provoke disobedience, leading to a state of death and helplessness for the individual.

### Theme: Christian Identity and Freedom
True Christian life is defined by a radical change through union with Christ, where the believer is no longer 'under the Law' but living in the 'newness of the Spirit'.
