# The Atonement Debate
**Author:** [[Derek Tidball, David Hilborn, and Justin Thacker]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The Atonement Debate, published by Zondervan, serves as a collection of papers from the London Symposium on the theology of atonement. It features contributions from theologians and scholars such as Steve Chalke, Chris Wright, Howard Marshall, and Joel Green, among others. The volume examines the central tenets of evangelicalism, particularly the doctrines surrounding the substitutionary atonement of Christ, and addresses controversies triggered by publications like 'The Lost Message of Jesus'. The text explores the historical development of the Evangelical Alliance's Basis of Faith, the challenges presented by critical perspectives on penal substitution, and the ongoing discourse regarding the nature of God's relationship to human sin, suffering, and redemption.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Penal Substitutionary Atonement
The central theological debate concerning whether Christ's death on the cross was a literal payment for human sin to satisfy divine justice and wrath, or if this view misrepresents God's character.

### Theme: Evangelical Identity and Doctrine
The struggle to define the boundaries of evangelicalism while maintaining core biblical assertions, specifically regarding the 'centrality of the cross' in Christian faith.

### Theme: Hermeneutics and Exegesis
The tension between traditional theological models of the atonement and contemporary exegesis that seeks to re-evaluate biblical language in light of modern cultural and ethical sensibilities.
