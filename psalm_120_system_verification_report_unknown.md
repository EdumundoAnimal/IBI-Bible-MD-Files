## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theology of Alienation
The Psalm depicts the spiritual agony of living under systematic deception and social hostility, framing the believer's life as an exilic pilgrimage.

### Theme: Divine Retributive Justice
The imprecation in verses 3-4 is interpreted not as personal vendetta, but as a non-vindictive appeal to Yahweh's lex talionis, matching divine justice to human falsehood.

### Theme: Christological Resolution
The Psalm points forward to Christ, who entered the ultimate exile, was slandered by false witnesses, and bore the judgment for human deceit to provide shalom.

### Theme: Lexical Weaponization
The document highlights how deceptive speech (slander/perjury) acts as an offensive weapon in covenantal contexts, mirroring broader ancient Near Eastern wisdom literature.
