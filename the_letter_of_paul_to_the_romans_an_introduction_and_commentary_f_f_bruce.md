# The Letter of Paul to the Romans: An Introduction and Commentary
**Author:** [[F. F. Bruce]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological commentary on the Epistle to the Romans, specifically focusing on chapters 7. It explores Paul's autobiography, the tension of the law, the nature of sin, and the internal spiritual conflict experienced by believers. It discusses the 'dawn of conscience' in verses 7:7-13 and the internal conflict within verses 7:14-25, emphasizing the transition from the law to life in the Spirit. The text includes scholarly interpretations, references to various biblical translations, and historical context regarding Paul's conversion and struggle.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Autobiographical Interpretation
The text examines whether Romans 7 reflects Paul's personal pre-conversion experience or his later state as a believer, arguing that it captures the universal struggle of human nature under the law.

### Theme: The Role of the Law
An analysis of the law's function as a mirror of human sinfulness, proving that the law is holy but unable to provide the strength to overcome indwelling sin.

### Theme: The Conflict of the Two Natures
Paul's description of the spiritual warfare between the desires of the flesh and the spirit, characterizing the believer as one living between the old age and the new age.
