# Alignment and Effectiveness Framework
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Core Framework

Focus on how God has created and developed you / calls you to contribute most significantly. What re-charges you? Release those things you do well but not best. Spiritual Gifts: Given by the Spirit and developed by God through experiences and training. Talents: Learned skills: what you do well but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion. Passions: What excites you - Where you find real (not perceived) motivation. Limited sustainable energy & results. GREATEST ALIGNMENT & EFFECTIVENESS. Limited sustainable energy & results. Release those things that sap your energy and pass them on to those who are energized by those tasks..

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Stewardship of Energy and Calling
The document argues for a strategic focus on areas of 'Greatest Alignment & Effectiveness' by identifying the intersection of Spiritual Gifts, Talents, and Passions. It emphasizes that one should release tasks that are done well but not 'best' to avoid losing focus and passion.

### Theme: Spiritual and Personal Development
The framework posits that Spiritual Gifts are developed by God through experiences and training, while Passions provide the necessary motivation for sustainable energy.
