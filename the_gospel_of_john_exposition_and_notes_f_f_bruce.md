# The Gospel of John: Exposition and Notes
**Author:** [[F. F. Bruce]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of scholarly exposition on the Gospel of John, specifically focusing on the events and theological discourse surrounding Chapters 9 and 10. The text explores the healing of the blind man, the subsequent interrogation by religious authorities, and the ensuing parable of the Good Shepherd, emphasizing the divine claims of Jesus and the reactions of both the Jewish establishment and his followers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Identity and Sonship
The text argues that Jesus's actions and declarations, particularly in the Gospel of John, serve as definitive evidence of his divine origin, presenting him as the one commissioned by the Father.

### Theme: Ecclesiological Exclusion
The document examines the historical context of the synagogue's rejection of early believers, represented by the threat of excommunication for those who acknowledged Jesus as the Messiah.

### Theme: The Good Shepherd Motif
An analysis of the pastoral imagery used by Jesus to distinguish his ministry from that of false leaders, emphasizing his willingness to lay down his life for his own people.
