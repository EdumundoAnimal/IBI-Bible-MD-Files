## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theocratic Kingship
The Davidic monarch is not an autonomous ruler but a sub-vassal accountable to Yahweh's ethical sovereignty, requiring personal integrity before public administration.

### Theme: Systemic Logic of Sanctification
Private character and domestic habits serve as the 'source-code' of a leader, where secret sins like slander and pride inevitably permeate and corrupt the entire institutional or national infrastructure.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
Historical Davidic kings failed the oath of Psalm 101, pointing to the need for Jesus Christ as the true Davidic King whose perfection fulfills the law and whose eschatological reign purges evil.
