# Evaluation of Hebrews and Revelation
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A. Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview and analysis of two biblical books: The Epistle to the Hebrews and The Book of Revelation. For Hebrews, it covers authorship, the nature of the writing, date, and core christological arguments, emphasizing Christ's superiority over the Old Covenant and the Levitical system. For Revelation, it outlines the apocalyptic genre, various interpretive schools (Preterist, Historicist, Idealist, Futurist), and millennial views (Premillennialism, Postmillennialism, Amillennialism), ultimately focusing on the triumphant return of Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Supremacy of Christ in Hebrews
The central thesis that Christ is superior to the Old Covenant, the Levitical priesthood, and the angels, serving as the mediator of a New Covenant.

### Theme: Apocalyptic Genre and Interpretation
Analysis of Revelation's symbolic language and the four primary schools of interpretation: Preterist, Historicist, Idealist, and Futurist.

### Theme: Millennial Views
Evaluation of the timing and nature of Christ's reign relative to the millennium, including Premillennial, Postmillennial, and Amillennial frameworks.
