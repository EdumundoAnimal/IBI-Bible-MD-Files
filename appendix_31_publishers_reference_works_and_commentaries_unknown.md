## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Academic Rigor
The necessity of discerning the scholarly quality of a publisher, distinguishing between popular/devotional presses and those focused on critical academic research.

### Theme: Source Evaluation
Using a star-based rating system to identify the utility of reference works and distinguishing between scholarly commentaries and homiletical or devotional materials.

### Theme: Research Methodology
The importance of using diverse, recent, and high-quality sources, while exercising caution when using older or non-academic reference sets.
