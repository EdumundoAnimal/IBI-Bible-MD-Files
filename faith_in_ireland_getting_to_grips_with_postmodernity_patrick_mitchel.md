# Faith in Ireland: Getting to Grips with Postmodernity
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of postmodernity, focusing on its core reversals of modernist characteristics. It highlights themes such as the disbelief in human progress, the questioning of individual identity, and the rejection of absolute certainty. It contrasts 'pilgrims' with 'tourists' as a metaphor for postmodern experience, discusses the rise of subjective individualism and 'DIY spirituality,' and critiques the movement by listing its perceived advantages and inherent dangers, particularly the moral implications of extreme relativism.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Postmodern Reversals
Postmodernism is defined by its systemic reversal of modernism's key characteristics, including a shift away from universal truths toward subjective experiences.

### Theme: Certainty and Relativism
The document identifies postmodernism's rejection of absolute certainty as a central pillar, while critiquing the resulting moral relativism as logically incoherent and potentially dangerous.

### Theme: Subjective Individualism
The emergence of 'DIY spirituality' and the prioritization of individual narrative over objective, shared truth.
