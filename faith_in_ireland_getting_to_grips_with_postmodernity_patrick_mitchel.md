# Faith in Ireland: Getting to Grips with Postmodernity
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document outlines the core themes of postmodernism, contrasting its characteristics with modernism and evaluating its impact on contemporary society and faith in Ireland. It explores the rejection of linear progress, the questioning of the self, and the skepticism toward absolute certainty. The text further categorizes the advantages (plurality of perspectives, inclusivity) and the inherent dangers (incoherence, moral relativism, and the risk of unsustainable viewpoints) associated with postmodern thought.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Reversals of Modernism
Postmodernism critiques and flips modernist tenets, such as the rejection of absolute truth, linear progress, and the stability of the authorial voice.

### Theme: Moral Relativism and Subjectivity
The promotion of individual 'truth' and subjective experience leading to an age of DIY spirituality and religious mixing.

### Theme: Weaknesses of Postmodernism
A critique of postmodernism suggesting that extreme relativism is logically inconsistent, morally absurd, and ultimately unsustainable.
