# 4 John: Jesus and Nicodemus
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an analysis of John 3:1-36, detailing the discourse between Jesus and Nicodemus regarding spiritual rebirth, the kingdom of God, and the nature of belief. It explores the significance of Jesus' mission, the meaning of 'belief' as trust, and John the Baptist's witness to the supremacy of Christ. The text emphasizes that salvation is a regenerative act of God, and rejection of the Son leads to divine wrath.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Rebirth
The necessity of being born again/from above, emphasizing that salvation is a sovereign act of God rather than human effort or adherence to tradition.

### Theme: Eschatological Kingdom
The kingdom of God as both a present reality in the hearts of believers and a future reality at the end of the age.

### Theme: Nature of Belief
Belief is defined not as intellectual assent but as complete trust and faith in Christ, which is the sole requirement for eternal life.

### Theme: Supremacy of Christ
The assertion that Jesus is from above and therefore above all, requiring that John the Baptist decreases while Christ increases.
