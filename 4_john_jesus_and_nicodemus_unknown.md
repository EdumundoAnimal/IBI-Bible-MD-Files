# 4 John: Jesus and Nicodemus
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text provides an exegetical study of John 3, focusing on the discourse between Jesus and Nicodemus, the concept of being 'born again', the meaning of 'heavenly things', and John's theological commentary on the mission of the Son. It explores the theological necessity of regeneration by God for entering the kingdom of God, the typological significance of the bronze snake, and the nature of belief, judgment, and the supremacy of Christ as witnessed by John the Baptist and subsequent commentary.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Born Again
The necessity of being regenerated by God to enter the kingdom of God, contrasting human birth with spiritual birth from above.

### Theme: Eschatological Kingdom
The kingdom of God as a present reality in the hearts of believers and a future reality at the end of the age.

### Theme: Redemptive Typology
The use of Old Testament imagery, such as the bronze snake, to explain the crucifixion, resurrection, and the saving work of Christ.

### Theme: Supremacy of Christ
The theological stance that Jesus is from above, possesses the Spirit without limit, and is the sole authority whose testimony authenticates God's truth.
