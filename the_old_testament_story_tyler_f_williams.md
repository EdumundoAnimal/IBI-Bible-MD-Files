# The Old Testament Story
**Author:** [[Tyler F. Williams]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a structural overview of the Old Testament narrative, outlining the chronological flow from creation to the return from exile. It categorizes the Old Testament books into thematic sections including Law, History, and Prophets. Additionally, it offers a detailed outline of the Book of Genesis, focusing on primeval and patriarchal stories, and presents a timeline of major periods in Israelite history such as the Patriarchs, Exodus, Conquest, United Kingdom, Divided Kingdom, Exile, and Restoration. A map of the Ancient Near East provides geographical context to these historical events.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal History
The historical development of Israel as a nation, defined by divine covenants (starting with Abraham) and the cyclical patterns of faithfulness and judgment.

### Theme: Structural Canonization
The organization of Old Testament literature into specific categories—Law, History, and Prophets—which guide the reader through the 'Big Story' of the Bible.

### Theme: Geopolitical Context
The reliance of biblical history on the geography of the Ancient Near East (ANE), specifically the Fertile Crescent, as the stage for geopolitical interactions between Israel and empires like Egypt, Assyria, and Babylon.
