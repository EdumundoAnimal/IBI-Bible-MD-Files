# The Old Testament Story
**Author:** [[Tyler F. Williams]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Where the OT books fit into the Big Story

PART 1: From Adam to the Noah - Law - Genesis 1-9. PART 2: From the Noah to Abraham - Law- Genesis 10-11. PART 3: From Abraham to Moses - Law - Genesis 12 - Exodus 1. PART 4: From Moses to King David - Law - Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, History - Joshua, Judged, Ruth and 1st and 2nd Samuel. PART 5: From King David to the Zerubbabel - History - 1st and 2nd Kings, 1st and 2nd Chronicles; Prophets - Isaiah, Jeremiah, Daniel, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk and Zephaniah. PART 6: From Zerubbabel to Christ - History - Ezra, Nehemiah and Esther, Prophets - Haggai, Zechariah and Malachi. Poetic books - Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes and Song of Solomon were written over an extended period of time.

### An Outline of the Book of Genesis

A. Primeval Stories. Prologue: Creation of Heaven and Earth (1:1-2:3). 1. Introductory statement (1:1). 2. Negative state at time of creation (1:2). 3. Six days of creation (1:3-31). 4. Summary statement (2:1). 5. Epilogue: Sabbath rest (2:2-3). 1. Toledot of the Heavens and Earth (2:4-4:26) [Narrative]. 2. Toledot of Adam's genealogy (5:1-6:8) [Vertical Genealogy]. 1. Superscription (5:1a). 2. Covenant line of Seth (5:1b-32). 3. Escalation of sin before the flood (6:1-8). 3. Toledot of Noah (6:9-9:29) [Narrative]. 1. Superscription (6:9a). 2. Account of the flood (6:9b-8:22). 3. Covenant not to destroy the earth (9:1-17). 4. Prophecies about Noah's sons (9:18-27). 5. Epilogue (9:28-29). 4. Toledot of Shem, Ham, and Japheth (10:1-11:9) [Horizontal Genealogy]. 1. Superscription (10:1). 2. Table of nations (10:2-32). 3. Escalation of evil in Babylon (11:1-9). 5. Toledot of Shem (11:10-26) [Vertical Genealogy]. B. Patriarchal Stories. 6. Toledot of Terah (Abraham) (11:27-26:11) [Narrative].

### The Old Testament Story

PATRIARCHS (2200-1550 BCE) - Abraham. EXODUS (ca. 1300 BCE) - Moses - Exodus from Egypt Early occurs under Pharaoh Raamses II (ca. 1290 BC). CONQUEST & SETTLEMENT (1200-1000 BCE) - Joshua - Conquest of Canaan begins under Joshua. UNITED KINGDOM (1020-922 BCE) - David - David conquers Jerusalem and makes it the central capital (ca. 1000 BC) - Solomon - Solomon builds the first Temple and walls of Jerusalem. Division of the united kingdom. Jeroboam I, new king of the north, establishes religious centers at Dan and Bethel to ensure national unity. TWO KINGDOMS (922-721 BCE) - Northern Kingdom of Israel - Destruction of the Northern Kingdom by the Assyrians (721 BC) led by Sargon II. Northern Israel dispersed throughout the Assyrian empire. Southern Kingdom of Judah - Isaiah, Hosea, Hezekiah, Micah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Josiah. JUDAH ALONE (721-587 BCE) - The exile of Judah by the Babylonians under Nebuchadnezzar. This was the darkest time in Israel's history. Jerusalem, and the Temple, are destroyed (ca. 587 BC). BABYLONIAN EXILE (587-539 BCE) - Time of the Prophets: Most of the biblical prophets active during the last days of the kingdom of Judah (Amos, Hosea), and the Kingdom of Judah (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, etc.). Called to covenant faithfulness before it was too late. ANE: Babylon displaces Assyria as the super-power of the ANE. Israel: Despite some high points (Hezekiah, Josiah), Judah follows the kingdom of Israel to apostasy and its exile in Babylon. The Babylonian exile was a time of redefining Israelite faith and religion. Synagogues likely developed during this time, and many biblical books were completed. RETURN & RESTORATION (539-333 BCE) - Ezra, Nehemiah - Under the Persian King Cyrus, Jews return from Babylon. Under Ezra the Temple is rebuilt (called the 'Second Temple'), and Nehemiah directs the rebuilding of the walls of Jerusalem. ANE: The Persians under Cyrus take control of the ANE. Israel: Jews allowed to go home under Cyrus. Judaism emerges from the furnace of the exile. Israel is no longer an independent nation, but a province ('Yehud') of the mighty Persian empire.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal History
The document structures the Old Testament as a series of historical epochs defined by covenants, starting from the creation of heaven and earth, through the patriarchal period, the exodus, the united kingdom, the exile, and the restoration.

### Theme: The Toledot Structure
The outline of Genesis is organized around the 'Toledot' (generations) formula, which serves as a literary framework to transition between primeval and patriarchal narratives.

### Theme: The Role of the Prophets
The prophets are identified as key figures who called the kingdoms of Israel and Judah to covenant faithfulness, particularly during the period of the two kingdoms and the Babylonian exile.
