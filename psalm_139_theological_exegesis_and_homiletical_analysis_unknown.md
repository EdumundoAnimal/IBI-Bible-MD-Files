## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sovereign Immanence
The argument that God's knowledge and presence are not distant, reactive, or judgmental in a mechanical sense, but are essential, sustaining, and intimately woven into the very fabric of human existence from conception.

### Theme: Ontological Teleology
The concept that human value and design are established prior to conscious breath or functional utility, as evidenced by God's role as the 'weaver' of the embryo (rāqam).

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The interpretation of Psalm 139 as finding its ultimate realization in Jesus Christ, whose incarnation, descent into death, and resurrection reconcile the believer to God's presence, transforming 'inescapability' from a threat into a promise of covenantal security.
