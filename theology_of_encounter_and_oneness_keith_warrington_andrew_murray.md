# Theology of Encounter and Oneness
**Author:** [[Keith Warrington / Andrew Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains excerpts from works exploring theological themes, specifically focused on Pentecostalism, the nature of God, the Trinity, and Oneness theology. It features analytical segments on the personhood of God, the relationship between the Father and the Son, and critical perspectives on modalism. Additionally, it contains a segment of an exposition on the book of Hebrews, focusing on the divinity of Christ and his role as the begotten Son of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness Theology vs. Trinitarianism
The text examines the tensions between the Oneness Pentecostal view, which emphasizes a singularity in the Godhead, and traditional Trinitarian orthodoxy, which identifies distinct persons within the Godhead.

### Theme: The Divinity and Humanity of Christ
The discussion revolves around the duality of Jesus, exploring how he is fully divine and fully human, and how his resurrection and role as Son relate to his relationship with the Father.

### Theme: Modalism Critique
The text highlights arguments against modalism, asserting that scriptural evidence for subject-object relationships between the Father and the Son contradicts modalistic interpretations.
