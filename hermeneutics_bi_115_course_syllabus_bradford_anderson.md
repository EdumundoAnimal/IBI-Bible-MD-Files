# Hermeneutics (BI 115) Course Syllabus
**Author:** [[Bradford Anderson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Carraig Eden Theological College Hermeneutics (BI 115) First Semester 2008/09. This course explores the principles of biblical exegesis and contemporary application. The curriculum covers the history of hermeneutics, the grammatical-historical method, literary devices (riddles, fables, allegory, parables), and genre-specific interpretation rules. Assessment includes a 1,250-word exegetical essay on an Old Testament passage and a final comprehensive examination.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Exegesis
The systematic study of biblical texts to determine their original historical, literary, and theological meanings.

### Theme: Hermeneutical Method
The application of the grammatical-historical method and the analysis of literary genres and figures of speech to interpret scripture.

### Theme: Contemporary Application
The process of connecting ancient biblical texts to modern contexts.
