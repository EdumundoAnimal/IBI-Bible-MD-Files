### Theme: Psalm Structure
The use of acrostic forms, chiastic patterns, and parallelism to organize theological expression.

### Theme: Metaphorical Theology
Using imagery such as 'arid land', 'orchid flower', and 'white stallion' to represent human weakness, vulnerability, and the future hope of Christ's return.

### Theme: Stages of Lament and Praise
The progression from a cry of distress and repentance toward ultimate confidence and praise in God's mercy.
