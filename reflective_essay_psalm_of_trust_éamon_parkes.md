## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Psalm Classification
The author categorizes the creative work as a 'psalm of trust' and a 'psalm of lament', identifying a thematic arc that begins with an invocation of need, moves to repentance, and concludes with thanksgiving.

### Theme: Poetic Analysis
The text employs various literary devices including alphabetical acrostics, similes (comparing the soul to arid land and an orchid), and chiasmus to emphasize emotional intensity and structural symmetry.

### Theme: Theological Imagery
The work utilizes imagery related to God's providence, including 'soft summer rain', 'a white dove' as a symbol of forgiveness, and 'a white stallion' representing the eschatological hope of Christ's return.
