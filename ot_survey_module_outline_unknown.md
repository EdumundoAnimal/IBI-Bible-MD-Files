# OT Survey Module Outline
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines an Old Testament survey module covering the historical books from Joshua to Esther. It addresses foundational questions regarding the division of the historical books (primary vs. secondary history), the issues surrounding the division of the kingdom of Israel, the nature of the Babylonian Exile, and the challenges following the end of the exile. It also provides a list of recommended reading materials for further study.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Historical Narrative
Explains the classification of biblical books into primary and secondary history, tracing Israel's national story and development.

### Theme: Exilic and Post-Exilic Context
Analyzes the causes and impacts of the Babylonian Exile on the people of Judah and the subsequent challenges during the restoration period.

### Theme: Theological Development
Examines how the biblical narratives function as an arena for divine revelation and God's relationship with humanity.
