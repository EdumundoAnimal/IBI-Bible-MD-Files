# Theology I - Week 5: Critical Methods and Biblical Understanding
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines various critical methods in biblical studies, including Textual, Literary-source, Form, Redaction, Historical, Comparative-religions, Structural, and Reader-response criticism. It provides background on the Synoptic Gospels and the Gospel of John, specifically discussing the development of oral traditions, the 'Q' source hypothesis, and the distinct theological focuses of each Gospel writer. Tables and comparative analysis are provided regarding the Synoptic versus Johannine accounts of events such as the Last Supper and the cleansing of the Temple, as well as symbolic representations of Christ.
