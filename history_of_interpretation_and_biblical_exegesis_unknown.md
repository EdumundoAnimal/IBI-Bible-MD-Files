## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutical Evolution
The progression of interpretive methodologies from the allegorical focus of the Patristic and Medieval periods to the Reformation's 'sola scriptura', the Post-Reformation's rationalism, and the eventual rise and critique of historical-critical methods.

### Theme: Contextual Interpretation
The necessity of grounding biblical interpretation in historical, geographical, and literary contexts to move beyond superficial reading and engage with the original intent of the text.

### Theme: Theological Synthesis
The modern goal of combining the tools of scientific historical analysis with a devotional, faith-based reading that emphasizes the text as 'Scripture' rather than just a historical artifact.
