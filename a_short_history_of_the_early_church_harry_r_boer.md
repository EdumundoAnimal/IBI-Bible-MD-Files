# A Short History of the Early Church
**Author:** [[Harry R. Boer]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text provides a survey of the early church, focusing on doctrinal controversies like Arianism, Sabellianism, and the Trinitarian debates. It outlines the development of the Nicene Creed, the role of emperors like Constantine, and the significant influence of Church Fathers such as Athanasius and Augustine of Hippo. The text also covers the Pelagian controversy and the defense of the church against Donatism, highlighting the core theological debates that shaped Christian orthodoxy.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarianism
The evolution of the doctrine regarding the nature of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, specifically debating the divinity of Christ and his relationship to the Father (homoousios vs. heteroousios).

### Theme: Church-State Relations
The intertwining of imperial political power with ecclesiastical authority, notably demonstrated by the intervention of Emperor Constantine in church synods and doctrinal disputes.

### Theme: Augustinian Theology
The development of doctrine concerning sin, grace, predestination, and the authority of the church, as formulated in the conflict against Pelagianism.
