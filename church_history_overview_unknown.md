## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Ecclesiastical Division
Exploration of the schisms within Christianity, specifically the 1054 AD Great Schism between the Roman Catholic Church and the Eastern Orthodox Church.

### Theme: Imperial Christianity
The transition of Christianity from a persecuted group to the state religion under Emperor Constantine and the influence of Roman political structures on church hierarchy.

### Theme: Patristics
The study of Church Fathers as a primary source for understanding the development of early Christian doctrine and theology during the post-apostolic era.
