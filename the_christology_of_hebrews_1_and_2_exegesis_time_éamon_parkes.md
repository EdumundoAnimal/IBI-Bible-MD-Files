## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Historical Context of Hebrews
Explores the dating (60 AD), location (Rome), and the socio-political pressure faced by early Christians under the Roman Empire.

### Theme: Threat of Apostasy
Examines the danger of believers abandoning their faith in Christ due to persecution, specifically the temptation to return to Judaism for safety.

### Theme: Forms of Lapsed Christianity
Identifies three primary distractions or deviations: legalistic Judaism, state-run idol worship, and Proto-Gnostic angel worship.

### Theme: Community Decay
Refers to the loss of initial conversion fervor and the subsequent decline in communal support and spiritual growth.
