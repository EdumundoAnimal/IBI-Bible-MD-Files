## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Socio-Religious Context of Hebrews
The author examines the historical necessity of Hebrews, specifically identifying the tension between the recognized status of Judaism and the persecuted, unrecognized status of early Christianity in Rome.

### Theme: Apostasy and Theological Decline
The text identifies the danger of believers abandoning their faith in Christ to escape persecution, categorizing potential departures into legalism, paganism, and Gnostic-influenced angel worship.

### Theme: Community Decay
Drawing on Achtemeier and Green, the analysis highlights the transition from initial conversion-driven communal strength to a state of spiritual stagnation and loss of zeal for service.
