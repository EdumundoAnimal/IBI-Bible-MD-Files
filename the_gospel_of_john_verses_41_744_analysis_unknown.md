# The Gospel of John: Verses 4:1-7:44 Analysis
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of commentary on the Gospel of John, covering verses from chapter 4 through 7. The text provides historical, linguistic, and theological context for the Samaritan woman, the dialogue at the well, the nature of 'living water,' the debate regarding the proper place of worship (Gerizim vs. Jerusalem), and Jesus' invitation during the feast.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Living Water
Symbolic representation of spiritual refreshment and the eternal life offered by Jesus, contrasted with physical water from Jacob's well.

### Theme: Place of Worship
The theological dispute between Samaritans and Jews regarding the legitimate location of worship (Gerizim vs. Jerusalem), resolved by Jesus' teaching that true worship transcends physical location.

### Theme: Messianic Expectation
The identification of Jesus as the Messiah, particularly regarding the Samaritan hope for the 'Taheb' and the Jewish expectation for a prophetic figure.
