## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of the New Covenant
The notes contrast the Old Testament covenant, characterized by limited access, temporary priesthood, and works-based requirements, with the New Covenant, which offers eternal access through Christ as the eternal High Priest based on grace.

### Theme: Hermeneutics and Interpretation
The document references J.L. Austin's 'How to Do Things with Words' and discusses the 'hermeneutics of suspicion' versus seeking meaning in the text, emphasizing the need for interpreters to balance humility and conviction.
