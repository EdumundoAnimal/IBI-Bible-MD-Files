## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The High Priesthood of Christ
The notes emphasize the divine appointment of the High Priest, noting that he is 'Chosen by God not by man,' and highlight the empathetic nature of Jesus' ministry.

### Theme: Motivations for Prayer
The text points to Hebrews 4:14-5:10 as a source for practical motivations for prayer, specifically citing the assurance that one 'will be heard' (Heb 5:7) and the ability of Christ to empathize with human experience.
