# Authorship, Date and Place of The Gospel; The Acts of the Apostles
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an academic overview of the Gospel of John and the Acts of the Apostles. For the Gospel of John, it explores authorship (the 'beloved disciple'), date, and provenance, alongside theological themes such as the divinity of Christ (Logos), the I AM sayings, and the role of the Paraclete. For the Acts of the Apostles, it examines the Luke-Acts literary unity, the historical and theological purpose of the book, and its role as a bridge between the life of Jesus and the established church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Literary Unity of Luke-Acts
Arguments for viewing Luke and Acts as a two-volume work intended to present an orderly narrative of the origins of Christianity and the spread of the church.

### Theme: The Paraclete
The unique theological teaching in John regarding the Holy Spirit's role as Comforter/Counsellor, guiding believers after Christ's departure.

### Theme: Christology in John
Examination of the divinity of Christ (Logos) through the seven 'signs' and the seven 'I AM' sayings which reveal his divine nature.
