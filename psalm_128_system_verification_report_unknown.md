## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Beatitude
The state of blessedness for those who fear YHWH is not defined by emotional states but by objective covenantal alignment and existence under divine favor.

### Theme: Sanctification of Labor
The Psalm reclaims human labor from the curse of futility, framing the enjoyment of one's work as a sign of divine covenantal blessing.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
Psalm 128 is read through the lens of Christ, the ultimate 'fearer' of YHWH, whose finished work on the cross and resurrection secures these blessings for the Church.

### Theme: Epistemic Collision
The report highlights the tension between the ancient 'Psamic Realism' (integrating labor and faith) and the modern 'Secular Autonomy' (which treats labor and family as transactional or burdensome).
