## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Vindication
The central argument that God vindicates the suffering king (David/Christ) not based on absolute merit, but on covenantal faithfulness and integrity, demonstrating the superiority of Yahweh over forces of death and chaos.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
Psalm 18 acts as a prophetic narrative for Jesus Christ, where the suffering of the psalmist prefigures the passion, death, and resurrection of Christ, shifting the text from historical retrospective to Messianic prophecy.

### Theme: Polemical Theophany
The text systematically appropriates and subverts Ancient Near Eastern storm-god myths (like Baal) to assert that Yahweh is the true Sovereign who controls creation, rendering nature and death as mere servants to His will.
