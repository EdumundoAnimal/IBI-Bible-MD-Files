# Old Testament Study Outlines
**Author:** [[Bandstra]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides structural outlines and summary storylines for the books of Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy. It covers key narrative arcs including the exodus from Egypt, the establishment of the Law and tabernacle, the wilderness journey, and the final sermons of Moses in Deuteronomy before his death.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Relationship
The ongoing dynamic between Yahweh and Israel, marked by the giving of the Law, the establishment of the tabernacle, and repeated cycles of rebellion and renewal.

### Theme: Holiness and Purity
The emphasis on maintaining ritual and moral purity within the community, especially regarding the priesthood and sacrificial rites as outlined in Leviticus.

### Theme: Leadership and Succession
The central role of Moses as a mediator between God and the people, and the transition of authority to Joshua to lead the people into the Promised Land.
