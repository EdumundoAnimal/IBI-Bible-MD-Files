## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cosmic Theophany
The Psalm depicts the physical creation responding to the immediate presence of God, framing the Exodus not merely as a human event but as a moment of cosmic upheaval.

### Theme: Christological Re-creation
Intertextual mapping reveals the Psalm's imagery as a precursor to the life, death, and resurrection of Christ, positioning Him as the true Rock and the source of living water.

### Theme: Anti-Imperial Subversion
The text serves as a counter-narrative to imperial claims, reducing political and geographical powers to insignificance before the covenant-making God.
