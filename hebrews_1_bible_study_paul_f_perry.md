# Hebrews 1 Bible Study
**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Lesson 1 Hebrews 1:1-2:4. The New Revelation vs. The Old Revelation. God spoke in the past through the prophets to the fathers, but has now spoken to us in this final age by his Son. Jesus is God's appointed heir and the manifestation of God's prophetic voice. The greatness of Jesus is evidenced by his roles: creative agent, personified glory, perfect revelation, cosmic sustainer, unique sacrifice, and exalted ruler at God's right hand. Hebrews 2:1-4 warns of the consequences of neglecting this great salvation, emphasizing the authority of the message confirmed by signs and wonders.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
Exploration of the divinity and preeminence of Jesus Christ as the Son of God, his role in creation, and his status as the final revelation of God to humanity.

### Theme: Supremacy of the Son
The argument that Jesus is superior to angels and prophets, holding a unique status, function, and dignity that warrants worship.

### Theme: Responsibility of Believers
The ethical and spiritual mandate to pay close attention to the gospel, given its superior origin compared to the old covenant revelation.
