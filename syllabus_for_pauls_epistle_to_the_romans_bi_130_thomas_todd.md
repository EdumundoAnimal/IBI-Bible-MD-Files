# Syllabus for Paul's Epistle to the Romans (BI 130)
**Author:** [[Thomas Todd]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Course Information

Carraig Eden Theological College. Syllabus for Paul's Epistle to the Romans (BI 130). Second Semester 2008/09. Course Instructor: Thomas Todd. Level: 4. Credits: 10. Prerequisites: None. Learning Hours: 100 hours made up as follows: 20 hours lectures, tutorials and seminars. 15 hours of reading assigned material. 40 hours preparation of research paper. 1½ hours examination. 23½ hours revision for examination.

### Aim(s) and Learning Outcomes

Aim(s): The aim of this course is to provide the student with a good understanding of the book of Romans in its historical and cultural setting and its application and relevance for today. On completion of this course, the student will be able to: A. Discuss the background of this epistle such as date of writing, occasion for writing, and the recipients of this epistle. B. Demonstrate understanding of key theological passages in Romans. C. Identify the specific and unique contributions of this letter to the discipline of biblical theology. D. Appreciate more deeply the life, character and ministry of the apostle Paul. E. Understand more fully the way of the cross with the denial of self and the life in the Spirit that is to be discovered in this epistle.

### Teaching and Learning Strategies

Lectures and Seminars, Directed learning

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Historical and Cultural Context
The course aims to ground the study of Romans in its original historical setting, specifically focusing on the date of writing, the occasion for the letter, and the identity of the recipients.

### Theme: Theological Application
The curriculum emphasizes both the academic understanding of key theological passages and the practical application of the 'way of the cross,' 'denial of self,' and 'life in the Spirit' for contemporary believers.
