## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Carraig Eden Theological College Syllabus for Paul's Epistle to the Romans (BI 130). Second Semester 2008/09. Learning outcomes focus on historical context, theological passages, and Paul's ministry. A bibliography of key commentaries is provided. A deadline extension for an assignment is announced, moving the due date to 16 March.
