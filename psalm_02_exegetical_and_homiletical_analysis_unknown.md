**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a comprehensive analysis of Psalm 2, covering its theological themes, linguistic and syntactic structure, historical and cultural background, and canonical trajectory. The psalm is presented as a four-movement dramatic-expository discourse that centers on the sovereignty of God, the rebellion of the nations, the enthronement of the Son, and the call to wisdom. It examines key textual variants, such as the interpretation of 'naššəqû-ḇar' and 'təro‘ēm', and synthesizes views from Patristic, Reformation, and modern scholars. The analysis also explores the existential friction between ancient cosmic order and modern radical autonomy, culminating in a homiletical framework for understanding the Messianic Kingdom.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sovereign Royal Eschatology
The theme focuses on the absolute authority of YHWH and His Anointed King (Christ) over all geopolitical powers, framed within a cosmic battle between divine order and human rebellion.

### Theme: Divine Sonship and Covenantal Adoption
Explores the Davidic covenantal framework where the King is adopted as the Son of God, a status later linked to the Resurrection of Christ and His universal inheritance.

### Theme: Autonomy vs. Cosmic Order
Addresses the tension between the modern secular desire for radical autonomy and the ancient biblical assertion that reality is hierarchical and inherently subject to divine constraints.
