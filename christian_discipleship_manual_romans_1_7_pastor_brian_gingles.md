It outlines the journey of becoming a disciple, emphasizing trust, forgiveness, and death to self. The manual also includes practical reflections, case studies, and a list of ways to praise God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Discipleship vs Conversion
Conversion is the initial acceptance of Christ; discipleship is the ongoing process of intimacy, submission, and maturity.

### Theme: Justification by Faith
Paul argues that justification is credited by faith, independent of law-keeping or human boasting, using Abraham as a case study.

### Theme: The Role of Suffering
Suffering is framed as an intervention that produces perseverance, character, and hope, leading to a deeper reliance on God.

### Theme: Effects of Sin
Sin results in spiritual blindness, separation from God, corrupted motives, and broken relationships, necessitating dependence on Christ's sacrifice.
