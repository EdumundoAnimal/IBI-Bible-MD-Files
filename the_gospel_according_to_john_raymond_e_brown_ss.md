## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Structure of the Gospel
The Gospel is divided into a Prologue, the 'Book of Signs' (revelation to the world), the 'Book of Glory' (revelation to the Father), and an Epilogue.

### Theme: Johannine Community Evolution
The community evolved through phases including conflict with the Jewish establishment and eventual splintering, influenced by the experience of expulsion from synagogues.

### Theme: The Beloved Disciple
Analysis of the anonymous figure who serves as the primary witness, often contrasted with Peter, and the complexity of attributing the text to this individual versus later redactors.

### Theme: Stylistic and Theological Features
Focus on the intimate wedding of style and theology, the use of dualistic imagery (light/darkness), and the specific theological emphasis on Jesus as the Word made flesh.
