# The Gospel According to John
**Author:** [[Raymond E. Brown, S.S.]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as an analytical introduction to the Gospel According to John, exploring its stylistic features, structure, composition history, and theological themes. Key sections include the Prologue, The Book of Signs, and The Book of Glory. The author discusses the Johannine community, its sources, and its relationship to the Synoptic Gospels, alongside the identity of the Beloved Disciple. The text also addresses issues for reflection, such as the polemics against 'the Jews' and internal tensions within the community.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine Dualism
The Gospel is characterized by a dualistic worldview, contrasting light and darkness, truth and falsehood, and heavenly versus earthly realities.

### Theme: Christology
Exploration of the divinity of Jesus, his preexistence as the Word (Logos), and his eventual glorification through death and resurrection.

### Theme: Johannine Community History
A multi-stage reconstruction of the community's development, including its tension with Judaism, internal schisms, and theological evolution.

### Theme: The Beloved Disciple
The enigmatic figure identified as an eyewitness and the source of the Gospel tradition, often contrasted with Peter.
