# The Epistle to the Hebrews: A Commentary on the Greek Text
**Author:** [[Paul Ellingworth]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume provides a comprehensive commentary on the Greek text of the Epistle to the Hebrews, covering historical background, authorship questions, the use of the Old Testament, structural analysis, and key theological themes such as Christology and the role of the Holy Spirit.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
Explores the centrality of Christ in the Epistle to the Hebrews, specifically his role as high priest, his superiority to angels, and his human and divine nature.

### Theme: Authorship
Examines the historical debates regarding the authorship of Hebrews, noting the uncertainty of attributing it to Paul and evaluating other hypotheses like Barnabas, Apollos, or Silas.

### Theme: Salvation and Eschatology
Analyzes the writer's urgent call to perseverance, the warning against apostasy, and the understanding of salvation as both a present reality and a future hope.
