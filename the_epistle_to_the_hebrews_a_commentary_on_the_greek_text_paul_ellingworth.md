# The Epistle to the Hebrews: A Commentary on the Greek Text
**Author:** [[Paul Ellingworth]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume is a detailed academic commentary on the Epistle to the Hebrews, specifically focusing on the Greek text. It covers authorship, destination, date, canonization, and theological themes including Christology, the Holy Spirit, the Church, and the person and work of Christ. The text explores the background of the epistle, including influences from Gnosticism, Philo, and Qumran, and provides a structured literary analysis of the epistle. It also addresses the use of the Old Testament within Hebrews and includes a comprehensive section on the textual criticism of the Greek manuscript evidence.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The epistle emphasizes Christ's superiority over angels, His role as the High Priest in the order of Melchizedek, and His incarnation as a fulfillment of God's purpose for mankind.

### Theme: Literary Structure
The commentary analyzes the epistle as a complex rhetorical work, noting its structural shifts between doctrinal exposition and moral exhortation.

### Theme: Use of Old Testament
Hebrews utilizes the Old Testament primarily through the Septuagint (LXX), reinterpreting Jewish covenantal categories in light of the New Covenant in Christ.
