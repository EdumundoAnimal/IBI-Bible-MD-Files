# Spiritual Alignment and Effectiveness Model
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document presents a Venn diagram model designed to identify 'Greatest Alignment & Effectiveness' through the intersection of three key domains: Spiritual Gifts, Passions, and Talents. Spiritual Gifts are defined as being given by the Spirit and developed through experiences and training. Passions are defined as what excites you and where you find real (not perceived) motivation. Talents are described as learned skills: what you do well but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion. The model advises to focus on how God has created and developed you to contribute most significantly and to release those things that you do well but not best, or those that sap your energy.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Alignment
The concept that personal effectiveness is maximized at the intersection of spiritual gifting, personal passion, and learned talents.

### Theme: Stewardship of Energy
The principle of identifying tasks that 're-charge' an individual versus those that 'sap' energy, advocating for the delegation or release of the latter.

### Theme: God-Given Purpose
The theological argument that individuals should focus their vocational and ministerial efforts on how God uniquely created and developed them.
