**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an introduction to Biblical Hermeneutics, defining it as the art and science of interpretation. It emphasizes that while the Bible is God's word, there are four 'distances' that act as obstacles to our understanding: the distance of time, the distance of culture, the distance of geography, and the distance of language. To overcome these, one must analyze the historical, literary, and theological contexts. The document uses Jeremiah 29:11 as a case study for applying these contexts, explores the background of Zechariah 7, and includes an exercise distinguishing between 'permanent' and 'temporary' biblical commands.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutics
Defined as the art and science of interpretation, focusing on understanding the intent and meaning of a message.

### Theme: The Four Distances
The analytical framework of time, culture, geography, and language that separates the contemporary reader from the original biblical context.

### Theme: Contextual Analysis
The methodology of using historical, literary, and theological contexts to arrive at a faithful interpretation of scripture.
