# The IVP Dictionary of the New Testament: Gospel of John
**Author:** [[Daniel G. Reid]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The IVP Dictionary of the New Testament provides an in-depth examination of the Gospel of John, covering its origin, structure, genre, and theology. The Gospel is analyzed in the context of its second-generation witnesses, addressing the relationship between early Christians and Jews, the role of the Holy Spirit, and the purpose of the Gospel as stated in John 20:31. The text explores themes of mission, unity, and the Christian community's interaction with the 'world' (kosmos). Structure is divided between the Book of Signs and the Book of Glory. Extensive bibliography of commentaries is included.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Purpose of the Gospel
The Gospel is intended as an evangelistic document to encourage belief that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, providing life through his name.

### Theme: Unity in the Community
A central theological theme is the unity of believers modeled on the relationship between the Father and the Son, characterized by shared purpose and love.

### Theme: The World (Kosmos)
The world is portrayed in multiple senses: the created order, the sphere of humanity, and a force rejecting God, which the church must bear witness to.
