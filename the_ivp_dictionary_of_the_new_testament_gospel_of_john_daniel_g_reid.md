# The IVP Dictionary of the New Testament: Gospel of John
**Author:** [[Daniel G. Reid]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The Gospel of John is examined for its origins, structure, genre, and theology. It explores the needs of the Johannine community, the purpose of the Gospel as stated in John 20:31, and the theological emphasis on unity, the mission of the church, and the relationship between the Father and the Son. The text also includes an extensive bibliography of scholarly commentaries.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Purpose of the Gospel
The Gospel of John functions as an evangelistic document designed to strengthen faith and encourage perseverance, specifically citing John 20:31 as the primary statement of intent.

### Theme: Unity in Johannine Theology
A central theme of the Gospel is the unity of believers, modeled after the relationship between the Father and the Son, intended to testify to the world.

### Theme: Johannine Community Context
The Gospel reflects a community transitioning from Jewish roots, dealing with ostracism from the synagogue and defined by its relationship to the surrounding world.
