# General Epistles and Johannine Epistles Analysis
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an overview and analysis of the General Epistles (James, 1 & 2 Peter, Jude) and the Johannine Epistles (1, 2, & 3 John). It covers historical background, authorship questions, internal contents, themes, and challenges such as proto-Gnostic heresy, false teachers, and the nature of salvation and faith.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Faith and Works
Explores the tension between Pauline teaching on justification by faith and the emphasis on works in James, arguing they are complementary rather than contradictory; true faith manifests in action.

### Theme: Proto-Gnostic Heresy
Identifies early challenges in the Johannine epistles concerning the denial of Christ's true humanity, specifically addressing Docetism.

### Theme: Christian Conduct and Suffering
Emphasizes the necessity of perseverance through trials and suffering, and the call to holy living and moral purity in the face of external and internal threats.
