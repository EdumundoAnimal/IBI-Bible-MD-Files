### Theme: High Priesthood of Christ
Exploration of the divine appointment of Jesus as High Priest and his empathetic nature towards humanity.

### Theme: The Efficacy of Prayer
The theological argument that believers can approach God through prayer because of Christ's mediation and willingness to hear.
