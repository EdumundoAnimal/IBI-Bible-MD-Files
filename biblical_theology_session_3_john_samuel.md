# Biblical Theology: Session 3
**Author:** [[John Samuel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Irish Bible Institute - Expository Preaching - Session 3 - Biblical Theology. 1. What are the main, basic kinds of theology? Systematic theology covers what Christians believe now about the faith. Historical theology records the development of Christian doctrine over time. Pastoral theology focuses on growth and care in the local church. Biblical/Exegetical theology explores how God progressively revealed Himself within Scripture. 2. Biblical Theology. 'Helicopter' theology provides a map of the whole story of the Bible. Progressive theology respects the development of the Bible over time, especially from Old Testament to New Testament. Christ-centred theology relates the whole of the Bible to Jesus Christ and his Cross. Colossians 1:15-20 examines Christ as the visible creator, head, and the means of incarnational redemption.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Types of Theology
A classification of theological study into four distinct categories: Systematic, Historical, Pastoral, and Biblical/Exegetical.

### Theme: Biblical Theology Frameworks
Interpretive lenses for understanding the Bible including 'Helicopter' (holistic), Progressive (chronological development), and Christ-centred (focal point on Jesus).

### Theme: Christology in Colossians
An analysis of Colossians 1:15-20 highlighting Christ's roles as the visible creator, the head of the church, and the agent of incarnational redemption.
