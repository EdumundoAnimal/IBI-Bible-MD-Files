## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Providence vs. Autonomous Striving
The document argues that human effort, when detached from divine ordination, is fundamentally futile (shaw'). It contrasts the 'Autonomous Mode' of self-reliance with the 'Covenant Mode' of trust in God's sovereign governance.

### Theme: Covenantal Teleology
The text posits that children are not economic liabilities but a 'nakhalah' (heritage/grace-gift) from God, serving as 'arrows' for the defense of the family and the community in the judicial gate.
