# The Present-Day Christological Debate
**Author:** [[Klaas Runia]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text reviews Klaas Runia's 'The Present-Day Christological Debate', an evaluation of contemporary Christological theories. It categorizes theologians into groups including those maintaining Chalcedonian orthodoxy, those seeking to go 'beyond' it, and those challenging the compatibility of human and divine attributes. The review notes how Runia addresses the historical Jesus, the authority of Scripture, and the doctrines of the Trinity and Incarnation. It also touches on Richard Shaull's 'Heralds of a New Reformation', which discusses economic inequities and the role of the poor in North and South America.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Chalcedonian Orthodoxy
The theological adherence to the definition of Christ as possessing both full divinity and full humanity, which some modern theologians challenge as incompatible.

### Theme: Christological Divergence
The categorization of modern theologians into those remaining within traditional orthodoxy and those attempting to move 'beyond' classical formulas.

### Theme: Socio-Economic Theology
The critique of global economic disparities and the call for structural change as presented in the context of a new reformation.
