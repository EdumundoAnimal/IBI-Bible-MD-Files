# The Present-Day Christological Debate
**Author:** [[Klaas Runia]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document contains book reviews of 'The Present-Day Christological Debate' by Klaas Runia and 'Heralds of a New Reformation: The Poor of South and North America' by Richard Shaull. The review of Runia's work examines contemporary Christological theories, categorizing theologians into groups based on their adherence to or departure from the Chalcedonian dogma. The text highlights a division between those who maintain traditional Christology and those who seek 'beyond' the traditional formulas. The review of Shaull's book addresses the economic injustices impacting the global poor and calls for a radical restructuring of the capitalist system in light of biblical imperatives.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Categorization
The text classifies 20th-century theologians into those who uphold the traditional Chalcedonian doctrine of Christ's divinity and humanity, and those who seek to move 'beyond' these definitions, viewing them as outdated or incompatible with modern thought.

### Theme: Economic Justice as Theology
The review of Richard Shaull emphasizes a theological obligation to address global economic inequities, framing the 'haves' and 'have-nots' divide as a moral crisis requiring a radical shift from current capitalist models.
