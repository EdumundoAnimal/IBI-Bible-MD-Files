# The Present-Day Christological Debate
**Author:** [[Klaas Runia]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text reviews Klaas Runia's 'The Present-Day Christological Debate', which examines contemporary Christologies. Runia categorizes theologians into three groups: those adhering to Chalcedonian orthodoxy, those seeking to go beyond Chalcedon, and those abandoning it entirely. The text highlights significant figures such as Barth, Tillich, Bultmann, Pannenberg, Moltmann, Schillebeeckx, and Kung, while addressing the tension between traditional divinity of Christ and modern theological revisions.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Orthodoxy
The defense of the Chalcedonian definition of the two natures of Christ against modern theological revisions that seek to abandon or significantly alter this traditional framework.

### Theme: Theological Categorization
The systematic division of modern theologians into groups based on their relationship to the Chalcedonian creed, ranging from strict adherence to rejection.

### Theme: Bible and Doctrine
The tension between biblical authority and the historical-critical method in modern theology, particularly regarding the divinity and incarnation of Christ.
