# Romans: Verse by Verse
**Author:** [[William R. Newell]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a verse-by-verse exposition of the Book of Romans, specifically focusing on chapters 6, 7, and 8. The author emphasizes the distinction between the believer's standing in Christ, the struggle against indwelling sin, and the liberation provided by the Holy Spirit. Key theological themes include the death of the believer to the Law of Moses, the distinction between the 'old man' and the new identity in Christ, and the rejection of legalism in favor of life in the Spirit.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Believer's Relation to the Law
The text argues that through the death of Christ, the believer is no longer under the Law of Moses but is joined to the Risen Christ, moving from the oldness of the letter to the newness of the Spirit.

### Theme: The Struggle with Indwelling Sin
An analysis of Romans 7 describing the internal conflict of the believer where 'the flesh' remains, yet the believer has been set free from the Law's condemnation.

### Theme: The Ministry of the Holy Spirit
Exploration of Romans 8, emphasizing that the Holy Spirit provides the power for victory over the flesh and secures the believer's standing in Christ.
