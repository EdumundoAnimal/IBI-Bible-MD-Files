# Romans: Verse by Verse
**Author:** [[William R. Newell]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a verse-by-verse commentary on the Book of Romans, specifically focusing on chapters 6, 7, and 8. The text explores Paul's explanation of the believer's standing in Christ, the struggle with indwelling sin, and the liberation found in the Holy Spirit. Newell argues that the believer is dead to the Law and sin, having been justified by the blood of Christ. He emphasizes the distinction between the regenerate man's walk in the Spirit and the unregenerate man under the bondage of the Law. The commentary provides theological analysis on concepts such as justification, the legal principle of the Law, the indwelling Spirit, and the triumph of faith in Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification
The author asserts that justification is the foundation of Christian theology, achieved through the atoning blood of Christ and not through adherence to the Law.

### Theme: Indwelling Sin
The struggle described in Romans 7 is analyzed as the internal conflict of a believer who is no longer under the Law but still possesses an old nature that wars against the new spirit.

### Theme: The Law
The text argues that the believer has been released from the Law of Moses through identification with Christ's death, emphasizing that the Law was not meant to provide life but to reveal sin.

### Theme: The Role of the Holy Spirit
The Holy Spirit is presented as the power for victory in the believer's life, empowering the individual to walk in holiness and overcome the power of indwelling sin.
