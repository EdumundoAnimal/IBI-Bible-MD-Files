## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a sermon study titled The Spirit who is not a ghost by Gordon Curley. The text provides an overview of the Holy Spirit, arguing for his status as a person rather than just a force. It emphasizes the Holy Spirit's role in the lives of believers, the importance of being filled with the Spirit, and symbolic representations found in the Bible. Key themes include the Trinity, the Holy Spirit's personality and characteristics, the concept of being indwelt by the Spirit, and the practical application of spiritual living as commanded in the New Testament.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Holy Spirit as a Person
The text argues that the Holy Spirit is not merely an influence or a ghost but possesses personhood, including a mind, will, and the ability to feel and be grieved.

### Theme: Filling of the Spirit
Central to the sermon is the command to be filled with the Spirit, which is described as an ongoing, plural, and passive experience for all Christians, characterized by submission and holiness.

### Theme: Symbolism and Names
The author explores various biblical names and symbols for the Holy Spirit, such as wind, fire, and water, to elucidate his multifaceted work and character.
