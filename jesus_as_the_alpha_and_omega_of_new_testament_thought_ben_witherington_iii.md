# Jesus as the Alpha and Omega of New Testament Thought
**Author:** [[Ben Witherington III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article explores the centrality of Jesus Christ in New Testament thought, focusing on Pauline letters and the broader early Christian context. It highlights how the historical Jesus and his teachings, death, and resurrection became the interpretative lens through which the early church understood Jewish scripture, eschatology, and the nature of God. The text examines Christology, soteriology, ecclesiology, and the transformative power of the 'Christ event' on the early believers' worldview, arguing that Jesus was not merely a historical figure but the definitive foundation of early Christian faith and life.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christ-centered Theology
The author argues that Jesus Christ is the primary focus of the New Testament, functioning as the Alpha and Omega of Christian theological framework and identity.

### Theme: Eschatological Transformation
Early Christians viewed the world and history through the lens of the resurrection, believing that the end-times hope was inaugurated in the person of Jesus.

### Theme: Reinterpretation of Judaism
Paul and other early writers transformed Jewish concepts like 'messiah', 'law', and 'people of God' based on their experience of Jesus.
