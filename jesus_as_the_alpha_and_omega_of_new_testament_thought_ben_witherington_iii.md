# Jesus as the Alpha and Omega of New Testament Thought
**Author:** [[Ben Witherington III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text explores the central position of Jesus Christ in New Testament thought, focusing on the historical and religious context of early Judaism. The author argues that Christology, eschatology, and ecclesiology are deeply integrated into the early Christian understanding of the Christ event. Key themes include the narrative shape of Paul's Christology, the identification of Jesus as the human face of God, the redefined concept of salvation and the people of God, and the foundational role of Christ in early Christian worship and experience.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christ-centered Theology
The argument that the Christ event (his life, death, and resurrection) is the fundamental focal point that redefines Jewish categories of God, salvation, and the community of faith.

### Theme: Pauline Christology
An analysis of how Paul integrates various Christological traditions, including pre-existence, incarnation, and exaltation, to understand Jesus as the Messiah and Lord within a monotheistic framework.

### Theme: Eschatological Transformation
The conviction that the coming of Christ inaugurated the 'already' of the new creation, transforming Jewish understandings of time, history, and the hope of resurrection.
