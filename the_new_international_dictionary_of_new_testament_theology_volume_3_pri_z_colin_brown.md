## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Remain (Meno)
Explored as a verb signifying staying, waiting, or holding a state. Theologically, it relates to the believer's continuous relationship with God and Christ, emphasizing constancy, faithfulness, and the negation of transient worldly existence.

### Theme: Vine and Wine
Analyzed through both agricultural and metaphorical lenses. The vine is a symbol of Israel and Christ, while wine signifies joy, blessing, or, conversely, judgment and wrath depending on the biblical context.

### Theme: Christology
The document examines the relationship between Christ and the believer, often articulating this through the concept of 'remaining' in Christ, which is foundational to the believer's state of grace.
