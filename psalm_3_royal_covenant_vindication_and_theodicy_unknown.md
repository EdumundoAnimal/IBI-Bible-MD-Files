# Psalm 3: Royal Covenant Vindication and Theodicy
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Psalm 3 is analyzed as a royal lament reflecting David's crisis during Absalom's rebellion (2 Sam 15-18). The study examines linguistic nuances of the Masoretic Text, the structural chiastic composition, and the canonical placement within the Psalter. It maps the historical trajectory from David's flight to Christ's passion, highlighting themes of covenantal security, theodicy, and divine vindication.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Royal Covenant Vindication
The argument that David's suffering and subsequent restoration serve as a paradigm for covenant survival, demonstrating that the Davidic covenant is indestructible.

### Theme: Theodicy in Crisis
The struggle of reconciling empirical reality (the king's flight and mockery) with the promise of divine presence, resolved through prayer and theological realignment.

### Theme: Eschatological Morning of Resurrection
The interpretation of David's sleep and awakening as a prophetic archetype of Christ's death and resurrection, shifting the psalm's focus to ultimate cosmic victory.
