# The Circles of the Believer's Life
**Author:** [[Rodelio Mallari]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The sermon presents a hierarchical model of Christian commitment described as five concentric circles. 1. The Circle of Faith (The Five Hundred) representing all saved believers. 2. The Circle of Service (The Seventy) representing those active in ministry. 3. The Circle of Fellowship (The Twelve) representing those in close ministry relationship with Christ. 4. The Circle of Privilege (The Three: Peter, James, and John) representing those chosen for critical encounters with Christ's deity and suffering. 5. The Circle of Love (The Beloved Disciple, John) representing total dedication and intimate love for Jesus. The conclusion challenges the reader to assess their current level of commitment and prioritize a personal relationship with Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Degrees of Christian Commitment
The author argues that Christian life is categorized by varying levels of closeness to Jesus, moving from general belief to intimate love.

### Theme: Formula for Maturity
The author posits that spiritual maturity is achieved through a combination of faith, service, and fellowship.
