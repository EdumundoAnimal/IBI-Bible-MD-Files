# The Circles of the Believer's Life
**Author:** [[Rodelio Mallari]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The Circles of the Believer's Life by Rodelio Mallari. The article outlines five spiritual circles that define levels of Christian commitment and relationship with Jesus: 1. The Circle of Faith (The Five Hundred) representing all believers saved by grace. 2. The Circle of Service (The Seventy) representing those commissioned for specific work. 3. The Circle of Fellowship (The Twelve) representing those in close ministry and intimate fellowship with Jesus. 4. The Circle of Privilege (The Three: Peter, James, and John) representing those chosen for highly significant spiritual experiences. 5. The Circle of Love (The Beloved Disciple/John) representing those who embody profound, sacrificial love for Jesus. The conclusion challenges the reader to move beyond mere belief into active service, fellowship, and love for Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Progressive Sanctification
The author argues that there are escalating degrees of Christian devotion, starting from general salvation (faith) to intimate sacrifice (love).

### Theme: The Theology of Presence
The text suggests that closeness to Jesus is marked by participation in specific events in His life and ministry.
