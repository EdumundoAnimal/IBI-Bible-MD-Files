# The Circles of the Believer's Life
**Author:** [[Rodelio Mallari]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article outlines five levels of spiritual commitment and relationship with Jesus Christ, categorized as circles. 1. The Circle of Faith (The 500): Represents all saved believers who have faith but may lack zeal. 2. The Circle of Service (The 70): Represents those chosen for active ministry work, requiring courage. 3. The Circle of Fellowship (The 12): Those who share a deeper ministry and personal bond with Jesus. 4. The Circle of Privilege (The 3): Peter, James, and John, who were selected for critical, intimate moments of Jesus' ministry. 5. The Circle of Love (John): The deepest level characterized by profound, sacrificial love for the Savior. The author concludes by challenging the reader to assess which circle they occupy and to prioritize a personal relationship with Jesus.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Hierarchy
The text proposes a progressive model of Christian growth, moving from general faith to intimate fellowship and sacrificial love.

### Theme: Christian Zeal
The author distinguishes between 'saved' believers and 'zealous' servants, arguing that mere salvation is the starting point, not the goal.
