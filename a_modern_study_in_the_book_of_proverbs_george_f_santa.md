# A Modern Study in the Book of Proverbs
**Author:** [[George F. Santa]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a study on Proverbs, specifically chapters 10 and 11. It explores theological themes regarding the strength of the righteous versus the fate of the wicked, the necessity of divine trust, and the moral implications of speech and business practices. It incorporates commentary on biblical passages, emphasizing spiritual maturity, the 'gracious tongue', and the standard of divine integrity in daily life.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Resilience
The argument that the righteous draw strength from divine power rather than self-sufficiency, enabling them to remain steadfast amidst hardships.

### Theme: Integrity and Ethics
The assertion that religious devotion must manifest in honest business dealings, specifically rejecting deceptive practices like false balances.

### Theme: The Power of Speech
An analysis of the 'gracious tongue' as a indicator of wisdom, emphasizing that words should reflect God's truth and avoid perversion.
