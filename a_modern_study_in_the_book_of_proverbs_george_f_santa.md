# A Modern Study in The Book of Proverbs
**Author:** [[George F. Santa]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text provides a verse-by-verse commentary and devotional study of The Book of Proverbs. Chapter 29 focuses on spiritual strength and the righteousness of the godly versus the wickedness of those who will not endure. Chapter 30-31 discusses the stability of the righteous, the contrast between the righteous and the wicked, and the nature of the gracious tongue. Chapter 11 focuses on the importance of integrity in business and communication, emphasizing that God values honesty over human praise.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Resilience
The author explores the idea that righteousness provides an unshakeable foundation, contrasting the endurance of the godly with the instability of the wicked.

### Theme: Ethical Communication
A focus on 'The Gracious Tongue' and 'Testimony Above Praise,' emphasizing that how one speaks and conducts business is a moral reflection of one's relationship with God.
