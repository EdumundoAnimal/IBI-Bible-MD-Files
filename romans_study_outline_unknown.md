## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification by Faith
Explores the theological transition from righteousness through law to righteousness through faith as established in the Epistle to the Romans.

### Theme: Benefits of Justification
An analysis of the practical and eternal outcomes of being justified, including psychological peace, divine relationship, and future hope.
