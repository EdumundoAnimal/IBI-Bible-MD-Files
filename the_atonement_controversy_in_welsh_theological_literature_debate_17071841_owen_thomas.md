# The Atonement Controversy in Welsh Theological Literature & Debate 1707–1841
**Author:** [[Owen Thomas]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the theological evolution of Welsh Calvinism between 1707 and 1841. It details the transition from traditional Calvinist doctrines of the atonement toward more liberal, Arminian, and modernistic interpretations. Key figures such as Lewis Edwards and John Elias are analyzed within the context of the internal and external pressures influencing the Welsh Methodist movement, including the impact of Higher Criticism, revivalism, and the introduction of American evangelical methods.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Calvinism vs. Arminianism
The tension between the traditional doctrine of particular redemption (Limited Atonement) and the emerging influence of universal atonement (Arminianism) within Welsh theological circles.

### Theme: Theological Modernism
The impact of Higher Criticism, liberal education, and new philosophical ideas that shifted Welsh theological thought away from rigid, confessional orthodoxies.

### Theme: Ecclesiastical Authority
The role of the 'Association' (specifically the Bala Association) in managing and attempting to suppress theological disagreements among ministers.
