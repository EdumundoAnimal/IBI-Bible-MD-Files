## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cosmic Doxology
The concept that the entire created order, from celestial beings to inanimate nature, is intrinsically designed to praise the Creator.

### Theme: Creation-Covenant Continuity
The theological framework connecting the sovereignty of God as Creator with His particular redemptive grace toward the covenant community.

### Theme: Christological Recapitulation
The identification of Jesus Christ as the 'Horn of Salvation' who reconciles all things, fulfilling the covenant promises of the Old Testament.

### Theme: Anti-Autonomous Ontology
The refutation of human self-sufficiency, placing humanity as a fellow-creature among other creatures, all subject to the sovereign decree of God.
