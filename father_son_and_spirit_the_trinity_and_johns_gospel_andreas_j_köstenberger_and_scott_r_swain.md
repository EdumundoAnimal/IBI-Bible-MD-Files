# Father, Son and Spirit: The Trinity and John's Gospel
**Author:** [[Andreas J. Köstenberger and Scott R. Swain]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text serves as a concluding theological synthesis regarding the relationship between the triune God and the life of the believer as presented in the Fourth Gospel. The authors argue that eternal life is a relational gift mediated by the indwelling Holy Spirit, connecting the believer to the Father through the Son. It emphasizes the mission of the Son and Spirit, noting that the ultimate goal is the glorification of the Father. The analysis covers the role of the Spirit in guiding believers to the truth, the salvific importance of Jesus' crucifixion as a glorification, and the missional calling of the church to continue the work of Christ. The text concludes by framing trinitarian theology as a basis for the Christian life and the ultimate glorification of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Relationality
The authors posit that the Godhead is characterized by unity among persons and eternal relationships, which the believer enters through the gift of the Spirit.

### Theme: Salvation as Participation
Salvation is not just an escape from death, but a relational participation in the gracious overflow of the Father's love for the Son.

### Theme: Missional Theology
The mission of the Son and the Spirit is designed to bring glory to the Father, and this mission continues through the church's witness in the world.
