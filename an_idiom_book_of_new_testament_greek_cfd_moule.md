# An Idiom Book of New Testament Greek
**Author:** [[C.F.D. Moule]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text discusses grammatical nuances of New Testament Greek, specifically focusing on the usage of participles and the impact of the definite article on nouns. It provides critical commentary on various verses (e.g., John xv, Acts xix, 2 Corinthians iv, Romans iv) to illustrate how grammatical choices affect meaning. The excerpt examines the difference between phrases with and without the definite article, the role of participles in subordination, and the influence of Semitic styles on New Testament Greek.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Participle Syntax
Examination of how participles function in Greek, particularly regarding subordination and the variation in meaning when a participle is or is not used with an article.

### Theme: Semantic Influence
The argument that certain stylistic choices in the New Testament, such as the use of 'hanging' nominative participles, may be attributed to Semitic linguistic influence.

### Theme: Grammatical Precision
Analysis of the definite article's function as a marker of quality or exclusivity versus generic reference within the Greek text.
