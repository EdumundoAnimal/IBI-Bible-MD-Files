# An Idiom Book of New Testament Greek
**Author:** [[C.F.D. Moule]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text discusses grammatical nuances of New Testament Greek, specifically focusing on the usage of participles and the function of the definite article. It explores how noun and verbal functions overlap, citing examples from the Gospel of John and other epistles. The author examines cases where the presence or absence of the article alters the emphasis or meaning, specifically regarding how nouns and participles act as distinct modifiers. It also touches upon semitic influences and the usage of negative constructions with participles, referencing scholarly debates and cross-referencing other grammatical authorities.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Participle Grammar
Analysis of the functional difference between nominal and verbal participles in New Testament Greek, particularly when modified by the definite article.

### Theme: Translation Nuance
A critique of how modern English translations (A.V., R.V.) interpret Greek syntax, specifically regarding the theological implications of specific grammatical structures like the 'anarthrous' versus 'arthrous' phrase.

### Theme: Semitic Influence
The observation of how Semitic linguistic patterns affected the Greek of the New Testament, leading to unique stylistic or syntactical choices.
