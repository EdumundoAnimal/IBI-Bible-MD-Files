# New Testament Introduction: The Epistle to the Hebrews
**Author:** [[Donald Guthrie]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text provides a scholarly introduction to the Epistle to the Hebrews. It details the superiority of Christianity and Christ over Old Testament figures and systems, including angels, Moses, and the Aaronic priesthood. It further maps out the rhetorical structure of the epistle, contrasting the old covenant with the new, and provides thematic exhortations for believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of Christ
Christ is presented as superior to angels, Moses, and the Levitical priesthood, serving as the mediator of a new and better covenant.

### Theme: New Covenant
The argument that the new covenant established by Christ is permanent and superior to the obsolete old covenant system.

### Theme: Exhortation to Perseverance
The document emphasizes the need for Christian endurance, warning against apostasy and encouraging believers to maintain their faith.
