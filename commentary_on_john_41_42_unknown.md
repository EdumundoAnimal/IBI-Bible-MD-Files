# Commentary on John 4:1-42
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This analysis focuses on the Johannine account of Jesus and the Samaritan woman. It explores themes of theological tension, the symbolism of living water, and the historical context of Jewish-Samaritan relations. The passage highlights Jesus as the fulfillment of Jewish promises, challenging the exclusivity of both the Jerusalem temple and Mount Gerizim. The commentary details the transition from traditional religious sites to a new form of worship in Spirit and truth, emphasizing the universal nature of salvation offered by Jesus.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Living Water
Symbolic representation of Jesus' life-giving message and the gift of the Holy Spirit, contrasting with limited traditional water sources.

### Theme: Universalism vs. Particularism
Jesus transcends the historical, ethnic, and religious boundary disputes between Jews and Samaritans by declaring that salvation is not tied to a specific location.

### Theme: Jewish-Samaritan Relations
The complex cultural and religious prejudices, including notions of impurity and historical rivalry over worship sites, that contextualize the encounter.
