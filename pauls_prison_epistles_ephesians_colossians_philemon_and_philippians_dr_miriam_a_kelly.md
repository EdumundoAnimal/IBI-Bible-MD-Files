## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Church as the Body of Christ
The epistle to the Ephesians emphasizes the universal nature of the church, with Christ as its head and the unifying force that abolishes divisions between Jews and Gentiles.

### Theme: Christ's Pre-eminence
In Colossians, Paul asserts that Christ is head of all creation, and all authority is subject to Him, refuting heretical 'elemental spirits' or Gnostic tendencies.

### Theme: Christian Forgiveness and Reconciliation
In Philemon, Paul models the application of the Gospel to social structures, specifically slavery, by urging Philemon to accept Onesimus as a 'dear brother' rather than a slave.

### Theme: Joy in Suffering
Philippians highlights Paul's capacity to maintain joy and contentment through the Gospel, even while imprisoned, rooted in his single-minded devotion to Christ.
