# Seminar Evaluation: Christology
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Characteristics of Wisdom Psalms: More horizontally than vertically oriented; Didactic in nature; Frequently contains a blessing; Contrasts the lifestyle of the righteous with the wicked. Wisdom is the skill of living. Parallel literature. --- Christology Seminar Evaluation by Patrick Mitchel, IBI 2010-11: Eamon, well done on a very well prepared seminar. You had good rapport with the class and managed to cover a lot of ground in a short space of time, while involving the class in useful and relevant discussion. You decided to read the text, which meant that you covered everything thoroughly, but was a wee bit more inflexible in engagement and interaction. Your engagement with different perspectives was helpful, if at times leaving little room to explain views in more depth - such as Bauckham and Dunn for example. Your broader approach meant that actual time in the text was limited - this is an observation more than a criticism, a seminar can't do everything. But perhaps a little too quickly surveying the Christology of Hebrews 1-2 in broad strokes. You brought out well the creative tension in Hebrews of Jesus' humanity and deity and how this is connected to his priesthood. The theme of perfection could have been emphasized a bit more I think. I enjoyed your very creative and imaginative 'application' of contemporary 'drift' from faith to IBI students in our Western context. And this was well connected to the text. Well done. The discussion questions were a little hard to grasp. Perhaps you could have made a little bit more of the subverting empire' theme that you raised. Overall, a very good seminar; good content and application, showing awareness of different views and creative engagement with a good variety of reading.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Wisdom Theology
Defined as 'the skill of living,' wisdom literature is characterized by a horizontal orientation, didactic nature, the inclusion of blessings, and moral contrasts.

### Theme: Christological Analysis
Exploration of the creative tension in the Book of Hebrews regarding the humanity and deity of Jesus and its theological connection to his priestly role.

### Theme: Hermeneutical Application
The pedagogical necessity of bridging ancient biblical texts (like Hebrews) with contemporary cultural issues, such as 'drift' from faith and subverting imperial ideologies.
