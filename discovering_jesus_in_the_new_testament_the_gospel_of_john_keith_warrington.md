# Discovering Jesus in the New Testament: The Gospel of John
**Author:** [[Keith Warrington]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is Chapter 2 of the book 'Discovering Jesus in the New Testament', which focuses on the Gospel of John. The text examines the unique christological perspectives of the Fourth Gospel, emphasizing the divinity of Jesus, his role as the eternal Son of God, and the specific signs and miracles that testify to his identity. It covers major theological concepts including the Logos, the Son of God, the Son of Man, the Messiah, and the Giver of the Spirit, while also discussing the purpose of signs and the narrative of Jesus's death and resurrection.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Uniqueness
The Gospel of John presents a distinct portrait of Jesus that highlights his pre-existence as the eternal Word (Logos) and his full divinity, differing from the Synoptic Gospels by focusing on his role as the Son of God.

### Theme: The Function of Signs
Miracles in the Gospel of John serve not just as acts of compassion, but as specific signs intended to lead observers to recognize Jesus as the Savior and the Son of God, aiming to foster faith.

### Theme: The Logos
Utilizing the philosophical term 'Logos', the author explains how John frames Jesus as the creative, divine principle that existed before the universe, bridging the gap between God and humanity.
