# Christology: Hebrews 1:1-4
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
V. CONCLUSIONS 1. Chapter 1 could scarcely express in stronger terms Christ's unity with God. Christology and soteriology are closely linked in the letter. 2. Carefully structured focus on the identity and exaltation of Jesus in a pastoral context. 3. A high and early Christology. 4. A Call to authentic Christian faith. Hebrews is a call to authentic Christian faith when under pressure. 'Let us fix our eyes on Jesus, the author and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy set before him endured the cross, scorning its shame, and sat down at the right hand of the throne of God. Consider him who endured such opposition from sinful men, so that you will not grow weary and lose heart.' IV. A UNIQUE LETTER AND A UNIQUELY EXPRESSED CHRISTOLOGY. Hebrews 1:1-4 The Unique Son of God. Vs 1: God's self-disclosure has found its ultimate expression in Jesus of Nazareth. It is ONLY Jesus who is qualified to be the one through whom God speaks his final word. Vs 2: The eternal, all powerful, incarnate Son. Now 'in these last days' fulfilled through the Son of God. Now Jesus is the final present word of God - all that has come before is only preparatory. Heir of all things - allusions to Ps 2:8 (Royal Son) and Ps 110 (Royal Priest). Through whom he made the universe - the creative agent of God. The divine son as pre-existent Son of God. Vs 3: The divine, glorious, saving Son. The radiance of God's glory - in himself he bears the light of God's own glory. The exact representation of God's being. Representation (charakter) only here in NT - a true and trustworthy picture of who the Father is. In other words the writer is, as emphatically as he could, saying that Jesus represents the perfect, visible expression of the reality of God. Sustaining all things by his powerful word. A cosmic work of the Son.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: High Christology
The text emphasizes the divinity, pre-existence, and status of Jesus as the creative agent and exact representation of God's glory.

### Theme: Soteriological Application
Christology in Hebrews is not purely academic but linked directly to the believer's call to faith and perseverance under pressure.

### Theme: Superiority of the Son
Jesus is presented as the final, ultimate revelation of God, superior to all preparatory covenants and historical figures.
