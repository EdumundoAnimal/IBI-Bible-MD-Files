## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sovereign Kenosis
The theological argument that true majesty is defined by condescending love and self-giving provision rather than dominating coercion.

### Theme: Intergenerational Doxology
The view that faith is an intergenerational relay, where each generation is tasked with passing the record of God's redemption to the next.

### Theme: Providential Immanence
The belief that the cosmos is an intentional, sustained household (oikonomia) where God actively distributes resources to all creatures.

### Theme: The Inclusivity Tension
The debate regarding the tension between the universal scope of divine care (all flesh) and the particularity of divine judgment on the wicked.
