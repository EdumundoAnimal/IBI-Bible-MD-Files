# Romans: The Law
**Author:** [[D. M. Lloyd-Jones]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text provides an exposition of Romans 7:7 through Romans 7:13, focusing on the function, limits, and spiritual nature of the Law. The author explores how the Law acts as a 'fulcrum' to reveal the depths of sin in the human heart, arguing against the idea that the Law can provide sanctification or justify a man. The core argument is that the Law serves to expose the human condition of total inability and helplessness apart from Christ. By defining 'covetousness' as the fundamental sin, the author illustrates how the Law convicts the conscience and drives the believer to total dependence on the grace of God in Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Function of the Law
The Law does not provide sanctification but rather serves to expose sin, revealing the depth of human depravity and the total need for a Savior.

### Theme: The Law as a Fulcrum
The Law acts as a spiritual fulcrum; instead of making men holy, it stimulates the latent sin in the human heart to show that one cannot be justified by works.

### Theme: Sin as Deceitful
Sin is described as possessing a deceitful nature that misuses the Law to create an antagonism against God, leading to human rebellion and self-sufficiency.
