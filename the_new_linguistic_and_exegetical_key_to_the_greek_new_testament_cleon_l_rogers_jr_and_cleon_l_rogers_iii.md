# The New Linguistic and Exegetical Key to the Greek New Testament
**Author:** [[Cleon L. Rogers Jr. and Cleon L. Rogers III]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a scholarly linguistic and exegetical resource for the Greek New Testament. It provides verse-by-verse analysis of Greek words, parsing their grammatical forms (such as aorist subjunctive, present indicative, etc.) and offering exegetical insights into the meaning of specific phrases within their historical and theological context. The text includes references to various scholarly authorities and comparative studies on Greek syntax, viticulture metaphors, and the theological implications of Jesus' discourses in the Gospel of John. The document also includes pedagogical instructions for students on how to conduct New Testament exegesis, including comparing various English translations and performing structural analyses.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Greek Linguistic Analysis
The systematic grammatical parsing and morphological breakdown of New Testament Greek to inform exegetical understanding.

### Theme: Exegetical Methodology
A structured approach to studying biblical texts, emphasizing the comparison of multiple modern translations and the structural mapping of arguments.

### Theme: Johannine Imagery
The interpretation of specific metaphors in the Gospel of John, such as the vine, and their connection to broader theological concepts like Jewish symbolism and the role of the Comforter.
