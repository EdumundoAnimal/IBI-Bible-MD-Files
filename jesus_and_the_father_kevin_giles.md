# Jesus and the Father
**Author:** [[Kevin Giles]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of chapters from the book 'Jesus and the Father' by Kevin Giles, focusing on the historical and theological debate regarding 'subordinationism' in the doctrine of the Trinity. It examines biblical interpretation, early church creeds, and theological arguments from scholars and historical figures like Athanasius, Tertullian, and Origen to challenge the contemporary evangelical view that the Son is eternally subordinate to the Father.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Subordinationism
The author critically examines and opposes the doctrine of eternal functional subordination (EFS) of the Son to the Father, labeling it a departure from historic orthodox Trinitarian theology.

### Theme: Trinitarian Coequality
The argument that the three persons of the Trinity are equal in essence, glory, and authority, rejecting any notion of inherent functional hierarchy.

### Theme: Historical Interpretation
The text leverages patristic writings and historical creeds to argue that the early church did not support modern evangelical views on the eternal submission of the Son.
