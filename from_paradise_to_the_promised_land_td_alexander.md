# From Paradise to the Promised Land
**Author:** [[T.D. Alexander]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a detailed examination of the book of Exodus, framing it as a narrative about knowing God through personal experience. It explores the themes of divine manifestation, the holiness of God, and the establishment of the Sinai Covenant. The text further links these Old Testament experiences to the New Testament, particularly emphasizing Jesus Christ as the fulfillment of various signs, symbols, and covenants established in Exodus. Significant attention is given to the 'I AM' statements of Jesus, which are presented as deliberate theological allusions to God's revelation to Moses at the burning bush.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Knowledge of God
The overarching theme of Exodus is described as knowing God, not just through intellectual apprehension, but through a personal, relational experience involving divine encounters, signs, and wonders.

### Theme: Divine Holiness and Sovereignty
God reveals his majesty, holiness, and sovereignty through the deliverance of the Israelites, the giving of the Law at Sinai, and the provision of the tabernacle.

### Theme: Typology and New Testament Fulfillment
The author argues that events and symbols in Exodus, such as the Passover, the Sinai Covenant, and the tabernacle, serve as types that point toward and are fulfilled by the person and work of Jesus Christ.
