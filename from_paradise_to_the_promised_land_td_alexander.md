# From Paradise to the Promised Land
**Author:** [[T.D. Alexander]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The book examines the Pentateuch, specifically focusing on the book of Exodus. It explores themes of God revealing Himself through personal experience, the establishment of the Sinai Covenant, the construction of the tabernacle, and how these Old Testament events are linked to the New Testament, particularly the mission of Jesus. The text details the narrative of the Israelites in Egypt, the significance of the plagues, and the subsequent theological developments regarding God's holiness, sovereignty, and the concept of knowing God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Knowing God
Exodus is presented as a book focused on knowing God through personal experience, moving from the burning bush encounter to the revelation of God's name, attributes, and sovereign majesty.

### Theme: Covenant and Law
The text explores the Sinai Covenant as a way for God to reside among His people, emphasizing the obligations, holiness, and the ritualistic requirements necessary for maintaining this relationship.

### Theme: Typology and New Testament Connections
The author draws connections between the events of Exodus (Passover, Sinai, Tabernacle) and the life, mission, and identity of Jesus as presented in the New Testament.
