# The Old Testament Story: Overview and Historical Context
**Author:** [[Tyler F. Williams]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a comprehensive outline and historical timeline of the Old Testament. It begins by categorizing the Old Testament books into six parts based on historical periods: from Adam to Noah, Noah to Abraham, Abraham to Moses, Moses to King David, King David to Zerubbabel, and Zerubbabel to Christ. It further provides a structural outline of the book of Genesis, detailing primeval stories (Prologue through Noah) and patriarchal stories (Abraham through Terah). Finally, it offers a visual and textual timeline of 'The Old Testament Story,' tracing major eras from Patriarchs (2200-1550 BCE) to the Restoration period (539-333 BCE), highlighting key events, kingdoms, and prophetic timelines within the context of the Ancient Near East (ANE).

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Structural Organization
The categorization of OT books into historical and law-based partitions to show the progression from creation to the Messianic era.

### Theme: Historical Timeline of Israel
A chronological mapping of Israelite history from the Patriarchal period through the United Kingdom, divided Kingdoms (Israel/Judah), the Exile, and the Restoration.

### Theme: ANE Contextualization
Integrating biblical history with geographical and political frameworks of the Ancient Near East, such as Egyptian influence and later Babylonian and Persian dominance.
