## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Gospel Synoptic Comparison
The document highlights both the harmonies and the distinct emphases in how the four evangelists record the final hours of Jesus' life.

### Theme: Fulfillment of Scripture
Emphasis on the events surrounding the death of Jesus as a direct fulfillment of prophetic scripture, particularly highlighted in the Gospel of John.

### Theme: Testimony of Divine Identity
The reactions of centurions and witnesses serve as a thematic bridge confirming Jesus' identity as the Son of God or a righteous man.
