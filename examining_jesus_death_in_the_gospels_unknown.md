## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Fulfillment of Scripture
The document highlights specific instances where Jesus' death fulfills Old Testament prophecy, particularly in the Gospel of John, citing 'Not one of his bones will be broken' and 'They will look on the one they have pieced'.

### Theme: The Testimony of the Centurion
The document compares the reaction of the Centurion across the Synoptic Gospels, noting the progression from recognizing Jesus as the 'Son of God' in Matthew and Mark to praising God and calling him a 'righteous man' in Luke.
