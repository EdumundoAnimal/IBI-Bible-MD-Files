## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Inadequacy of the Old Covenant
The text emphasizes the repeated nature of old sacrifices as insufficient, contrasting them with the adequacy of Christ's single sacrifice.

### Theme: Exhortation to Faithfulness
The author calls the audience to respond to theological truths through specific actions: drawing near to God, holding fast to their confession, and encouraging one another.

### Theme: Concentric Symmetry
The author structures his theological arguments in Hebrews 10:1-18 using a concentric symmetry to explain the transition from the old law to the new covenant.
