## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Efficacy of Christ's Sacrifice
The author contrasts the repetitive nature of Old Testament sacrifices, which failed to take away sins, with the singular, sufficient sacrifice of Christ.

### Theme: Theological Application
The document moves from doctrinal explanation to practical exhortation, emphasizing that understanding the work of Christ should result in active faith, including drawing near to God and community encouragement.
