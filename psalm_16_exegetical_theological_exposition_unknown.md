## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Inheritance
The theological assertion that Yahweh, not land or temporal status, serves as the ultimate portion, cup, and inheritance for the believer, paralleling the sacerdotal status of the Levites.

### Theme: Eschatological Resurrection
The prophetic interpretation of Psalm 16:10 wherein the preservation of the Holy One from 'the Pit' (shaḥat) finds its literal and historical fulfillment in the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Christological Actualization
The view that David’s poetic language transcends his own historical crisis, serving as a prophetic typological shadow realized uniquely in the person and work of the Messiah.

### Theme: Anti-Syncretism
The radical rejection of cultural and religious compromise, specifically the practices of necromancy and blood libations, in favor of absolute devotion to Yahweh.
