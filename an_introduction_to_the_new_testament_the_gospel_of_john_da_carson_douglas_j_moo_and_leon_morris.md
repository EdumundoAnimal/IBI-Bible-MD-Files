# An Introduction to the New Testament: The Gospel of John
**Author:** [[D.A. Carson, Douglas J. Moo, and Leon Morris]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This excerpt from An Introduction to the New Testament examines the authorship, historical context, provenance, and purpose of the Gospel of John. The authors analyze scholarly debates regarding the 'Johannine Community,' the relationship of the fourth gospel to the Synoptic Gospels, and the timing of its composition. The text explores critical theories, including the potential influence of Gnosticism and the internal evidence for the gospel's unique theological emphasis on Jesus as the Son of God, his divine mission, and his pre-existence.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Authorship and Identity
The text discusses the ongoing debate regarding the authorship of the Fourth Gospel, balancing tradition against modern critical theories that posit a 'Johannine Community' or a multi-stage editorial process.

### Theme: Synoptic Relationship
Explores the scholarly challenges in reconciling the differences between John and the Synoptic Gospels (Matthew, Mark, and Luke), specifically regarding geographic focus, chronology, and the nature of the miracles.

### Theme: Theological Intent
Analyzes the unique Christology of the Fourth Gospel, focusing on its presentation of Jesus as the incarnate Son of God, the use of 'I am' sayings, and the emphasis on eternal life.
