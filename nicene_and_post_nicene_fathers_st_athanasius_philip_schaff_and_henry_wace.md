# Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers: St. Athanasius
**Author:** [[Philip Schaff and Henry Wace]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume contains the Select Works and Letters of St. Athanasius, detailing the historical and theological conflicts regarding the Arian heresy. It includes the Councils of Ariminum and Seleucia, along with the Letter of the Bishops of Africa to the Church. The text focuses on the defense of the Nicene Creed, the nature of the Son as 'Coessential' with the Father, and the rejection of Arian claims that Christ was a created being. The documents serve as a critical record of ecclesiastical history and theological debates in the 4th-century Church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Divinity of the Son
A central theological argument maintaining that the Son is of the same Essence (Coessential) as the Father, rejecting the Arian view that the Son is a created being or subordinate entity.

### Theme: Ecclesiastical Unity and Authority
The text emphasizes the importance of the Nicene Council as the definitive ecumenical authority, critiquing subsequent, splintered synods for creating confusion and theological division.

### Theme: Scriptural Consistency
The defense of the Nicene faith based on the argument that the Church's confession is rooted in a correct interpretation of the Scriptures, countering Arian claims that 'unscriptural' terminology was being introduced.
