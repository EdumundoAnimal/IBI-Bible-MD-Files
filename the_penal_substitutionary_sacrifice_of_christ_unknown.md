## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Penal Substitutionary Atonement
The argument that Christ's death was a sacrifice to satisfy God's justice against human sin and guilt, serving as a propitiation.

### Theme: Divine Justice and Wrath
The theological stance that God's justice necessitates retribution for sin, and that mercy is provided within the framework of this holiness.

### Theme: Biblical Narrative
The tension between focusing on the specific mechanics of the cross versus the broader cosmic and creational dimensions of the biblical story.
