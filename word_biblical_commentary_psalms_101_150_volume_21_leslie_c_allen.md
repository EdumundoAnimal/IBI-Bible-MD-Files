## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is an excerpt from the Word Biblical Commentary on Psalms 101-150, specifically focusing on the exegesis and commentary of Psalm 110. It covers the 'God and Priest' (110:1-7) section, providing a translation, extensive bibliographic references, critical notes and comments on the Hebrew text, and a detailed Form/Structure/Setting analysis. The document discusses scholarly debate regarding the royal nature of the psalm, its historical context (ranging from the Davidic monarchy to the post-exilic period), and theological implications including the messianic interpretation and the role of the priesthood in ancient Israel. Finally, it provides an 'Explanation' section synthesizing these critical insights for the reader.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Royal Ideology
The examination of Psalm 110 as a royal psalm, reflecting themes of divine empowerment of the king, the legitimization of the Davidic dynasty, and the intersection of kingship and priesthood.

### Theme: Messianic Interpretation
The transition of the royal psalm from its historical setting in the Jerusalem court to its later application in New Testament literature as a messianic prophecy concerning the ascension and eternal priesthood of Christ.

### Theme: Historical Criticism
An analysis of the source-critical, form-critical, and tradition-historical debates regarding the origin and composition of Psalm 110, specifically the relationship between the Jebusite priesthood and the Davidic line.
