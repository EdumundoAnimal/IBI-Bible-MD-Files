# Word Biblical Commentary: Psalms 101-150 (Volume 21)
**Author:** [[Leslie C. Allen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text is an excerpt from the Word Biblical Commentary series covering Psalm 110:1-7. It provides a detailed scholarly examination of the passage, including bibliography, translation, notes on the Hebrew text, structural analysis, and theological explanation. The commentary explores the royal and messianic nature of the Psalm, its potential connection to the Davidic monarchy, Jerusalem, and later New Testament applications regarding the divinity and exaltation of Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Royal Zion Theology
The Psalm is interpreted as a royal oration associated with the Davidic monarchy in Jerusalem, emphasizing Yahweh's divine support for the king and his victories over enemies.

### Theme: Melchizedekian Priesthood
The passage explores the unique synthesis of royal and priestly roles granted by divine oath, setting the king apart as a perpetual priest.

### Theme: Messianic Interpretation
The commentary highlights how the New Testament reinterprets these royal oracles to describe the exaltation of Jesus Christ, his divinity, and his eternal high-priestly status.
