## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Messianic Interpretation
The document discusses the trajectory of Psalm 110 from its original royal setting within the Davidic monarchy to its later application in the New Testament as a foundational text for understanding the divine status and priesthood of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Royal Psalm Context
Analysis of the psalm as a royal coronation or enthronement text, rooted in ancient Near Eastern court etiquette, where the king is presented as a vassal or co-regent of Yahweh.

### Theme: Historical Ambiguity
A review of scholarly debate concerning the dating of the Psalm, fluctuating between a pre-exilic origin linked to Davidic battles and a post-exilic, eschatological, or messianic re-reading.
