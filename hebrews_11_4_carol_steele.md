# Hebrews 1:1-4
**Author:** [[Carol Steele]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The author provides a theological analysis of the Book of Hebrews, focusing on the opening verses of chapter 1. The text explores the transition in divine revelation from the prophets to the Son, examining the theological implications of the 'but' that distinguishes the new revelation from the old. The author argues that Hebrews does not necessarily replace the old tradition but offers a 'particularity' of God's message in Christ. The commentary delves into the nature of Christ's pre-existence, his relationship to the created order, and his role as the final and supreme revelation of God. It also addresses the historical and contemporary challenge of interpreting these passages without promoting supersessionism in Jewish-Christian relations.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Supersessionism
The author critically addresses the danger of interpreting New Testament passages, specifically Hebrews, as a replacement for Jewish scripture and history.

### Theme: Divine Revelation
An analysis of the transition from the fragmented revelation through prophets to the complete and final revelation through the Son.

### Theme: Christology
A focus on Christ's role as the 'exact imprint' of God's being, emphasizing both his continuity with and distinction from the Old Testament figures.
