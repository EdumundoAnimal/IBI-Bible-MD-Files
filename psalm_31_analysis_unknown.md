## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Entrustment
The theological movement of shifting one's life and security from self-authored, autonomous control into the custody of God, grounded in His righteousness and redemptive history.

### Theme: Somatic and Social Vulnerability
The analysis of the psalmist's real experience of physical decay, social ostracism ('broken vessel'), and psychological siege, which are integrated into the life of faith rather than suppressed.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The trajectory of Psalm 31 from the Davidic sufferer to the ultimate Righteous Sufferer in Jesus Christ, whose passion and resurrection provide the final theological foundation for believers.

### Theme: Existential Dialectics
A contrast between the modern secular framework of 'buffered self-ownership' and the ancient Israelite worldview of radical covenantal dependence on a sovereign Creator.
