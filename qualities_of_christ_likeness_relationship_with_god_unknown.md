# Qualities of Christ-Likeness: Relationship with God
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text details various spiritual markers of Christ-likeness, emphasizing personal relational growth with God, the importance of being part of the Body of Christ, and adopting specific attitudes towards life's challenges. Key qualities identified include consistent intimacy with Christ, a sense of destiny, commitment to the Great Commission, love for Scripture, moderation in all things (except Christ), a fear of God, and the ability to endure suffering and attack without fear.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Relational Intimacy with God
Christ-likeness is defined not by static conformity but by a dynamic, relational, and maturing journey with God, characterized by an awareness of His presence and a growing desire to love Him.

### Theme: Corporate Identity in the Body of Christ
A core aspect of Christ-likeness involves active participation in the community of believers, valuing the wisdom of others, and understanding that spiritual maturity is often cultivated through collective body life.

### Theme: Perspective on Suffering and Trials
True Christ-likeness involves accepting past mistakes, enduring persecution without pride or fear, and reframing life's chaos as something under God's sovereignty rather than solely the work of the Enemy.
