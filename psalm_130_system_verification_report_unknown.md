## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Redemption
The document argues that redemption is not a result of human merit but a sovereign act of God, grounded in His revealed character as described in Exodus 34:6-7.

### Theme: Justifying Pardon (Selihah)
Selihah is defined as a divine prerogative, exclusively belonging to God, which serves to cultivate holy reverence (Yirah) rather than moral laxity.

### Theme: Eschatological Hope
The posture of the believer is compared to a watchman waiting for the dawn, representing an expectant, guaranteed longing for God's promise.
