## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Redemption
The theological framework that grace and forgiveness (selihah) are not abstract, but grounded in God's revealed character and bound by His covenantal promises.

### Theme: Theological Depth of the Abyss
The interpretation of 'ma'amaqqim' (depths) as an objective moral state before a holy God rather than an existential void.

### Theme: Paradox of Gospel Fear
The argument that true forgiveness does not lead to moral laxity, but rather to a deep, reverent awe (yirah) before the Sovereign.

### Theme: Forensic Justification
The understanding that humanity cannot stand (ya'amod) before a holy judge on their own merit, requiring divine acquittal.
