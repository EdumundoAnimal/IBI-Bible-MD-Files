# Becoming More Like Christ
**Author:** [[P.R. Holmes and S.B. Williams]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the theological concept of 'Christ-likeness' as an ongoing journey of transformation. It posits that being Christ-like is not about external religious practices or spectacles, but about a holistic, relational, and deeply personal process of becoming 'more human' through the work of the Holy Spirit. The text emphasizes that as individuals mature, they embrace their unique identity, purpose, and relational nature, moving toward greater holiness and intimacy with God, while also recognizing that this process is communal and necessitates ongoing spiritual growth.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Authentic Maturity
True spiritual maturity is defined as a continuous journey rather than a static goal. It is not about following rituals or achieving perfection, but about the 'kneading' process of sanctification where one aligns their life with Christ's nature.

### Theme: Relational Identity
Christ-likeness is inherently relational, drawing on the concept of the Trinity. It emphasizes that we are called to be in communion with God and others, using our unique personalities and gifts to support the Body of Christ.

### Theme: Humanity and Sanctification
Rather than separating oneself from the world, Christ-likeness involves fully embracing one's humanity. It is about integrating the reality of Christ within us into our daily lives, transforming our struggles, uniqueness, and human experiences into a reflection of Him.
