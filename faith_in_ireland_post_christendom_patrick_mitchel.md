# Faith in Ireland: Post-Christendom
**Author:** [[Patrick Mitchel]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the concept of post-Christendom in Ireland, defining it as a cultural shift where Christian influence declines. It presents polling data on societal attitudes toward sexuality and morality, and outlines the decline of Christian influence across various sectors including social welfare and family. It further proposes a set of shifts for the church, moving from a central position of institutional power and control to a role of witness and mission from the margins of society.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Post-Christendom
A cultural condition where Christian faith loses its central influence and coherence in society, forcing a transition from institutional dominance to a minority voice.

### Theme: Ecclesiological Shift
The argument that the Church must move from maintenance, privilege, and institutional control toward mission, plurality, and becoming a movement from the margins.
