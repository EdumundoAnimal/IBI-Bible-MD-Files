# Four Views on the Warning Passages in Hebrews
**Author:** [[Herbert W. Bateman IV (General Editor)]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This book explores four different theological perspectives regarding the warning passages in the New Testament book of Hebrews. The contributors, including Gareth L. Cockerill, Buist M. Fanning, Randall C. Gleason, and Grant R. Osborne, examine how these passages address the spiritual state of believers, the potential for apostasy, and the nature of God's judgment and grace. The work seeks to provide an irenic dialogue between competing theological views such as Classical Reformed, Wesleyan Arminian, and other frameworks, while emphasizing the importance of Christian community and hermeneutical care. George H. Guthrie concludes the volume by reflecting on the necessity of maintaining theological humility while navigating these complex biblical texts.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Warning Passages in Hebrews
Exegesis and theological interpretation of specific passages (2:1-4; 3:7-4:13; 5:11-6:12; 10:19-39; 12:14-29) that emphasize the need for believers to persevere in faith.

### Theme: Eschatological Tension
The theological interplay between current temporal reality and the future consummation of God's promises in Hebrews, specifically regarding salvation, rest, and judgment.

### Theme: Lesser to Greater Argument
The literary structure (qal wahomer) utilized throughout Hebrews, emphasizing the superiority of the new covenant revelation in Christ compared to the old Mosaic covenant.

### Theme: Theological Dialogue
An approach to biblical scholarship that fosters irenic discussion between differing theological camps (Reformed vs. Arminian) without sacrificing academic integrity.
