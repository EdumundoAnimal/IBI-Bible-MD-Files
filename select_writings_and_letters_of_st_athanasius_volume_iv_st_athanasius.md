# Select Writings and Letters of St. Athanasius, Volume IV
**Author:** [[St. Athanasius]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological treatise focusing on the discourses of Athanasius against the Arians. It addresses complex christological debates regarding the nature of the Son in relation to the Father, specifically analyzing scriptural passages used by the Arians to imply the Son's subordination or ignorance. Athanasius provides systematic explanations for these passages, arguing that the Son's human limitations, such as appearing to lack knowledge, are attributable to His assumed flesh rather than an essential ignorance in His divine nature. The text emphasizes that all that the Son has, He has from the Father, reaffirming the unity of essence between the Father and the Son while clarifying the role of the incarnation in the redemptive mission of Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Arianism and Orthodox Christology
The text serves as a counter-argument to Arian claims that the Son's apparent ignorance or temporal limitations prove His essential difference from the Father.

### Theme: Incarnational Theology
Athanasius posits that scriptural references to the Son's lack of knowledge or limitations must be understood within the context of the Incarnation, where the Son truly takes on human experience to facilitate human salvation.

### Theme: Divine Unity
The text defends the eternal divinity of the Son, arguing that whatever the Son 'receives' from the Father is an eternal communication of essence, not a temporal acquisition.

*Context Source: Discourse III, Chapter XXVII, Page 413*

*Context Source: Discourse III, Chapter XXVII, Page 413*

> "For as He has in Himself, so He has given also to the Son that He has, for the Son's natural likeness and propriety towards the Father."
