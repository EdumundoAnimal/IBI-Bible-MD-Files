The document provides a comprehensive survey of Old Testament history and the minor prophets, detailing the chronological timeline of the Kings of Babylon and Persia from 605 BC to 330 BC. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Sovereignty and Judgement
God is portrayed as the ultimate judge over both Israel and foreign nations, using historical powers like Babylon and Assyria to execute discipline, while remaining committed to eventual restoration.

### Theme: Restoration and Covenant
A recurring focus on the return of exiles to Jerusalem and the rebuilding of the temple, symbolizing God's faithfulness to His people despite historical cycles of rebellion and judgement.

### Theme: Social Justice vs. Spiritual Sin
The prophets frequently condemn the breakdown of moral society, highlighting that religious ritual without social justice and moral integrity is unacceptable to God.
