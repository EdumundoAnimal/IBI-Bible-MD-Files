## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Deuteronomistic Theology
The historical narrative in Kings is framed by the concept of retribution, where the faithfulness of the kings and the people to the law of God directly impacts the prosperity or downfall of the nation.

### Theme: Fulfillment of Prophetic Word
The text heavily emphasizes that the history of Israel and Judah is marked by the recurring motifs of prophetic promises (e.g., Ahijah, Elijah, Elisha) being fulfilled by historical events, proving God's sovereignty.

### Theme: God's Faithfulness vs. Man's Faithlessness
The overarching theological message is the contrast between the persistent faithfulness of God and the cyclical patterns of sin and faithlessness exhibited by both the kings and the people.
