# The Father in John's Gospel
**Author:** [[Andreas J. Köstenberger and Scott R. Swain]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This chapter explores the unique Johannine presentation of the relationship between God and the Word, focusing on the revelation of God as Father. It examines how Jesus asserts his divine identity through his works and his relationship with the Father, often leading to controversy and accusations of blasphemy from his opponents. The text details various discourse segments, including the Father's role in the 'Book of Glory' and the 'Farewell discourse', emphasizing the oneness between Jesus and the Father, while also touching upon the identity of the 'Son of Man'.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Filiation
The theological argument that Jesus's identity as the Son of God is inextricably linked to his relationship with the Father, a central theme of the Fourth Gospel.

### Theme: Conflict and Blasphemy
The recurring pattern in the Gospel of John where Jesus's claims to divine oneness with the Father are met with Jewish opposition and accusations of blasphemy based on interpretations of the Hebrew Scriptures.

### Theme: The Word and the Father
The prologue's establishment of the relationship between the Word (Logos) and God, emphasizing the intimate connection and the mission of the Son to reveal the Father.
