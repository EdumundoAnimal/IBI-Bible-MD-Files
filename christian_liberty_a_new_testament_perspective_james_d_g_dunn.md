# Christian Liberty: A New Testament Perspective
**Author:** [[James D. G. Dunn]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the concept of Christian liberty from a New Testament perspective. It emphasizes that Christian liberty is not a license for self-pleasing but is conditioned by love and the community of faith. The author contrasts biblical views on liberty with secular notions, particularly those of John Stuart Mill, highlighting that true freedom in Christ involves a balance between individual responsibility and communal obligation, rooted in the spirit rather than legalistic adherence to the law.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christian Liberty
Defined not as individual autonomy, but as a freedom from sin and self-deception, enabled by God's grace and exercised through love for the neighbor.

### Theme: Community and Responsibility
Pauline theology views the community (the body of Christ) as essential, where individual freedom is tempered by the needs of others and the unity of the group.

### Theme: The Law and Freedom
Christian freedom is a liberation from the law as a mechanism for salvation, moving instead to a walk by the Spirit.
