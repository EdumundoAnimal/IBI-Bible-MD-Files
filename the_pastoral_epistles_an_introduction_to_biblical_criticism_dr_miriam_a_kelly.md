# The Pastoral Epistles: An Introduction to Biblical Criticism
**Author:** [[Dr. Miriam A Kelly]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an introduction to the Pastoral Epistles (1 and 2 Timothy, Titus) and outlines various methods of Biblical Criticism. It covers authorship questions, historical backgrounds, and provides an overview of critical methodologies including Textual Criticism, Form Criticism, Tradition-Historical Criticism, Redaction Criticism, and others like Reader-Response and Socio-economic criticism.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Pastoral Epistles Authorship
Explores the tension between the traditional view of Pauline authorship and modern critical scholarship which raises questions regarding historical context and style.

### Theme: Biblical Criticism Methods
A survey of analytical techniques applied to the Bible, ranging from lower criticism (textual) to various forms of higher criticism (form, redaction, tradition-historical) and literary approaches.
