# Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, Volume IV: St. Athanasius
**Author:** [[Philip Schaff and Henry Wace]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume is part of the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers series, focusing on Select Writings and Letters of Athanasius, Bishop of Alexandria, edited by Archibald Robertson. The text contains deep theological analysis, particularly focusing on Christological arguments, the divine nature of the Son, and refutations of various heresies such as Arianism. Key chapters explore the interpretation of specific Gospel passages where Christ speaks of receiving power or lacking knowledge, addressing the nuances of his human nature versus his divine authority. The discourse clarifies that while Christ as man may speak in terms of humanity, as the Son of God, he possesses equality and oneness with the Father.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
Exploration of the dual nature of Christ, emphasizing how theological passages concerning Christ's human limitations or 'ignorance' must be reconciled with his divine omniscience and unity with the Father.

### Theme: Arianism Refutation
The text systematically dismantles Arian arguments that use Gospel verses describing the Son as receiving power or glory from the Father to imply the Son is a created being of a lower order.

### Theme: Biblical Exegesis
Scholarly commentary providing theological interpretation for specific instances in the Gospels where Christ's words might be misunderstood if read strictly as referring to his divine essence rather than his incarnate state.
