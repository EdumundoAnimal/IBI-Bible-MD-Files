## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as a comprehensive exegetical and homiletical report on Proverbs 31, framing the text as a theological masterpiece rather than a literal daily checklist. It explores the linguistic, structural, and historical context, arguing that the 'eshet chayil (Woman of Valor) is a personification of Wisdom. The report provides a verse-by-verse exposition, linking the King's warning in verses 1-9 to Christological themes of redemptive justice, and interprets the Woman of Valor in verses 10-31 as an eschatological symbol for the Church, emphasizing her economic agency, charity, and covenantal security.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Wisdom
Proverbs 31 is interpreted as a theological capstone where the Woman of Valor symbolizes Lady Wisdom, ultimately pointing toward the person and work of Christ as the true wisdom of God.

### Theme: Covenantal Redemption
The text frames wisdom as a lived-out reality of the Fear of Yahweh, where justice for the voiceless and economic agency are expressions of one's covenantal identity.

### Theme: Eschatological Bride
The Church is identified as the ultimate fulfillment of the Woman of Valor, characterized by righteous deeds and fearless anticipation of the future, awaiting reward at the final gates.

### Theme: Demystifying Proverbs 31
The report rejects the 'Proverbs 31 Syndrome' by clarifying that the poem is a poetic, lifetime mosaic of character rather than an oppressive, daily behavioral checklist for individual women.
