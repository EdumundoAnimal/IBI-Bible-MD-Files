## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Supremacy of Christ
The essay argues that Christ is portrayed as superior to angels, who are described as ministering servants, whereas the Son is the heir and the divine mediator of God's message.

### Theme: Warning against Apostasy
Utilizing the rhetoric of 'qal wahomer' (lesser-to-greater), the author of Hebrews warns readers that neglecting the 'greater' salvation offered through Christ will result in more severe judgment than that experienced by the Israelites in the wilderness.

### Theme: Theological Eschatology
The essay contrasts the temporal, changing nature of the created world with the eternal, unchanging nature of the Son, referencing Psalm 102 to establish Christ as the Creator.
