## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Forensic Justification vs. Human Self-Defense
The psalm emphasizes that no human can stand before the divine bar of justice through personal merit. Redemption begins only when the individual abandons all self-justification and appeals to God's covenantal faithfulness.

### Theme: Pneumatological Sanctification
The movement from a crushed human spirit to the guidance of God's 'Good Spirit' highlights that sanctification is a work of divine grace, transforming human desires and providing moral direction.

### Theme: Covenantal Lament
The text explores how individual suffering is interpreted through the lens of Israel's covenantal framework, where external crisis reflects an internal judicial standing before God.
