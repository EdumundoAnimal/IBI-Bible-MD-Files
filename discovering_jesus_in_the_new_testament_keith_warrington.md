# Discovering Jesus in the New Testament
**Author:** [[Keith Warrington]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an analytical overview of the Epistle to the Hebrews, specifically exploring the Christology of the book. It discusses the superiority of Jesus over Old Testament prophets, angels, Moses, and the Levitical priesthood. It highlights Jesus as the high priest and mediator, emphasizing his role in salvation, his divine status, and his empathetic nature as a human representative.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Superiority
The text argues that Jesus is superior to all previous mediators and figures in the Jewish faith, including prophets, angels, Moses, and the Levitical high priests.

### Theme: High Priesthood and Mediation
Jesus serves as a perpetual, empathetic high priest who offers a superior sacrifice, resulting in an eternal covenant and direct access to God.

### Theme: Soteriology
The document emphasizes that Jesus' sacrifice was final, replacing the repetitive Jewish sacrificial system and offering a permanent cleansing for sins.
