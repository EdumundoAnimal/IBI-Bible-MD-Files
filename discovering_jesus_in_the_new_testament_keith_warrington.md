# Discovering Jesus in the New Testament
**Author:** [[Keith Warrington]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological analysis of the Epistle to the Hebrews from the book 'Discovering Jesus in the New Testament'. It explores the concept of 'Jesus, Superior Savior' by examining his superiority over Old Testament figures like the prophets, angels, and Moses. It details the theological significance of his high priesthood, Melchizedekian order, and his role in establishing a new covenant. Key theological themes include Christology, Soteriology, and the nature of Jesus as both human and divine.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Superiority
The author argues that Jesus is superior to all Old Testament authorities, including prophets, angels, and Moses, serving as the ultimate revelation of God.

### Theme: The High Priesthood of Jesus
Jesus functions as a superior high priest who is not bound by the Levitical system, holding a permanent, eternal priesthood based on the order of Melchizedek.

### Theme: Humanity and Empathy
The text emphasizes that despite his superiority, Jesus's humanity allows him to be a merciful and empathetic high priest who truly understands human suffering.

### Theme: Soteriology
Jesus provides an eternal salvation through his own blood, serving as a mediator of a new covenant that offers complete forgiveness of sins.
