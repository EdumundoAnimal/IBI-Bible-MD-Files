# Church History Survey: Pentecost to the Reformation
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines the syllabus for a Church History module (CH 105) covering the history of the Christian church from Pentecost to the Reformation. It includes a course description, learning outcomes, and assessment criteria (essays and exams). The module covers major events, figures (including Church Fathers such as John Chrysostom, Jerome, and Augustine), and key definitions for academic analysis. Additionally, it contains sample student-level content on the life and mission of St. Patrick and an essay analyzing the spread of the Gospel in the early Roman Empire.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Theological Development
Examination of the Church Fathers and their influence on doctrine, including the debates at the Council of Nicea and the development of the Nicene Creed.

### Theme: Missiology and Expansion
Analysis of the early church's expansion through the Roman Empire, utilizing Pax Romana, universal infrastructure, and the common language (koine Greek) to spread the Gospel.

### Theme: Critical Analysis
Methodological framework for theological study requiring students to evaluate claims using evidence, logic, and historical context rather than mere description.
