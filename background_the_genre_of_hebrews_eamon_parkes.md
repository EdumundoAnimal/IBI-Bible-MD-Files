# Background: The Genre of Hebrews
**Author:** [[Eamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an analysis of the genre and structure of the Epistle to the Hebrews. It identifies the book as a homily or sermon characterized by the oscillation between exposition and exhortation. The text categorizes features such as familiar second-person address, rhetorical questions, and the use of 'we' and 'brethren' to build rapport with the audience. Furthermore, it includes structural outlines detailing the Five Warnings of Hebrews and the overarching theological movement of the text, emphasizing that teaching (truth) serves as the foundation for warnings, with the ultimate goal being to encourage the faith of the readers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Homiletic Genre
The Epistle to the Hebrews is framed as a homily where the author shifts between theological exposition and moral exhortation.

### Theme: Expository-Exhortatory Dynamic
The author establishes theological truth (Exposition) as the necessary foundation upon which warnings and ethical directives (Exhortation) are built to encourage the congregation.

### Theme: The Five Warnings
A structural analysis of Hebrews that highlights specific warnings against spiritual apathy and apostasy embedded within the arguments.
