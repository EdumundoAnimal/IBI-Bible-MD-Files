## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine Dualism
The Gospel utilizes binary contrasts such as light versus darkness, truth versus falsehood, and the Word versus the world to define the spiritual state of believers and the reality of the incarnation.

### Theme: The Beloved Disciple
An essential figure in Johannine tradition representing an eyewitness to the ministry and passion of Jesus, often serving as a model of faithful witness and apostolic authority.

### Theme: Community Development
The text reconstructs the evolution of the Johannine community from its roots in Judaism, through expulsion from the synagogue, to its eventual internal schisms and reconciliation with the wider church.
