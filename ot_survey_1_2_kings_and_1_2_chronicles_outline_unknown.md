## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Deuteronomistic Theology
The concept that national history and individual fates are determined by obedience or disobedience to God's law as outlined in Deuteronomy.

### Theme: God's Faithfulness vs. Man's Faithlessness
The overarching message of 1 & 2 Kings, juxtaposing God's consistent covenant loyalty against the frequent failures of the people and their kings.

### Theme: Temple as Spiritual Center
The role of the Temple as the focal point of covenantal life, with its destruction and subsequent rebuilding serving as a barometer for the nation's spiritual state.
