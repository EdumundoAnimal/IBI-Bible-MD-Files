## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Preservation (Shamar)
The theological motif of God's active, military, and custodial guardianship over the believer's life, extending beyond passive observation to active intervention.

### Theme: Redemptive-Historical Trajectory
The progression of the promise of divine protection from the patriarchal blessing (Genesis 28:15) through the pilgrimage of Israel (Psalm 121) to the ultimate fulfillment in Christ and the eschatological state (Revelation 7:16-17).

### Theme: Cosmic Demythologization
The assertion that God is sovereign over astral bodies (Sun, Moon) and localized high places (bāmôt), neutralizing pagan anxieties regarding these forces.

### Theme: Theodicy and Suffering
The distinction between physical mortality and 'ultimate destruction' (ra‘), framing suffering not as a failure of God's protection but as a path through which the believer's true life (nephesh) is preserved.
