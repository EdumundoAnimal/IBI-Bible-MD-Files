## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This report provides a systematic exegetical and theological analysis of Psalm 129. It covers linguistic and syntax reports, textual variants, grammatical mechanics, structural mechanics, canonical and intertextual connections, historical reception, and homiletical application. The text emphasizes the contrast between the 'plowed back' of the suffering community and the 'rooftop grass' of the wicked, using the agricultural imagery of ancient Israel to explain divine justice and covenantal preservation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Preservation
The argument that despite the historical and visceral suffering of God's people ('plowing'), they are preserved because they are rooted in the covenantal soil of God's sovereignty.

### Theme: Retributive Justice
The theological theme that God actively intervenes to cut the 'cords' of the wicked, ensuring their ungodly prosperity is ephemeral and doomed to barrenness.

### Theme: Theologia Crucis (Theology of the Cross)
The concept that the church is recognized by its suffering and that this suffering is not meaningless, but is the preparation for the sowing of the Gospel seed.
