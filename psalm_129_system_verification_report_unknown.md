Modern Semitic philology (cf. Akkadian šalāpu, "to pull out," but in Northwest Semitic dialects occasionally "to sprout/shoot up") creates a functional divergence: Does the grass wither before it is plucked (dying prematurely purely from lack of soil depth), or before it shoots into stalk? The MT syntax and LXX rendering strongly favor the former: its doom is intrinsic; it perishes before human hands even bother to pull it.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Preservation
The document argues that Israel's survival is not due to military might but is a miracle of sovereign preservation, rooted in the covenantal identity established during the Exodus.

### Theme: Retributive Justice
The text posits that God's righteousness (YHWH ṣaddîq) is an active, forensic intervention that cuts the cords of the wicked, ensuring that systemic oppression is met with divine judgment.

### Theme: Theologia Crucis
The psalm is interpreted as a classic articulation of the theology of the cross, where the suffering of the Church (the body of Christ) is not a sign of defeat but a preparation for the harvest of the Gospel.
