# The Christendom Shift
**Author:** [[Stuart Murray]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text examines the transition of the early church from a marginalized community to the state-sanctioned institution of Christendom. It highlights the influence of Constantine and Augustine in re-engineering church structures, theology, and mission. Key areas explored include the adoption of infant baptism, the use of coercive power, the integration of Old Testament legal structures, and the marginalization of Jesus' core teachings through an imperial-focused theology. The document critically reviews the long-term impact of these institutional changes on Christian identity, ethics, and the role of the church in society.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Christendom Shift
The historical process whereby the church moved from a persecuted or marginalized minority to an official, state-supported power structure, fundamentally altering its nature.

### Theme: Augustinian Theology
Augustine's influence in justifying coercion, state power, and the 'just war' theory by reinterpreting Old Testament and New Testament texts to support imperial objectives.

### Theme: The Marginalization of Jesus
The argument that the institutional church's preoccupation with social order and state legitimacy led to the relegation of Jesus' radical teachings on enemy love and non-violence.
