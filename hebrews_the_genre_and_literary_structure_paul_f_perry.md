# Hebrews: The Genre and Literary Structure
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an analysis of the genre and structure of the Book of Hebrews. It categorizes the book's content into expository sections and hortatory (exhortation) sections. It highlights five major warnings distributed throughout the text, designed to provide spiritual endurance. The text emphasizes that the overall purpose of Hebrews is to 'encourage' by building a truth foundation that allows these warnings to stand firm.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Homiletic Structure
The Book of Hebrews is analyzed as a homily or sermon, alternating between exposition (teaching) and exhortation (application/warning).

### Theme: Purpose of Exhortation
The exhortations and warnings serve as a mechanism to encourage the reader, functioning only after a theological 'truth foundation' has been established.

### Theme: The Five Warnings
A structural analysis of the book identifying five distinct warnings placed within the discourse to ensure the audience's spiritual perseverance.
