## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Source Hierarchy
Classification of academic resources into primary, secondary, and tertiary (reference) categories, noting that the distinction can sometimes overlap depending on the specific research question.

### Theme: Critical Evaluation
The necessity of discerning the scholarly rigor of a source, particularly differentiating between popular/devotional commentaries and academic commentaries.

### Theme: Research Strategy
The 'work cycle' approach which recommends starting with reference materials to obtain broad overviews, then moving to secondary sources for analysis, and finally engaging with primary sources for deeper study.
