## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Notes covering a study of the Book of Romans. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Law and Sin
Exploration of the tension between 'the flesh' and the Spirit, defining the Law as a tool that can arouse sinful desire while revealing human inability to obey God.

### Theme: Theology of Election
Comparative analysis of three major theological perspectives (Arminian, Barthian, Calvinist) regarding predestination and human merit in salvation.

### Theme: Christian Liberty
Analysis of non-essential practices, such as dietary restrictions and holy days, distinguishing between 'strong' believers (free from restrictions) and 'weak' believers (bound by regulations).
