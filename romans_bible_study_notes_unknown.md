## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Justification
The theological process of being made righteous before God through faith rather than works, centered on the letter to the Romans.

### Theme: Blessings of Grace
The practical and existential benefits resulting from justification, including peace, access to God's love, and eschatological hope.
