# Worthy is the Lamb and Revelation Song
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

Worthy is the Lamb: Thank You for the cross, Lord. Thank You for the price You paid. Bearing all my sin and shame. In love You came and gave amazing grace. I thank You for this love, Lord. Thank You for the nail pierced hand. Wash me in Your cleansing flow. Now all I know, Your forgiveness and embrace. Worthy is the Lamb. Seated on the throne. I crown You now with many crowns. You reign victorious. The Darling of Heaven crucified. Worthy is the Lamb. Worthy is the Lamb. Revelation Song: Worthy is the Lamb who was slain. Holy, holy is He. We sing a new song to Him who sits on Heaven's mercy seat. Holy, holy, holy is the Lord, God Almighty. Who was and is and is to come, yeah. With all creation I sing, praise to the King of Kings. You are my everything and I will adore You. Clothed in rainbows of living color. Flashes of lighting, rolls of thunder. Blessing and honor, strength and glory and power be to You, the only wise King. Filled with wonder. Awestruck wonder. At the mention of Your name. Jesus, Your name is power. Breath and living water. Such a marvelous mystery. Holy Forever: A thousand generations falling down in worship. To sing the song of ages to the Lamb. And all who've gone before us and all who will believe. Will sing the song of ages to the Lamb. Your Name is the highest, Your Name is the greatest. Your Name stands above them all. All thrones and dominions, all powers and positions. Your Name stands above them all. And the angels cry holy. All creation cries holy. You are lifted high, holy. Holy forever. If you've been forgiven and if you've been redeemed. Sing the song forever to the Lamb. And if you walk in freedom, if you bear His Name. Sing the song forever to the Lamb. Hear Your people sing holy. And to the King of kings, holy. And You will always be holy. Holy forever.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Theology of the Lamb
The text centers on the sacrificial atonement of Jesus Christ ('the Lamb who was slain'), emphasizing His crucifixion and the resulting forgiveness and redemption for humanity.

### Theme: Divine Sovereignty and Transcendence
The lyrics emphasize the eternal nature of God ('Who was and is and is to come') and His absolute authority over all 'thrones and dominions' and 'powers and positions'.

### Theme: Awe and Worship
A core theme is the reactive response of creation and believers, marked by 'awestruck wonder,' 'falling down in worship,' and the collective act of singing in praise.
