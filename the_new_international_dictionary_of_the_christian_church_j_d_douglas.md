# The New International Dictionary of the Christian Church
**Author:** [[J. D. Douglas]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The New International Dictionary of the Christian Church provides historical and theological context for various subjects. Notable entries include detailed analyses of the Council of Trent, the nature of the Trinity, and theological concepts like Tritheism. The text explores historical ecumenical councils, the development of Trinitarian dogma, and the distinction between historical facts and doctrinal interpretations within Christianity.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Theology
Exploration of the biblical foundation of the Trinity, emphasizing the unity and distinct personality of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit as presented in scripture and ecclesiastical tradition.

### Theme: Ecclesiastical History
Examination of major historical events such as the Council of Trent, which addressed Reformation challenges and codified Catholic doctrine.

### Theme: Tritheism vs. Orthodoxy
The struggle to define the nature of God while avoiding polytheism, distinguishing between the unity of the divine essence and the three persons of the Trinity.
