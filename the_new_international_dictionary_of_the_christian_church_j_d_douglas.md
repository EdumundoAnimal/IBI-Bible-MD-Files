# The New International Dictionary of the Christian Church
**Author:** [[J. D. Douglas]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The text serves as a reference volume detailing historical and theological entries, specifically covering topics such as the Council of Trent, Trinitarian theology, and various religious orders. The dictionary provides theological definitions, historical contexts of church councils, and detailed examination of the doctrine of the Trinity, explaining its development and scriptural basis. It also touches upon academic figures like Ernst Troeltsch.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Doctrine
The document explores the orthodox Christian belief in the Trinity, citing scriptural foundations, the Athanasian Creed, and the historical debates regarding the nature of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Church History (Council of Trent)
The text provides an overview of the Council of Trent, discussing its role in the Counter-Reformation, its impact on the formulation of Catholic dogma, and its reactions to Protestant theology.

### Theme: Theological Methodology
The dictionary examines how theological concepts are derived from biblical texts and historical consensus, emphasizing the difficulty of presenting comprehensive historical topics in a concise format.
