## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Abiding in Christ
The metaphor of the vine and branches illustrates the essential, vital connection required between the believer and Christ to bear spiritual fruit.

### Theme: Divine Peace
Christ promises a distinct, spiritual peace that transcends worldly circumstances, intended to comfort followers during times of transition.

### Theme: The Paraclete
The Holy Spirit is presented as the 'Counselor' who proceeds from the Father to bear witness to Christ and testify to believers.

### Theme: Hostility of the World
Followers are warned that identification with Christ will inevitably lead to persecution by the world, mirroring the rejection Christ himself faced.
