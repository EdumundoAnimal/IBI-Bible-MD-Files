# Synopsis of the Four Gospels
**Author:** [[Kurt Aland]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a page-by-page synopsis of the Four Gospels, specifically focusing on the Johannine discourses (John 14-15) and their parallel placement. It includes segments titled 'The Gift of Peace', 'The True Vine', 'Abide in My Love', 'The World's Hatred', and 'The Witness of the Paraclete'. The edition is based on the Nestle-Aland 26th Edition and Greek New Testament 3rd Edition, featuring the Revised Standard Version text.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine Discourse
The text systematically presents the Farewell Discourse of Jesus in the Gospel of John, emphasizing themes of peace, the vine/branches metaphor, abiding in love, and the hatred of the world toward disciples.

### Theme: Textual Criticism
The work serves as a reference for scholars using the Nestle-Aland Greek base text, cross-referencing these sections with the Revised Standard Version for comparative study.
