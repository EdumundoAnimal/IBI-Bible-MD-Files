# Synopsis of the Four Gospels
**Author:** [[Kurt Aland]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a scholarly synopsis of the four canonical Gospels, presented in an English edition. It provides parallel arrangements of biblical passages to facilitate comparative study. The text utilized is from the Revised Standard Version, based on the Nestle-Aland 26th Edition and Greek New Testament 3rd Edition. The content includes thematic groupings of verses such as 'The Gift of Peace' (John 14:27-31), 'Jesus the True Vine' (John 15:1-8), 'Abide in My Love' (John 15:9-17), 'The World's Hatred' (John 15:18-25), and 'The Witness of the Paraclete' (John 15:26-27).

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Abiding in Christ
The metaphor of the vine and the branches serves as a core theological construct for the necessity of continuous spiritual connection with Jesus to bear fruit.

### Theme: The Paraclete
Refers to the Holy Spirit as the Counselor or Witness who proceeds from the Father to guide and support the believers.

### Theme: Conflict with the World
An exploration of the dichotomy between the followers of Jesus and the world, noting that hatred towards the believer is ultimately hatred towards Jesus and the Father.
