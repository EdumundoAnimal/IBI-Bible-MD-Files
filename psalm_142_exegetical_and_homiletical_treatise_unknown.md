## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Refuge and Lament
Psalm 142 functions as a model for individual lament, where the sufferer finds safety and identity in Yahweh despite physical and social isolation, rather than through human power.

### Theme: Christological Typology
The psalm is presented as a typology of the passion of Christ, specifically tracing the progression from abandonment in the garden to resurrection and communal glory.

### Theme: Structural Chiasm
The text displays an inverted symmetrical structure (chiasm) pivoting on the central confession in verse 5, moving from vocal outcry to communal restoration.

### Theme: Existential Dialectic
A contrast between secular existentialism, which views isolation as absurd and final, and Biblical theism, which views the 'cave' as a providential crucible for vertical communion.
