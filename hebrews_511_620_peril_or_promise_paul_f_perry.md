# Hebrews 5:11-6:20: Peril or Promise
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a study guide exploring the theological complexities of Hebrews 5:11-6:20. The author examines the warning against spiritual stagnation, the interpretation of falling away, and the security found in God's promises. The text discusses the necessity of 'hearing' the message of God, the danger of becoming 'hard of hearing', and the theological implications of apostasy. It analyzes various views on Hebrews 6:4-6, including non-Calvinist and hypothetical interpretations, while emphasizing the author's intent to motivate his hearers toward maturity and faithfulness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Stagnation
The author highlights the danger of becoming 'hard of hearing' to the message of God, which hinders spiritual growth and maturity in believers.

### Theme: Apostasy and Security
The text explores the tension between warnings against falling away and the security of believers, analyzing different scholarly perspectives on Hebrews 6:4-6.

### Theme: Patronage and Reciprocity
The document utilizes the cultural concept of patronage to explain the relationship between God as benefactor and the believer as client, emphasizing reciprocal faithfulness.
