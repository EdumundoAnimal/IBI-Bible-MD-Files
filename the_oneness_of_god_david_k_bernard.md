## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness Theology
Exploration of the theological doctrine asserting the singular nature of God within the context of Pentecostal belief, contrasted against traditional Trinitarian views.

### Theme: Biblical Authority
The text establishes the King James Version as the primary source for scriptural evidence in the theological argumentation presented.
