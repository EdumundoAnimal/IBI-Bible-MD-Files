# The Oneness of God
**Author:** [[David K. Bernard]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The Oneness of God by David K. Bernard, Series in Pentecostal Theology, Volume 1. Copyright 1983 by David K. Bernard. Printing history: 1983-1993. Published by Word Aflame Press. This text explores the nature of God, specifically focusing on the doctrine of Oneness within Pentecostal theology, contrasting it with traditional Trinity doctrines.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness Theology
A theological perspective within Pentecostalism that emphasizes the absolute unity of God rather than the traditional Trinitarian view of three distinct persons.

### Theme: Pentecostal Theology
The formal study and documentation of doctrines held within the Pentecostal movement, as exemplified by the series in which this volume belongs.
