## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Contextual Theology
Paul's ideas of community are not systematic treatises but responses to specific problems and needs of early Christian communities, reflecting his deep involvement with the realities of his time.

### Theme: Social Adaptation
Paul utilized a policy of accommodation, engaging with the prevailing cultural norms while maintaining distinct Christian convictions to ensure the gospel reached diverse groups.

### Theme: Graeco-Roman Influence
The structure of early house churches was influenced by existing social concepts like 'politeia' (citizenship) and 'oikonomia' (household management) within the broader Graeco-Roman world.
