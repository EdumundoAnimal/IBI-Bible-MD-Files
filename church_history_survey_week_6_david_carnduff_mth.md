# Church History Survey - Week 6
**Author:** [[David Carnduff MTh]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a historical survey of Saint Jerome and Saint Ambrose of Milan, focusing on their contributions to early Church history, biblical translation, and theological development. Saint Jerome is characterized as a key figure known for his work on the Latin Vulgate, his scholarly output including scriptural commentaries, and his ascetic life. Saint Ambrose of Milan is examined as a pivotal ecclesiastical figure who influenced the Church through his administrative leadership, his resistance to Arianism, and his influence on prominent contemporaries such as Augustine. The text also covers the development of church music, early reading habits, and historical perspectives on clerical celibacy.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Translation and Hermeneutics
The evolution of the Latin Bible, specifically Jerome's shift from the Septuagint to the Hebrew Masoretic text for the Vulgate, and the resulting methodological debates between historical/literal and allegorical interpretation.

### Theme: Ecclesiastical Authority and Orthodoxy
The political and theological struggle against Arianism by leaders like Ambrose, highlighting the role of synods, imperial relations, and the enforcement of the Nicene creed.

### Theme: Asceticism and Monastic Life
The promotion of the ascetic ideal in the 4th and 5th centuries, exemplified by the lives of Jerome and Ambrose, emphasizing poverty, virginity, and withdrawal from worldly corruption.
