# Christian Discipleship Manual
**Author:** [[Pastor Brian Gingles]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a structured guide for Christian discipleship, focusing on the distinction between converts and disciples, the role of suffering, the nature of justification by faith as illustrated by Abraham, and the practical application of faith in daily life. It includes theological reflections on Romans, the effects of sin, and the spiritual necessity of trusting God through life's trials.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Discipleship vs. Conversion
Conversion marks the beginning of a relationship with God, whereas being a disciple involves ongoing growth, submission to Christ, and spiritual maturity.

### Theme: Suffering and Purpose
Suffering is framed as an instrument to cultivate 'single-mindedness' and dependency on God, leading to character development and hope.

### Theme: Justification by Faith
The theological argument that righteousness is credited by faith, not works, using Abraham's example to illustrate reliance on God's promises.
