# Romans: The Law: Its Functions and Limits
**Author:** [[D. M. Lloyd-Jones]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is an exposition of Romans 7, focusing on the function, limits, and nature of the Law. It discusses the Apostle Paul's personal experience of the Law, the deception of sin, the holiness of the Law, and how it serves as a catalyst for realizing one's own hopelessness and need for Christ. The author addresses common misinterpretations regarding moralism and antinomianism, arguing that the Law reveals the reality of sin rather than providing a path to justification.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Function of the Law
The Law acts as a mirror that reveals the nature of sin and human hopelessness rather than serving as a means for salvation.

### Theme: The Deceitfulness of Sin
Sin is described as using the Law as a fulcrum to deceive individuals into a false sense of security or self-righteousness.

### Theme: The Necessity of Christ
The ultimate goal of the Law is to lead the person to realize their utter inability and need for the salvation provided by Jesus Christ.
