## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Message of Isaiah
The book is divided into two primary parts: Chapters 1-39 (confrontation and bad news, judgment, and warning) and Chapters 40-66 (comfort and good news, the glory of the Lord, and future restoration).

### Theme: The Weeping Prophet
Jeremiah is depicted as a prophet who faced immense hardship, opposition, and rejection. His ministry focused on the impending destruction of Jerusalem, the Babylonian exile, and the necessity of repentance.

### Theme: Servant Songs in Isaiah
A series of passages (42:1-9, 49:1-7, 50:4-11, 52:13-53:12) detailing a 'servant' who suffers, restores Israel, brings salvation to the nations, and eventually dies to make atonement.

### Theme: Judgment and Restoration
A recurring prophetic pattern in both Isaiah and Jeremiah where divine judgment for sin (exile) is followed by a promise of divine comfort and eventual restoration for a faithful remnant.
