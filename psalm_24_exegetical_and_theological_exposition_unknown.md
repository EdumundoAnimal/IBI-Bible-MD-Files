**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a comprehensive exegetical, historical, and theological analysis of Psalm 24, structured into three phases: The Exegetical Network, The Hermeneutical Bridge, and The Homiletical & Application Network. The analysis explores the cosmic sovereignty of Yahweh, the ethical qualifications for sanctuary-access, and the eschatological enthronement of the King of Glory. It utilizes linguistic analysis, textual criticism (MT, LXX, DSS), and scholarly commentary (NICOT, WBC, ICC, BCOT) to trace the psalm's meaning from its ancient Near Eastern context to its canonical and Christological fulfillment.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cosmic Sovereignty
The assertion that Yahweh is the absolute Creator and owner of the entire universe, demythologizing chaotic forces like the sea by establishing them as foundational to His order.

### Theme: Ethical Sanctuary-Access
The requirement of internal and external purity (clean hands, pure heart) for communion with God, contrasting human moral failure with the holiness required by the Divine.

### Theme: Eschatological Enthronement
The prophetic and Christological movement where Jesus Christ, as the King of Glory and Victorious Warrior, ascends to the heavenly Zion, fulfilling the requirements for entry and establishing His eternal reign.
