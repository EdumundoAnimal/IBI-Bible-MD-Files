# Hebrews
**Author:** [[Paul J. Achtemeier, Joel B. Green, Marianne Meye Thompson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides an extensive exegetical and theological overview of the Epistle to the Hebrews. It explores the origins, authorship, audience, and the unique Christological themes presented in the book, including the superiority of Christ over angels, the concept of Jesus as a pioneer of faith, and his role as a High Priest. The text discusses the author's use of Old Testament citations, the historical context of the readers, and the theological significance of Christ's pre-existence and his work of salvation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The Epistle to the Hebrews presents a complex view of Jesus, emphasizing his pre-existence, his superiority to angels, his role as the pioneer of faith, and his identity as the ultimate High Priest.

### Theme: Audience Context
The intended readers were likely Jewish Christians experiencing persecution, potential temptation to drift from faith, and a need for encouragement to persevere in their commitment to Christ.

### Theme: Exegetical Methodology
The author utilizes rabbinic-style linking of OT texts, allegorical reading, and intensive use of the Septuagint to build theological arguments concerning the fulfillment of the old covenant in Christ.
