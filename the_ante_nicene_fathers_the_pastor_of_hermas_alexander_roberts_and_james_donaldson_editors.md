# The Ante-Nicene Fathers: The Pastor of Hermas
**Author:** [[Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson (Editors)]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a collection of writings from the Ante-Nicene Fathers, specifically focusing on 'The Pastor of Hermas' in Volume II. The text includes an introductory note discussing the historical context, the authorship, and the nature of the Shepherd of Hermas, which is presented as a work of primitive Christian literature. The document also contains excerpts from the 'Visions' and 'Similitudes' of the Pastor of Hermas, offering moral and spiritual instruction regarding the Christian life, faith, and the nature of salvation.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Primitive Christian Literature
The text characterizes The Pastor of Hermas as a significant, if once misunderstood, relic of the early church that provides insight into the spiritual preoccupations of the second century.

### Theme: Asceticism and Detachment
The text emphasizes the importance of detachment from worldly possessions and concerns, framing the Christian as a foreigner in the world who must focus on the laws and kingdom of God.

### Theme: Innocence and Childlike Faith
The Shepherd of Hermas highlights the spiritual value of being as a child, noting that those who remain innocent and free from wickedness are particularly dear to the Lord.
