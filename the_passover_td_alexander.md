# The Passover
**Author:** [[T.D. Alexander]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The chapter outlines the significance of the Passover in Exodus 12, focusing on the historical account, the ritual instructions, its purpose as a symbol of divine protection and redemption, and its subsequent connections to the New Testament, particularly the sacrifice of Jesus.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Redemptive History
The Passover serves as the foundational event in Israel's history, symbolizing the Lord's intervention to deliver his people from slavery.

### Theme: Ritual and Instruction
Detailed regulations concerning the sacrificial lamb, the use of blood, the exclusion of leaven, and the timing of the feast serve to perpetuate the memory of God's act of deliverance.

### Theme: Typology and New Testament Fulfillment
The author explores how the Passover serves as a paradigm for the crucifixion of Jesus, linking the themes of sin, sacrifice, and the sanctification of God's people.
