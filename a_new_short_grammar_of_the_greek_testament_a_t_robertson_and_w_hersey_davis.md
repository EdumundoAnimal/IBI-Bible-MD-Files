# A New Short Grammar of the Greek Testament
**Author:** [[A. T. Robertson and W. Hersey Davis]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Chapter XXVIII of the grammar focuses on 'Consecutive Clauses', specifically sub-final clauses and the use of conjunctions like 'iva', 'μη', 'ποτε', and 'μηπως'. The text explains that some clauses are not strictly 'purpose' clauses but act as subject or object clauses. It details the decline of the future indicative in favor of the subjunctive and provides examples of verbs of fearing and the use of the relative.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Consecutive Clauses
Examination of Greek grammatical structures that function as sub-final, subject, or object clauses rather than strict purpose clauses.

### Theme: Linguistic Evolution
Observation of how the subjunctive mood began to replace the future indicative in the Greek New Testament, particularly in specific clausal constructions.

### Theme: Verbs of Fearing
Analysis of the construction of clauses following verbs of fear, noting the usage of present subjunctive and occasionally the future indicative.
