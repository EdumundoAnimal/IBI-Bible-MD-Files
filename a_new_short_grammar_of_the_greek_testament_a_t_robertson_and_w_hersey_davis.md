# A New Short Grammar of the Greek Testament
**Author:** [[A. T. Robertson and W. Hersey Davis]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Chapter XXVIII of the text discusses 'Consecutive Clauses', specifically focusing on grammatical structures like Sub-Final (Subject and Object) Clauses. It details the usage of 'iva' (hina) as a conjunction, its evolution in New Testament Greek, and transitions to related constructions involving 'ὅπως' (hopōs), 'μή' (mē), 'μήποτε' (mēpote), and 'μήπως' (mēpōs). The text also touches upon the use of the subjunctive mood versus the future indicative after verbs of fearing and the function of relative clauses.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sub-Final Clauses
Examination of clauses that function between pure purpose and substantive subject/object clauses, particularly those introduced by 'iva'.

### Theme: Evolution of Greek Moods
The historical transition in the Greek language where the subjunctive mood increasingly replaced the future indicative in specific verbal constructions, particularly after verbs of fearing.

### Theme: New Testament Grammatical Syntax
Technical analysis of specific conjunctions and their governing roles within Hellenistic Greek as found in the New Testament.
