# Old Testament Wisdom: Wisdom Literature Theme: The Tongue
**Author:** [[Éamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The essay explores the theme of the tongue and lips within the Old Testament book of Proverbs, analyzing its historical context, literary usage, and theological implications. It discusses the connections between Proverbs and the Egyptian 'Teachings of Amenemope', examines the metaphorical use of mouth-related organs in the Bible, and provides a theological critique on the ethical usage of speech. The document emphasizes the power of words to either destroy or bring life, concluding with a call for divine intervention in controlling one's speech.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Historical Intertextuality
The essay posits that parts of the Book of Proverbs were influenced by or closely parallel the Ancient Egyptian 'Teachings of Amenemope', particularly regarding wisdom related to speech.

### Theme: Metaphorical Anatomy
In Hebraic thought, lips and the tongue are treated as synecdoche and metonymy for speech, character, and the inner state of the heart.

### Theme: Ethical Stewardship of Speech
The document categorizes speech into three theological modes: the necessity of silence (prudence), the destructive potential of evil speech (death and chaos), and the life-giving power of righteous speech (healing and encouragement).
