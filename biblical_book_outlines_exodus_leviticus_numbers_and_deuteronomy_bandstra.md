# Biblical Book Outlines: Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy
**Author:** [[Bandstra]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a structural summary and narrative outline of the first four books of the Old Testament: Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy. It covers the deliverance of the Israelites from Egypt, the establishment of the Mosaic covenant and the tabernacle, the priestly laws and holiness codes, the census and wilderness journeys of the Israelites, and Moses' final address before his death in Deuteronomy.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Relationship
The recurring theme of Yahweh establishing, testing, and renewing His covenant with the people of Israel through law, sacrifice, and the tabernacle.

### Theme: Wilderness Sojourn and Obedience
The narrative focus on the trials, rebellions, and organizational shifts the Israelites faced while wandering in the wilderness on their way to the Promised Land.

### Theme: Holiness and Purity
The emphasis on religious technicalities, priesthood rites, and moral codes necessary for a holy people to exist in the presence of Yahweh.
