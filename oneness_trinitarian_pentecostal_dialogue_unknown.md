## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology and the Godhead
Exploration of the tension between Oneness and Trinitarian views. Oneness theology stresses the absolute unity of God without distinction of persons, while Trinitarian theology emphasizes three distinct but inseparable persons in one divine nature.

### Theme: Salvation and Grace
A joint affirmation that salvation is achieved through the unmerited grace of God in Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit, involving redemption from the power of the devil and sin.

### Theme: Biblical Holiness
A discussion on the practical application of holiness as being rooted in the 'Holy One' (God), manifesting as righteous living, modest appearance, and liberation in relationships.
