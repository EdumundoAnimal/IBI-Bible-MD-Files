## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Abide (Theological Concept)
Exploration of the Greek word 'menō' and its usage in the New Testament to signify remaining, enduring, and the vital, dependent relationship between Christ and believers.

### Theme: Abolitionism
Historical account of the movement to end slave trade and slavery, driven by religious and secular leaders in Britain and America, and its connection to nineteenth-century social reform.

### Theme: Abomination of Desolation
Theological and prophetic interpretation of the phrase in Matthew 24:15 and Mark 13:14, linking it to the historical desolation of the temple by Antiochus Epiphanes.
