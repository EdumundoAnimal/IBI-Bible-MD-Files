# Evangelical Dictionary of Theology
**Author:** [[Walter A. Elwell]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an entry on Trinity, beginning with its definition as a term designating one God in three persons. It discusses the theological framework, noting that while the term itself is not biblical, the concept is found throughout Scripture, particularly in the New Testament with references to the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. The entry highlights the historical struggle of patristic theology to maintain the balance between the unity of God and the distinctness of the three persons, avoiding both tritheism and monarchianism. It also mentions the inclusion of Old Testament references and rationalist objections. A secondary section explores 'Truth' as a philosophical and theological concept, referencing Ernst Troeltsch and his historical perspective on religion and culture, suggesting that religious ideas are conditioned by their historical context.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Trinitarian Theology
The doctrine of the Trinity as one God in three persons, supported by New Testament references and the historical necessity of balancing divine unity with the distinct identity of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Historical Relativism in Religion
The analysis of Ernst Troeltsch regarding how religious ideas and moral values are shaped by, and interconnected with, their specific historical and cultural environments.
