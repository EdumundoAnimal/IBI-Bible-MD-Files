# Outline II Overview: Exodus
**Author:** [[Irving L. Jensen]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document serves as an academic lecture outline for the Book of Exodus. It defines the key event as the deliverance of Israel from slavery in Egypt. The purpose is structured into three parts: the judgment of the oppressor nation Egypt, the deliverance of Israel by Yahweh, and the establishment of Israel as God's special possession. The text provides a comprehensive structural outline of Exodus, divided into three major sections: I. Israel in Egypt (slavery, birth and call of Moses, the plagues), II. The journey from Egypt to Sinai (Exodus, song of Moses, wilderness experiences), and III. Covenant and law at Sinai (The Decalogue, covenant code, and the construction/dedication of the Tabernacle).

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Deliverance
The central theme of Exodus is the liberation of the Israelites from Egyptian bondage through the intervention of Yahweh.

### Theme: Covenantal Relationship
The establishment of a moral and ethical framework at Sinai (the Decalogue) which defines the reciprocal obligations between Yahweh and the people of Israel.

### Theme: The Tabernacle
The symbolic representation of God dwelling among His people, characterized by precise architectural and ritual requirements.
