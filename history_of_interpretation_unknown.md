## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of the history of biblical interpretation, covering various periods including the Old Testament, the Intertestamental period, the New Testament, the Patristic period, and the Middle Ages. Key themes include the definition and need for hermeneutics, the challenge of interpreting across linguistic, cultural, and geographical distances, and the development of interpretive methodologies such as allegory, typology, and literal-contextual reading. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutics
Defined as the art and science of interpretation, focusing on understanding the original intended meaning of a message despite barriers of time, culture, geography, and language.

### Theme: Allegory vs. Literalism
A recurring tension throughout history between seeking a hidden, spiritual 'true' meaning behind a text versus adhering to the plain, literal-contextual understanding.

### Theme: Typology
An interpretive method that identifies symbols or patterns that anticipate or predict future events or divine activity in history, especially linking the Old and New Testaments.

### Theme: Christological Reading
The New Testament practice of reinterpreting Old Testament scriptures specifically in the light of Jesus Christ as the promised Messiah.
