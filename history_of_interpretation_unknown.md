# History of Interpretation
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a summary of the history of biblical interpretation, covering the Old Testament, Intertestamental period, New Testament, Patristic period, and Middle Ages. It explores the definitions, obstacles, and various methodologies used in hermeneutics, such as literal, allegorical, and typological interpretation, while tracking the shift from early oral tradition to Scholasticism.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutics
Defined as the art and science of interpretation, focusing on understanding the principles used to interpret the meaning and intent of a message.

### Theme: Tension of Interpretation
The ongoing struggle between maintaining the eternal significance of sacred texts and the challenges posed by historical, cultural, geographical, and linguistic particularity.

### Theme: Methodological Evolution
The progression of interpretive strategies from literal-contextual understanding to allegorical interpretation, eventually incorporating typological and scholarly-rationalist approaches (Scholasticism).
