## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of Christ
The central theological argument that Christ is greater than the angels, serving as the ultimate revealer of God's word and the mediator of a new era.

### Theme: Warning and Encouragement
The exhortation to the audience to avoid 'drifting' or 'ignoring/rejecting' the message, emphasizing the need for active listening and commitment to the truth.

### Theme: OT Typology
The use of Old Testament scriptures, such as Psalm 102, to establish the divinity and pre-existence of Christ in contrast to the created nature of angels.
