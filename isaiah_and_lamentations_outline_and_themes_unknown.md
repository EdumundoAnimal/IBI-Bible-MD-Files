## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Judgment and Restoration
The text emphasizes God's faithfulness despite the judgment enacted upon Israel. It highlights the transition from warnings of destruction to messages of comfort and restoration.

### Theme: The Servant Songs
An analysis of the four servant songs in Isaiah (42, 49, 50, 52-53), which describe a servant who brings justice, restores Israel, experiences opposition, and ultimately suffers to provide atonement.

### Theme: Covenantal Fidelity
The document explores the idea that even during the exile, the covenant remained in effect, and God had not abandoned his people.
