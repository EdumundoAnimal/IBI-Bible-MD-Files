## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document outlines session materials for a course on biblical hermeneutics, covering special hermeneutics for specific genres including the Gospels, Old Testament narratives, and Old Testament poetry. It provides methodological approaches to interpreting these genres, focusing on historical, literary, and theological contexts. Specific examples include the analysis of the book of Daniel, the Psalms, and Lamentations 3, emphasizing the importance of understanding structure, authorial intent, and the historical circumstances of the texts.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Special Hermeneutics
Moving beyond general principles to apply specific interpretive frameworks suited to unique biblical genres such as narratives, poetry, and gospels.

### Theme: Contextual Interpretation
The necessity of grounding interpretation in the historical, literary, and theological context of the biblical text, often requiring horizontal and vertical reading strategies.

### Theme: The Function of Lament
Viewing poetry and lamentations not just as historical records, but as authorized, authentic expressions of human grief and protest in relationship with God.

### Theme: Narrative Analysis
Understanding that OT narratives often illustrate doctrines rather than stating them directly and that characters are not necessarily moral exemplars.
