## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hesed (Covenantal Love)
The foundational theological anchor of the psalm; it is characterized not as a passive emotion, but as an active, historical intervention of Yahweh to preserve covenant partners.

### Theme: Human Fragility and Crisis
The psalm utilizes four archetypal vignettes (Desert, Prison, Sickness, Storm-Tossed Sea) to demonstrate the collapse of human self-sufficiency and the necessity of divine rescue.

### Theme: Theological Reversal
A thematic movement where God humbles the arrogant/self-sufficient and exalts the destitute, reflecting divine sovereignty over creation, history, and human sociopolitical structures.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The interpretation of Psalm 107 within the New Testament context, viewing Jesus as the Incarnate Word (Logos) who fulfills the roles of Healer, Liberator, and sovereign Master over chaotic waters.
