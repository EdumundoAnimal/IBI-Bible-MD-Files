# Exegetical Analysis of John 15:1-5 and the Implications of Vine/Branch Imagery for Sustainable Church Leadership
**Author:** [[Éamon Parkes]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Dissertation Proposal Breakdown: 1. Definition of Problem 2. Determination of Purpose 3. Design of Methodology. The document outlines a research proposal addressing the high drop-out rates of church pastors and leaders in the United States. It seeks to apply the biblical imagery of the vine and branches from John 15:1-5 to modern leadership sustainability, focusing on themes of unity in Christ, fruitfulness, and life balance. The research intends to perform an exegetical analysis, examine first-century viticulture practices, and reconstruct the historical context of both Jesus' final discourse and the apostolic intent for a persecuted church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sustainable Church Leadership
Addressing the rising trend of pastoral and ministry leader drop-outs in the US through the lens of biblical wisdom.

### Theme: Biblical Exegesis
Critical analysis of John 15:1-5 to understand the theological implications of vine and branch imagery in the context of the first-century church.

### Theme: Historical and Cultural Context
Utilizing historical knowledge of 1st-century viticulture (wine making) as a parable to better understand the intended meaning of Jesus' teaching.
