## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Stewardship of Gifts
The biblical concept that God equips individuals uniquely through the Spirit for specific purposes, requiring believers to discern and focus on their primary calling.

### Theme: Sustainable Ministry
The principle that effective service relies on alignment with internal motivation (passions) and divine enablement (gifts) rather than merely relying on learned capabilities (talents), which can lead to burnout.
