# Theology of the New Testament: Volume One
**Author:** [[Rudolf Bultmann]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document consists of pages from Rudolf Bultmann's 'Theology of the New Testament', specifically covering sections on the 'Kerygma of the Hellenistic Church', focusing on the titles 'Lord and Son of God' and the early Christian understanding of the sacraments. The text explores the transition from Jewish-Christian contexts to Hellenistic environments, analyzing how concepts like 'Kyrios' (Lord) and 'Son of God' were adopted, reinterpreted, and used in liturgical, baptismal, and exorcistic practices of the early church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hellenization of Christology
Bultmann argues that as Christianity moved into the Hellenistic world, it integrated cultural concepts such as the 'divine man' (theios aner) and cosmic intermediary roles to express the significance of Jesus.

### Theme: Kyrios Title
The term 'Kyrios' (Lord) transformed from a traditional title to an expression of cultic worship, establishing Christ's divine authority within the community's liturgical life.

### Theme: Sacramental Theology
The text discusses how early baptism and the Eucharist were perceived as essential, objective rites for salvation, transitioning from mere symbolic acts to institutional requirements.
