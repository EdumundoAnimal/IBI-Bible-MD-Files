# The Ante-Nicene Fathers, Vol. II: The Pastor of Hermas
**Author:** [[Hermas]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This volume contains the writings of the Fathers of the second century, including Hermas, Tatian, Athenagoras, Theophilus, and Clement of Alexandria. The Pastor of Hermas, presented here in translation, consists of Visions, Commandments, and Similitudes, offering early Christian moral and theological instruction. The text explores themes of repentance, the nature of innocent faith, the relationship between worldly wealth and spiritual duty, and the promise of salvation for those who remain pure. Significant attention is given to the metaphor of the church as a building and the necessity of adhering to divine commandments.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Repentance and Purity
The text emphasizes the necessity of turning away from wicked thoughts and maintaining the purity of a child, suggesting that those who preserve innocence are particularly precious to God.

### Theme: Worldly Wealth vs. Spiritual Duty
A recurring theme involves the incompatibility of worldly attachments with the kingdom of God, advising believers to view themselves as sojourners and to use their resources for the benefit of the church rather than personal gain.

### Theme: The Church as a Building
The use of architectural metaphors, specifically the construction of a tower, represents the assembly of the faithful, where only those who are 'guileless' and 'white' are suitable for the divine edifice.
