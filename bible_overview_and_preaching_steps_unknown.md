# Bible Overview and Preaching Steps
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
A) Use 6 steps for preaching: 1. Pray at start. 2. Read the word passage first. 3. Pray. 4. Pray at the end also with main point. 5. Leave intro to end. 6. Use simple words. Use 3 letters like Hope, Holiness, Harmony. Don't say too much, keep it simple. Personalize. Hope set on Jesus Christ returns. Start with a bang. Repetition of main point. Agape-devoted love. For essay, compare yourself, he who wants to be greatest must be least. Bible Overview: A structural timeline of biblical history from Eden to the second coming, focusing on the kingdom of God.
