## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Nature of Righteousness
The text contrasts the actions of the Pharisee with the widow, defining righteousness as works of justice (7b10) and the kingdom of God as righteousness, peace, and joy (7a2, 8b3).

### Theme: Authority and Fear
The text establishes the fear of the Lord as the beginning of wisdom (7b2) and notes that while demons fear the Son (8a12, 7b5), the disciples of the Lord act with authority and truth (8b6).

### Theme: The Role of the Church
Elders are described as directing the church through love for the Lord and seeking wisdom (8b5), while teachers and servants are tasked with instruction and correspondence (7a9).
