## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Characteristics of wisdom Psalms: More horizontally than vertically oriented; Didactic in nature; Frequently contains a blessing; Contrasts the lifestyle of the righteous with the wicked. ## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices

Wisdom psalms are didactic, horizontally oriented regarding human conduct, often feature blessings, and contrast the righteous with the wicked.
