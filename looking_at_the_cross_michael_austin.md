# Looking at the Cross
**Author:** [[Michael Austin]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This text is an introductory theological study on Christology, focusing on the Letter to the Hebrews. It explores the significance of Jesus' death, the nature of salvation, the deity of Christ, and the relationship between Old Testament prophecy and New Testament fulfillment. The book is structured into chapters covering impressions of the cross, the nature of sin and death, the sovereign purpose of God, and the practical application for modern faith in a pluralistic world.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sovereignty and Redemption
The text argues that God's sovereign purpose in creation and salvation is central to the Epistle to the Hebrews, establishing that Christ's death is the fulfillment of God's overarching plan for humanity.

### Theme: Christology in Hebrews
A focus on the identity of Jesus as both fully divine and fully human, emphasizing his role as the mediator, the high priest, and the final sacrifice for sins.

### Theme: Modernity vs. Biblical Authority
The author contrasts contemporary pluralistic and postmodern worldviews with the biblical teaching of the absolute authority and finality of Scripture.
