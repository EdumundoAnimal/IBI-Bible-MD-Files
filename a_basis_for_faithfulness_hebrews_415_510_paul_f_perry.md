# A Basis for Faithfulness: Hebrews 4:15-5:10
**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Lesson 4: A Basis for Faithfulness - Hebrews 4:15-5:10. In Hebrews 3:1-4:14 the pastor sets forth for his friends a basis for faithfulness in the fact that the Son of God is faithful. We are called to consider the faithfulness of Jesus as an incentive to faithfulness in our own lives. Jesus compared to Moses: 1. The person of Moses the mediator of Israel's covenant and worship was of central importance to the Hebrews author. 2. The author has emphasized that Jesus is the transcendent Son of God, the mediator of God's final word. The preacher interpreted Ps. 95 7-11 in terms of the account of Israel at Kadesh-Barnea. Two conclusions: 1. Become partakers of God. We must be faithful with firm intention to hold to the end what we held in the beginning. 2. Israel was unable to enter God's rest, because of unbelief. Hebrews 4:1-14 explores the nature, timing, and attainment of 'this rest,' identifying it as a repeated plea calling us to faithfulness.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Supremacy
The text argues that Jesus is superior to Moses, transitioning from the role of a faithful servant in God's house to the Son who has authority over the house.

### Theme: The Theology of Rest
Explores the concept of 'God's rest' as a state of faithfulness that is inaccessible to those who fall into unbelief, paralleling the historical failure of Israel at Kadesh-Barnea.

### Theme: Incentivized Faithfulness
The author posits that meditating on the faithfulness of Jesus provides the necessary incentive for believers to maintain their own faithfulness throughout life's trials.
