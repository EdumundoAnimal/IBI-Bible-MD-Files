# Theology I - Week 1: God is both immanent and transcendent
**Author:** [[David Carnduff MTh]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The assignment explores the theological concept of God's dual nature: immanence and transcendence. The document examines biblical foundations for immanence through passages like Jeremiah 23, Acts 17, Romans 10, and Psalm 139. It further contrasts this with modern versions of immanentism, covering Classical Liberalism, Paul Tillich's existential view, and the Death of God Theology. Finally, the text lists models and implications for understanding divine transcendence.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Immanence
The theological belief that God is present within the created order and actively involved in the universe and human life, supported by biblical references emphasizing God's closeness.

### Theme: Divine Transcendence
The attribute of God being beyond the limits of time, space, and the material world, representing a distinction between the Creator and creation.

### Theme: Modern Interpretations
Critical analysis of how 20th-century movements, including existentialism and theological liberalism, have redefined God's presence as being strictly contained within or synonymous with nature.
