# 05 Synoptic Christologies
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Christology 05 Synoptic Christologies IBI 2010-11. Objectives: To explore the different portraits of Jesus in the Synoptic Gospels, with special focus on Mark. Introduction: The 3 synoptic gospels share common events and focus on the same person. Morna Hooker summarizes 4 common themes regarding the person of Jesus: origins in the purposes of God, Jesus as Messiah, Jesus as Son of God, and Jesus as anointed by the Holy Spirit. Mark's prologue focuses on the question 'WHO is Jesus?'. Jesus and the Kingdom of God: In Mark 1:14-15, Jesus inaugurates the Kingdom of God. Scot McKnight's analysis of the Gospels: The 4 Gospels tell the story of Israel in the light of Jesus, focusing on his death, resurrection, fulfillment of scripture, and the kingdom of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Synoptic Christology
Examination of the shared narrative and distinct theological flavors presented in the synoptic gospels regarding the identity of Jesus.

### Theme: The Kingdom of God
The central message of Jesus' ministry, representing the fulfillment of Israel's history and the inauguration of a new community through him.

### Theme: Self-Centered Theology
Scot McKnight's argument that Jesus' message focuses on himself as the fulfillment of Israel's scriptures, prophets, and temple, making his own identity the epicenter of the gospel.
