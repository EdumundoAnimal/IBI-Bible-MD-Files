# 05 Synoptic Christologies
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides an overview of Synoptic Christologies, exploring different portraits of Jesus in the Synoptic Gospels with a focus on Mark. Key concepts include the origins of the Gospel in the purposes of God, the Messiahship of Jesus, his status as the Son of God, and his empowerment by the Holy Spirit. It also examines the connection between Jesus' ministry and the Kingdom of God, highlighting the dramatic shift in history marked by his message. Scot McKnight's perspective on the Gospel is presented, emphasizing that the Gospels tell the story of Israel in the light of Jesus, focusing on his life, death, and resurrection as the fulfillment of Scripture and the establishment of a new community.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Synoptic Christology
The study of how the Gospel writers (specifically Mark) present the person of Jesus through narrative rather than systematic theology.

### Theme: Kingdom of God
The concept that the Kingdom of God is inaugurated through Jesus' ministry and that entry into this kingdom is dependent on a person's response to him.

### Theme: Fulfillment of Israel's Story
The perspective that Jesus' life, death, and resurrection are the fulfillment of Israel's scriptures and the completion of its long history.
