# Oneness-Trinitarian Pentecostal Final Report, 2002-2007
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a final report from a six-year study (2002-2007) conducted by the Society for Pentecostal Studies (SPS) to foster dialogue between Trinitarian and Oneness Pentecostals. It covers key theological areas including the Godhead, baptism, Christology, salvation, and holiness. The text outlines specific points of agreement and theological reasoning regarding the nature of Jesus, the Father, and the Holy Spirit, emphasizing the roles in redemption, the humanity and divinity of Christ, and the indwelling of the Spirit. It addresses how these groups interpret biblical texts regarding the Father and the Son, seeking to reconcile their differing perspectives on the nature of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness vs. Trinitarianism
An exploration of the dialogue between two major Pentecostal branches, seeking mutual understanding on the nature of the Godhead, the roles of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and the incarnation.

### Theme: Christology
The study focuses on the nature of Jesus Christ, arguing for his inseparable divine and human nature, and how he relates to the Father and the Spirit in the work of salvation.

### Theme: Pneumatological Redemption
The belief that the Holy Spirit acts as the presence of Jesus in believers, facilitating the application of salvation through the roles assigned to the Father, Son, and Spirit.
