# Oneness-Trinitarian Pentecostal Final Report, 2002-2007
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains a final report from a six-year study (2002-2007) by the Society for Pentecostal Studies regarding the dialogue between Trinitarian and Oneness Pentecostals. The report covers major theological discussions including the nature of God, baptism, Christology, and salvation. Page 4 and 5 focus on theological debates surrounding the relations within the Godhead, the roles of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and the nature of Jesus Christ's humanity and divinity.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Relations
Exploration of the relationship between the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, emphasizing the Son's incarnation and role in salvation while maintaining biblical fidelity regarding divine indwelling.

### Theme: Christology
Analysis of the inseparable union of natures in Christ, where Christ acts as the Word revealing the Father, and his eternal communion within the Godhead.

### Theme: Redemptive Roles
Discussion on how the titles Father, Son, and Holy Spirit describe redemptive roles rather than denoting three eternal persons in God, specifically concerning the plan of salvation.
