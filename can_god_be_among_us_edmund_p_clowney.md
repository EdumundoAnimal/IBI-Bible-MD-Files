# Can God Be Among Us?
**Author:** [[Edmund P. Clowney]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document is a theological exposition exploring the biblical concept of God's presence among His people, specifically focusing on the tabernacle, the holiness of God, and how these Old Testament symbols are fulfilled in Jesus Christ. The author addresses the tension between the fear of God's holiness and the human desire for His nearness, arguing that Jesus Christ provides the definitive way for God to dwell with humanity. It covers the narrative of the golden calf, the purpose of the tabernacle, the significance of God's name, and the transition from the symbolic presence in the tabernacle to the spiritual reality of Christ dwelling in believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: God's Holiness vs. Immanence
The tension between the 'distant' terror of God's holiness (as seen at Mount Sinai) and the desire for God's 'close' presence among His people.

### Theme: The Tabernacle as Typology
The Old Testament tabernacle functions as a symbolic structure that both protects sinful humans from God's holiness and provides a path for fellowship, which is ultimately fulfilled in the incarnation of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Covenant Renewal
God's willingness to dwell with a 'stiff-necked' people is framed as an act of grace and covenant mercy, rather than a right based on human righteousness.
