## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Servanthood as Authority
Leadership in the Christian context is defined by service and accountability to the Lord rather than personal power or rank.

### Theme: Task-Oriented Discipleship
The roles of apostle, manager, and servant are task-focused, emphasizing the accomplishment of divine mandates over ecclesiastical status.

### Theme: Cultural Metaphor Adaptation
Early church writers repurposed common social roles like household managers, soldiers, and laborers to explain the communal and sacrificial nature of the church.
