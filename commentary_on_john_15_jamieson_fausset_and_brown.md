# Commentary on John 15
**Author:** [[Jamieson, Fausset, and Brown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a detailed exegetical commentary on John 15, focusing on the metaphor of the vine and the branches to explain the spiritual relationship between Christ and His followers. Key theological points include the necessity of vital union with Christ, the process of spiritual purging, the importance of abiding in His word, and the transition from servant to friend in the disciples' relationship with Jesus. It also includes an outline structure detailing fruit bearing, loving, suffering, and witnessing as central themes of this chapter.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Vital Union with Christ
The metaphor of the vine and branches illustrates that spiritual fruitfulness is impossible without a continuous, active connection to Christ, who is the Source of life.

### Theme: Spiritual Purging
The pruning process, while painful, is presented as a necessary act by the Father to remove what is rank and to increase the fruitfulness of the believer.

### Theme: The Servant-to-Friend Transition
Jesus emphasizes a shift in the nature of his relationship with his followers, moving from master-servant to intimate friendship through the sharing of divine truth and the sacrifice of his life.
