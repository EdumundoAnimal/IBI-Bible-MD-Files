## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Sovereignty vs. Mortal Fragility
The Psalm establishes an absolute binary between the ephemeral power of human 'princes' (ben-’ādām) who return to dust, and the eternal, cosmic authority of YHWH.

### Theme: Covenant Mishpat
A theological framework where God performs concrete actions of justice and mercy toward the vulnerable (poor, hungry, prisoners, widows, orphans) as a signature of His covenant fidelity.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The argument that the redemptive actions of YHWH in Psalm 146 find their definitive incarnate fulfillment in the life, ministry, and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

### Theme: Counter-Imperial Manifesto
Psalm 146 is identified as a liturgical resistance text designed to decouple the community's hope from foreign patronage systems (Persian satraps) and anchor it exclusively in God.
