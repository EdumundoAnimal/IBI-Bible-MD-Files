# Coastline Church Statement of Faith and Mission
**Author:** [[Coastline Church]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Coastline Church Tramore Statement of Faith. About the Bible: We believe the Bible is the complete and final authoritative Word of God. About God: We believe in one God in three distinct persons. About Jesus Christ: We believe in the deity of Christ, his virgin birth, sinless life, death, resurrection, and ascension. About Salvation: We believe humanity is in need of salvation through faith in Jesus Christ. About the Holy Spirit: We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit in the life of the believer. About the Church: We believe the church is the body of Christ called to unity and agape love. Mission Statement: The purpose of the church is to exalt God, edify the saints, and evangelise the lost through the gospel. Objectives include establishing a local church, providing biblical scripture, outreach programs, and obedience to the Great Commission.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Biblical Authority
The foundational belief that the Bible is the inerrant and sufficient guide for all aspects of faith and practice.

### Theme: The Great Commission
The core mandate of the church to evangelize and make disciples of all nations, as established in Matthew 28:19.

### Theme: Trinitarian Theology
The belief in one God existing in three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
