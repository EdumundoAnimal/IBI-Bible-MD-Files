## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Shepherd Imagery
The transition from viewing God as an abstract deity to an intimate, protective Shepherd-King who provides, guides, and hosts the believer, subverting Ancient Near Eastern royal motifs.

### Theme: Existential Reality vs. Secularism
A contrast between the modern 'autonomous self' paradigm, which views dependence as a weakness, and the biblical 'creaturely dependence' on YHWH as an ontological reality and source of true security.

### Theme: Theodicy and Presence
The argument that the 'valley of the shadow of death' is not meant to be removed, but navigated through the proactive presence of God, transforming terror into a transit corridor of intimacy.
