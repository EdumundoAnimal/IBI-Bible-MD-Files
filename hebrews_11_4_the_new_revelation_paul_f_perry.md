# Hebrews 1:1-4: The New Revelation
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
Lesson 1: Hebrews 1:1-2:4. The Old Revelation - The New Revelation. God has spoken at various times and in many ways through the prophets in the past to the fathers. God's Former Speaking - God's Present Speaking. God has spoken in this final age to us by his Son. The Greatness of Jesus: 1. Jesus is God's appointed heir (Right of the firstborn son). 2. Jesus is God's creative agent. 3. Jesus is God's personified Glory (Radiance, reflection back). 4. Jesus is God's perfect revelation (the exact imprint of God's very being). 5. Jesus is God's cosmic sustainer (sustaining all that exists). 6. Jesus is God's unique sacrifice. 7. Jesus has been exalted to God's right hand. Jesus and Angels - comparison between: His name is greater than theirs; his dignity is worthy of worship; his status is greater than theirs; his function is greater than theirs. Christ and Personified Wisdom: Jewish writers assigned four tasks: (1) creation of the world, (2) providential sustaining, (3) revelation of truth, (4) reconciliation of persons to God. Hebrews 2:1-4 exhorts believers to pay close attention to the message spoken through the Son.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Progressive Revelation
The progression of God's communication from the prophets in the past to the final, complete revelation through His Son.

### Theme: Christology
A theological exploration of the nature of Jesus as the heir, creator, sustainer, and exact imprint of God, distinct from the angels.

### Theme: Wisdom Tradition
The connection of Christ's cosmic roles to the four tasks historically attributed to Wisdom in Jewish literature.
