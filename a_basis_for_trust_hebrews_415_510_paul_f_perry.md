# A Basis for Trust: Hebrews 4:15-5:10
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a bible study lesson focusing on the themes of Christ's high priesthood in the book of Hebrews, specifically verses 4:15 through 5:10. It explores the dual relationships of the high priest: the relationship to God and the relationship to the people. The text uses a chiastic structure to analyze the office of the high priest, contrasting the 'old' and 'new' aspects of this ministry. It highlights Jesus's humanity, his ability to sympathize with human weakness, and his obedience through suffering. Key theological concepts include the solidarity of the high priest with humanity, the significance of Christ's suffering, and the believer's access to the throne of grace through prayer.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Solidarity of the High Priest
The argument that the high priest must be selected from among humanity to effectively represent them before God, sharing in their weaknesses and experiences.

### Theme: Christ's Obedience through Suffering
An analysis of Jesus learning obedience through his earthly sufferings, qualifying him as the eternal high priest who can support the covenant people.

### Theme: Access to the Throne of Grace
The theological implication that because Jesus identifies with human weakness, believers are encouraged to approach God in prayer with boldness.
