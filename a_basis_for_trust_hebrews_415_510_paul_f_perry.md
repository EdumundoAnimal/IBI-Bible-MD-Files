# A Basis for Trust: Hebrews 4:15 - 5:10
**Author:** [[Paul F Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the theological significance of Christ's high priesthood in Hebrews, focusing on his solidarity with humanity through his earthly experiences of suffering and temptation. It outlines a chiastic structure for Hebrews 5:1-10, emphasizing the transition from the old to the new high priesthood. The text highlights how Jesus, by learning obedience through his passion, fulfills his role as an eternal high priest, thereby establishing a basis for believers to approach the throne of grace with confidence.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: High Priestly Solidarity
The argument that Jesus, as the high priest, shares human weakness and experience, allowing him to empathize with and effectively represent believers before God.

### Theme: Obedience and Passion
The theological link between Jesus' earthly suffering, his obedience to God's will, and his qualification as an eternal high priest.

### Theme: Chiastic Structure
An analysis of the literary symmetry in Hebrews 5:1-10 that contrasts the old Levitical priesthood with the new, superior priesthood of Christ.
