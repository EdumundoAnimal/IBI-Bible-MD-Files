## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Solidarity of the High Priest
The document argues that Jesus' high priestly ministry is effective because he identifies with human weakness. This is not merely psychological empathy but an experiential sharing of the human condition, rooted in his own suffering and temptations.

### Theme: Obedience through Suffering
The text posits that Jesus learned obedience through his passion, which was necessary to qualify him as the eternal high priest. This obedience is defined as fulfilling the call to suffer death according to God's will.

### Theme: Basis for Prayer
The author connects the high priest's solidarity with the believer's ability to approach the 'throne of grace' with boldness, contrasting the New Covenant access with the limitations of the Levitical system.
