## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Apostasy and Persecution
The text examines the pressure early Roman Christians faced to abandon their faith to avoid state-sanctioned persecution, leading to the risk of apostasy.

### Theme: Social Disunity
A decline in the communal bonds and 'joyful service' that characterized the early Christian community, as noted by scholars Achtemeier and Green.
