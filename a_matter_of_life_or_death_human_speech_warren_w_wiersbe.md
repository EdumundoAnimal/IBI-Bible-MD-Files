# A Matter of Life or Death (Human Speech)
**Author:** [[Warren W. Wiersbe]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document, chapter nine of 'Be Skillful', provides a detailed analysis of human speech through the lens of biblical wisdom, specifically referencing the book of Proverbs. It posits that speech is a divine gift which can be used to do good, to act as a destructive force, or to bear false witness. The author emphasizes that the condition of the heart ultimately dictates the quality of one's speech, arguing that the mouth is a fountain of either life or death. The text further categorizes improper speech into forms such as gossiping, flattery, and lying, while providing practical biblical guidance on how to steward this gift through spiritual discernment and accountability.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Power of Speech
Speech is conceptualized as a 'matter of life or death' (Prov. 18:21), possessing the dual capacity to heal or destroy depending on its usage and the speaker's intent.

### Theme: The Heart-Speech Connection
The document asserts that the mouth reflects the condition of the heart, suggesting that internal spiritual discipline is the primary prerequisite for godly and wise communication.

### Theme: Stewardship of Gifts
Speech is presented as a stewardship from God that must be used intentionally to do good, requiring believers to guard against the misuse of their tongues.
