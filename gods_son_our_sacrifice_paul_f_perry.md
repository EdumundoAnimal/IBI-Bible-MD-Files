# God's Son: Our Sacrifice
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a study lesson (Lesson 7) focusing on Hebrews 8:1-9:28. It examines the superiority of Christ's priestly ministry and sacrifice compared to the Old Covenant's Levitical system. The text contrasts the earthly tabernacle and repeated animal sacrifices with Christ's singular, eternal offering in the heavenly sanctuary. It details the 'New Covenant' as mediated by Christ, emphasizing that his sacrifice cleanses the conscience, unlike the limited cleansing provided by the Old Testament system. The study highlights the transition from limited, temporary access to God under the law to the permanent, unrestricted access provided by Christ's blood.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Superiority of the New Covenant
The document argues that Christ's ministry is superior to the Old Covenant because he serves as a mediator of a better covenant based on better promises, shifting from earthly to heavenly focus.

### Theme: Inadequacy of Levitical Sacrifices
The author asserts that the Levitical system provided only limited cleansing, failing to truly purify the inner person, thus necessitating a once-for-all sacrifice by Christ.

### Theme: The Heavenly Sanctuary
The text contrasts the earthly tabernacle, where high priests entered with blood annually, with Christ's presence in the heavenly sanctuary, making his sacrifice eternal and unrepeatable.
