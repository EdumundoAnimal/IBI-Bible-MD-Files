# God's Son: Our Sacrifice
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a study guide exploring Hebrews 8:1 through 9:28. It examines the superiority of Christ's priesthood and sacrifice compared to the Old Testament Levitical system. Key sections include the mediation of a new covenant, the cleansing limitations of the old system, and the eternal efficacy of Christ's blood as a sacrifice. The text argues that the old covenant arrangement had inherent weaknesses and was a shadow of the superior heavenly reality provided by Christ.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: New Covenant Superiority
Christ's ministry is presented as superior to the Levitical system because he serves as the mediator of a new and better covenant.

### Theme: Inadequacy of Levitical Sacrifices
The Old Testament ritual system was limited by the mortality of priests and the inability to provide internal cleansing or permanent forgiveness of sins.

### Theme: Christ as the Final Sacrifice
Christ's offering of his own blood is portrayed as an eternal, once-for-all event that secured permanent salvation and established a direct relationship with God.
