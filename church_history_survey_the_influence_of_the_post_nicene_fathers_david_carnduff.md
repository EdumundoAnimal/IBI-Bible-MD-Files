The text also contrasts various theological movements like Dynamic Monarchianism and Modalistic Monarchianism, ending with a summary of ecclesiological and sacramental developments in the early church.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Post-Nicene Theology
The evolution of Christian doctrine following the Council of Nicea in 325AD, specifically focusing on the institutionalization of the church and formalization of the Trinity.

### Theme: Trinitarian Development
The theological process of defining the relationship between the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, moving from economic views to the formulated doctrine in the Athanasian Creed.

### Theme: Monarchianism
An examination of early heretical or competing views on the Godhead, specifically Dynamic and Modalistic Monarchianism, which attempted to explain divine sovereignty.
