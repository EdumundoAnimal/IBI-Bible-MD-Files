# Becoming More Like Christ
**Author:** [[PR Holmes & S B Williams]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document explores the concept of 'Christ-likeness' as a transformative journey that is inherently human, relational, and ongoing. It refutes the idea that being Christ-like involves mere religious performance, such as perfect church attendance or memorization of scripture, arguing instead for a deep internal transformation that integrates faith with every aspect of human life. The authors emphasize that being Christ-like is to become more fully human, embracing our unique personhood, spirituality, and connection to the Body of Christ. They discuss the importance of social intelligence and the relational nature of the Trinity as a model for our own growth. Furthermore, the text outlines the marks of this maturity: a consistent intimacy with Christ, a sense of destiny, commitment to the Great Commission, and a lifestyle characterized by moderation, humility, and a fearless, grounded approach to suffering.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christ-likeness as Humanization
Christ-likeness is not about escaping one's humanity but rather achieving the full potential of human-personhood. It involves integrating spiritual realities with daily life, personality, and physical existence.

### Theme: Relational Personhood
Drawing from the nature of the Trinity, the authors argue that being Christ-like requires living in a community, valuing our relational capacities, and supporting others in their unique journeys.

### Theme: Continual Transformation
Maturation is described as a lifelong journey of being 'kneaded' by the Holy Spirit. It is a process rather than a static goal, requiring transparency about our disorders and a willingness to be held accountable.
