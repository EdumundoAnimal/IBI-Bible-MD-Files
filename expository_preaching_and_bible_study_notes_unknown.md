# Expository Preaching and Bible Study Notes
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Verbatim Source Text
### Expository Preaching Notes

Quote! All things are lawful but not all things are helpful! John Chapman. Go easy on Cross Ref. Only use if absolutely necessary. Illustrations against oneself. Let the text do the work. Ask John about Question. Alliteration - Rebellion, Religious, Respectable, Repentance. Charge! Evidence! Verdict! Choice. Explanation - Under Sin - metaphor for something common. Under sin means = Under the power of sin. Pointing us to the tested - Question + Give examples from experience. Colloquialism - an informal word or phrase that is more common in conversation than in formal speech or writing. What would the average Irish person find hard to swallow. i.e. most Irish people think that they are basically GOOD! Therefore this message [Rom 3] is counter cultural! - Personal.

### Bible Study Planning

Funnelling. Keep to teach. Time, culture, language. Presuppositions. 1. what, strenghts, where need growth, what not understand. 2. Principles topic - at this stage in development. 3. Crucial needs - Do they comprehend the foundations of their faith. 1 Salvation, 2 Forgiveness, 3 Spirit Filled Life, 4 Are they sharing Christ with others. 4. Make it 6 weeks.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Expository Preaching Methodology
The author emphasizes letting the text speak for itself, minimizing excessive cross-referencing, and using personal illustrations to connect with the audience.

### Theme: Cultural Contextualization
The notes highlight the importance of understanding the audience's cultural presuppositions, specifically noting how the message of Romans 3 challenges the common Irish belief that people are inherently 'good'.

### Theme: Curriculum Development
The 'Funnelling' approach to teaching involves assessing the audience's current strengths and growth areas, focusing on foundational doctrines like salvation and forgiveness, and structuring the study over a six-week period.
