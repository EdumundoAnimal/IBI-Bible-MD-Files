## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This report provides a comprehensive exegetical and homiletical analysis of Psalm 124. It explores the theological macro-theme of sovereign deliverance and counterfactual covenant redemption. The report breaks down the psalm through linguistic, structural, and historical lenses, examining its structure as a three-part dramatic arc: the hypothetical destruction (vv. 1-5), the actual deliverance (vv. 6-7), and the cosmic doxology (v. 8). It connects the psalm's imagery—specifically the swallowing beast, the raging torrent, and the fowler's snare—to both the historic Davidic context and its Christological fulfillment in the Resurrection. The document concludes with a framework for application, emphasizing radical dependency on the Creator and the fallacy of autonomous self-reliance.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Counterfactual Covenantalism
The theological framework asserting that human survival is mathematically impossible apart from the sovereign, unmerited intervention of Yahweh.

### Theme: Chaoskampf Demystification
The subversion of ANE mythological imagery where chaotic forces are demythologized from independent deities into creaturely instruments of hostility controlled by God.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The interpretation of Psalm 124 as a prototype for Christ's suffering at the hands of collective humanity and his subsequent resurrection breaking the 'snare' of death.
