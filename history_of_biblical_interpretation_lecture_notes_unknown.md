## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document serves as a lecture outline covering the history of biblical interpretation from the Old Testament period through the Middle Ages. Key topics include definitions of hermeneutics, the obstacles of time and culture, the development of OT interpretation, intertestamental commentaries (Jubilees, Philo), NT interpretive methods (typology, Christological reading), the Patristic period (Alexandria vs. Antioch, Church Councils, Augustine), and the Medieval Scholastic movement led by Thomas Aquinas.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hermeneutics
Defined as the art and science of interpretation, specifically focusing on the principles used to understand the intended meaning of a message.

### Theme: Interpretive Obstacles
The inherent challenges posed by the distance of time, culture, geography, and language when approaching ancient texts.

### Theme: Allegory vs. Literalism
A long-standing historical tension between reading texts for their literal, contextual meaning versus searching for hidden, spiritual, or allegorical realities.

### Theme: Theological Development
The evolution of interpretive methods from the biblical period through the church councils, often driven by the need for unity and the defense against heresy.
