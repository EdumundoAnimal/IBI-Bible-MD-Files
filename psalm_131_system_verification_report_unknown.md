## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenant Quietude
The intentional state of inner peace achieved through surrendering personal ambition and self-rule to God, framed as a form of spiritual weaning.

### Theme: Creaturely Humility
The ontological recognition of the boundary between the Creator's inscrutable providence and the creature's finite comprehension, resisting intellectual overreach.

### Theme: Christological Kenosis
The fulfillment of Psalm 131 in Jesus Christ, who renounced divine privilege and intellectual power to walk in humility, culminating in the Gethsemane surrender.

### Theme: Non-transactional Faith
The shift from viewing God as a source of material provision (the 'milk') to valuing God for His personhood, mirroring a weaned child's relationship with a mother.
