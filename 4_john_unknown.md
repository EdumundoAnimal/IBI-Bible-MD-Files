## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Rebirth
The necessity of being 'born again' or 'born from above' to enter the kingdom of God, emphasizing that this is a regenerative act of God rather than human effort.

### Theme: Eschatology
The kingdom of God as both a present reality in the hearts of believers and a future reality at the end of the age.

### Theme: Nature of Faith
Faith in the context of John's Gospel is defined not as intellectual assent, but as a commitment of trust and complete reliance on Christ.
