## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Covenantal Access
The argument that access to the divine is not based on inherent merit or self-righteousness, but is a byproduct of God's 'chesed' (steadfast love) and covenantal faithfulness.

### Theme: The Quarantine of Holiness
The conceptual model where God's holiness acts as a barrier that necessitates the exclusion of evil, characterizing divine judgment as a function of ontological purity rather than emotional vindictiveness.

### Theme: Christological Fulfillment
The interpretation of the Psalms as being fully realized in the person and work of Christ, particularly regarding the 'morning sacrifice' and the provision of an 'alien righteousness' for the believer.

### Theme: Anthropological Depravity
The view that human speech, when deceitful, reflects a state of spiritual death, supported by the Apostle Paul's use of Psalm 5:9 to illustrate the condition of humanity apart from grace.
