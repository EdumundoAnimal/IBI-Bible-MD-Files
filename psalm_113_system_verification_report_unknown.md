## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Sovereign Transcendence via Redemptive Condescension
The document argues that God's majesty is not defined by distance from creation, but by His intentional, self-initiated downward movement into human indignity to elevate the outcast.

### Theme: Theological Inversion
The psalm serves as a 'canonical inversion trajectory' where the status of the poor, barren, and destitute is reversed by divine grace, contrasting with ancient Near Eastern hierarchical models that exploited the weak.
