## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a lecture outline for a Church History Survey course. It provides an overview of Christian history from the apostolic period through the 20th century. Key topics include the Great Schism between the Eastern and Western churches, the development of various denominations (Methodists, Puritans, Pentecostals), and significant theological movements like Scholasticism and Conciliarism. The text also includes a chronological list by century outlining major figures and events such as the Council of Nicea, the Reformation, and the development of the Eastern Orthodox tradition.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: The Great Schism
The historical split between the Western (Roman) Church and the Eastern (Byzantine) Church, driven by doctrinal differences such as the filioque clause, the authority of the Pope, and cultural/linguistic divides.

### Theme: Ecclesiological Authority
Concepts such as Conciliarism argue for church governance by general councils rather than a single supreme figure, contrasting with the centralized authority model of Rome.

### Theme: Theological Development
The influence of Greek philosophy on medieval theology (Scholasticism) and the evolution of church tradition through periods like the Dark Ages and the Reformation.
