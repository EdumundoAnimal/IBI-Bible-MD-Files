# Finding Greatest Alignment and Effectiveness
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document presents a Venn diagram model identifying the intersection of Passions, Talents, and Spiritual Gifts to determine 'Greatest Alignment & Effectiveness'. Key components include: Passions defined as 'what excites you, where you find real (not perceived) motivation'; Talents defined as 'learned skills: what you do well but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion'; and Spiritual Gifts defined as 'given by the Spirit and developed by God through experiences and training'. The model suggests limited results occur when only two of these areas overlap. It concludes with prompts to focus on how God has created and called the individual, identify what re-charges them, and instructions to release tasks that sap energy or are done well but are not one's best contribution.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Stewardship of Gifts
The biblical principle of identifying how God has specifically crafted an individual to contribute effectively to the body of believers or kingdom work.

### Theme: Sustainable Ministry
The idea that alignment between passion, talent, and spiritual gifting is necessary to maintain long-term energy and prevent burnout.
