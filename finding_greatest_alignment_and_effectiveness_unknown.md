# Finding Greatest Alignment and Effectiveness
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a framework for personal discovery and ministry alignment using a Venn diagram model consisting of Spiritual Gifts, Passions, and Talents. The core area of Greatest Alignment and Effectiveness is found where these three dimensions intersect. Focus on how God has created and developed you and calls you to contribute most significantly. Spiritual Gifts are defined as being given by the Spirit and developed by experiences and training. Passions are described as what excites you and where you find real (not perceived) motivation. Talents are described as learned skills: what you do well, but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion. The document suggests releasing things that sap your energy and focusing on what re-charges you.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Alignment
The central argument that personal effectiveness is achieved by operating at the intersection of spiritual gifting, personal passion, and learned talent.

### Theme: Stewardship of Energy
The practical recommendation to prioritize tasks that re-charge one's spiritual and emotional energy while delegating or releasing tasks that sap energy.
