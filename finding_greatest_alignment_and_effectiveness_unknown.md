# Finding Greatest Alignment and Effectiveness
**Author:** [[Unknown]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document presents a Venn diagram model consisting of three overlapping circles: Spiritual Gifts, Passions, and Talents. The intersection of all three represents 'GREATEST ALIGNMENT & EFFECTIVENESS'. Spiritual Gifts are defined as given by the Spirit and developed by God through experiences and training. Passions are defined as what excites you and where you find real (not perceived) motivation. Talents are defined as learned skills: what you do well but if you spend too much time here, you lose focus and passion. The intersections denote: Spiritual Gifts and Passions equal 'What re-charges you'. Spiritual Gifts and Talents denote 'Limited sustainable energy & results'. Passions and Talents denote 'Limited sustainable energy & results'. The text concludes with practical advice: 'Focus on how God has created and developed you / calls you to contribute most significantly. What re-charges you? Release those things you do well but not best. Release those things that sap your energy and pass them on to those who are energized by those tasks.'

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Vocation
The document posits that one's highest contribution is found at the intersection of spiritual gifts, innate passions, and developed talents.

### Theme: Stewardship of Energy
An argument for focusing efforts on high-alignment tasks while delegating or releasing tasks that are skill-based but energy-draining.
