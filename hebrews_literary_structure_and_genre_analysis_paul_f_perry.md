# Hebrews: Literary Structure and Genre Analysis
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The document provides a scholarly examination of the genre and structure of the Epistle to the Hebrews. It analyzes the interplay between exposition (theological instruction) and exhortation (practical warnings). It emphasizes that the book is uniquely structured around a main thesis. The analysis includes a list of specific exhortations found in the text, a structural breakdown of themes such as the 'High Priestly Office of the Son', and a diagnostic mapping of the five major warnings within the book. The concluding portion suggests that the purpose of the exposition is to lay a 'Truth foundation' to support the warnings, ultimately aiming to encourage the believers.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Homiletic Structure
The author argues that Hebrews functions as a homily or sermon, alternating between exposition (theological instruction) and exhortation (practical application).

### Theme: Five Warnings
The text highlights a macro-structure within Hebrews centered around five critical warnings intended to address the spiritual state of the audience.

### Theme: Purpose of Exhortation
The theological exposition serves as a 'Truth foundation,' which makes the warnings viable and supports the overall goal of encouraging the congregation.
