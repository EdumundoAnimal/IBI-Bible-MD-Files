# An Introduction to Pentecostalism
**Author:** [[Allan Anderson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document covers the historical and theological development of Pentecostalism, with specific focus on the rise of Oneness theology. It details the emergence of various Oneness denominations, such as the United Pentecostal Church (UPC) and the Pentecostal Assemblies of the World (PAW), and critiques the core theological assertions of the movement, particularly its rejection of the traditional doctrine of the Trinity in favor of modalism. The text analyzes the implications of these teachings regarding the nature of God, the personhood of Jesus, and the soteriological claims of Oneness proponents, often contrasting them with historical and orthodox Christian theology.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Oneness Theology
The belief that God is a single entity who manifests in different modes (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) rather than three distinct persons in a Trinity.

### Theme: Modalism
An ancient heresy re-emerging in Oneness Pentecostalism, asserting that God's distinctions are merely temporary roles rather than eternal interpersonal realities.

### Theme: Soteriology
The doctrine of salvation, specifically in Oneness Pentecostalism, often centered on a literal interpretation of Acts 2:38 involving repentance, water baptism in the name of Jesus, and Spirit baptism.

### Theme: Critical Orthodoxy
The author's systematic critique of Oneness theology as being logically incoherent, internally inconsistent, and prone to reducing Christian revelation to an 'illusion' of interpersonal dialogue between the Father and Son.
