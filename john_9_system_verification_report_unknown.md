## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Redemptive Teleology
The theological paradigm shift from viewing suffering as retributive justice (karma) to viewing it as a space for the manifestation of divine works.

### Theme: Eschatological Realized Judgment
The argument that judgment is not merely future but realized in the present through one's response to the Light (Jesus).

### Theme: Aposynagōgos (Social Isolation)
The socio-historical reality of total excommunication from the covenant community, serving as a survival motif for the original audience.

### Theme: Christological Irony
The paradox where the uneducated blind man gains spiritual sight while the educated religious elite become increasingly blind.
