## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Aseity
The theological argument that Jesus Christ is the 'Attah Hu' (You are He) of Psalm 102, identifying Him not as a created being, but as the transcendent Creator who remains unchangeable while the cosmos perishes.

### Theme: Cosmic Exchatology (Aeternitas Dei vs. Mortalitas Hominis)
The contrast between the transient nature of the material universe, which is compared to a 'garment' that grows threadbare, and the eternal, unchanging nature of God.

### Theme: Exilic Lament and Restoration
The analysis of the psalm as a unified work reflecting the experience of the exilic community, linking individual suffering and somatic collapse to the corporate hope of Zion's restoration.
