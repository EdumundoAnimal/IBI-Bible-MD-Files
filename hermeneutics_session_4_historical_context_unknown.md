## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Tripartite Context of Interpretation
Biblical interpretation requires balancing three layers: historical context (author's circumstances), literary context (the text itself), and theological context (divine significance).

### Theme: Post-Exilic Biblical Analysis
Using Zechariah 7 as a model, the text demonstrates identifying the author, date, and political climate (Cyrus's decree) to properly situate a text within its historical framework.

### Theme: Hermeneutical Methodologies
The approach prioritizes starting with broad historical foundations before examining specific cultural references, social institutions, and terminology.
