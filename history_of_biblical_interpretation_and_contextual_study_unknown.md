An overview of how biblical interpretation has evolved from early OT and Intertestamental traditions to the post-modern pluralistic approaches of the 20th century.

### Theme: Contextual Exegesis
The methodology of using historical, literary, and cultural background to uncover the intended meaning of a passage, rather than applying modern assumptions.

### Theme: Theological Interpretation
The balancing act of employing academic tools and historical research while maintaining a devotional focus on faith, edification, and subjection to divine authority.
