## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Divine Gift in Labor
The interpretation that human satisfaction in work and life's simple pleasures is a direct bestowal from God, rather than merely an independent human achievement.

### Theme: Semantic Range of Phobos
The theological and linguistic exploration of the Greek word 'phobos', showing it ranges from terror and intimidation to reverential awe and 'holy fear' in a religious context.
