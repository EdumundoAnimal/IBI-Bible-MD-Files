# A New Explanation of Christological Origins: A Review of the Work of Larry W. Hurtado
**Author:** [[Crispin Fletcher-Louis]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
The article evaluates Larry W. Hurtado's three-volume work on early Christology. Fletcher-Louis examines Hurtado's methodology, specifically focusing on his use of Lessing's 'ugly ditch' between history and faith. The review critiques Hurtado's attempt to separate historical methodology from theological commitments while acknowledging his contribution to the study of early Christian devotions to Jesus as divine. It discusses the tension between historical scholarship and confessional stances, particularly how Hurtado frames Christological origins within the context of early Christian religious experiences and monotheistic modification.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Lessing's Ugly Ditch
The philosophical hurdle between historical facts (accidental truths) and religious faith (eternal truths), which Hurtado attempts to navigate in his work.

### Theme: Christological Origins
The examination of how early Christian devotion to Jesus as a divine figure emerged within the context of Second Temple Judaism.

### Theme: Historical vs. Theological Method
The tension between maintaining a detached historical critical perspective and the inevitable influence of theological and confessional presuppositions.
