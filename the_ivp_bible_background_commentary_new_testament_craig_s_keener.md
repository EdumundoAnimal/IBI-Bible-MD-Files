# The IVP Bible Background Commentary: New Testament
**Author:** [[Craig S. Keener]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is an academic commentary on the New Testament, focusing on providing essential cultural and historical contexts to help modern readers interpret biblical texts accurately. It covers topics such as the importance of understanding the original audience, the dangers of cultural blindness, the complexities of translating language and culture, and the necessity of academic rigor in biblical studies. The commentary also features specific exegetical discussions on various passages, such as the Gospel of John, covering authorship, historical settings, and interpretive challenges.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultural and Historical Context
The necessity of interpreting biblical texts within their original, first-century cultural, social, and political environments to avoid misinterpretations caused by modern, Western, or unrelated cultural assumptions.

### Theme: Interpretive Challenges
The inherent difficulty in bridging the gap between ancient cultural practices (e.g., footwashing, dietary laws, hospitality customs) and contemporary understanding.

### Theme: The Role of Scholarship
The responsibility of biblical scholars and teachers to provide accessible tools and contextual information to the church, ensuring that all believers can engage with the text deeply.
