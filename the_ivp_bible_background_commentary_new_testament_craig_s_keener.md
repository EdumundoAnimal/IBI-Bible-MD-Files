# The IVP Bible Background Commentary: New Testament
**Author:** [[Craig S. Keener]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a historical-cultural commentary on the New Testament, focusing on providing context for biblical narratives. It addresses topics such as the historical background of the biblical audience, the importance of cultural interpretation, and specific insights into the Gospel of John and other books of the New Testament. The text emphasizes that understanding the original cultural and historical settings is essential for accurately interpreting biblical messages and applying them to modern life.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultural-Historical Background
The necessity of understanding the original cultural context and the audience's setting to correctly interpret biblical texts and avoid modern misreadings.

### Theme: The Gospel of John
Analytical commentary on the Fourth Gospel, covering themes like its authorship, the historical setting of the Johannine community, its use of irony, and its emphasis on God's word and the Holy Spirit.

### Theme: Application to Modern Contexts
The tension between applying ancient biblical principles to modern, diverse, and often abstract cultural settings while respecting the original historical constraints.
