# The IVP Bible Background Commentary: New Testament
**Author:** [[Craig S. Keener]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a scholarly commentary focusing on the cultural and historical background of the New Testament. It emphasizes the necessity of understanding the original context, including cultural nuances, social norms, and historical settings, to interpret biblical texts accurately. The commentary provides extensive explanations of various passages, particularly within the Gospel of John, addressing issues of authorship, historical context, and the relevance of these ancient writings to contemporary life and faith. It explores themes such as the 'Spirit,' the meaning of terms like 'the Jews,' and how cultural blindness can affect modern interpretations.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Cultural-Historical Interpretation
The necessity of interpreting biblical texts within their original cultural, social, and historical framework to avoid modern misreadings and ensure accurate understanding.

### Theme: Relevance to Modern Context
How understanding the ancient cultural background of the Bible aids in making its message relevant to diverse contemporary cultures and addressing modern theological issues.

### Theme: Johannine Authorship and Context
Analysis of the authorship, setting, and purpose of the Gospel of John, including its relation to Jewish-Christian conflict in the first century.
