# Hebrews Study: Peril and Promise
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This study guide provides an analytical approach to the book of Hebrews, focusing on the priesthood of Christ, the warnings against apostasy, and the superiority of the new covenant. Key sections analyze the auditory and behavioral responses required by faith, the comparison between the Levitical and Melchizedekian priesthoods, and the implications of Christ's sacrifice. The notes address the tension between 'continuity' and 'discontinuity' in the transition from the Old to the New Covenant.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Hardness of Hearing
A theological state of sluggishness in responding to God, contrasting with the 'hearing' required for true spiritual growth.

### Theme: Melchizedek vs Levitical Priesthood
An argument for the superiority of Christ's priesthood, based on its eternal, non-Levitical nature and divine oath.

### Theme: Superiority of the New Covenant
Focus on how Christ's once-for-all sacrifice provides better access to God compared to the repeated sacrifices of the old system.
