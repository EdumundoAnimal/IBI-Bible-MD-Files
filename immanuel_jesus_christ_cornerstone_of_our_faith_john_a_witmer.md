# Immanuel: Jesus Christ: Cornerstone of Our Faith
**Author:** [[John A. Witmer]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the various names and titles attributed to Jesus Christ within Christian theology. It categorizes 108 distinct titles, presenting them as facets of a diamond that reveal the multifaceted nature of Christ's divinity and roles. The text is designed as a devotional and theological resource, emphasizing the richness of Christ's identity as described in Scripture.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christological Titles
The systematic categorization of Jesus's identity through 108 titles to illustrate his multifaceted role in theology, ranging from kingly authority to messianic and servant roles.

### Theme: Divine Facets
The metaphor of a diamond is used to explain that each title represents a different aspect of Christ's character, cumulatively reflecting the radiance of God's glory.
