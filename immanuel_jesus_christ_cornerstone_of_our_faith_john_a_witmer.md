## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Christology
The study of the nature, identity, and various roles of Jesus Christ as defined through biblical naming conventions.

### Theme: Biblical Titles
The categorization of divine and messianic titles attributed to Jesus, emphasizing the multifaceted nature of his character and divinity.
