Characteristics of wisdom Psalms

### Seminar Evaluation

Christology Seminar Evaluation Patrick Mitchel IBI 2010-11

Eamon

Well done on a very well prepared seminar. You had good rapport with the class and managed to cover a lot of ground in a short space of time, while involving the class in useful and relevant discussion.

You decided to read the text, which meant that you covered everything thoroughly, but was a wee bit more inflexible in engagement and interaction.

Your engagement with different perspectives was helpful, if at times leaving little room to explain views in more depth – such as Bauckham and Dunn for example.

Your broader approach meant that actual time in the text was limited – this is an observation more than a criticism, a seminar can't do everything. But perhaps a little too quickly surveying the Christology of Hebrews 1-2 in broad strokes.

You brought out well the creative tension in Hebrews of Jesus' humanity and deity and how this is connected to his priesthood.

The theme of perfection could have been emphasized a bit more I think.

I enjoyed your very creative and imaginative 'application' of contemporary 'drift' from faith to IBI students in our Western context . And this was well connected to the text. Well done.

The discussion questions were a little hard to grasp.

Perhaps you could have made a little bit more of the subverting empire' theme that you raised.

Overall, a very good seminar; good content and application, showing awareness of different views and creative engagement with a good variety of reading.

Patrick Mitchel

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Wisdom Literature
The document defines wisdom as the 'skill of living' and identifies key characteristics of Wisdom Psalms, including their didactic nature, horizontal orientation, and the use of contrasts between the righteous and others.

### Theme: Christological Study
The evaluation highlights the importance of balancing academic breadth with depth, specifically regarding the Christology of Hebrews, the tension between Jesus' humanity and deity, and the theme of perfection.
