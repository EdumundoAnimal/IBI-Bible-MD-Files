# New Century Bible Commentary: Psalm 90
**Author:** [[A. A. Anderson]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a scholarly commentary on Psalm 90, exploring its themes of human transience, divine eternity, and the existential relationship between man and God. It analyzes the Psalm as a communal lament, addressing the brevity of human life against the backdrop of sin and divine judgment, while also incorporating petitions for wisdom and restoration. The text offers detailed verse-by-verse exegesis, addressing textual variants, historical context, and theological implications such as the nature of mortality and the enduring sovereignty of God.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Human Transience
The Psalm emphasizes the fragility of human existence, comparing life to a dream, a watch in the night, or a passing sigh, framing it against the eternal nature of God.

### Theme: Divine Eternity vs. Human Mortality
The argument that God is the eternal refuge for humanity, contrasting the timelessness of the Creator with the fleeting nature of the creature, particularly in the context of sin and judgment.

### Theme: Petition for Wisdom
The theological call for God to grant humanity the wisdom to understand the shortness of their days, thereby enabling them to live with a 'good sense' or heart of wisdom.

### Theme: Communal Lament
A scholarly assessment of Psalm 90 as a liturgical composition used to address the community's suffering and its need for reconciliation and divine favor.
