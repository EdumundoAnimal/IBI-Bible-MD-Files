### First Declension Masculine Nouns

First Declension Masculine Nouns: sing. pl. nom profhths profhtai, voc profhta profhtai, acc profhthn profhtas, gen profhtou profhtwn, dat profhth profhtais. sing. pl. nom neanias neaniai, voc neania neaniai, acc neanian neanias, gen neaniou neaniwn, dat neania neaniais. Note: -as nouns = Doric genitive. If stem of -as nouns in any letter other than e, i, or p, the genitive singular ending is -a, not -ou. ekatontarxhs - centurion, Hrwlhs - Herod, Iwannhs - John, maqhths - disciple, Mwushs - Moses, profhths - prophet, stratiwths - soldier, telwnhs - tax collector, uphrethns - servant, assistant. Nouns That decline like neanias: Barnabas - Barnabas, Hlias - Elijah, Hsaïas - Isaiah, Ioudas - Judas, neanias - young man, Sataras - Satan. These take the doric genitive! Adverb: ou - not. Conjunctions: alla - but (note: becomes all' if before word that starts with a vowel), ara - therefore, gar - for (because), de - indeed, at least really, even, de - and, but (lighter), oun - therefore (heavier), palin - however, again also (mean same as alla), te - and (heavier).

### Enclitics

gar, ye, de, oun and te are enclitics. *: They NEVER come first in a sentence, but second. ou combines with de: oude - and not, neither, nor, not even. ou combines with te: oute - and not, neither, nor, not even.
