## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document contains a structured compilation of Greek language grammatical notes intended for biblical study. It outlines verb conjugations, declensions of masculine and feminine nouns across different cases (nominative, vocative, accusative, genitive, dative), usage of the definite article, and various conjunctions and particles. It focuses on vocabulary commonly found in the New Testament, categorizing words by their morphological structures such as Second Declension Masculine/Neuter, First Declension Feminine, and irregular forms.
