# Church History Survey: The Waldenses and Albigenses
**Author:** [[David Carnduff]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document provides a historical survey of the Waldenses and Albigenses as pre-Reformation movements. It covers the emergence of Valdes of Lyons in 1170, the theological opposition to the Roman Catholic Church, the severe persecutions faced by the Waldenses, and their lasting influence as precursors to the Protestant Reformation. It also contrasts the Waldenses with the Albigenses (Cathari), specifically highlighting the Albigenses' dualistic Gnostic-leaning beliefs and their subsequent suppression during the Albigensian Crusade.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Pre-Reformation Proto-Protestantism
The Waldenses are presented as a group that upheld biblical teachings, rejected Roman Catholic hierarchy, and prioritized Scripture, acting as a direct spiritual precursor to the Reformation.

### Theme: Ecclesial Opposition
The conflict between the Waldensian pursuit of biblical purity and the Roman Catholic institutional authority, focusing on the suppression of lay preaching and the Waldenses' eventual excommunication.

### Theme: Dualism vs. Biblical Orthodoxy
The document distinguishes between the orthodox-leaning Waldenses and the Gnostic-influenced Albigenses, whose belief system was fundamentally different in its dualistic view of the material world.
