# Encountering the Gospel of John
**Author:** [[Andreas J. Köstenberger]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document explores the historical and theological foundations of the Gospel of John. It investigates the authorship, date, occasion, and purpose of the book, contrasting its theological themes with the Synoptic Gospels and assessing its reliance on Jewish tradition and historical settings. The text also maps the history of interpreting the Gospel, from early church traditions through modern critical scholarship and the rehabilitation of its historical reliability.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Johannine Authorship
The text analyzes internal and external evidence confirming that John the apostle is the author, defending the traditional view against critical challenges.

### Theme: Purpose and Occasion
The author argues that the Gospel was written in the wake of the temple destruction (A.D. 70) to encourage Jewish and Gentile believers, affirming Jesus's identity as the Messiah.

### Theme: Theological Framework
Core themes include the incarnation of the Word, Jesus's deity and humanity, salvation through faith, and the role of the Holy Spirit (Paraclete) as a guide for the church.

### Theme: Historical Critical Perspective
The text traces the history of how scholars have interpreted the Gospel, noting a shift from skepticism to a contemporary appreciation of its historical groundedness in first-century Judaism.
