# Lesson 5: Peril or Promise
**Author:** [[Paul F. Perry]]

## 1. Cleaned Document Text
This document is a bible study guide focusing on Hebrews 5:11-6:20. The author examines the themes of spiritual maturity, the peril of apostasy, and the anchor of hope found in God's promises. It discusses the theological challenges of Hebrews 6:4-6, exploring various interpretative frameworks regarding the security of the believer and the meaning of 'falling away'. The text also analyzes the importance of listening and obedience within the Christian life, linking these themes to the broader narrative of the book of Hebrews.

## 3. Thematic Analysis Matrices
### Theme: Spiritual Maturity
The text emphasizes the necessity of moving beyond foundational teachings toward maturity, warning that spiritual 'sluggishness' hinders the ability to understand deep theological truths.

### Theme: Peril of Apostasy
An analysis of Hebrews 6:4-6, discussing whether apostasy refers to genuine believers falling from grace or those who profess faith but were never truly converted.

### Theme: The Anchor of Hope
A focus on the reliability of God's oaths and promises, providing stability and security for the believer amidst cultural instability.
